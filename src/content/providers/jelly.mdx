## About Jelly

Jelly is an innovative independent game studio that creates unique and engaging slot games through Yggdrasil Gaming's **YGS Masters program**. This partnership allows Jelly to develop and publish games using Yggdrasil's industry-leading platform and distribution network while maintaining complete creative independence.

Founded in 2020, Jelly quickly established itself as a creative force in the slots industry, known for developing games with distinctive themes, innovative mechanics, and atmospheric presentation. Their titles often feature unique gameplay twists that set them apart from standard slot offerings.

As part of the YGS Masters program, Jelly benefits from Yggdrasil's powerful GATI technology, regulatory support, and access to operator networks, allowing the studio to focus on what they do best: creating memorable gaming experiences with creative mechanics and engaging themes.
