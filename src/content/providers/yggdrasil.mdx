## About Yggdrasil

Yggdrasil Gaming is an innovative premium supplier of online gaming solutions, known for creating visually stunning slots with unique mechanics and features. Founded in 2013, Yggdrasil quickly established itself as one of the most creative studios in the industry.

The company's **YGS Masters program** partners with independent studios like Jelly, allowing them to develop and distribute games through Yggdrasil's platform while maintaining their creative independence. This partnership model has produced some of the industry's most innovative titles.

Yggdrasil is renowned for introducing groundbreaking features and mechanics, consistently pushing boundaries with atmospheric themes, engaging gameplay, and player-focused innovation. Their commitment to quality and creativity has earned multiple industry awards and a dedicated following among slots enthusiasts.
