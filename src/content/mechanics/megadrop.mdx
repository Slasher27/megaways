## What is Megadrop?

Megadrop is a unique jackpot mechanic developed by **Relax Gaming** that adds time-sensitive progressive jackpots to slot games. Unlike traditional progressives that can grow indefinitely, Megadrop jackpots **must drop within specific time windows**, creating urgency and guaranteed winners every hour and day.

## How Megadrop Works

### Timed Jackpot System

Megadrop features two guaranteed-drop jackpots:

- **Hourly Jackpot**: Must drop within the hour
- **Daily Jackpot**: Must drop before midnight (local time)

Both jackpots have visible countdown timers and prize meters that show current values.

### Drop Mechanism

As the deadline approaches:

1. Jackpot can trigger on any spin
2. Probability increases as time runs out
3. **Must pay out** before the timer expires
4. New jackpot cycle starts immediately after
