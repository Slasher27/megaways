## What is Megaways?

Megaways is a revolutionary slot mechanic developed by **Big Time Gaming** in 2015 that fundamentally changed how online slots work. Unlike traditional slots with fixed paylines, Megaways slots feature **variable reel sizes** that change with every spin, creating anywhere from hundreds to **117,649 ways to win**.

## How Megaways Works

### Variable Reels

Each reel displays between **2 and 7 symbols** on every spin. With 6 reels, this creates a massive number of potential winning combinations:

- **Minimum ways**: 64 (2x2x2x2x2x2)
- **Maximum ways**: 117,649 (7x7x7x7x7x7)

The number of ways to win changes dynamically with each spin, making every play unique and exciting.

### Cascading Wins

Most Megaways slots include **cascading wins** (also called Reactions or Avalanches):

1. Winning symbols are removed from the reels
2. New symbols drop down to fill the gaps
3. If new wins form, the process repeats
4. Continues until no new winning combinations appear

This feature allows for **multiple wins from a single spin**, dramatically increasing win potential.
