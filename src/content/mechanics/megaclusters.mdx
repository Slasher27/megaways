## What is Megaclusters?

Megaclusters is an innovative slot mechanic developed by **Big Time Gaming** in 2020 that combines cluster pays with symbol splitting. Unlike traditional slots, winning symbols split into four smaller symbols, dramatically increasing the number of symbols on screen and creating massive winning potential.

## How Megaclusters Works

### Symbol Splitting

When symbols form a winning cluster:

1. Winning symbols are removed from the grid
2. Remaining symbols split into **4 smaller symbols each**
3. New symbols cascade down to fill empty spaces
4. Process repeats with new wins

This cascading splitting mechanic can create grids with **hundreds or even thousands of symbols**, leading to enormous cluster wins.

### Cluster Pays System

Megaclusters uses a **cluster pays** mechanism:

- Win by landing **5 or more matching symbols** touching horizontally or vertically
- Symbols don't need to be on paylines
- Larger clusters pay significantly more
- Maximum symbols on screen can exceed 10,000
