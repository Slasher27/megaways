## Game Features

### Collection Pot

The Collection Pot is the cornerstone feature of Gifts of Fortune, bringing prosperity through Chinese Feng Shui coins. Here's how this innovative system works:

**Base Game Collection:**

- When winning symbols are removed, they can reveal glistening gold **fortune prizes** (coin values)
- Wild Red Packets appear on the **extra reel** above reels 2, 3, 4, and 5
- Each Wild Red Packet collects all visible fortune prizes on the reels
- The collection pot displays a multiplier that can reach up to **x10**
- All collected fortune prizes are multiplied by the pot multiplier

This feature adds an exciting collection mechanic to the base game, where landing Wild Red Packets becomes crucial for gathering fortune prizes. The pot multiplier can appear at any time, potentially multiplying your collected wealth by up to x10, bestowing true happiness and riches!

**Key Points:**

- Wild Red Packets only appear on the extra top reel (reels 2-5)
- Fortune prizes are revealed behind winning symbols
- Multipliers up to x10 boost all collected prizes
- Creates additional win opportunities beyond standard payline wins

### Free Spins

Reveal 3 glimmering gold ingot Scatters to enter the ornate purple realm of the Free Spins! The transformation from the golden base game to the deep purple free spins environment is visually spectacular.

**Initial Award:**

- 3 scatters = **10 Free Spins** + 2 additional spins for each scatter beyond the third
- Example: 4 scatters = 10 + 2 = 12 free spins, 5 scatters = 10 + 4 = 14 free spins

**Free Spins Features:**

- **Unlimited pot multiplier** - Starts at x1 and increases by +1 for every Wild Red Packet that appears
- No cap on the multiplier - it can grow indefinitely throughout the bonus
- Collection Pot system continues to operate
- Wild Red Packets continue collecting fortune prizes

**Retrigger Mechanism:**

- Landing 3+ scatters during free spins awards **4 additional free spins**
- Plus 2 extra spins for each scatter beyond the third
- Pot multiplier carries over and continues increasing
- Multiple retriggers can lead to massive multiplier values

The unlimited multiplier is what makes the free spins truly special. Unlike the base game's capped x10 multiplier, here your pot multiplier continues climbing with each Wild Red Packet, creating exponentially increasing win potential as the bonus progresses.

### Enhanced Free Spins

Enhanced Free Spins represent the premium bonus round in Gifts of Fortune, accessed by landing a special **Megascatter** as one of your triggering scatters.

**What Makes It Enhanced:**

- At least **one Wild Red Packet is guaranteed** in every single free spin
- This ensures constant collection of fortune prizes
- The unlimited multiplier increases on literally every spin
- Dramatically higher win potential compared to regular free spins

**Same Mechanics, Enhanced Frequency:**

- Still awards 10 initial spins (plus 2 per extra scatter)
- Unlimited pot multiplier still applies
- Retriggers work the same way (3+ scatters = 4+ extra spins)
- The key difference is the guaranteed wild collection on every spin

The Enhanced Free Spins transform the bonus from an exciting feature into a truly opulent experience, where the combination of guaranteed wilds and unlimited multipliers can lead to spectacular riches. This is where the game's maximum potential truly shines!

### Win Exchange™

Use Win Exchange™ to grant yourself a chance at riches by converting big base game wins into shots at the Free Spins bonus!

**How It Works:**

- **Wins between 25x-100x stake:** You can gamble your entire win for a chance to be awarded 10 Free Spins. The probability is displayed in green on a wheel spinner.
- **Wins of 100x stake or more:** You can exchange exactly 100x your stake for a guaranteed 10 Free Spins.

**Strategic Considerations:**

The Win Exchange™ feature adds an interesting risk-reward decision. When you land a solid win in the base game, you can:

1. **Take the guaranteed cash win**
2. **Gamble for Free Spins** (if 25x-100x) - risky but potentially more rewarding
3. **Pay 100x for guaranteed Free Spins** (if 100x+) - secure access to the bonus

This feature is particularly valuable in very high volatility slots like this, where free spins bonus rounds often deliver the biggest wins. A 50x base game win might seem nice, but exchanging it for a shot at free spins with unlimited multipliers could yield far greater returns!
