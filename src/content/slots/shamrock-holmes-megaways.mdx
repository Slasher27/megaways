## The Megaways™ Engine: 117,649 Ways Through the Forest

### Understanding Megaways™ in Shamrock Holmes

Shamrock Holmes Megaways runs on the licensed **Megaways™** engine pioneered by Big Time Gaming — the variable-ways system in which every reel displays a different number of symbols on every spin, and the total ways to win are recalculated dynamically. Its arrival here was genuinely significant: this was the first time Microgaming's vast platform carried the mechanic, with All41 Studios handling development duties.

**How Megaways™ Works in Shamrock Holmes:**

- **6 vertical reels**, each showing between **2 and 7 symbols** per spin
- An **extra horizontal reel** sits above reels two, three, four and five, adding **four more symbols** to every spin
- **Ways to win** are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
- **Maximum ways**: 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 = **117,649 ways to win**
- Wins pay for **matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right**, regardless of position

**Win Formation Principles:**

- Symbol size and exact row position are irrelevant — only the reel sequence matters
- A winning combination must begin on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
- Multiple separate winning combinations can land and pay on a single spin
- Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your **total stake**
- The ways count changes on every spin, so the grid never feels static

### The Extra Top Reel

The horizontal tracker reel above the main grid is more than decoration. It contributes one symbol each to reels two through five, effectively deepening those middle reels — and it is the exclusive home of two crucial symbols. The **rainbow Wild** appears only here, substituting for all paying symbols when it lands. The top reel is also where **retrigger scatters** land during free spins, so your eyes will be drawn upward at the most dramatic moments.

### Rolling Reels™: The Cascade Mechanic

Shamrock Holmes uses Microgaming's long-running **Rolling Reels™** system — its house version of the cascading wins found across the Megaways genre. When you land a winning combination:

1. The symbols that formed the win are removed from the grid
2. Symbols above tumble down to fill the gaps
3. Fresh symbols roll in from above to complete the reels
4. If the new arrangement forms another win, it pays and the process repeats
5. Rolling continues until a spin produces no further wins

In the base game, Rolling Reels™ simply extend your winning chains. In the free spins round they become the beating heart of the whole game, because **every winning cascade pushes the progressive multiplier one step higher** — and that multiplier never resets during the bonus.

## Free Spins: The Unlimited Multiplier Bonus

The main bonus round is where Shamrock Holmes Megaways earns its 28,000x reputation. - **Each additional scatter** beyond the fourth adds **2 extra spins**
- So five scatters award 12 spins, six scatters award 14 spins, and so on

### The Unlimited Progressive Multiplier

The defining mechanic of the bonus is its **win multiplier**, which follows the classic Bonanza-style blueprint:

- The multiplier **starts at 2x** — every win in the bonus is at least doubled from the very first spin
- It **increases by 1** with each winning cascade delivered by Rolling Reels™
- There is **no cap** — the multiplier can climb indefinitely
- It **never resets** between spins for the duration of the bonus

This is where the mathematics turn explosive. A long Rolling Reels™ chain doesn't just pay several wins — it ratchets the multiplier higher for every subsequent spin of the round. Ten spins into a lively bonus you might be sitting on a 15x or 20x multiplier, at which point even a modest multi-way hit becomes a serious payout. The **28,000x maximum win** lives at the far end of this curve: a high multiplier meeting a big multi-way combination on a wide board.

### Retriggers

The bonus can extend itself. During free spins, scatters landing on the **extra top reel** add more spins:

- **3 scatters** on the top reel = **3 additional free spins**
- **4 scatters** on the top reel = **4 additional free spins**

Because the multiplier never resets, every retrigger is disproportionately valuable — extra spins late in a bonus are played at whatever elevated multiplier you have already built.

**Example Free Spins Sequence:**

The structure rewards patience within the round itself: the final spins of a good bonus are always the most valuable ones you play.
