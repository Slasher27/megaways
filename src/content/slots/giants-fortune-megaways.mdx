## The Megaways™ Engine: 117,649 Ways Up the Beanstalk

### Understanding Megaways™ in Giant's Fortune

At the core of Giant's Fortune Megaways sits the licensed **Megaways™** engine, the variable-ways system that replaced fixed paylines with a constantly shifting grid. On each spin, every reel can display a different number of symbols, and the total number of ways to win is recalculated dynamically. This is what produces the game's headline figure of 117,649 ways.

**How Megaways™ Works in Giant's Fortune:**

- **6 vertical reels**, each capable of showing between **2 and 7 symbols** per spin
- **Ways to win** are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
- **Maximum ways**: 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 = **117,649 ways to win**
- **Minimum ways**: 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 64 ways to win
- Wins pay for **matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right**, regardless of position

**Win Formation Principles:**

- Symbol size and exact row position do not matter — only the reel sequence counts
- A winning combination must begin on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
- Multiple separate winning combinations can land and pay on a single spin
- Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your **total stake**, not a per-line bet
- The number of ways changes every spin, so no two spins feel mathematically identical

### Cascading Reels

Like most Megaways™ slots, Giant's Fortune Megaways uses **cascading reels** (also called tumbles or avalanches). When you land a winning combination:

1. The symbols that formed the win are removed from the grid
2. The symbols above them drop down to fill the empty spaces
3. New symbols fall in from above to complete the reels
4. If the new arrangement creates another win, it pays and the process repeats
5. Cascades continue until no further wins land

Cascades are central to this game's potential because they feed directly into both the holding wilds and the climbing multiplier. A single paid spin can chain into several consecutive wins, and when Super Stake or the free-spins multiplier is involved, each link in that chain can be worth progressively more.

## Holding Feather Wilds: Sticky Help on the Reels

### Wilds That Wait Their Turn

One of the most distinctive base-game mechanics in Giant's Fortune Megaways is the **holding feather wild**. Wild symbols appear as golden feathers, and unlike standard wilds that vanish at the end of a spin, these **stick to the reels until they help form a winning way**.

**How Holding Wilds Work:**

- A feather wild lands and substitutes for paying symbols to complete combinations
- If the wild does not contribute to a win on the spin it lands, it **remains in place**
- The wild carries over from spin to spin until it finally helps build a winning way
- Once it has been used in a win, it is removed during the cascade

**Why Holding Wilds Matter:**

- **Building Potential**: A wild that sticks around effectively gives you a head start on the next spin
- **Cascade Synergy**: A held wild can join a fresh batch of symbols to spark a cascade chain
- **Anticipation**: Watching a stuck wild wait for the right symbols builds genuine tension

Because the wilds persist, the reels can gradually accumulate help over a series of spins, and when several held wilds finally combine with high-paying symbols, the result can be one of the slot's standout base-game moments.

## Super Stake: Double Your Bet, Climb the Multiplier

### Raising the Stakes for Bigger Rewards

The **Super Stake** mechanic is Giant's Fortune Megaways' signature optional feature, and it fundamentally changes how the base game plays. When you activate Super Stake, you **double your total stake**, and in exchange the game unlocks a powerful set of advantages.

**What Super Stake Does:**

- **Doubles your wager** — a £1.00 bet becomes a £2.00 Super Stake bet
- Introduces a **base-game win multiplier** that starts at x1
- The multiplier **increases by 1 with every cascade** (win reaction)
- **Lowers the bonus trigger requirement** — features can trigger on 3 symbols instead of 4
- The accumulated multiplier **carries over into the free-spins round** if you trigger it

**Why Super Stake Matters:**

- **Compounding Wins**: The climbing multiplier turns a good cascade chain into a great one
- **Easier Features**: Reducing the scatter and bonus-symbol requirement makes the lucrative rounds noticeably more reachable
- **Multiplier Carryover**: Entering free spins with a multiplier already built up is one of the most powerful situations in the entire game

**Strategic Note:**

Super Stake is not free — doubling your stake means your bankroll depletes twice as fast on losing spins. It is a genuine high-risk, high-reward toggle. Players chasing the bigger features and the 18,272x ceiling will find it tempting, while those preserving their bankroll can leave it off and still enjoy the standard game.

## The Egg Collector Bonus

### Pick, Collect and Fill the Grid

The first of the slot's two bonus rounds is the **Egg Collector**, triggered by landing **four or more golden egg bonus symbols** (or three when Super Stake is active). This is a pick-and-collect style round where you accumulate multiplier values across a grid.

**How the Egg Collector Works:**

- You begin the round with a set number of spins (typically three)
- Egg symbols that land carry **bet multiplier values**
- Collected multipliers fill positions on the grid
- **Completing a full row expands** the available positions and resets your spins
- **Filling the entire grid** awards the round's headline prize of **10,000x your stake**

**Why the Egg Collector Matters:**

- **Tangible Goal**: Filling the grid is a clear, satisfying objective to chase
- **Re-energising Spins**: Completing rows refreshes your spin count, extending the round
- **Big Ceiling**: The full-grid 10,000x reward is one of the slot's biggest single payouts

The Egg Collector is a change of pace from the spinning reels — a collection round with a clear target and a genuinely large reward for filling every position. It rewards patience and a good run of egg symbols.

## Free Spins: An Unlimited Climbing Multiplier

### Where the Biggest Wins Are Forged

The headline bonus is the **Free Spins** round, triggered by landing **four or more scatter symbols** (the castle icon), or **three** with Super Stake enabled. This is where Giant's Fortune Megaways reaches toward its highest potential.

**How Free Spins Works:**

- You are awarded **12 free spins** to start, plus **three extra spins for each additional scatter** over the trigger amount
- A **win multiplier** is active throughout the round
- The multiplier **increases by 1 with every cascade (win reaction)** — and crucially, it has **no upper limit**
- Unlike the base game, the multiplier **does not reset between spins** during the round
- Landing more scatters **retriggers** the feature for additional spins

**Why Free Spins Matters:**

- **Unlimited Multiplier**: With no cap, a long run of cascades can build a genuinely enormous multiplier
- **Compounding Power**: Each cascade both pays a win and raises the multiplier for the next
- **Super Stake Carryover**: Entering with a pre-built multiplier from Super Stake gives the round a flying start
- **Retriggers**: Extra scatters extend the feature, giving the multiplier more time to climb

**Example:**

- £1.00 stake, free spins running with the multiplier at x8 after a strong cascade run
- A symbol pays 25x across a high-ways board
- With the x8 multiplier: 25x × 8 = **200x stake = £200.00** from that win alone, before any further cascade chains

The combination of the Megaways™ engine, cascading reels and an uncapped climbing multiplier is exactly how Giant's Fortune Megaways approaches its 18,272x maximum win. The free-spins round is the engine room of the slot's big-win potential.

## How to Win: Strategy & Tips

Giant's Fortune Megaways is a high-volatility game, so a patient, disciplined approach is essential. Here is how to give yourself the best climb up the beanstalk.

### Bankroll Management

- **Size your bankroll for variance**: High volatility (4/5) means longer gaps between meaningful wins. Divide your session budget so you can comfortably absorb a stretch of dry spins while you wait for a feature.
- **Account for Super Stake**: Remember that Super Stake doubles your stake. If you play with it on, your bankroll lasts roughly half as long, so size your bets accordingly.
- **Set win and loss limits**: Decide in advance when you will walk away. The 18,272x ceiling is a big target, but most sessions will revolve around feature triggers rather than the absolute maximum.

### Base Game Strategy

- **Decide on Super Stake deliberately**: Super Stake makes features easier to trigger and builds a multiplier, but at double the cost. Weigh that trade-off against your bankroll and risk appetite.
- **Value the holding wilds**: Held feather wilds give you accumulated help on future spins, so a board with stuck wilds is more dangerous than it looks.
- **Use cascades to your advantage**: A single paid spin can chain into multiple wins, and with Super Stake each cascade also raises the multiplier.

### Bonus Round Strategy

- **Treat the Egg Collector as a target chase**: Completing rows extends the round, and filling the whole grid awards 10,000x — keep an eye on how close you are.
- **Let the free-spins multiplier build**: The uncapped multiplier is where the slot's potential lives. The longer your cascade run, the higher it climbs, so retriggers are especially valuable.
- **Practice in the demo first**: Use the free-play version to understand how Super Stake, the holding wilds and the climbing multiplier interact before committing real funds.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the RTP of Giant's Fortune Megaways?**

Giant's Fortune Megaways has a default Return to Player (RTP) of **96.0%**, which sits right on the modern Megaways average. Note that some operators may run lower configurations, so it is always worth checking the RTP displayed in the game's information panel at the casino where you play.

**What is the maximum win in Giant's Fortune Megaways?**

The maximum win is **18,272x your stake**. The Egg Collector round can also award a fixed **10,000x** for filling the entire grid, but the slot's overall theoretical ceiling of 18,272x is most realistically approached through the free-spins round and its uncapped climbing multiplier.

**How volatile is Giant's Fortune Megaways?**

The game is rated **high volatility (4 out of 5)**. This means longer gaps between meaningful wins in exchange for bigger potential payouts, so it suits players who are comfortable riding out the swings of a high-variance slot.

**Does Giant's Fortune Megaways have a bonus buy?**

**No.** Giant's Fortune Megaways does not offer a bonus buy feature, so you must trigger the Egg Collector and Free Spins rounds organically. Activating **Super Stake** lowers the trigger requirement from four symbols to three, which makes the features noticeably easier to reach.

**What is the Super Stake feature?**

Super Stake is an optional toggle that **doubles your stake**. In return, it adds a base-game win multiplier that starts at x1 and climbs by 1 with each cascade, lowers the bonus trigger requirement to three symbols, and carries any accumulated multiplier into the free-spins round.

**How do the holding wilds work?**

Wilds appear as golden feathers and are **sticky** — if a wild does not contribute to a win on the spin it lands, it **stays on the reels** and carries over to subsequent spins until it finally helps form a winning way, at which point it is removed during the cascade.

**How do you trigger the Free Spins round?**

The Free Spins round is triggered by landing **four or more castle scatter symbols** (or three with Super Stake active). You start with 12 free spins plus three extra for each additional scatter, and the round features an uncapped multiplier that climbs with every cascade. Extra scatters can retrigger the feature.

**Who makes Giant's Fortune Megaways and when was it released?**

Giant's Fortune Megaways was developed by **Touchstone Games for Stakelogic** and released on **June 17, 2021**. It is built on the licensed Megaways™ engine and themed around the Jack and the Beanstalk fairy tale.
