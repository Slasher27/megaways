## The Megaways™ Engine: 117,649 Ways of Festive Cheer

### Understanding Megaways™ in Santa King

At the heart of Santa King Megaways beats the licensed **Megaways™** engine, the variable-ways system that transformed slot mathematics. Rather than fixed paylines, every reel can display a different number of symbols on each spin, and the total ways to win are recalculated dynamically. This is what gives the game its constantly shifting rhythm and its headline figure of 117,649 ways.

**How Megaways™ Works in Santa King:**

- **6 vertical reels**, each capable of showing between **2 and 7 symbols** per spin
- **Ways to win** are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
- **Maximum ways**: 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 = **117,649 ways to win**
- **Minimum ways**: 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 64 ways to win
- Wins pay for **matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right**, regardless of their position on the reel

**Win Formation Principles:**

- Symbol size and exact row position are irrelevant — only the reel sequence matters
- A winning combination must start on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
- Multiple separate winning combinations can land and pay on a single spin
- Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your **total stake**, not a per-line bet
- The number of ways changes every spin, so no two spins feel mathematically identical

### Cascading Reels

Like most Megaways™ slots, Santa King Megaways employs **cascading reels** (sometimes called tumbles or avalanches). When you land a winning combination:

1. The symbols that formed the win are removed from the grid
2. The symbols above them drop down to fill the empty spaces
3. New symbols fall in from above to complete the reels
4. If the new arrangement creates another win, it pays and the process repeats
5. Cascades continue until no further wins land

This mechanic is doubly important in Santa King Megaways, because cascades are not just a source of chained wins — they are also what light the reels and drive you toward the Reel King bonus. Every successful cascade brings you one step closer to the game's signature feature, so even modest base-game wins carry extra significance.

## Lit Reels: Building Toward the Reel King Bonus

### One Cascade at a Time

The defining mechanic that ties Santa King Megaways to its Reel King heritage is the **Lit Reels** system. During base-game play, the six reels can **light up yellow** one at a time, and the trigger for lighting them is cascading.

**How Lit Reels Work:**

- Land a winning combination to start a cascade
- Each cascade **lights one of the six reels** yellow
- Chain enough consecutive cascades on a single spin and the reels light from left to right
- When **all six reels are lit**, the **Reel King** feature activates

This turns every cascade into a small drama: you are not only collecting wins, you are racing to light the full board. A spin that produces a long cascade chain can light all six reels and tip you straight into the Reel King bonus, which is where the real festive prizes live.

**Why Lit Reels Matter:**

- They give cascades a second purpose beyond paying wins
- They build anticipation, since you can watch your progress toward the bonus
- They reward longer cascade chains, encouraging high-ways boards
- They are the only route into the Reel King feature, so they anchor the whole game

## The Reel King Bonus: Mini-Slots and Instant Prizes

### Santa Takes the Reels

Lighting all six reels triggers the headline **Reel King** feature — the mechanic borrowed and dressed up from the classic Reel King brand. One or more **King symbols** (here given a festive Santa makeover) appear over the reels, and each one spins **a one-line mini slot**.

**How the Reel King Bonus Works:**

- One or more King symbols pop up over the grid when all six reels are lit
- Each King spins its own **one-line mini slot**
- Landing **three matching coloured symbols** in a mini slot awards a cash prize
- Cash awards are worth **1x, 2x, 5x, or 10x your stake**
- Symbols with **yellow backgrounds** advance you along the **free spins trail**

This dual-purpose payout structure is clever: each mini-slot spin can either drop instant cash into your balance or push you toward the free spins round. The more mini slots that resolve favourably, the better your haul — and the closer you get to triggering free spins.

**Super King and Mega King Upgrades:**

The Reel King bonus does not stop at its base form. If you keep cascading after the feature begins, the game can upgrade the round:

- **Super King** — a higher-value variant of the Reel King mini-slot bonus
- **Mega King** — the top-tier variant, offering the strongest prizes of the three

These upgrades reward sustained cascading momentum, so a hot spin that keeps tumbling can escalate from a modest Reel King round into a far more lucrative Mega King event.

**Example Reel King Scenario:**

## Free Spins: Unlimited Multipliers Under the Tree

### Climbing the Multiplier

Once the Reel King bonus has advanced you far enough along the free spins trail, you enter the **Free Spins** round — and this is where Santa King Megaways reaches for its biggest wins. The free spins are powered by a **win multiplier** that has **no upper limit**.

**How Free Spins Work:**

- You begin the round with a set number of free spins
- The win multiplier starts at **x1**
- Every **cascade** during free spins increases the multiplier by **+1**
- There is **no cap** on how high the multiplier can climb
- The Megaways™ engine remains fully active, so the full 117,649 ways stay in play

**Red Reels and Extra Spins:**

Just as the base game lights reels yellow, the free spins round lights reels **red** following cascades. When **all six reels turn red**:

- **Two scrolls** are revealed
- They award **1 to 3 additional free spins**

This creates a snowballing effect. A strong run of cascades during free spins both climbs the multiplier and lights the reels red, which then adds spins and pushes the multiplier even higher. With no cap on the multiplier, a single sustained hot streak is the most realistic route to the game's 10,000x ceiling.

**Example Free Spins Scenario:**
