## The Megaways™ Engine: 15,625 Ways That Pay Both Directions

### Understanding the Grid

At the core of Lightning Strike Megaways sits the licensed **Megaways™** engine, but Blueprint has given it a distinctive spin. The game uses six reels, each capable of displaying a variable number of symbols, for a fixed maximum of **15,625 ways to win**. That figure comes from a grid where each reel can show up to five symbols (5 × 5 × 5 × 5 × 5 × 5 = 15,625), rather than the seven-high configuration that produces the more familiar 117,649-way Megaways layouts.

**How the Megaways™ system works here:**

- **6 vertical reels**, each capable of showing a variable number of symbols per spin
- **Ways to win** are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
- **Maximum ways**: up to **15,625 ways to win** when the reels are at their fullest
- The number of active ways changes every spin, so no two spins feel mathematically identical
- Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your **total stake**, not a per-line bet

### The Pay-Both-Ways Twist

The headline mechanical innovation of Lightning Strike Megaways is that it **pays both ways**. In a conventional Megaways slot, a winning combination must begin on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels to the right. Here, the game also awards wins that begin on the **rightmost reel** and run left.

**Why pay-both-ways matters:**

- It effectively **doubles the directions** in which a winning combination can form
- Symbols that would normally be "wasted" on the right side of the grid can now anchor a win
- It increases the hit frequency of small and medium wins, softening some of the high-volatility edges
- It makes the placement of the expanding Thor wilds even more valuable, since a single full reel of wilds can contribute to combinations in both directions at once

This is the feature that gives Lightning Strike Megaways its identity. While the underlying volatility remains high, the pay-both-ways engine ensures the reels feel busier and more rewarding on a spin-to-spin basis than the raw RTP figure might suggest.

## Thor's Hammer Wilds & Lightning Strike Respins

### Mjölnir Strikes the Reels

The signature in-play feature of Lightning Strike Megaways is the **Thor's Hammer expanding wild**, a thematic embodiment of Mjölnir crashing down across the grid. This wild can land on the central reels — **reels 2, 3, 4, and 5** — and when it does, it expands to fill the entire reel, dramatically boosting the number of ways a winning combination can form.

**How Thor's Hammer Wilds work:**

- The hammer wild appears on the four central reels (2-5)
- When it lands, it **expands to cover the full reel**, becoming a stacked wall of wilds
- The expanded wild carries a **multiplier**, typically ranging from 1x to 3x
- Because the game pays both ways, a full reel of wilds boosts combinations in both directions simultaneously

### The Lightning Strike Respin

The expanding wild does not just sit there — it triggers the **Lightning Strike respin** mechanic. When a Thor's Hammer wild lands and expands, it **locks in place** and the game awards a respin, giving you another chance to land additional wilds and extend the sequence.

**How the respin sequence builds:**

1. A Thor's Hammer wild lands and expands to fill its reel, carrying a multiplier
2. That wild **locks**, and a respin is triggered
3. If another hammer wild lands during the respin, it too expands, locks, and triggers a further respin
4. The wild multipliers **combine across the sequence**, and stacking can build them up to a substantial **81x** at the peak
5. Respins continue while new wilds keep landing, then the total win is paid

Because these respins trigger relatively often during base play, the Lightning Strike mechanic is the engine that keeps the game's pulse going between bigger features. When multiple hammer wilds chain together with rising multipliers — and those wilds feed combinations in both directions — the base game can produce genuinely explosive payouts.

## Mystery Symbols: The Yggdrasil Tree

### Unpredictable Transformations

Layered on top of the Megaways engine is a **Mystery Symbol** mechanic, represented by the golden **Yggdrasil tree** — the mythical world tree of Norse legend. When mystery symbols land on the reels, they all **reveal the same random standard symbol**, transforming what was an uncertain grid into a potentially aligned, paying configuration.

**How Mystery Symbols work:**

- One or more golden Yggdrasil mystery symbols can land on any spin
- All mystery symbols on screen then **reveal the same standard paying symbol**
- If they reveal a high-value symbol across multiple reels, they can combine into a substantial multi-way win
- Because the game pays both ways, a swarm of matching revealed symbols is doubly valuable

The mystery symbol feature adds a welcome layer of unpredictability to the base game. A board that looks like a near-miss can suddenly resolve into a strong win when several mystery symbols all reveal the same premium icon — and the pay-both-ways engine maximises the value of those revealed clusters.

## The Mega Spins Bonus: Cash Ladder & Free Spins

### Climbing the Ladder

The flagship bonus of Lightning Strike Megaways is the **Mega Spins** feature, and the route in is unusual. Rather than triggering on a set number of scatters, the bonus is offered whenever you land a base-game win of **5x your stake or more**. When that happens, you are invited into a **prize-wheel and ladder gamble** that gives you genuine control over the outcome.

**How the Mega Spins gamble works:**

1. You qualify by landing a win of **5x stake or higher**
2. You enter a **prize wheel connected to a ladder progression system**
3. From here you can choose to **take your cash winnings** and bank them safely
4. Alternatively, you can **gamble** to climb the ladder for a larger cash prize
5. Or you can **exchange** your position for entry into the **Mega Spins free spins round**

This is an all-or-nothing decision point. Gambling for a higher rung is genuinely risky — a failed gamble can cost you the qualifying win entirely — but it is also the gateway to the game's most lucrative feature.

### Inside the Mega Spins Round

If you choose to exchange your gamble for free spins, you enter the **Mega Spins** round, where the reels are enriched to maximise your win potential. You can win up to **100 free spins** through this system, and the reels are loaded to favour the most exciting outcomes.

**What makes Mega Spins special:**

- The reels are **enriched** with extra wilds and mystery symbols
- Thor's Hammer wilds appear more frequently, increasing respin and multiplier potential
- Mystery symbol swarms become more common, boosting big-win chances
- With the pay-both-ways engine still active, every enriched spin has double the combination potential
- The maximum of **100 spins** offers a long runway for those big hits to land

The Mega Spins round is where Lightning Strike Megaways reaches toward its 10,000x ceiling. The combination of enriched reels, stacking wild multipliers, and pay-both-ways scoring is precisely the kind of environment in which a high-volatility slot delivers its headline payouts.

- **Lean on the pay-both-ways edge**: Remember that combinations form in both directions, so the game hits more often than a one-way Megaways slot at the same volatility. This is your steadying influence between features.

### Bonus Round Strategy

- **Decide your gamble appetite in advance**: When a 5x+ win invites you onto the ladder, know whether you want to bank the cash, climb for more, or exchange for Mega Spins.
- **Chase Mega Spins for the big targets**: The enriched-reel free spins round is where the 10,000x potential really lives, so if you are hunting the ceiling, the exchange is the path.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the RTP of Lightning Strike Megaways?**

Lightning Strike Megaways has a Return to Player (RTP) of **95.96%**. This sits slightly below the modern Megaways average of around 96%, so it is worth factoring into your expectations, though the pay-both-ways mechanic helps the game hit more frequently than the raw figure suggests.

**What is the maximum win in Lightning Strike Megaways?**

The maximum win is **10,000x your stake**. While that is more modest than some ultra-volatile Megaways titles, it remains a substantial target, most realistically approached through the enriched-reel Mega Spins free spins round where stacking wild multipliers can compound.

**How volatile is Lightning Strike Megaways?**

The game is **high volatility (rated 4 out of 5)**. This means wins are less frequent but tend to be larger when they land, so it suits players who are comfortable with longer dry spells in pursuit of bigger hits.

**Does Lightning Strike Megaways have a bonus buy?**

**No.** Lightning Strike Megaways does not offer a bonus buy feature. The Mega Spins bonus must be earned organically by landing a base-game win of 5x your stake or more and then choosing to exchange the ladder gamble for free spins.

**What is the pay-both-ways feature?**

Pay-both-ways means winning combinations are counted in **both directions** — from left to right and from right to left. This effectively doubles the directions in which a combination can form, boosting your chances of landing a paying spin compared with a standard one-way Megaways slot.

**How do Thor's Hammer wilds work?**

The Thor's Hammer wild lands on the central reels (2-5) and **expands to fill the entire reel**, carrying a multiplier. It then locks in place and triggers a Lightning Strike respin. Chaining multiple wilds can build the multipliers up to a peak of around 81x.

**How do I trigger the Mega Spins bonus?**

The Mega Spins feature is offered whenever you land a base-game win of **5x your stake or more**. This invites you onto a prize-wheel ladder gamble, where you can bank your cash, gamble for more, or exchange your position for the Mega Spins free spins round (up to 100 spins on enriched reels).

**Who makes Lightning Strike Megaways and when was it released?**

Lightning Strike Megaways was developed by **Blueprint Gaming** and released in **February 2019**. It was notable at launch for combining the Megaways™ engine with a pay-both-ways mechanic, set against a Norse mythology theme.
