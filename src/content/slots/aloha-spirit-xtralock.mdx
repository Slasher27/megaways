## The Tiki XtraLock Feature: Lock, Merge and Climb the Jackpot Ladder

### Triggering the XtraLock

The beating heart of Aloha Spirit XtraLock is the **Tiki XtraLock feature**, the mechanic that gives the slot its name and its enormous win potential. The feature is triggered when you land **six or more Tiki Mask symbols** anywhere in view on a single spin. The moment that sixth mask drops into position, the carved Tiki faces lock firmly in place and the game shifts into its signature respin round, awarding you **five Tiki Spins** to build on what you have collected.

This is not a free spins round in the conventional sense — it is a **hold-and-respin** mechanic where the goal is to fill as many of the **15 grid positions** as possible with Tiki Masks.

### How the Tiki Spins Work

Once the XtraLock feature begins, the mechanic plays out as follows:

1. The Tiki Masks that triggered the feature **lock into place** and stay put
2. You are awarded **five Tiki Spins** (respins) to land additional masks
3. **Every new Tiki Mask** that lands also locks and **resets the respin counter back to five**
4. As masks accumulate, **adjacent masks merge** into larger combined shapes
5. The feature continues until you run out of respins or **all 15 positions** are filled by one giant Tiki shape

The reset-to-five mechanic is what makes the XtraLock feature so tense and rewarding. Each fresh mask not only adds value but buys you more time to chase the bigger prizes, so a hot streak can keep the feature alive far longer than the initial five spins suggest.

### Merging Masks and the Grid

The merging mechanic is where Aloha Spirit XtraLock distinguishes itself. As Tiki Masks accumulate and sit beside one another, they **fuse into progressively larger shapes**. The more of the 15-position grid these merged shapes cover, the higher up the jackpot ladder you climb — and covering enough of the grid is the only route to the game's most lucrative rewards.

**Why the XtraLock Feature Matters:**

- **Sticky Tension**: Watching masks lock and reset the counter creates genuine suspense
- **Jackpot Pathway**: Grid coverage directly determines which jackpots you can unlock
- **Self-Extending**: Each new mask refreshes your respins, rewarding momentum
- **The Big-Win Engine**: This feature is the only realistic route to the 200,500x ceiling

## The Four Jackpots: Minor, Major, Super and Grand

### A Four-Tier Prize Ladder

Aloha Spirit XtraLock is fundamentally a **jackpot slot**, and the four progressive jackpots are awarded through the Tiki XtraLock feature based on how much of the grid your merged masks cover. The four tiers, from smallest to largest, are:

- **Minor Jackpot** — the entry-level prize for modest grid coverage
- **Major Jackpot** — a step up for filling more positions
- **Super Jackpot** — a substantial reward for strong coverage
- **Grand Jackpot** — the headline prize, requiring extensive grid coverage

### Unlocking the Grand Jackpot

The **Grand Jackpot** is the ultimate goal and the source of the game's colossal **200,500x maximum win**. To claim it you need to cover an extensive portion of the 15-position grid with Tiki shapes — in practice this means landing a near-complete or complete board of merged masks during the XtraLock feature. It is an extremely rare outcome, which is exactly why it carries such an enormous reward.

**How the Jackpots Are Won:**

- The jackpots are tied to **grid coverage** during the Tiki XtraLock feature
- More positions covered by merged Tiki shapes = a higher jackpot tier
- The **Grand Jackpot** typically requires covering at least 12 of the 15 positions
- Filling the **entire grid** with one giant Tiki shape unlocks the top reward

This jackpot structure is what gives Aloha Spirit XtraLock its high-volatility profile. The four-tier ladder offers a tantalising progression, but the truly life-changing prizes sit at the very top and demand a near-perfect feature outcome.

## Free Spins: A Wild Central Reel

### Triggering the Free Spins

Separate from the XtraLock jackpot feature, Aloha Spirit XtraLock offers a more traditional **free spins** bonus. This round is triggered by landing **three or more Postage Stamp scatter symbols** anywhere in view, and it awards **12 free spins**.

### The Golden Pineapple Wild Reel

What makes the free spins special is the **Golden Pineapple Wild Reel**. Throughout the entire bonus round, the **central reel (reel three)** is completely covered by Golden Pineapple Wild symbols. Because reel three sits in the middle of the grid, a fully wild central reel dramatically increases the frequency of winning combinations across the 50 paylines.

**How the Free Spins Play Out:**

- Land **3+ Postage Stamp scatters** to trigger the round
- Receive **12 free spins**
- The **entire central reel** is wild for every spin of the bonus
- Wilds substitute for all paying symbols to complete combinations

With the middle reel guaranteed wild, the free spins round delivers far more consistent hits than the base game, making it the lower-variance of the slot's two main features. It is a welcome counterbalance to the high-stakes, all-or-nothing nature of the jackpot chase.
