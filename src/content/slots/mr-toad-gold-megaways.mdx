## The Megaways™ Engine: 117,649 Ways Through the Reeds

### Understanding Megaways™ in Mr Toad Gold

At the core of Mr Toad Gold Megaways sits the licensed **Megaways™** engine, the variable-ways system that turned slot mathematics on its head. Instead of fixed paylines, each reel can display a different number of symbols on every spin, and the total ways to win are recalculated dynamically. That is what gives the game its constantly shifting rhythm and its headline figure of **117,649 ways**.

**How Megaways™ Works in Mr Toad Gold:**

- **6 vertical reels**, each capable of showing between **2 and 7 symbols** per spin
- **Ways to win** are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
- **Maximum ways**: 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 = **117,649 ways to win**
- **Minimum ways**: 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 64 ways to win
- Wins pay for **matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right**, regardless of position

**Win Formation Principles:**

- Symbol size and exact row position are irrelevant — only the reel sequence matters
- A winning combination must start on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
- Multiple separate winning combinations can land and pay on a single spin
- Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your **total stake**, not a per-line bet
- The number of ways changes every spin, so no two spins feel mathematically identical

### Tumbling Wins

Like most modern Megaways™ slots — and in keeping with Pragmatic Play's house style — Mr Toad Gold Megaways uses **tumbling wins** (also known as cascades or avalanches). When you land a winning combination:

1. The symbols that formed the win are removed from the grid
2. The symbols above them drop down to fill the empty spaces
3. New symbols tumble in from above to complete the reels
4. If the new arrangement creates another win, it pays and the process repeats
5. Tumbles continue until no further wins land

This mechanic means a single paid spin can produce a chain of consecutive wins. It is especially powerful in the free spins rounds, where wilds and their multipliers can attach to a sequence of tumbles and turn a single trigger into a snowballing payout.

## Wild Multipliers: The Toad's Golden Touch

### Wilds That Pay Their Way

The most important special symbol in the base game is the **Wild**, and in Mr Toad Gold Megaways the wild is far more than a simple substitute. Each wild that lands can carry a **multiplier of x2 or x3**, and that multiplier is applied to any win the wild helps to complete.

**How Wild Multipliers Work:**

- A wild substitutes for any regular paying symbol to help form a winning combination
- When a wild is part of a win, its multiplier value (x2 or x3) is applied to that win
- If multiple multiplier wilds contribute to the same win, their values combine for an even bigger boost
- Because wilds interact with the tumbling engine, a single wild can boost several chained wins in a row

**Why Wild Multipliers Matter:**

- **Direct Boost**: A x3 wild instantly triples the value of the combination it completes
- **Tumble Synergy**: Wilds that stay in play across tumbles keep boosting subsequent wins
- **Path to Big Hits**: Stacked multiplier wilds during free spins are where the slot's biggest payouts are forged

**Example — Multiplier Wild in Action:**

- €1.00 stake
- A high-value symbol lands across five reels for a 20x win
- A x3 multiplier wild completes the combination
- The win is boosted: 20x × 3 = **60x stake = €60.00**

Multiplier wilds are the engine of Mr Toad Gold Megaways' excitement. In the base game they punctuate play with welcome boosts, but it is in the free spins — where wilds are far more plentiful — that they truly come alive.

**How Sticky Wilds Free Spins Works:**

- Every wild that lands sticks in its position for the remainder of the round
- Each sticky wild retains its x2 or x3 multiplier
- As wilds accumulate, the multipliers can combine on a single winning combination for enormous boosts

**Why Choose Sticky Wilds:**

- **Combined Multipliers**: Several sticky multiplier wilds completing one win can multiply it dramatically
- **Late-Round Drama**: The biggest hits often arrive on the final spins, once the board is dense with wilds

Sticky Wilds is the patient player's choice — it rewards staying power, building toward a climactic finish as the multipliers pile up.
