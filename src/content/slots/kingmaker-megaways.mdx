If you appreciate a slot that rewards patience with explosive payoffs, Kingmaker Megaways belongs on your campaign map.

The presentation is steeped in medieval grandeur. Whether you are drawn by the 16,807 ways, the strategic build of the House multipliers, or the promise of a 24,250x crown jewel, this is a slot for players who can stomach the swings in pursuit of a throne-room jackpot. ## The Megaways™ Engine: 16,807 Ways to Rule

### Understanding Megaways™ in Kingmaker

At the heart of Kingmaker Megaways beats Big Time Gaming's own **Megaways™** engine — the variable-ways system the studio invented and which now defines an entire genre. Rather than fixed paylines, every reel can display a different number of symbols on each spin, and the total ways to win are recalculated dynamically. This is what gives the game its constantly shifting rhythm and its headline figure of **16,807 ways**.

**How Megaways™ Works in Kingmaker:**

- **5 vertical reels**, each capable of showing between **2 and 7 symbols** per spin
- **Ways to win** are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
- **Maximum ways**: 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 = **16,807 ways to win**
- **Minimum ways**: 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 32 ways to win
- Wins pay for **matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right**, regardless of their position on the reel

**Win Formation Principles:**

- Symbol size and exact row position are irrelevant — only the reel sequence matters
- A winning combination must start on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
- Multiple separate winning combinations can land and pay on a single spin
- Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your **total stake**, not a per-line bet
- The number of ways changes every spin, so no two spins feel mathematically identical

Note that Kingmaker uses a five-reel layout, which gives a maximum of 16,807 ways — fewer than the six-reel, 117,649-way Megaways slots — but this tighter grid is part of what keeps the multiplier-building mechanic focused on its four key gem symbols.

### Cascading Reactions

Like most Megaways™ slots, Kingmaker Megaways employs **cascading reactions** (also called tumbles or avalanches). When you land a winning combination:

1. The symbols that formed the win are removed from the grid
2. The symbols above them drop down to fill the empty spaces
3. New symbols fall in from above to complete the reels
4. If the new arrangement creates another win, it pays and the process repeats
5. Cascades continue until no further wins land

This mechanic means a single paid spin can produce a chain of consecutive wins. In Kingmaker it is especially valuable during the free spins, where every cascade plays out on a fully expanded 16,807-way board with your accumulated House multipliers attached to every win.

## Gem Multipliers: The Four Houses Build Your Fortune

### One Banner Per House

The signature mechanic of Kingmaker Megaways is its **Gem Multiplier** system, tied to the four rival Houses of the realm. To the left of the reels sit **four multiplier banners**, one for each House's coloured gemstone. Every banner begins a session at **1x**, and the central tension of the game is watching them climb.

**How the Gem Multipliers Build:**

- Each of the four Houses is represented by a distinct coloured gemstone symbol
- These gems are the game's **top-paying symbols**
- Every time you land a **five-of-a-kind win** with a House's gem, that House's banner increases by **+1**
- The multipliers are **persistent** — they do not reset between base-game spins
- The banners keep growing for as long as you play without triggering the free spins

**Why This Matters:**

- **Rising Base-Game Payouts**: As banners climb, the base game itself becomes more rewarding, because those multipliers apply to the relevant gem wins
- **Bonus Setup**: The accumulated multipliers become your starting multipliers when free spins trigger, so a long, patient base game sets up a far more powerful bonus
- **Strategic Tension**: There is a genuine push-pull — you want the bonus, but the longer it stays away, the bigger your multipliers grow
- **Four Separate Engines**: Because each House builds independently, a session can favour one or two Houses heavily, shaping how your eventual bonus pays

**Example Build Scenario:**

- You spin the base game for an extended stretch
- The red House gem lands several five-of-a-kind wins, pushing its banner to 6x
- The blue House gem builds to 4x, the green to 3x, the gold to 2x
- When the free spins finally trigger, those exact multipliers carry over as your starting values for the bonus

This persistent-build mechanic is the strategic spine of Kingmaker Megaways. It rewards session commitment and turns what could be a dull dry spell into a deliberate accumulation of firepower for the round that matters most.

## Free Spins: Max Megaways™ and Combined Multipliers

### Triggering the Bonus

The free spins round is where Kingmaker Megaways earns its high-volatility reputation. It is triggered by landing **crown scatter symbols** anywhere on the reels:

- **3 scatters** award **7 free spins**
- **4 scatters** award **12 free spins**
- **5 scatters** award **20 free spins**

Because this is a high-variance game with no bonus buy, reaching the free spins is the central challenge — but the reward is substantial.

### Max Megaways™ Every Spin

The defining feature of the bonus is **Max Megaways™**: during the free spins, the reels are **fully expanded on every single spin**, locking the board to its maximum **16,807 ways to win**. There is no waiting for the reels to land tall — every spin gives you the full grid, dramatically increasing the chance of large multi-way combinations and explosive cascades.

### Combined Multipliers Carry Over

The genius of the round is how it marries Max Megaways™ to the **Gem Multipliers** you built in the base game. The four House banners carry over as your **starting multipliers**, and they continue to grow during the bonus as you land more five-of-a-kind gem wins.

**How the Bonus Pays:**

- Every spin plays on the full 16,807-way board
- Your accumulated House multipliers apply to the corresponding gem wins
- Multipliers can **combine** across the Houses for towering values
- Cascades during free spins chain multiplied wins together
- Continued five-of-a-kind hits push the banners even higher mid-bonus

**Example Bonus Scenario:**

- You enter the bonus with banners of 6x, 4x, 3x and 2x
- £1.00 stake
- On the full board, several gem combinations land and are boosted by their respective House multipliers
- A cascade chains two further multiplied wins
- The combined multipliers and Max Megaways™ board deliver a payout far beyond what any base-game spin could produce

This is where the game's **24,250x maximum win** lives. The bonus frequency is deliberately low and dead spins are common, but a well-built set of multipliers landing on a full board during free spins is precisely the combination that can push toward that ceiling.

## How to Win: Strategy & Tips

Kingmaker Megaways is a high-volatility game, so a disciplined, patient approach is essential. Here is how to give yourself the best campaign for the throne.

### Bankroll Management

- **Build a deep buffer**: High volatility means long stretches without meaningful wins. Divide your session budget so you can comfortably absorb several hundred spins while you wait for the multipliers and scatters to align.
- **Set firm win and loss limits**: Decide in advance when you will walk away. The 24,250x ceiling is a tantalising target, but the majority of sessions will hinge on reaching and capitalising on a single strong bonus.
- **Bet within your means**: With a range of £0.20 to £20.00, choose a stake that lets you stay in the hunt long enough for the multipliers to build, rather than one that empties your balance before the bonus arrives.

### Base Game Strategy

- **Embrace the build**: The Gem Multipliers reward patience. A long base game without a bonus is not wasted time — it is multiplier accumulation. View rising banners as progress toward a bigger payoff.
- **Watch all four Houses**: Because each House builds independently, keep an eye on which gems are landing five-of-a-kinds. A session that favours two or three Houses heavily can set up a particularly explosive bonus.
- **Let the engine work**: Spin outcomes are random, so focus on bet sizing and session length rather than chasing patterns.

### Bonus Round Strategy

- **The bonus is the goal**: With no bonus buy, your entire strategy points toward triggering and maximising the free spins. The more multipliers you have built beforehand, the more powerful the round.
- **Hope for higher triggers**: Landing 4 or 5 scatters (12 or 20 spins) gives you far more chances to land multiplied gem wins on the Max Megaways™ board.
- **Practise in the demo first**: Use the free-play version to understand how the four multipliers build and carry over before committing real funds.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the RTP of Kingmaker Megaways?**

Kingmaker Megaways has a Return to Player (RTP) of **96.65%**. This sits comfortably above the modern Megaways average of around 96%, which is a genuine plus for a high-volatility title where the maths matters over a long session.

**What is the maximum win in Kingmaker Megaways?**

The maximum win is **24,250x your stake**. As a high-volatility slot, this big-hit potential is concentrated in the free spins, where the Max Megaways™ board combines with your accumulated House multipliers to reach toward the ceiling.

**How volatile is Kingmaker Megaways?**

The game is **high volatility**. Expect long dry spells and frequent dead spins in the base game; the rewards are real but they are concentrated in the relatively infrequent free spins round, so a patient approach and a deep bankroll are advised.

**Does Kingmaker Megaways have a bonus buy?**

**No.** Kingmaker Megaways does not offer a bonus buy feature, so you must trigger the free spins organically by landing three or more crown scatters. This is part of what makes the game a true high-variance experience.

**How do the Gem Multipliers work?**

Each of the four Houses has its own multiplier banner that starts at 1x. Every time you land a five-of-a-kind win with that House's gem, its banner increases by +1. The multipliers are persistent through the base game and carry over as your starting multipliers when the free spins trigger.

**How are the free spins triggered and how many do I get?**

The free spins are triggered by crown scatter symbols. Landing **3, 4 or 5 scatters** awards **7, 12 or 20 free spins** respectively. During the round, every spin plays on a fully expanded Max Megaways™ board of 16,807 ways.

**What is Max Megaways™ in the bonus?**

Max Megaways™ means that during the free spins, the reels are fully expanded on every single spin, locking the board to its maximum 16,807 ways to win. Combined with your carried-over House multipliers, this is where the game's biggest payouts are forged.

**Who makes Kingmaker Megaways and when was it released?**

Kingmaker Megaways was developed by **Big Time Gaming**, the studio that invented the Megaways™ mechanic, and was released on **August 28, 2019**. Note that it is distinct from the later **Kingmaker Fully Loaded Megaways**, a separate 2021 title with a different multiplier mechanic.
