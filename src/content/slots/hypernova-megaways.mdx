## The Megaways™ Engine: 117,649 Ways Across the Cosmos

### Understanding Megaways™ in Hypernova

At the core of Hypernova Megaways sits the licensed **Megaways™** engine, the variable-ways system that transformed modern slot mathematics. Instead of fixed paylines, every reel can show a different number of symbols on each spin, and the total ways to win are recalculated dynamically. This is what produces the game's constantly shifting rhythm and its headline figure of 117,649 ways.

**How Megaways™ Works in Hypernova:**

- **6 vertical reels**, each capable of showing between **2 and 7 symbols** per spin
- An **extra horizontal reel** sits above the central reels, adding symbols to the calculation
- **Ways to win** are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
- **Maximum ways**: when every reel is at its tallest, the grid reaches **117,649 ways to win**
- Wins pay for **matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right**, regardless of their exact position

**Win Formation Principles:**

- Symbol size and exact row position are irrelevant — only the reel sequence matters
- A winning combination must start on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
- Multiple separate winning combinations can land and pay on a single spin
- Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your **total stake**, not a per-line bet
- The number of ways changes every spin, so no two spins feel mathematically identical

The variable layout means a tall, full-height board can erupt with a huge number of ways, while a short board still offers respectable potential. Combined with the cascading engine below, this gives Hypernova its signature ebb and flow.

### Cascading Reels

Like most Megaways™ slots, Hypernova Megaways employs **cascading reels** (also called tumbles or avalanches). When you land a winning combination:

1. The symbols that formed the win are removed from the grid
2. The symbols above them drop down to fill the empty spaces
3. New symbols fall in from above to complete the reels
4. If the new arrangement creates another win, it pays and the process repeats
5. Cascades continue until no further wins land

This mechanic means a single paid spin can produce a chain of consecutive wins. In a high-ways board, a well-timed cascade can stack several payouts on top of one another, and it is one of the most reliable sources of the base game's larger hits.

## The Jackpot Re-Spins Feature: Hypernova's Signature Bonus

### Lock In the Stars

The defining mechanic of Hypernova Megaways is its **Jackpot Re-Spins** feature, which deliberately replaces a conventional free spins round with something more focused and tense. Rather than awarding a batch of spins with a multiplier, the game invites you to lock in special symbols and chase a set of fixed jackpots through a series of respins.

**Triggering the Feature:**

- The feature is triggered by landing **6 or more supernova scatter symbols** across the reels on a single spin
- This is a demanding requirement, which is why the feature carries genuine weight when it does activate

**How the Jackpot Re-Spins Work:**

1. When you trigger the feature, all qualifying scatter symbols **lock in place** on the grid
2. You begin with **3 respins**
3. On each respin, only the **non-locked positions** spin again
4. Whenever a **new scatter lands**, it locks in place and the respin counter **resets back to 3**
5. The feature continues until no new scatters land within the respin allowance
6. At the end, the locked symbols are tallied and any jackpots or cash values are paid out

This "land-and-reset" loop is what gives the feature its escalating tension. Each new scatter not only adds value but also extends your run, so a hot sequence can keep the feature alive far longer than the initial three respins suggest.

### The Four Fixed Jackpots

The headline rewards in the Jackpot Re-Spins feature are four fixed jackpots, each worth a set multiple of your stake:

- **Bronze Jackpot** — **10x** your stake
- **Silver Jackpot** — **50x** your stake
- **Gold Jackpot** — **250x** your stake
- **Platinum Jackpot** — **2,000x** your stake — the top prize and the feature's ultimate goal

In addition to the jackpots, individual scatter symbols can carry their own **cash values ranging from 1x up to 100x** your stake. These cash values are accumulated as scatters lock in, so even a feature that does not reach the Platinum tier can still deliver a satisfying payout from the combined cash symbols.

**Why the Jackpot Re-Spins Matter:**

- **Clear Target**: The four fixed jackpots give players a tangible, named goal to chase
- **Escalating Tension**: The respin reset keeps every new scatter genuinely exciting
- **Cumulative Value**: Cash symbol values stack up even without a top jackpot
- **Distinct Identity**: The lock-and-respin design sets Hypernova apart from free-spins-driven Megaways slots

**Example Jackpot Re-Spins Scenario:**
