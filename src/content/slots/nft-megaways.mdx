## The Megaways™ Engine: 200,704 Ways to Win

### Beyond the Standard 117,649

Most Megaways slots top out at the familiar 117,649 ways — six reels, each showing up to seven symbols. NFT Megaways goes further. Above the main grid sits the **Crypto Punks Bar**, a horizontal top reel that adds an extra symbol position to each of the four middle reels. When every reel lands at full height, the sums look like this:

**How the Ways Are Calculated:**

- **Reels 1 and 6**: up to **7 symbols** each
- **Reels 2, 3, 4 and 5**: up to **7 symbols** each, **plus one** from the top reel = up to **8 effective positions**
- **Maximum ways**: 7 × 8 × 8 × 8 × 8 × 7 = **200,704 ways to win**
- **Minimum ways**: with every reel at its shortest, the board can shrink to just a few dozen ways
- The number of symbols on each reel changes every spin, so the ways total is recalculated dynamically
- The top reel **slides horizontally** rather than spinning vertically, feeding one symbol to each middle reel

**Win Formation Principles:**

- Wins pay for **matching symbols on adjacent reels starting from the leftmost reel**
- Exact row position is irrelevant — only the reel sequence matters
- Multiple separate winning combinations can pay on a single spin
- All payouts are expressed as multiples of your **total stake**
- Because the ways count shifts constantly, no two spins are mathematically identical

The practical upshot is that NFT Megaways has a noticeably higher ceiling on any given spin than a standard Megaways game. A full 200,704-way board with a premium symbol chain running left to right is a genuinely spectacular sight — especially if a multiplied-up CryptoPunk happens to be part of the action.

### Chain Reactions

Like most of the Megaways family, NFT Megaways runs on **Chain Reactions** — Red Tiger's name for the cascade mechanic:

1. Symbols in a winning combination are removed from the grid
2. Remaining symbols drop down to fill the gaps
3. New symbols fall in from above (and slide in on the top reel)
4. If a new win forms, it pays and the process repeats
5. The chain continues until a spin produces no further wins

Chain Reactions matter more here than in an ordinary Megaways slot, because every cascade is another chance to feed low-value crypto symbols to a CryptoPunk wild and pump its multiplier. A long chain does not just pay repeatedly — it actively builds the machinery for a bigger payout later in the same sequence.

**Why Cascades Are the Game's Heartbeat:**

- Each removal-and-refill cycle can bring **new coin symbols** for the Punks to collect
- The top reel slides with each cascade, so **fresh Punks can enter play** mid-chain
- In the base game, cascades are the **only window** in which a built multiplier can pay out before resetting
- During Crypto Spins, every cascade compounds the round-long accumulation

A spin that chains four or five reactions deep, feeding a Punk along the way and then landing a premium win through it, is the base game operating exactly as designed — and it is a genuinely thrilling sequence when it connects.

## The Crypto Punks Bar: Wilds With a Business Plan

### Four Punks, Four Multiplier Meters

The signature mechanic of NFT Megaways lives on the **Crypto Punks Bar**, the sliding top reel above the main grid. This is the exclusive home of the game's four **CryptoPunk wild symbols** — pixel-perfect recreations of the famous NFT avatars — and each one operates as a wild with its own personal multiplier meter.

**How the CryptoPunk Wilds Work:**

- Each Punk **substitutes for all paying symbols**, helping complete ways across the middle reels
- Every Punk starts with a base multiplier and its own **collection meter**
- When a win involves low-value crypto symbols, the participating Punk **collects** them
- Each collection increases that Punk's multiplier by **+1**
- A Punk's multiplier can grow to a maximum of **30x**
- Any win a Punk participates in is **multiplied by its current meter value**

**The Dual-Punk Jackpot Maths:**

Here is where the game gets genuinely exciting. If **two CryptoPunks participate in the same winning combination**, their multiplier values do not add together — they **multiply each other**. Two well-fed Punks at 10x and 12x turn a single win into a 120x-boosted payout; at the theoretical extreme, two capped Punks represent a 900x compound multiplier on one win. This compounding interaction is the primary route toward the game's 10,499x maximum win.

**The Catch — Base Game Resets:**

In the base game, Punk multiplier meters **reset between spins**. A collection streak only pays off if the multiplied win lands within the same spin's chain of cascades. This is a deliberate volatility lever: most spins see meters build a little and evaporate, while the rare spin where cascades, collections and a multi-Punk win align is where the fireworks happen. It also makes the free spins round — where meters persist — dramatically more valuable.

**Why the Crypto Punks Bar Matters:**

- **Dual-purpose wilds**: The Punks are simultaneously your way-completers and your multiplier engines
- **Four independent meters**: Each Punk tracks its own collections, so several multipliers can build in parallel
- **Compounding, not adding**: The dual-Punk multiplication rule is rare among Megaways slots and defines the game's ceiling
- **Cascade synergy**: Every Chain Reaction is a fresh chance to collect, so long chains are doubly valuable
- **Bonus transformation**: The same mechanic, with resets removed, becomes the entire engine of Crypto Spins

### Example CryptoPunk Scenarios

**Scenario 1 — Single-Punk Chain:**

- £1.00 stake; a win involving a Punk and several low-value coin symbols pays and cascades
- The Punk collects the coins, ticking its multiplier from 1x to 4x across the chain
- A fresh cascade forms a premium win through the same Punk: a 15x base win becomes 15x × 4 = **60x stake (£60.00)**
- The chain ends, the meter resets, and the hunt begins again

**Scenario 2 — Dual-Punk Compounding:**

- £1.00 stake; two Punks sit on the bar above reels 2 and 4
- A long cascade sequence feeds both, leaving them at 6x and 5x
- A premium symbol then chains across reels 1-5, passing through both Punks
- The win is boosted by 6 × 5 = **30x**, turning an ordinary way win into a session highlight

**Scenario 3 — The Near Miss:**

- A Punk climbs to 9x through diligent collections, but the chain ends without a premium win through it
- The spin concludes, the meter resets to base, and nothing carries over
- This is the base game's core tension — and precisely why the persistent meters of Crypto Spins are so coveted

## Crypto Spins: Free Spins Where the Meters Never Sleep

### Triggering the Bonus

The free spins round, fittingly branded **Crypto Spins**, is triggered by landing **three or more scatter symbols** anywhere in view. There is no bonus buy, so scatters are the only way in. The award scales with the number of scatters:

| **Scatters** | **Free Spins Awarded** |
| ------------ | ---------------------- |
| 3            | 10 free spins          |
| 4            | 15 free spins          |
| 5            | 20 free spins          |
| 6            | 25 free spins          |

### Why Crypto Spins Changes Everything

The rules of the bonus round are identical to the base game with one transformative exception: the four CryptoPunk multiplier meters **do not reset between spins**. Every collection made on every spin of the bonus persists until the round ends.

**What Persistent Meters Mean in Practice:**

- Early bonus spins act as an **accumulation phase**, feeding the Punks and building meters
- By the later spins, multiple Punks can be sitting on double-digit multipliers simultaneously
- Any premium win through a charged Punk — or better, **two** charged Punks — is amplified enormously
- The longer the round runs, the higher the meters climb, which makes retriggers exponentially valuable rather than merely additive

### Retriggers

Landing **three or more scatters during the bonus** retriggers the feature, adding extra spins on the following scale:

| **Scatters (during bonus)** | **Extra Spins** |
| --------------------------- | --------------- |
| 3                           | 5               |
| 4                           | 10              |
| 5                           | 15              |
| 6                           | 20              |

Because every additional spin arrives with the meters already charged, a mid-bonus retrigger is arguably the single best event in the entire game: you get more spins precisely when the multipliers are at their most dangerous. Ten extra spins on fresh meters are merely nice; ten extra spins with two Punks in double digits are a genuine shot at the game's upper payout range.

**Example Bonus:**

- £1.00 stake, 4 scatters award 15 Crypto Spins
- Across the first ten spins, steady collections push two Punks to 8x and 11x
- On spin twelve, a premium symbol chains across five reels through both Punks
- The combined multiplier is 8 × 11 = 88x, turning a modest base win into a three-figure-multiplier payout
- A late retrigger adds 10 more spins with both meters still loaded

This structure gives Crypto Spins a satisfying narrative arc — slow accumulation, rising tension, explosive potential — and it is where the vast majority of the game's biggest recorded wins originate.

## How to Win: Strategy & Tips

NFT Megaways is a high-volatility slot with a below-average RTP, so discipline matters more here than in a comfortable medium-variance game. Here is how to approach it sensibly.

### Bankroll Management

- **Budget for variance**: With high volatility and a hit frequency around 20%, plan your session so you can absorb at least 150-200 spins at your chosen stake. The £0.10 minimum bet makes that achievable on modest budgets.
- **Set win and loss limits before you spin**: The 10,499x ceiling is real but rare. Decide in advance when you will bank a profit and when you will walk away, and stick to it.
- **Respect the reset**: Base-game multiplier meters wiping between spins means you should never "chase" a partly built meter — it carries no value into the next spin.
- **Size down rather than out**: If a session runs cold, resist the urge to raise stakes to "force" a bonus. Scatter odds are identical at every bet level; a higher stake only accelerates the drawdown.
- **Bank the bonus result**: Because so much of the game's return is concentrated in Crypto Spins, a strong bonus outcome is a natural point to reassess — or end — the session ahead.

### Base Game Strategy

- **Understand where the value lives**: The base game's job is essentially to deliver you into Crypto Spins. Wins built on dual-Punk multipliers within a single cascade chain do happen, but they are the exception.
- **Appreciate long cascades**: Chain Reactions that involve coin symbols and a Punk are the base game's best-case scenario, since collections and the multiplied payoff must occur within the same spin.
- **No bonus buy means patience**: Red Tiger has not fitted a feature-buy here, so there is no shortcut to the free spins. Factor the wait into your session planning.

### Bonus Round Strategy

- **The early spins are an investment**: Do not be discouraged if the first half of a Crypto Spins round pays little — that accumulation phase is exactly how the feature is designed.
- **Retriggers are gold**: Extra spins arriving on charged meters are worth far more than the same spins at the start of the round. A retriggered bonus is where this game's headline wins happen.
- **Try the demo first**: The collection and dual-multiplier maths take a few sessions to internalise. Free play is the smart way to learn the rhythm before staking real money.

### Who Should Play NFT Megaways?

- **A good fit for**: experienced Megaways players, volatility chasers comfortable with 200+ spin droughts, fans of collection/multiplier mechanics, and anyone charmed by the CryptoPunks aesthetic
- **A poor fit for**: players who want steady base-game returns, bonus-buy enthusiasts, RTP purists who refuse anything under 96%, and small-bankroll sessions where a single bad run ends the night
