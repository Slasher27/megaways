## The Megaways™ Engine: 117,649 Ways to Conquer

### Understanding Megaways™ in Centurion

At the heart of Centurion Megaways beats the licensed **Megaways™** engine, the variable-ways system that revolutionised slot mathematics. Rather than fixed paylines, every reel can display a different number of symbols on each spin, and the total ways to win are recalculated dynamically. This is what gives the game its constantly shifting rhythm and its headline figure of 117,649 ways.

**How Megaways™ Works in Centurion:**

- **6 vertical reels**, each capable of showing between **2 and 7 symbols** per spin
- **Ways to win** are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
- **Maximum ways**: 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 = **117,649 ways to win**
- **Minimum ways**: 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 64 ways to win
- Wins pay for **matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right**, regardless of their position on the reel

**Win Formation Principles:**

- Symbol size and exact row position are irrelevant — only the reel sequence matters
- A winning combination must start on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
- Multiple separate winning combinations can land and pay on a single spin
- Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your **total stake**, not a per-line bet
- The number of ways changes every spin, so no two spins feel mathematically identical

### Cascading Reactions

Like most Megaways™ slots, Centurion Megaways employs **cascading reactions** (sometimes called tumbles or avalanches). When you land a winning combination:

1. The symbols that formed the win are removed from the grid
2. The symbols above them drop down to fill the empty spaces
3. New symbols fall in from above to complete the reels
4. If the new arrangement creates another win, it pays and the process repeats
5. Cascades continue until no further wins land

This mechanic means a single paid spin can produce a chain of consecutive wins, and it is especially valuable when the Maximus Winnus feature has loaded the board with a high number of ways or a swarm of matching mystery symbols. A well-timed cascade during a full 117,649-way board can be where some of the game's most satisfying base-game payouts originate.

## Maximus Winnus: The Mystery Feature That Changes Everything

### One Symbol, Five Possible Rewards

The signature mechanic of Centurion Megaways is the **Maximus Winnus** feature, embodied by a special golden mystery symbol that can land during base-game play. What makes it so compelling is unpredictability: when Maximus Winnus activates, the game randomly selects one of **five different boosts**, each of which can dramatically improve your next spin or your immediate win.

**The Five Maximus Winnus Outcomes:**

1. **Big Win** — Awards you a guaranteed minimum prize of **20x your stake**. This is the instant-gratification outcome that rewards you directly without needing additional symbols to align.

2. **Maximum Ways** — Guarantees that the **next spin plays with the full 117,649 ways to win**. With every reel locked to seven symbols, the probability of large multi-way combinations and explosive cascades soars.

3. **Minimum Ways** — Sets a **guaranteed minimum number of winning ways** for the next spin. Even if the reels would naturally land short, you are protected from a low-ways dud spin, ensuring solid win potential.

4. **Mystery Symbol** — Scatters **up to 100 matching mystery symbols** across the reels. When these all reveal the same paying symbol, they can combine into substantial multi-way wins, particularly when paired with cascades.

5. **Super Bonus Reels** — Adds **extra scatter symbols** to the reels, significantly increasing your chance of triggering the main bonus round on the spin.

**Why Maximus Winnus Matters:**

- **Constant Variety**: Because the outcome is random, no two activations feel the same
- **Multiple Win Pathways**: Some outcomes pay instantly, others set up the next spin, and one pushes you toward the bonus round
- **Engagement Anchor**: The feature triggers often enough to keep base-game play lively without devaluing the bonus rounds
- **Synergy with Cascades**: The Maximum Ways and Mystery Symbol outcomes both feed directly into the cascading engine for chained wins

**Prizes on Parade** is the instant-cash option in the bonus selector. Rather than spinning reels, you make picks to reveal cash prizes expressed as multiples of your stake. **Prize Ranges:**

## The Gamble Feature: Risk It for the Super Tier

### Doubling Down on Your Bonus

One of the most distinctive elements of Centurion Megaways is the **Gamble feature**, which lets you **risk your awarded bonus** for the chance to upgrade it to the more generous **Super** version. After the bonus selector hands you a regular bonus, you can choose to lock it in safely — or gamble it.

**How the Gamble Feature Works:**

1. You are awarded a regular bonus (e.g., Caesar's Free Spins at the regular tier)
2. The game offers you the choice to **gamble** for the Super version
3. If the gamble succeeds, your bonus is **upgraded to the Super tier**
4. If the gamble fails, you can **lose the upgrade** — and potentially the bonus itself

**Strategic Considerations:**

- **Risk vs. Reward**: The Super tiers are significantly more valuable, so the upside is real
- **All-or-Nothing**: A failed gamble is genuinely costly, so this is not a risk-free button
- **Practice in Demo**: The Centurion Megaways free-play demo is the ideal place to learn the gamble's rhythm before risking real money
- **Bonus-Dependent Value**: The upgrade is most worthwhile on Prizes on Parade (guaranteed payout) and on free-spins rounds where the higher multiplier ceiling can transform results

The Gamble feature injects genuine player agency into the bonus round. It turns a passive "watch and hope" bonus into a decision point, and for many players that extra layer of control is a highlight of the game.

## How to Win: Strategy & Tips

Centurion Megaways is a medium-volatility game, which means a sensible, patient approach pays dividends. Here is how to give yourself the best campaign.

### Bankroll Management

- **Match your bet to your bankroll**: With a range of £0.20 to £20.00, divide your session budget so you can comfortably absorb at least 100-150 spins. Medium volatility means features arrive at a reasonable pace, so you do not need the enormous buffer that a very-high-volatility slot demands.
- **Set win and loss limits**: Decide in advance when you will walk away — both if you are ahead and if you are down. The 12,500x ceiling is a big target, but the bulk of sessions will revolve around the more frequent feature wins.
- **Treat the Gamble as optional**: Because a failed gamble can cost you the entire bonus, factor its risk into your bankroll plan. Conservative players can simply decline it and still enjoy the regular bonuses.

### Base Game Strategy

- **Let the Megaways™ engine do the work**: There is no skill in spin outcomes, so focus on bet sizing and session length rather than chasing patterns.
- **Value the Maximus Winnus feature**: This is where most of your base-game excitement lives. Outcomes like Maximum Ways and Mystery Symbol can produce strong wins, and Super Bonus Reels actively pushes you toward the bonus round.
- **Use cascades to your advantage**: Remember that a single paid spin can chain into multiple wins, especially after a Maximum Ways boost loads the full 117,649 ways.

### Bonus Round Strategy

- **Match the bonus to your goal**: Choose **Prizes on Parade** for a guaranteed, lower-variance payout; pick **Caesar's Free Spins** for multiplier-driven potential; select **Wild Power Spins** when you want the most volatile, big-hit-oriented option.
- **Weigh the Gamble carefully**: The Super upgrade is genuinely worthwhile — Super Prizes on Parade nearly doubles the floor, and the free-spins rounds gain extra spins and higher multipliers — but only gamble what you are willing to lose.
- **Practice in the demo first**: Use the free-play version to understand how each bonus and the gamble behave before committing real funds.
