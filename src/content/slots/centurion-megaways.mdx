## How Centurion Megaways Works

Centurion Megaways is a six-reel slot on the licensed [Megaways™ engine](/megaways/), showing between two and seven symbols per reel for anywhere from 64 to **117,649 ways to win**. Wins pay for matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right, position on the reel is irrelevant, and every payout is a multiple of your total stake. Because the ways count is recalculated on each spin, no two spins are mathematically identical — a tall board multiplies the value of everything that lands on it.

Winning spins also trigger **cascading reactions**: the symbols in a win are removed, everything above drops down, and new symbols fall in to fill the gaps, chaining fresh wins from a single paid spin until the board goes quiet. The cascade engine matters most when a feature has stacked the deck — a full 117,649-way board or a swarm of matching mystery symbols can turn one spin into a long sequence of connected payouts.

Released on **3 July 2020**, the game is Inspired Gaming's online expansion of the Centurion brand that UK players know from land-based arcade and bookmaker cabinets, dressed in marble columns, eagle standards and an orchestral score. Stakes run from **0.20 to 20.00** per spin, and the medium-volatility model means features arrive at a reasonable pace — a budget covering 100–150 spins is a sensible session floor rather than the enormous buffer an extreme-variance slot demands.

## What Are the Bonus Features in Centurion Megaways?

The feature set splits into three layers: a random base-game mystery feature, a bonus selector with three distinct rounds, and a gamble that bridges the two tiers of each round.

### Maximus Winnus: Five Boosts From One Golden Symbol

Maximus Winnus is the base game's signature mechanic — a golden mystery symbol that, when it activates, randomly awards one of **five** boosts. **Big Win** pays a guaranteed minimum of **20x your stake** on the spot; **Maximum Ways** locks the next spin at the full **117,649 ways**; **Minimum Ways** guarantees a floor of winning ways so the next spin cannot land as a low-ways dud; **Mystery Symbol** scatters **up to 100 matching mystery symbols** across the reels, all revealing the same paying symbol; and **Super Bonus Reels** adds extra scatters to boost your odds of triggering the bonus round.

The variety is the point. One activation pays instantly, two set up the following spin, one seeds a mystery-symbol board, and one pushes you toward the selector — so the feature never settles into a single repetitive pattern. It also feeds the cascade engine directly: a Maximum Ways spin or a mass mystery-symbol reveal is precisely the setup from which chained multi-way wins flow.

### The Bonus Selector: Caesar's Free Spins, Prizes on Parade and Wild Power Spins

Landing **three or more scatters** opens a selection screen where you choose one of three rounds — and each exists in a regular and a more valuable **Super** tier.

**Prizes on Parade** is the low-variance pick: a pick-a-prize round in which each choice reveals a cash award tied to your stake. The regular version pays between **10x and 43x**; the Super version pays between **18x and 65x**, lifting the floor to nearly double the regular minimum as well as raising the ceiling. It is the only option with a guaranteed outcome, which is exactly why cautious players gravitate to it.

**Caesar's Free Spins** is the multiplier route. The regular round awards **6 to 12 free spins** with a starting multiplier of **1x to 3x**; the Super round awards **8 to 16 free spins** with a starting multiplier of **1x to 5x**. With the full Megaways engine active and the multiplier applying to cascading chains, this is the round through which the game's 12,500x ceiling is most plausibly approached.

**Wild Power Spins** trades multiplier maths for **stacked wilds** that can blanket entire reels, turning them into guaranteed contributors to any combination that crosses them. The Super version layers **win multipliers** on top of the stacked-wild action, making it arguably the most volatile choice of the three — quiet spins punctuated by full-board moments.

### The Gamble Feature: Risking a Regular Bonus for the Super Tier

After the selector hands you a regular bonus, the Gamble feature offers a genuine decision: lock it in, or risk it for the **Super** upgrade. A successful gamble converts your round to the Super tier; a failed one can cost you the upgrade and potentially the bonus itself. That all-or-nothing edge is what separates Centurion Megaways from the passive trigger-and-watch structure of most Megaways bonuses — but it deserves respect, because declining is always a legitimate choice, and the free demo is the sensible place to learn how the gamble behaves before real money is on the line.

## Symbols & What They Pay

The paytable is a two-tier Roman hierarchy topped by **the Centurion himself**, the game's highest-paying symbol, followed by the **Roman shield**, **gladiator helmet** and **laurel wreath** premiums. The low tier is filled by royal card values — **A, K, Q, J, 10 and 9** — carved in stone and metal styling to match the theme. As with every Megaways title, all awards are quoted as multiples of your total stake and pay for matching symbols on consecutive reels from the left, so even mid-tier symbols become meaningful when they stretch across all six reels of a high-ways board.

Four special symbols drive the features: the golden **Maximus Winnus symbol** that fires the five-outcome mystery feature, the **scatter** (three or more anywhere open the bonus selector), the **wild** that substitutes for paying symbols and appears in stacks during Wild Power Spins, and the **mystery symbol** placed in bulk by the Mystery Symbol boost, every instance revealing the same paying symbol at once.

## Final Verdict — Should You Play Centurion Megaways?

Centurion Megaways earns its **4.1 out of 5** by doing something the Megaways catalogue rarely bothers with: giving the player agency. The bonus selector's three genuinely different rounds, the Super tiers behind each of them, and the gamble that connects the two levels turn every trigger into a small strategic moment rather than a spectator event. Add the five-outcome Maximus Winnus feature stirring the base game and you have one of the most varied feature sets in the genre, wrapped in a brand with real land-based heritage.

The flaws are equally concrete. A **95.9% RTP** gives up measurable ground to the Megaways field, the **12,500x** ceiling is unremarkable next to the genre's volatility monsters, there is no bonus buy for players who prefer to pay their way into features, the gamble can wipe out a bonus entirely, and the presentation — clean and legible as it is — looks its age against current releases.
