## Pass GO and Collect Massive Wins in Monopoly Megaways™

Do not pass GO, do not collect £200—actually, definitely land on GO for maximum Megaways™! **Big Time Gaming** brings the world's most iconic board game to the reels with **Monopoly Megaways™**, a slot that brilliantly merges the strategic property-building gameplay of Monopoly with BTG's revolutionary Megaways™ engine. Released on December 4, 2019, this remarkable collaboration transforms every spin into a journey around the legendary Monopoly board, where **Mr. Monopoly himself** guides you through property acquisitions, house construction, and ultimately, Free Spins riches.

The visual presentation immediately transports you to the familiar Monopoly universe—the classic green board encircles the 6-reel grid, complete with iconic properties like Park Lane, Mayfair, and the four Train Stations. **Mr. Monopoly** stands proudly to the left of the reels, animated and reactive, moving his top-hatted figure around the board with each winning combination. The attention to licensing detail is meticulous: authentic property cards, the distinctive Monopoly font, colored property groups, and the familiar Chance and Community Chest spaces all recreate the board game experience with remarkable fidelity.

What sets Monopoly Megaways™ apart from both standard Megaways™ slots and typical branded games is the **Reel Adventures™ mechanic**—previously featured only in BTG's Holy Diver Megaways. This innovative system transforms Mr. Monopoly's board journey into genuine gameplay progression: every winning combination moves him forward, landing on properties builds houses, collecting complete property sets enhances Free Spins, and special spaces like GO, Chance, and Community Chest trigger unique benefits that directly impact your win potential.

The **Houses system** creates tangible progression toward the Free Spins feature. Landing on a property on your **third reaction** adds a house to that street. Every subsequent reaction adds additional houses. Once you've constructed **5 houses on any single property**, the Free Spins feature triggers with a minimum **8 spins** (up to 12 if you own all 4 Train Stations). During Free Spins, an **unlimited multiplier** activates, increasing by +1 for every house on each property you land on—meaning those carefully constructed property portfolios pay exponential dividends when

 the feature finally hits.

Alternative trigger pathways add strategic variety: landing on **Chance** can award instant Free Spins, maximum Megaways™, or a fortunate property placement with up to 4 houses. Landing on **GO** activates the maximum **117,649 Megaways™** for that spin, creating explosive win potential when combined with premium symbols. **Train Stations** and **Utilities** you own award extra Free Spins when landed upon during the feature.

With an excellent **RTP of 96.50%**, medium-high volatility that balances regular action with substantial win sequences, and a maximum win potential of **14,700x bet**, Monopoly Megaways™ delivers both nostalgia and innovation. The slot successfully captures the spirit of property empire building from the board game while integrating it seamlessly into Megaways™ mathematics—creating a gaming experience where strategy, progression, and pure chance converge in a way that feels genuinely unique within BTG's portfolio.

Whether you're drawn by childhood memories of marathon Monopoly sessions, fascination with the Reel Adventures™ progression mechanic, or simply appreciation for BTG's creative approach to branded slots, Monopoly Megaways™ offers a comprehensive experience where every spin advances your property empire. Let's explore every facet of this board game-meets-Megaways™ adventure.

## The Megaways™ Mechanic: 117,649 Ways to Property Riches

### Understanding Standard Megaways™ in Monopoly

Monopoly Megaways™ utilizes Big Time Gaming's legendary Megaways™ engine in its classic 6-reel configuration, providing the familiar variable-ways mathematics that has defined modern slot innovation.

**How Megaways™ Works:**

- **6 vertical reels** with 2-7 symbols per reel
- **Horizontal top reel** above reels 2-5 (standard BTG layout)
- **Ways to win calculated** by multiplying symbols on each reel
- **Maximum ways**: 7×7×7×7×7×7 = **117,649 ways to win**
- **Minimum ways**: 2×2×2×2×2×2 = 64 ways

**Win Formation:**

Wins are awarded for **left-to-right adjacent symbols regardless of size**:

- Symbol size irrelevant—small and large symbols count equally
- Only consecutive reels from left to right form winning combinations
- Multiple winning combinations can occur simultaneously
- All payouts multiplied by total stake
- **Cascading Reactions**: Winning symbols removed, new symbols drop, creating chain wins
- Each cascade advances Mr. Monopoly one space on the board

**GO Space Significance:**

The **GO space** on the Monopoly board activates a special benefit: when Mr. Monopoly lands on GO, that spin immediately activates **maximum 117,649 Megaways™** regardless of how many symbols would naturally appear. This creates guaranteed maximum ways for one spin, dramatically increasing win potential if premium symbols align favorably.

Landing on GO doesn't award Free Spins or special bonuses beyond the maximum ways activation, but when combined with substantial symbol wins, it can produce base game hits rivaling small Free Spins sessions.

## Reel Adventures™ Mechanic: Mr. Monopoly's Board Journey

### Understanding Reel Adventures™

The **Reel Adventures™** mechanic is Monopoly Megaways™'s defining innovation and what transforms this from a simple branded slot into a genuine board game-slot hybrid. This system was previously featured only in BTG's Holy Diver Megaways (featuring a brave knight instead of Mr. Monopoly), making it a relatively rare mechanic within the studio's catalog.

**How Reel Adventures™ Works:**

**Mr. Monopoly's Movement:**

1. **Starting Position**: Mr. Monopoly begins at GO
2. **Winning Combinations**: Each winning combination advances Mr. Monopoly one space clockwise
3. **Multiple Cascades**: Each cascade from the same spin advances him additional spaces
4. **Board Loop**: After passing GO, he continues around the board indefinitely
5. **Space Landing**: Effects trigger based on which space he lands on after all cascades complete

**The Monopoly Board Layout:**

The board contains 40 spaces arranged in the classic Monopoly configuration:

- **22 Properties**: Colored property groups (brown, light blue, pink, orange, red, yellow, green, dark blue)
- **4 Train Stations**: King's Cross, Marylebone, Fenchurch Street, Liverpool Street
- **2 Utilities**: Electric Company, Water Works
- **3 Chance Spaces**: Corner spaces triggering card draws
- **3 Community Chest Spaces**: Triggering beneficial outcomes
- **4 Corner Spaces**: GO, Jail (Just Visiting), Free Parking, Go To Jail
- **2 Tax Spaces**: Income Tax, Super Tax (no special effect)

**Space Landing Effects:**

**Properties (22 spaces):**
- **1st Landing**: Nothing happens
- **2nd Landing**: Nothing happens
- **3rd Landing**: **First house added** to that property
- **4th+ Landing**: **Additional houses added** (up to 5 maximum per property)
- **5 Houses**: Triggers Free Spins feature

**Train Stations (4 spaces):**
- No houses can be built
- During Free Spins: Award **+1 spin** if you own the station
- Ownership determined by landing on them during base game

**Utilities (2 spaces):**
- No houses can be built
- During Free Spins: Award **+1 spin** if you own the utility
- Ownership determined by landing on them during base game

**GO:**
- Activates **maximum 117,649 Megaways™** for that spin
- No houses, no direct Free Spins trigger

**Chance (3 spaces):**
- Draws a Chance card awarding one of three benefits:
  1. **Maximum Megaways™**: Next spin has 117,649 ways
  2. **Free Spins**: Instant Free Spins trigger
  3. **Property Advancement**: Moves Mr. Monopoly to random property with **1-4 houses added**

**Community Chest (3 spaces):**
- Always awards the same benefit
- Moves Mr. Monopoly to **random street with 1-4 houses added**
- Accelerates house building significantly

**Go To Jail:**
- Sends Mr. Monopoly to Jail (Just Visiting)
- No penalty, just repositions him on the board

**Other Spaces (Jail/Just Visiting, Free Parking, Tax):**
- No special effects
- Simply occupy board positions

### Strategic Implications of Reel Adventures™

**Why Reel Adventures™ Matters:**

**Progression Visualization:**
- You can see exactly how close you are to triggering Free Spins
- Counting houses on properties provides tangible goal (5 houses needed)
- Creates engagement beyond pure spin-and-wait mechanics

**Multiple Trigger Pathways:**
- **Organic Building**: Land on same property 5+ times (3rd landing starts houses)
- **Chance Acceleration**: Chance card can award instant Free Spins
- **Community Chest Help**: Adds 1-4 houses to random property, potentially completing sets
- **Strategic Luck**: Properties near GO receive more landing opportunities

**Property Management:**
- You naturally build houses unevenly across the board
- Properties near your starting position after features tend to receive more houses
- Complete property color sets don't provide bonuses (unlike real Monopoly)
- Only individual property house counts matter

**Base Game Engagement:**
- Every winning combination feels meaningful (advances Mr. Monopoly)
- Long cascading sequences can advance 5-10+ spaces, rapidly building houses
- Watching Mr. Monopoly approach properties with 4 houses creates anticipation

**Learning Curve:**
- New players may find the mechanic initially confusing
- Understanding requires familiarity with Monopoly board layout
- Once learned, provides strategic depth and visual storytelling

**Comparison to Traditional Scatter-Based Features:**

Unlike slots where 3+ scatters trigger bonuses randomly:
- Monopoly requires **board progression** (more interactive)
- Feels less random (you see houses building toward goal)
- Provides clearer "near-miss" feedback (4 houses on property = almost there)
- Creates session continuity (house progress carries between spins)

## Building Your Property Empire: The Houses System

### How Houses Accumulate

The **Houses system** is intrinsically tied to Reel Adventures™ and represents your tangible progress toward the Free Spins feature. Understanding how houses accumulate is crucial to appreciating Monopoly Megaways™'s unique progression mechanics.

**House Building Process:**

**Landing Count Requirements:**

1. **1st Landing on Property**: No house added
2. **2nd Landing on Property**: No house added
3. **3rd Landing on Property**: **1st house added**
4. **4th Landing on Property**: **2nd house added**
5. **5th Landing on Property**: **3rd house added**
6. **6th Landing on Property**: **4th house added**
7. **7th Landing on Property**: **5th house added** → **Free Spins Trigger**

**Visual Indicators:**

Properties display their current house count through visual representation:
- **0 houses**: Empty property card
- **1-4 houses**: Small green houses appear on the property
- **5 houses**: Property displays full development, Free Spins imminent

**Example House Building Sequence:**

**Session Start:**
- Spin 1: Win creates 3 cascades, Mr. Monopoly lands on Park Lane (1st landing, no house)
- Spin 5: Win creates 2 cascades, Mr. Monopoly lands on Mayfair (1st landing, no house)
- Spin 12: Win creates 4 cascades, Mr. Monopoly lands on Park Lane again (2nd landing, no house)
- Spin 18: Win creates 5 cascades, Mr. Monopoly lands on Park Lane (3rd landing, **1st house added**)
- Spin 25: Win creates 3 cascades, Mr. Monopoly lands on Park Lane (4th landing, **2nd house added**)
- Spin 32: Win creates 2 cascades, Community Chest sends him to Park Lane with +2 houses = **4 houses total**
- Spin 38: Win creates 4 cascades, Mr. Monopoly lands on Park Lane (7th landing, **5th house added**)
- **Free Spins Trigger!**

This example demonstrates how house building accelerates: the first 2 landings produce nothing, but once the 3rd landing occurs, each subsequent landing adds a house. Community Chest and Chance cards can dramatically accelerate this process.

### Strategic Property Considerations

**Which Properties Build Fastest:**

Properties receive uneven house development based on:

**Proximity to Starting Position:**
- Properties near GO receive more landing opportunities early
- Brown and Light Blue properties (first two color groups) often reach 5 houses first

**Board Position After Features:**
- When Free Spins end, Mr. Monopoly resumes from his current position
- Properties ahead of this position become next development targets

**Cascade Length:**
- Longer cascading sequences advance Mr. Monopoly further
- Long cascades can skip over properties, creating uneven distribution

**Chance/Community Chest Impact:**
- These spaces send Mr. Monopoly to random properties with houses
- Can instantly give struggling properties 1-4 houses
- Introduces beneficial randomness that prevents endless grinding

**Property Color Sets:**

Unlike actual Monopoly:
- **Owning complete color sets provides NO special bonus**
- Individual property house counts are all that matters
- You can trigger Free Spins with houses scattered across multiple unrelated properties

### Accelerating House Building

**Community Chest:**

Landing on any of the 3 Community Chest spaces:
- Always awards the same benefit
- Moves Mr. Monopoly to **random property**
- Adds **1-4 houses immediately** to that property
- Can instantly create 5-house trigger if property already had 1-4 houses

**Chance Cards:**

Landing on any of the 3 Chance spaces draws a card awarding one of:
- **Maximum Megaways™** (doesn't help houses directly)
- **Instant Free Spins** (bypasses house building entirely)
- **Property advancement**: Random property + 1-4 houses (same as Community Chest)

**Long Cascading Sequences:**

The most reliable way to build houses is:
- Land substantial wins that create 5-10+ cascades
- Each cascade advances Mr. Monopoly one space
- Multiple landings on same property within one spin possible with perfect spacing

**Example Mega-Cascade:**

- Spin lands massive win creating 12 consecutive cascades
- Mr. Monopoly advances 12 spaces total
- Lands on Regent Street 3 times during this sequence (spaces 3, 6, 11)
- First landing: nothing
- Second landing: nothing
- Third landing: **1st house added**
- All from one spin!

This illustrates why massive cascading wins feel doubly rewarding in Monopoly Megaways™—they produce both large immediate payouts AND significant board progression.

## Free Spins Feature: Unlimited Multiplier Riches

### Triggering Free Spins

Unlike traditional scatter-based triggers, Monopoly Megaways™ offers **multiple pathways** to the Free Spins feature, all integrated through the Reel Adventures™ board game mechanics.

**Primary Trigger: 5 Houses on Any Property**

- Build **5 houses on any single property** through board progression
- Minimum **8 Free Spins** awarded
- **Bonus spins for complete sets**:
  - Own all **4 Train Stations**: +4 Free Spins (**12 total**)
  - Own **other complete property sets**: No additional spins (sets don't matter beyond trains)

**Secondary Trigger: Chance Card**

- Land on any Chance space
- Draw a card that *might* award instant Free Spins (1 in 3 chance)
- Awards same **8+ spins** as house-building trigger

**Free Spins Starting Configuration:**

When Free Spins trigger:
- Your **house distribution carries over** (all properties retain their house counts)
- Your **property ownership carries over** (Train Stations, Utilities remain owned)
- Mr. Monopoly's **board position carries over** (continues from where he was)
- This creates continuity from base game into feature

### The Unlimited Multiplier Mechanic

The **unlimited multiplier** is Monopoly Megaways™'s Free Spins crown jewel—a persistent, ever-growing multiplier that transforms even modest wins into spectacular payouts.

**How the Multiplier Works:**

**Starting Value:**
- Multiplier begins at **x1** (no enhancement)

**Multiplier Growth:**
- During each Free Spin, Mr. Monopoly continues moving with each cascade
- When he **lands on a property you own** (one with at least 1 house):
  - Multiplier increases by **+1 for every house on that property**
- When he lands on spaces without houses (GO, Chance, etc.):
  - Multiplier doesn't increase

**Multiplier Application:**
- Current multiplier value applies to **all wins** on that spin
- Multiplier **persists between spins** (never resets or decreases)
- Multiplier **grows cumulatively** throughout entire Free Spins session

**Example Multiplier Growth:**

**Free Spins Entry State:**
- Park Lane: 5 houses
- Mayfair: 3 houses
- Regent Street: 2 houses
- Bond Street: 1 house
- All other properties: 0 houses

**Free Spins Progression:**
- **Spin 1**: Win creates 2 cascades
  - Mr. Monopoly lands on Regent Street (2 houses)
  - Multiplier: x1 + 2 = **x3**
  - Wins on this spin multiplied by x3

- **Spin 2**: Win creates 4 cascades
  - Mr. Monopoly lands on Park Lane (5 houses)
  - Multiplier: x3 + 5 = **x8**
  - Wins on this spin multiplied by x8

- **Spin 3**: Win creates 1 cascade
  - Mr. Monopoly lands on Income Tax (0 houses, not owned)
  - Multiplier: **x8** (no change)
  - Wins on this spin multiplied by x8

- **Spin 4**: Win creates 5 cascades
  - Mr. Monopoly lands on Mayfair (3 houses)
  - Multiplier: x8 + 3 = **x11**
  - Wins on this spin multiplied by x11

- **Spin 5**: Win creates 3 cascades
  - Mr. Monopoly lands on Bond Street (1 house)
  - Multiplier: x11 + 1 = **x12**
  - Wins on this spin multiplied by x12

- Continue for remaining spins with multiplier growing each time properties with houses are landed upon...

**Maximum Multiplier Potential:**

The multiplier is **truly unlimited**—there is no cap. In theory:
- 8 Free Spins with consistent property landings
- Average 3-4 property landings per spin (with good cascades)
- Average 2-3 houses per property landed
- Could reach x60-x100+ multiplier by final spins

In practice:
- Most sessions reach x20-x40 multiplier
- Excellent sessions reach x60-x80 multiplier
- Epic sessions approach x100+ multiplier

### Train Stations and Utilities Bonus

**Train Station Ownership:**

The **4 Train Stations** provide dual benefits:

**Base Game:**
- Landing on stations during base game establishes ownership
- No houses built (stations don't use house system)

**Free Spins:**
- Each time Mr. Monopoly lands on a **Train Station you own**:
  - Awards **+1 Free Spin**
  - Does NOT increase multiplier (stations have 0 houses)

**Owning All 4 Stations:**
- Triggers Free Spins with **12 spins instead of 8** (bonus +4 spins)
- Each station landed upon during feature awards +1 spin
- Potential for significant spin extension if stations landed frequently

**Utility Ownership:**

The **2 Utilities** work identically:

**Base Game:**
- Landing on utilities establishes ownership

**Free Spins:**
- Each time Mr. Monopoly lands on an **owned utility**:
  - Awards **+1 Free Spin**
  - Does NOT increase multiplier (utilities have 0 houses)

**Strategic Value:**

Owning all Train Stations and both Utilities means **6 board spaces** that award extra spins:
- More landings = more spins = more multiplier growth opportunities
- Properties with houses increase multiplier
- Stations/Utilities extend feature duration
- Combined, they create longer features with higher multipliers

### Retriggers During Free Spins

**Can You Retrigger?**

Unlike many slots, Monopoly Megaways™ **does not have traditional retriggers** (landing 3+ scatters during feature for +X spins).

Instead, spin extension occurs through:
- Landing on owned **Train Stations**: +1 spin each
- Landing on owned **Utilities**: +1 spin each
- Building additional properties to 5 houses: Theoretically possible but **does NOT award additional spin sets** (only first 5-house completion triggers entry)

**Practical Spin Counts:**

- **Minimum**: 8 spins (basic trigger, no stations owned, no stations/utilities landed)
- **With All Stations**: 12 spins (entry bonus)
- **Maximum Realistic**: 15-25 spins (entry bonus + multiple station/utility landings)
- **Theoretical Maximum**: Unlimited (if Mr. Monopoly exclusively lands on stations/utilities every spin, though mathematically improbable)

## How to Win: Strategy & Tips

### Understanding Win Mechanics

Monopoly Megaways™'s win formation follows standard Megaways™ principles with the added dimension of board game progression affecting feature triggers and multipliers.

**Core Win Formation:**

1. **Left-to-Right Adjacent**: Consecutive reels from left required
2. **Minimum Match**: 3-of-a-kind minimum for most symbols
3. **Ways Multiplication**: Total ways = product of symbols on each reel
4. **Wild Substitution**: Wilds substitute for all regular pays
5. **Cascading Reactions**: Winning symbols removed, new symbols drop, Mr. Monopoly advances
6. **Board Progression**: Each cascade moves Mr. Monopoly one space, potentially adding houses

### Bankroll Management Strategy

Medium-high volatility requires balanced bankroll management—less extreme than very high volatility titles but still demanding adequate cushion for variance.

**Recommended Bankroll Sizing:**

**Conservative Approach:**
- **Base Game Strategy**: 300-400x your chosen stake
- **Example**: €1.00 stakes = €300-400 bankroll

**Moderate Approach:**
- **Base Game Strategy**: 200-300x your chosen stake
- **Example**: €1.00 stakes = €200-300 bankroll

**Aggressive Approach:**
- **Base Game Strategy**: 120-180x your chosen stake
- **Example**: €1.00 stakes = €120-180 bankroll

**Bankroll Examples:**

| Strategy Type     | €0.20 Stake | €0.50 Stake | €1.00 Stake | €2.00 Stake |
| ----------------- | ----------- | ----------- | ----------- | ----------- |
| Conservative      | €60-80      | €150-200    | €300-400    | €600-800    |
| Moderate          | €40-60      | €100-150    | €200-300    | €400-600    |
| Aggressive        | €24-36      | €60-90      | €120-180    | €240-360    |

Medium-high volatility means Monopoly is **more forgiving** than titles like Kingmaker or Gold, but still requires discipline during cold streaks.

### Base Game Strategy

**Optimal Approach:**

**Stake Selection:**
- Choose stakes aligned with your bankroll
- €0.10 minimum makes game accessible to smaller budgets
- €20 maximum accommodates higher rollers

**Board Awareness:**
- **Monitor house counts** on properties (visible on board)

**Managing Expectations:**

Base game house building can feel slow:

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does the Reel Adventures™ mechanic work?

Reel Adventures™ is the system where Mr. Monopoly moves around the Monopoly board with each winning combination. Every cascade from a win advances him one space clockwise. Landing on properties on your 3rd+ visit adds houses (1 house per landing after the 2nd visit). Once you build 5 houses on any single property, Free Spins trigger. Landing on special spaces (GO, Chance, Community Chest) awards various benefits.

### How do I trigger Free Spins?

There are two ways: **(1) Primary Method**: Build 5 houses on any single property through Mr. Monopoly landing on it repeatedly (requires 7 total landings: 2 do nothing, then 5 add houses). **(2) Secondary Method**: Land on any Chance space and draw a Chance card—one of the three card options awards instant Free Spins (1 in 3 chance).

### How does the Free Spins multiplier work?

The Free Spins feature includes an **unlimited multiplier** that starts at x1. During each spin, Mr. Monopoly continues moving around the board with each cascade. When he lands on a **property you own** (has at least 1 house), the multiplier **increases by +1 for every house on that property**. For example, landing on a property with 4 houses increases the multiplier by +4. The multiplier persists between spins and applies to all wins, growing cumulatively throughout the entire feature with no cap.

### What do Train Stations and Utilities do?

**Train Stations** and **Utilities** don't build houses but provide spin extension benefits. During base game, landing on them establishes ownership. During **Free Spins**, each time Mr. Monopoly lands on a Train Station or Utility you own, it awards **+1 Free Spin**. Additionally, owning **all 4 Train Stations** when Free Spins trigger grants a bonus **+4 starting spins** (12 total instead of 8).

### What happens when Mr. Monopoly lands on GO?

Landing on the **GO space** activates **maximum 117,649 Megaways™** for that spin, regardless of how many symbols would naturally appear. This guarantees full ways to win for one spin, dramatically increasing win potential if premium symbols align favorably. GO doesn't award Free Spins or houses—only maximum ways activation.

### What do Chance and Community Chest spaces do?

**Chance** (3 spaces on board): Draws a card awarding one of three benefits: (1) Maximum Megaways™ on next spin, (2) Instant Free Spins trigger, or (3) Moves Mr. Monopoly to random property with 1-4 houses added. **Community Chest** (3 spaces): Always awards the same benefit—moves Mr. Monopoly to random property with 1-4 houses added. Both accelerate house building significantly.

Not in the traditional sense (no scatter-based retriggering). However, Free Spins can be **extended** by landing on **Train Stations** or **Utilities** you own (+1 spin each time).
