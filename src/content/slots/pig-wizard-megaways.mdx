## The Megaways™ Engine: 15,625 Ways to Conjure

### Understanding Megaways™ in Pig Wizard

At the heart of Pig Wizard Megaways beats the licensed **Megaways™** engine, the variable-ways system that transformed slot mathematics. Rather than fixed paylines, every reel can display a different number of symbols on each spin, and the total ways to win are recalculated dynamically. This is what gives the game its constantly shifting rhythm and its headline figure of **15,625 ways**.

**How Megaways™ Works in Pig Wizard:**

- **6 vertical reels**, each capable of showing between **2 and 5 symbols** per spin
- **Ways to win** are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
- **Maximum ways**: 5 × 5 × 5 × 5 × 5 × 5 = **15,625 ways to win**
- **Minimum ways**: 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 64 ways to win
- Wins pay for **matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right**, regardless of their position on the reel

**Win Formation Principles:**

- Symbol size and exact row position are irrelevant — only the reel sequence matters
- A winning combination must start on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
- Multiple separate winning combinations can land and pay on a single spin
- Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your **total stake**, not a per-line bet
- The number of ways changes every spin, so no two spins feel mathematically identical

Note that Pig Wizard Megaways tops out at 15,625 ways rather than the 117,649 figure of taller Megaways grids — its reels show a maximum of five symbols each rather than seven. That makes for a slightly tighter, faster-reading board than the busiest Megaways slots, without sacrificing the trademark unpredictability of the format.

### Cascading Reactions

Like most Megaways™ slots, Pig Wizard Megaways employs **cascading reactions** (sometimes called tumbles or avalanches). When you land a winning combination:

1. The symbols that formed the win are removed from the grid
2. The symbols above them drop down to fill the empty spaces
3. New symbols fall in from above to complete the reels
4. If the new arrangement creates another win, it pays and the process repeats
5. Cascades continue until no further wins land

This mechanic means a single paid spin can produce a chain of consecutive wins, and it is especially valuable during free spins, where the Mystery Mirror multiplier applies to every cascade in the chain. A well-timed cascade with a high multiplier attached can be where some of the game's most satisfying payouts originate.

## Mystery Mirror: The Modifier That Drives the Magic

### One Reel, Several Possible Boosts

The signature mechanic of Pig Wizard Megaways is the **Mystery Mirror**, a special modifier reel that sits to the left of the main Megaways grid. On any base-game spin the mirror can come alive and conjure one of several in-play boosts, each designed to rescue, improve, or extend your spin. What makes it so compelling is unpredictability — you never quite know which trick the Pig Wizard will pull next.

**The Mystery Mirror Modifiers:**

1. **Spinnus Repeatum** — When matching symbols land, they are **held in place** while the remaining reels respin. The respins continue until no new winning symbols arrive, giving you repeated chances to build or extend a win from a single trigger.

2. **Magical Win Spin** — Adds extra **wild symbols or mystery symbols** to the reels. Because mystery symbols all reveal the same paying symbol when they land, this modifier is built to manufacture a guaranteed win, often a sizeable multi-way one.

3. **Winnus Revealo** — The **Pig Wizard himself appears** at the end of a spin to either turn a losing board into a winning one or upgrade an existing win into something larger. It is the game's safety net and its most charismatic moment.

**Why the Mystery Mirror Matters:**

- **Constant Variety**: Because the trigger and outcome are random, no two activations feel the same
- **Multiple Win Pathways**: Some modifiers rescue a dead spin, others manufacture or extend wins
- **Engagement Anchor**: The mirror triggers often enough to keep base-game play lively
- **Synergy with Cascades**: Held symbols and added wilds feed directly into the cascading engine

**Example Mystery Mirror Scenarios:**

**Scenario 1 — Spinnus Repeatum Build:**
- £1.00 stake
- The mirror triggers Spinnus Repeatum and holds three matching wizard hats
- The other reels respin twice, each time adding to the win before stopping
- The combined held-symbol win far exceeds what the original spin offered

**Scenario 2 — Magical Win Spin Swarm:**
- £1.00 stake
- The mirror adds a cluster of mystery symbols that all reveal the spellbook
- The spellbooks line up across multiple reels for a solid multi-way payout

**Scenario 3 — Winnus Revealo Rescue:**
- £1.00 stake
- The board lands with no win
- The Pig Wizard appears and transforms symbols to create a winning combination

The Mystery Mirror is the engine room of Pig Wizard Megaways' base game. It is generous in frequency, varied in outcome, and full of personality — and it gives the slot a charm that distinguishes it from generic Megaways clones.

## Free Spins: Multipliers Up to 50x

### Triggering the Bonus

The headline feature of Pig Wizard Megaways is its **free spins** round, triggered by landing **three or more scatter symbols** anywhere on the reels. The number of scatters you land determines how many free spins you start with — typically **10 to 20 spins** depending on the trigger — and additional scatters during the feature can award **further spins**, with the retriggers carrying no stated upper limit.

**How Free Spins Are Awarded:**

- **3 scatters**: a base allocation of free spins
- **4 or more scatters**: a larger starting allocation
- **Retriggers**: extra scatters landed during the round add more spins (commonly 5 to 25 more)
- **No stated cap**: in principle a hot run of retriggers can extend the round considerably

### The Mystery Mirror Bonus

What makes the free spins so explosive is that the **Mystery Mirror activates on every single spin** of the round. On top of the modifiers it can conjure, the mirror applies a **random multiplier to all wins on that spin**, and that multiplier can climb as high as **50x**.

**How the Multiplier Works:**

- Every free spin receives its own randomly determined multiplier
- The multiplier is applied to the **total win** generated on that spin, cascades included
- Multipliers can reach **up to 50x**, the single biggest driver of the game's potential
- Combined with the held-symbol and added-wild modifiers, multiplied multi-way wins can be substantial

**Example:**
- £1.00 stake, free spins active
- A spin pays a 30x multi-way win across a high-ways board
- The Mystery Mirror applies a 20x multiplier to that spin
- 30x × 20 = **600x stake = £600.00** from that spin alone, before any cascade chains

The free spins round is where Pig Wizard Megaways reaches toward its 10,000x ceiling. A lucky 50x multiplier landing on a high-ways, cascading board during a retriggered round is the dream scenario, and it is the moment every spin builds toward.

**Who makes Pig Wizard Megaways and when was it released?**
