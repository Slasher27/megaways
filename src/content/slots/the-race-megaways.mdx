## Wild Duplication: The Core Mechanic

The **Wild Duplication** feature is the beating heart of The Race Megaways™ (pun intended). This is the mechanic that creates the game's most explosive moments in both the base game and free spins.

### How Wild Duplication Works

When wild symbols land on the reels during any spin, they have the potential to **duplicate upward**, filling positions above them on the same reel with additional wild symbols.

**Base Game Wild Duplication:**
1. A wild symbol lands on any position on any reel
2. The wild has a chance to duplicate upward
3. If duplication triggers, additional wilds appear in the positions above
4. Duplication can fill the entire reel (up to 7 positions) with wilds
5. Duplicated wilds do NOT carry multipliers in the base game
6. Only the original wild position matters—duplication fills upward

**Free Spins Wild Duplication:**
1. A wild symbol lands on any position on any reel
2. The wild has the SAME duplication chance as base game
3. If duplication triggers, additional wilds appear above
4. **Each duplicated wild carries a multiplier of 2x, 3x, 4x, or 5x**
5. Multiple wilds on a reel each have their own multiplier
6. Wild multipliers apply to wins that include them
7. Wild multipliers can combine with the unlimited win multiplier

### Wild Duplication Examples

**Base Game Example:**

**Spin Result:**
- Reel 1: A, A, K, Q (4 symbols)
- Reel 2: K, K, K, K, K (5 symbols)

- Reel 4: K, K, K (3 symbols)
- Reel 5: K, K, K, K (4 symbols)
- Reel 6: K, K (2 symbols)

**Win Calculation:**
- K symbols on reels 1-2-3-4-5-6

- Ways: 2 (A symbols on reel 1) × 5 × 4 (wilds) × 3 × 4 × 2 = 960 ways

This single wild duplication created a 384x bet win, demonstrating the power of filling an entire reel with wilds.

## The Heart Bonus: Collecting Lives

The **Heart Bonus** is the pre-free-spins minigame that determines how many lives (spins) you'll have during the free spins feature. This is one of the most innovative bonus trigger mechanics Big Time Gaming has ever created.

### Triggering the Heart Bonus

The Heart Bonus is triggered by landing **3 or more scatter symbols** (game logo) anywhere on the reels during a base game spin. Unlike traditional free spins where 3 scatters = fixed number of spins, in The Race Megaways™, 3+ scatters simply grant access to the Heart Bonus.

### Heart Bonus Mechanics

When you trigger the Heart Bonus, you're presented with a **4x5 grid** (20 positions total) and given **3 spins** to collect hearts.

**Heart Collection Rules:**
1. You start with 3 spins
2. Each spin reveals symbols on the grid
3. **Blue heart symbols** = +1 life for free spins
4. **Red "X" terminator symbols** = Blocker (no benefit, no penalty)
5. Each time you land a heart, the spin counter resets to 3
6. The bonus continues until either:
   - You complete 3 consecutive spins with no hearts landing, OR
   - All 20 positions are filled with hearts (maximum)

**Heart Bonus Example Sequence:**

**Spin 1:**
- 5 hearts land on the grid
- Lives collected: 5
- Spins remaining: 3 (reset)

**Spin 2:**
- 3 hearts land on the grid
- Lives collected: 8 total
- Spins remaining: 3 (reset)

**Spin 3:**
- 0 hearts land (all red X symbols)
- Lives collected: Still 8
- Spins remaining: 2

**Spin 4:**
- 2 hearts land on the grid
- Lives collected: 10 total
- Spins remaining: 3 (reset)

**Spin 5:**
- 0 hearts land
- Lives collected: Still 10
- Spins remaining: 2

**Spin 6:**
- 0 hearts land
- Lives collected: Still 10
- Spins remaining: 1

**Spin 7:**
- 0 hearts land
- Lives collected: Final total: 10
- Heart Bonus ends (3 spins with no improvement)

**Result:** You enter free spins with **10 lives**.

### Heart Bonus Variance

The Heart Bonus adds significant variance to the free spins feature:

**Best Case Scenario:**
- Collect all 20 hearts (fill the entire grid)
- Enter free spins with 20 lives
- Maximum spin potential for building unlimited multiplier

**Average Scenario:**
- Collect 6-10 hearts
- Enter free spins with 6-10 lives
- Moderate runway for multiplier building

**Worst Case Scenario:**
- Collect only 3-4 hearts (initial hearts, then three dead spins)
- Enter free spins with minimal lives
- Very limited opportunity to build multipliers

The Heart Bonus essentially creates a **two-stage variance system**: first you gamble on how many lives you collect, then you gamble on how effectively you use those lives during free spins.

### Psychological Impact

The Heart Bonus is brilliant from a psychological perspective because it:

1. **Extends the anticipation**: You don't go straight to free spins; you have the mini-game first
2. **Creates narrative**: "Collecting lives" feels more engaging than "awarded 10 spins"
3. **Adds agency illusion**: Though it's RNG, it feels like you're actively "earning" your spins
4. **Increases variance perception**: A bad Heart Bonus makes you feel unlucky before free spins even start
5. **Amplifies big wins**: A 20-heart collection followed by a great free spins session feels monumental

However, it also creates frustration when you collect minimal hearts and know your free spins will be short-lived.

## Win Exchange™: Gamble to Enter Free Spins

The **Win Exchange™** feature is a unique BTG innovation that allows you to exchange significant base game wins for a chance to trigger the Heart Bonus (and subsequently, free spins).

### How Win Exchange™ Works

**Trigger Condition:**
When you win between **25x and 100x or more** of your stake in the base game, you're offered the option to gamble the entire win for a chance at the Heart Bonus.

**Win Exchange Process:**

**Scenario 1: Wins between 25x-99x stake**
- A wheel appears showing your win amount
- The wheel displays a percentage chance (shown in green) to win the Heart Bonus
- The percentage varies based on win size (higher wins = better odds)
- You choose: **Gamble** (spin the wheel) or **Collect** (keep your win)
- If wheel lands on green: Heart Bonus triggers, your win is forfeited
- If wheel lands on red: You lose your entire win, back to base game

**Scenario 2: Wins of 100x stake or more**
- You're offered the option to exchange exactly 100x stake for the Heart Bonus
- This is a guaranteed exchange (no wheel gamble)
- You choose: **Exchange 100x** (triggers Heart Bonus, keep excess over 100x) or **Collect** (keep full win)

### Win Exchange Examples

**Example 1: 45x Stake Win**

You win 45x your stake (€9.00 on €0.20 bet) in the base game.

**Win Exchange Offer:**
- Wheel shows 45% chance (green portion of wheel)
- Gamble: 45% chance to win Heart Bonus, 55% chance to lose €9.00
- Collect: Keep the €9.00 guaranteed

**If you gamble and win:**
- Heart Bonus triggers
- Your €9.00 win is forfeited
- Proceed to collect hearts

**If you gamble and lose:**
- €9.00 is lost
- Back to base game with no compensation

**Example 2: 250x Stake Win**

You win 250x your stake (€50.00 on €0.20 bet) in the base game.

**Win Exchange Offer:**
- Option to exchange 100x stake (€20.00) for guaranteed Heart Bonus
- Keep: €50.00 guaranteed
- Exchange: Forfeit €20.00, trigger Heart Bonus, keep €30.00 profit

**If you exchange:**
- €20.00 is deducted
- You keep €30.00 profit
- Heart Bonus triggers

This is essentially paying 100x stake for the Heart Bonus while keeping your excess winnings.

### Win Exchange Strategy

**When to Gamble:**

**Consider Gambling When:**
- Win is 25x-50x with 40%+ wheel chance
- You have sufficient bankroll to absorb the loss
- You're chasing a big free spins hit
- Your session goal is maximum win hunting

**Avoid Gambling When:**
- Win is 80x-99x (too valuable to risk, too small for guaranteed exchange)
- Your bankroll is limited
- You're playing for steady accumulation
- You're already profitable and want to lock gains

**When to Exchange (100x+ Wins):**

**Consider Exchanging When:**
- Win is exactly 100x-120x (small premium over exchange cost)
- You strongly prefer bonus features over base game
- You're on a bonus buy-style strategy

**Avoid Exchanging When:**
- Win is 200x+ (the win itself is significant; keep it)
- You've already had several poor free spins sessions
- You're near session loss limit

### Expected Value Considerations

The Win Exchange™ gamble is generally **negative expected value** compared to collecting your win. The wheel percentages are designed to favor the house, meaning:

- On average, gambling 50x stake wins with 40% wheel chance will lose more often than win
- The long-term optimal strategy is usually to collect wins
- However, variance seekers may enjoy the gamble for shot at big bonus

The guaranteed 100x exchange for wins over 100x is neutral EV—you're paying exactly what the bonus buy costs, but you get to keep excess winnings.
