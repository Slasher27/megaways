## The Megaways™ Engine: 117,649 Ways to Win

### Understanding Megaways™ in Reel Lucky King

At the heart of Reel Lucky King Megaways beats the licensed **Megaways™** engine, the variable-ways system that transformed slot mathematics. Instead of fixed paylines, every reel can display a different number of symbols on each spin, and the total ways to win are recalculated dynamically. This is what gives the game its constantly shifting rhythm and its headline figure of 117,649 ways.

**How Megaways™ Works in Reel Lucky King:**

- **6 vertical reels**, each capable of showing between **2 and 7 symbols** per spin
- **Ways to win** are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
- **Maximum ways**: 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 = **117,649 ways to win**
- **Minimum ways**: 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 64 ways to win
- Wins pay for **matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right**, regardless of their position on the reel

**Win Formation Principles:**

- Symbol size and exact row position are irrelevant — only the reel sequence matters
- A winning combination must start on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
- Multiple separate winning combinations can land and pay on a single spin
- Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your **total stake**, not a per-line bet
- The number of ways changes every spin, so no two spins feel mathematically identical

### Cascading Reactions

Like most Megaways™ slots, Reel Lucky King Megaways uses **cascading reactions** (also called tumbles or explosions). When you land a winning combination:

1. The symbols that formed the win explode and are removed from the grid
2. The symbols above them drop down to fill the empty spaces
3. New symbols fall in from above to complete the reels
4. If the new arrangement creates another win, it pays and the process repeats
5. Cascades continue until no further wins land

This mechanic means a single paid spin can produce a chain of consecutive wins. In Reel Lucky King Megaways, cascades do more than chain payouts — they are the route into the game's headline bonus, because each cascade lights an additional reel and brings you closer to the Reel King feature.

## Lit Reels: Building Toward the Bonus

### How the Lit Reels System Works

A defining touch in Reel Lucky King Megaways is the **Lit Reels** mechanic that runs throughout the base game. As you play, reels turn **yellow** with a satisfying "ding" effect, lighting up from left to right. The lighting is tied directly to cascades — each winning cascade lights an additional reel.

**How Lit Reels Build:**

- Reels illuminate yellow from left to right as you cascade
- Every consecutive cascade lights one more reel
- Lighting **all six reels** triggers the Reel King feature
- Alternatively, **six consecutive cascades** also trigger the feature

This system gives the base game a clear sense of momentum and anticipation. Even when individual cascade wins are modest, watching the reels light one by one builds genuine tension as you edge toward the bonus. It also rewards the strings of chained wins that the Megaways™ cascading engine is built to produce.

## The Reel King Feature: Three Tiers of Fortune

### Triggering the Bonus

The headline attraction of Reel Lucky King Megaways is the **Reel King feature**, the modernised descendant of the classic Reel King mini-slot bonus. It activates in one of two ways:

1. **Lighting all six reels yellow** via the Lit Reels mechanic, or
2. **Landing six consecutive cascades** on a single paid spin

When the feature triggers, special **Reel King mini-slots** are overlaid onto the reels. Each Reel King holds a small slot of three symbols that spins to award **cash prizes** or to advance you along a **free-spins trail**. The more Reel Kings that appear, the richer the feature — and the tier you land determines just how generous things get.

### The Three Feature Tiers

Reel Lucky King Megaways offers three escalating versions of the Reel King feature, each adding more Reel Kings and more free spins:

- **Reel King** — the entry tier, awarding **1-2 Reel Kings** and **8-15 free spins**
- **Super King** — the middle tier, awarding **2-3 Reel Kings** and **8-20 free spins**
- **Lucky King** — the top tier, awarding **3-6 Reel Kings** and up to **25 free spins**

Each tier represents a meaningful step up. The Lucky King tier, with up to six Reel King mini-slots spinning simultaneously and up to 25 free spins, is where the game's biggest payouts are forged. The presence of more Reel Kings means more chances for cash awards and more opportunities to extend your free-spins trail.

## Free Spins & The Unlimited Multiplier

### A Multiplier With No Ceiling

The true engine of Reel Lucky King Megaways' big-win potential is the **unlimited progressive multiplier** that powers the free-spins round. This is the mechanic that separates a quiet bonus from a session-defining one.

**How the Multiplier Works:**

- The multiplier **starts at 1x** when free spins begin
- It **increases by 1x after every winning cascade** (reaction)
- Crucially, there is **no cap** on how high it can climb
- The multiplier applies to your wins during the free-spins round

Because the Megaways™ cascading engine can chain multiple reactions from a single free spin, a productive run can rapidly inflate the multiplier. With up to **25 free spins** available in the Lucky King tier, and every cascade pushing the multiplier higher, a strong sequence can compound into very large payouts — which is precisely how the game reaches its **12,500x** maximum win.

**Why the Unlimited Multiplier Matters:**

- **Compounding potential**: Each cascade not only pays but also raises the multiplier for the next win
- **No artificial ceiling**: Unlike capped-multiplier slots, a hot streak is never throttled
- **Synergy with cascades**: The more the reels chain, the more explosive the round becomes
- **Tier reward**: More free spins in higher tiers mean more chances to build the multiplier

**Example — Building the Multiplier:**

- £1.00 stake, Lucky King tier awards 25 free spins
- Early cascades lift the multiplier to 6x
- A high-value symbol lands across multiple reels for a 40x base win
- With the 6x multiplier: 40x × 6 = **240x stake = £240.00** from that single reaction
- Further cascades push the multiplier higher still for the spins that follow

The unlimited multiplier is the beating heart of this slot's high-volatility profile. Many spins may pass with modest returns, but when the multiplier climbs during a long free-spins run, the rewards can be substantial.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the RTP of Reel Lucky King Megaways?**

Reel Lucky King Megaways has a Return to Player (RTP) of **95.56%**. This sits below the modern Megaways average of around 96% and is slightly lower than the original Reel King Megaways, so it is worth factoring into your expectations.

**What is the maximum win in Reel Lucky King Megaways?**

The maximum win is **12,500x your stake**. At the maximum bet of £20.00 that equates to a top prize of £250,000. The biggest wins are most realistically reached through the free-spins round, where the unlimited multiplier can compound multi-way cascade wins.

**How volatile is Reel Lucky King Megaways?**

The game is **high volatility**. This means you should expect longer dry spells between meaningful wins, with the larger payouts concentrated in the Reel King feature and its free-spins round. A patient approach and a suitable bankroll are recommended.

**Does Reel Lucky King Megaways have a bonus buy?**

**No.** Reel Lucky King Megaways does not offer a bonus buy feature, so you must trigger the Reel King feature organically by lighting all six reels or landing six consecutive cascades.

**How do I trigger the Reel King feature?**

The Reel King feature triggers in one of two ways: by lighting up all six reels yellow via the Lit Reels mechanic, or by landing six consecutive cascades on a single spin. When it activates, mini-slot Reel Kings overlay the reels to award cash and free spins.

**What are the three feature tiers?**

The feature comes in three escalating tiers: **Reel King** (1-2 Reel Kings, 8-15 free spins), **Super King** (2-3 Reel Kings, 8-20 free spins), and **Lucky King** (3-6 Reel Kings, up to 25 free spins). Higher tiers offer more Reel Kings and more free spins.

**How does the unlimited multiplier work?**

During the free-spins round the multiplier starts at 1x and increases by 1x after every winning cascade, with no cap. Because cascades can chain repeatedly, a strong run can lift the multiplier very high and compound your wins toward the 12,500x maximum.

**Who makes Reel Lucky King Megaways and when was it released?**

Reel Lucky King Megaways was developed by **Inspired Gaming** and released on **8 February 2021**. It is the Irish-themed follow-up to Reel King Megaways, the third slot to feature the smiling Reel King as the leading character.
