## The Megaways™ Engine: 512 Ways in a Compact Grid

### Understanding Megaways™ in Candyways Bonanza

At the core of Candyways Bonanza Megaways is the licensed **Megaways™** mechanic, but in a smaller, faster form than the six-reel giants most players know. Instead of fixed paylines, each reel can display a different number of symbols on every spin, and the total ways to win are recalculated dynamically.

**How Megaways™ Works in Candyways Bonanza:**

- **3 vertical reels**, each capable of showing between **3 and 8 symbols** per spin
- **Ways to win** are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
- **Maximum ways**: 8 × 8 × 8 = **512 ways to win**
- Wins pay for **matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right**, regardless of their exact position on the reel

**Win Formation Principles:**

- Only the reel sequence matters — symbol size and exact row position are irrelevant
- A winning combination must start on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
- Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your **total stake**, not a per-line bet
- Because the symbol count shifts each spin, the number of active ways changes constantly

The 512-ways figure is modest next to the 117,649 ways of a full-size Megaways slot, and that is an honest limitation to acknowledge. The trade-off is pace and clarity: with only three reels to track, Candyways Bonanza Megaways plays quickly and stays easy on the eye, making it well suited to mobile and to players who find the six-reel grids overwhelming.

### Tumbling Reels

Like most Megaways™ slots, Candyways Bonanza Megaways uses a **tumble** (cascade) mechanic on its winning spins:

1. The symbols that formed a winning combination are removed from the grid
2. The remaining symbols drop down to fill the gaps
3. Fresh symbols fall in from above to complete the reels
4. If the new arrangement creates another win, it pays and the process repeats
5. Tumbles continue until no further wins land

This means a single paid spin can chain into several consecutive wins, and in a candy-themed slot the satisfying "pop" of dissolving sweets gives every tumble a pleasing sense of momentum.
