## The Megaclusters™ Reactor: Four-Way Reaction Innovation

### Understanding Megaclusters™ in Diamond Fruits

The **Megaclusters™ Reactor** is the revolutionary engine powering Diamond Fruits™, and it introduces a unique twist on the standard Megaclusters™ formula: **four-way reactions**. Understanding how this system works is essential to appreciating the game's explosive potential.

**How Megaclusters™ Reactor Works**:

**Initial Spin Setup**:
1. **5×5 Grid**: The game board contains 25 potential positions
2. **Random Activation**: Between **7 and 25 positions** activate each spin (random)
3. **Symbol Types**: Activated positions display either:
   - **1 large symbol** (occupies full position)
   - **4 smaller symbols** (2×2 cluster within position)
4. **Win Evaluation**: Wins form from matching clusters of symbols

**Winning Sequence**:
1. **Win Detection**: Matching symbols forming winning clusters are identified
2. **Symbol Removal**: Winning symbols removed from grid
3. **Symbol Splitting**: Each removed symbol position splits into **2×2 cluster** (4 smaller symbols)
4. **New Symbols**: New symbols fill the split positions
5. **Four-Way Shift**: Smaller winning symbols shift in one of four directions (up, down, left, right)
6. **Cascade Creation**: Shifted and new symbols evaluated for new wins
7. **Reaction Counter**: Each cascade counts as one reaction
8. **Continue Until Done**: Process repeats until no new wins form

**Free Spins Differences**:
- **Minimum 11 Positions**: Free Spins guarantee at least 11 active positions (vs. 7-25 in base game)
- **Same Mechanics**: Symbol splitting and four-way reactions work identically
- **Win Multiplier Active**: Every reaction increases multiplier by +1

**Four-Way Reaction Explained**:

The four-way reaction system is what distinguishes Diamond Fruits from other Megaclusters™ games:

- **Traditional Megaclusters™**: Symbols drop straight down after removal
- **Diamond Fruits™**: Smaller symbols shift **up, down, left, or right** after forming wins
- **Direction**: Shift direction appears random but creates dynamic, unpredictable gameplay
- **New Positions**: Shifted symbols can land adjacent to other symbols, creating new clusters
- **Chain Potential**: A single initial win can trigger multiple directional shifts and cascades

**Symbol Density Progression**:

**Example Sequence**:
1. **Spin 1**: 15 positions activate with mix of large and small symbols
2. **First Win**: 5 large watermelon symbols form cluster
3. **Split**: 5 positions split into 20 smaller symbols (5 × 4)
4. **Shift**: Smaller watermelons shift right, landing near cherry cluster
5. **New Win**: Cherries form winning cluster
6. **Split Again**: Cherries split, new symbols drop in
7. **Continue**: Reactions build until grid fills with small symbols
8. **Maximum Density**: After extended reactions, up to **100 symbols on screen simultaneously**

**Visual Spectacle**:

The four-way reaction system creates mesmerizing visual dynamics:
- Symbols don't just disappear—they burst with particle effects
- Split symbols animate into their 2×2 configurations
- Smaller symbols shift visibly in their chosen direction
- New symbols drop from top (or appear from sides based on shift direction)
- Entire grid can transform from sparse to densely packed within single spin

**Why Four-Way Reactions Matter**:

This mechanic creates several unique advantages:
- **Unpredictability**: You never know which direction symbols will shift, creating suspense
- **Chain Reaction Potential**: Directional shifts can trigger cascades distant from original win
- **Grid Coverage**: Symbols spread across board rather than clustering in one area
- **Extended Sequences**: Four-way movement creates more opportunities for ongoing reactions
- **Visual Engagement**: Directional shifts are more interesting than standard top-down cascades

The Megaclusters™ Reactor with four-way reactions is what elevates Diamond Fruits™ from a standard cluster-pays game into a dynamic, evolving puzzle where every spin creates unique symbol arrangements and unexpected win patterns.

### How does the Megaclusters™ Reactor work?

The Megaclusters™ Reactor features four-way reactions where winning symbols split into 2×2 clusters of smaller symbols. After splitting, smaller symbols shift in one of four directions (up, down, left, right) rather than just dropping down. This creates dynamic, unpredictable cascades. Each spin activates 7-25 positions on the 5×5 board, displaying either large symbols or four smaller symbols.

### What is the Wild Cannon Bonus?

The Wild Cannon Bonus triggers after 3 reactions in both base game and Free Spins. It adds 5-15 random Wild symbols to the grid, which substitute for all symbols. The number of Wilds is random (5-15 range), and they're placed in random positions across active grid spaces. The feature can trigger multiple times in extended reaction sequences.

### How do you trigger Free Spins?

Free Spins are triggered by achieving 8 reactions in a single base game spin sequence. Each cascade after a win counts as one reaction. You're awarded 8 Free Spins initially, plus 1 additional Free Spin for each reaction beyond the 8th. For example, triggering with 10 reactions awards 8 + 2 = 10 Free Spins.

During Free Spins, a Win Multiplier starts at x1 and increases by +1 after every reaction. There's no upper limit—it can grow indefinitely.
