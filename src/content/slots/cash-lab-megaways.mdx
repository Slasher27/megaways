## The Megaways™ Engine: 200,704 Ways in the Lab

### Understanding the Layout

At the core of Cash Lab Megaways sits the licensed **Megaways™** system, but iSoftBet has wired it up with extra apparatus. The grid uses **six vertical reels**, and on the four middle reels (reels 2 to 5) it adds **horizontal reels** running along the top and bottom of the board. These horizontal reels feed additional symbols into the columns they touch, pushing the maximum symbol count per reel up to **eight** and inflating the total ways far beyond a standard Megaways slot.

**How the Ways Are Calculated:**

- **6 vertical reels**, with the outer reels (1 and 6) and the middle reels carrying a variable number of symbols
- **Horizontal reels** on the top and bottom of reels 2-5 add extra symbols to those columns
- **Ways to win** are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
- **Maximum ways**: this configuration produces up to **200,704 ways to win**
- Wins pay for **matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right**, regardless of row position

**Win Formation Principles:**

- Only the sequence of reels matters — symbol position within a reel is irrelevant
- A winning combination must start on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
- Several separate winning combinations can land and pay on a single spin
- Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your **total stake**, not a per-line bet
- The number of ways shifts every spin, so the board is in constant flux

### Cascading Wins

Like most Megaways™ titles, Cash Lab Megaways uses **cascading wins** (also called tumbles). When a winning combination lands:

1. The symbols that formed the win are removed from the grid
2. Remaining symbols drop down to fill the gaps
3. Fresh symbols fall in from above to complete the reels
4. Any new winning combination pays, and the process repeats
5. Cascades continue until no further wins appear

Cascades are especially important here because they interact directly with the Gold Link multiplier, which climbs with each successive reaction. A single paid spin that triggers a Gold Link and then cascades repeatedly can stack a powerful multiplier onto an already huge ways count.

- A fully wild reel acts as a guaranteed contributor to any combination crossing it
