## The Megaways™ Engine: 117,649 Ways to Party

### Understanding Megaways™ in Ted

At the core of Ted Megaways sits the licensed **Megaways™** engine, the variable-ways system pioneered by Big Time Gaming and put to prolific use under Blueprint's licence. Instead of fixed paylines, each reel displays a different number of symbols on every spin, and the total ways to win are recalculated dynamically each time.

**How Megaways™ Works in Ted Megaways:**

- **6 vertical reels**, each capable of showing between **2 and 7 symbols** per spin
- A **horizontal top reel** adds one extra symbol position above each of reels two, three, four and five
- **Ways to win** are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
- **Maximum ways**: the full board delivers **117,649 ways to win**
- **Minimum ways**: the leanest possible board still provides **324 ways**
- Wins pay for **matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right**, regardless of position

**Win Formation Principles:**

- Symbol size and exact row position are irrelevant — only the reel sequence matters
- A winning combination must start on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
- Multiple separate winning combinations can land and pay on a single spin
- Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your **total stake**
- The ways count changes every spin, so no two spins feel mathematically identical

The 324-way floor is worth noting: it is meaningfully higher than the 64-way minimum found on many classic Megaways grids, thanks to the top reel and the game's reel configuration, so even Ted's laziest spins keep a reasonable number of ways in play.

### Cascading Reactions

Like all the best Megaways™ titles, Ted Megaways runs on **cascading reactions**. When you land a winning combination:

1. The symbols that formed the win are removed from the grid
2. The symbols above drop down to fill the empty spaces
3. New symbols fall in from above to complete the reels
4. If the new arrangement creates another win, it pays and the process repeats
5. Cascades continue until no further wins land

In the base game, cascades are a pleasant way to stretch a single spin into a chain of payouts. In the Thunder Buddies free spins, they become the entire engine of the game's win potential — because every cascade there pushes the unlimited multiplier one step higher.

## Thunder Buddies Free Spins: The Unlimited Multiplier

Once the free spins begin, Ted Megaways reveals its true win engine: a **progressive win multiplier** that starts at **1x** and increases by **+1 with every cascading win**, with **no upper limit** and no reset between spins for the duration of the round.

**How the Free Spins Multiplier Works:**

- The round begins with the multiplier at 1x
- Every cascade during the round — from any spin — adds +1 to the multiplier
- The multiplier **never resets** during the bonus; it only climbs
- All wins are multiplied by the current value, so late-round wins are worth far more than early ones

This is the classic Blueprint-style Megaways bonus mathematics, and it is where the **50,000x** ceiling lives. A long chain of cascades early in the round sets up a towering multiplier, and any substantial multi-way win landed afterwards gets amplified accordingly.

## How to Win: Strategy & Tips

Ted Megaways is a genuinely high-volatility game, and your approach should respect that. Here is how to party responsibly.

### Bankroll Management

- **Budget for drought**: High volatility means the base game can run cold for long stretches. Size your bets so your session bankroll covers at least 200-250 spins; the £0.10 minimum makes this achievable on modest budgets.
- **Set win and loss limits**: The 50,000x ceiling is real but rare. Decide in advance when you will walk away, and treat any bonus round that pays well as a natural exit point.
- **Price the bonus buy honestly**: At 150x stake, the buy is a large outlay that lands you on a gamble wheel. Only use it with money you can comfortably lose, and never chase a poor wheel result with another immediate purchase.

### Base Game Strategy

- **Mystery Ted is your base-game engine**: Outside the bonus, the transformation feature is where the meaningful wins come from. There is no way to influence it — patience is the only strategy.
- **Respect the wild's limits**: With wilds confined to the horizontal top reel, base-game combinations lean heavily on natural symbol alignment and cascades. Do not expect wild-driven rescues.
- **Use the demo first**: The free-play version lets you learn the rhythm of Mystery Ted and the bonus trigger frequency before staking real money.

### Bonus Round Strategy

- **Safe spins versus the Thunder Wheel**: The guaranteed 12 spins are a solid, known quantity. The wheel offers upgraded packages with higher guaranteed Megaways — the key stat for round quality — but can also land below the safe option. Conservative players should collect; variance-tolerant players chasing the big number should spin.
- **Value the Megaways floor over raw spin count**: A round with fewer spins but a high guaranteed Megaways floor will usually outperform a longer round on a lean board, because more ways means more cascades and a faster-climbing multiplier.
- **Let the multiplier do the work**: The unlimited, never-resetting multiplier is the entire win model. Long rounds that keep collecting Ted symbols are where the game's headline potential lives.
