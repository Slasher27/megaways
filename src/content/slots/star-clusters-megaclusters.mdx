## The Megaclusters™ Mechanic: Symbol Splitting Redefined

### How Megaclusters™ Works


The Megaclusters™ mechanic is the beating heart of Star Clusters Megaclusters™, and understanding how it functions is key to appreciating the game's unique appeal. Unlike traditional Megaways slots where reels spin independently with variable symbol heights, Megaclusters operates on an entirely different principle—**symbol splitting and grid expansion**.


Here's the cosmic process in detail:

**Base Game Grid (4x4)**:
- The game begins with a standard 4x4 grid containing 16 symbol positions
- Each position displays a single full-size symbol
- When you land a winning combination, those winning symbols are removed
- Here's where the magic happens: **each removed symbol position splits into four smaller symbol positions**
- These four smaller positions are filled with new symbols, effectively quadrupling the symbols in those spaces
- The grid temporarily expands to accommodate these smaller symbols
- This process creates a cascade effect, with potential for new winning combinations
- The cycle continues as long as new wins keep forming

**Free Spins Grid (8x8)**:
- The Free Spins feature takes place on a significantly larger 8x8 grid
- This expanded grid contains 64 symbol positions initially
- The same splitting mechanic applies, but with far greater potential
- When symbols split during Free Spins, the number of smaller symbols on screen can reach astronomical levels
- The theoretical maximum is **256 individual symbols** displayed simultaneously
- With symbol splitting creating 4x the positions, the ways to win multiply exponentially
- This creates the potential for up to **16,777,216 ways to win** in optimal conditions

**The Reaction Chain**:
The term "reaction" in Star Clusters refers to each cascade that occurs after a win:
1. **Initial Spin**: Symbols land on the grid
2. **First Win**: Winning symbols identified and removed
3. **Reaction 1**: Removed positions split into four, new symbols fill the spaces
4. **Subsequent Reactions**: Process repeats for each new winning combination
5. **Reaction Counter**: The game tracks total reactions, which determines Free Spins triggers

**Why Megaclusters™ Matters**:
This mechanic creates several unique advantages:
- **Exponential Growth**: Each reaction potentially multiplies the active symbols on screen
- **Increasing Win Potential**: More symbols mean more possible winning combinations
- **Visual Spectacle**: Watching symbols burst and split creates engaging, dynamic gameplay
- **Chain Win Psychology**: Every win creates anticipation for the next cascade
- **Strategic Depth**: Understanding how splits work helps you appreciate big win sequences

The Megaclusters™ system represents a genuine innovation in slot mathematics, creating gameplay that feels distinctly different from both traditional slots and standard Megaways games. It's a mechanic that rewards patience during base game play and delivers explosive potential during those critical Free Spins sessions.

The Gold Wild Multiplier is the second pillar of Star Clusters Megaclusters™'s appeal, and it's this feature that can transform modest wins into genuinely stellar payouts. The system is elegantly simple in concept but powerful in execution:


**Gold Wild Basics**:

- Up to **two Gold Wild symbols** can land simultaneously
- Gold Wilds substitute for all regular pay symbols to create or extend winning combinations
- Each Gold Wild that participates in a winning combination increases the multiplier by 1x
- The multiplier applies to the total win for that cascade

- **Carryover Value**: Triggering Free Spins with an already elevated multiplier gives your bonus round a powerful head start
