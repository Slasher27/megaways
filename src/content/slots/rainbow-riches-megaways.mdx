## The Megaways™ Mechanic: 117,649 Ways Across the Rainbow

### Understanding Megaways™ in Rainbow Riches

At the heart of Rainbow Riches Megaways sits the Megaways™ system, the variable-ways engine that revolutionised modern slots. Rather than offering a fixed number of paylines, Megaways™ assigns a random number of symbols to each reel on every spin, dramatically changing the number of ways to win from one moment to the next.

**How Megaways™ Works Here:**

- **6 main vertical reels**, each able to show between **2 and 7 symbols**
- A **horizontal top reel** adds an extra symbol to reels 2, 3, 4 and 5
- Ways to win are calculated by **multiplying the symbol counts across all reels**
- **Maximum configuration**: 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 = **117,649 ways to win**
- **Minimum configuration**: as few as 64 ways when reels are short

**Win Formation Principles:**

- Matching symbols on **adjacent reels from left to right** form a win
- A symbol's size or position on a reel is irrelevant — only the reel sequence matters
- Several winning combinations can land on a single spin simultaneously
- All payouts are expressed as a **multiple of your total stake**, not per line
- Every win triggers a **cascade**, removing winners and dropping in replacements

**Why Megaways™ Matters in This Slot:**

The variable-ways structure does more than inflate the headline number. Because the grid constantly reshuffles, no two spins feel identical — a tall reel configuration can suddenly unlock tens of thousands of ways, while the cascade mechanic means a single lucky drop can chain into several wins. In Rainbow Riches Megaways, this rhythm of cascades and shifting ways keeps the base game lively as you hunt for the Pots of Gold that unlock the free spins.

## Cascading Reels: Consecutive Wins From One Spin

### How the Cascade Works

Like virtually every Megaways™ slot, Rainbow Riches Megaways uses a **cascading reels** (also called tumbling or avalanche) mechanic that rewards each winning spin with the chance for more wins at no extra cost.

**The Cascade Sequence:**

1. **You spin** and the reels fill with symbols across the variable Megaways™ grid
2. **A win lands** — matching symbols on adjacent reels from the leftmost reel
3. **Winning symbols are removed** from the grid, leaving empty spaces
4. **New symbols tumble down** from above (and from the top reel) to fill the gaps
5. **The grid re-evaluates** for new wins formed by the freshly dropped symbols
6. **The cycle repeats** until a drop produces no further winning combination

**Why Cascades Are Crucial:**

- A single paid spin can produce a **chain of consecutive wins**, stacking up payouts
- Cascades are the mechanism that **drives the free spins multiplier** (more on that below)
- They maintain on-screen action and anticipation between bigger features
- Longer cascade chains correlate directly with larger overall spin payouts

In the base game, cascades simply add value to winning spins. But once you enter the Pot of Gold free spins, cascades become the engine that powers Rainbow Riches Megaways' most exciting feature — the unlimited win multiplier.

## Super Gem Bonus: One Colour to Pay Them All

### The Multicoloured Gem on the Rainbow Reel

The **Super Gem Bonus** is one of Rainbow Riches Megaways' most distinctive features and a genuine point of difference from a standard Megaways title. It revolves around a special **multicoloured gem symbol that appears only on the horizontal top reel** — the "rainbow reel" that crowns the main grid.

**How the Super Gem Bonus Works:**

1. **The multicoloured gem lands** on the top rainbow reel during a base game spin
2. The feature **triggers automatically** when this special gem appears
3. **A single gem colour is randomly selected** by the game
4. **Every gem symbol currently on the reels transforms** into that one chosen gem
5. The grid then **evaluates for wins** with all the now-matching gems counted together
6. Because so many positions can suddenly share the same symbol, the result can be a **huge cluster of matching ways**

**Example Super Gem Scenario:**

- You spin with a tall grid showing a scattered mix of red, green, blue and purple gems
- The multicoloured gem lands on the top rainbow reel and triggers the Super Gem Bonus
- The game randomly selects the **green gem**
- Every gem on the board — red, blue, purple — transforms into green
- A grid that previously held a handful of matching green gems now overflows with them
- The Megaways™ engine counts all the matching green ways across the reels, paying out a substantial combined win, with cascades potentially following

**Strategic Significance:**

- The Super Gem Bonus can convert a near-miss board into a major paying spin
- It rewards **tall reel configurations**, where more positions mean more gems to transform
- Combined with cascades, a single Super Gem trigger can snowball into a string of wins
- It keeps the base game engaging even before the free spins are in reach

The Super Gem Bonus is a clever nod to the gem-driven heritage of the wider Rainbow Riches series, repurposed for the Megaways™ format in a way that produces some of the slot's most satisfying base-game moments.

**Strategic Implications:**

## How to Win: Strategy & Tips

Rainbow Riches Megaways is a high-volatility slot, which shapes how you should approach it. No strategy can change the underlying RTP of roughly 95.99%, but smart play helps you survive the variance and reach the features where the real wins live.

### Bankroll Management

- **Size your bets for longevity.** With high volatility and dry spells likely, keep individual stakes to a small fraction (1-2%) of your session bankroll.
- **Use the low minimum.** The 20p minimum bet lets you extend your number of spins, which matters when you're waiting for four Pots of Gold to appear.
- **Set win and loss limits** before you play, and treat the free spins as the goal rather than every spin.
- **Avoid chasing.** Because the multiplier-driven free spins carry the slot's potential, resist the urge to ramp up stakes after a losing streak.

### Base Game Tips

- **Watch the top rainbow reel.** The multicoloured Super Gem only lands there — every appearance is a base-game bonus that can rescue an otherwise modest spin.
- **Tall reels are your friend.** The more symbols on screen, the more ways to win and the bigger any Super Gem transformation becomes.
- **Let cascades play out.** Each cascade is a free re-evaluation of the grid; don't rush the animations.
- **Be patient for pots.** Four Pots of Gold is the magic number — the base game is largely a waiting room for the free spins.

### Free Spins & Bonus Strategy

- **Aim for more than four pots.** Extra Pots of Gold add +5 spins each, giving the unlimited multiplier more time to climb.
- **Embrace the snowball.** The multiplier rises +1x per cascade and never resets, so the back half of a long round is where wins multiply hardest.
- **Don't judge early.** A slow start to the free spins still builds the multiplier with every cascade — the biggest hits often come late.
- **No bonus buy means no shortcuts.** Since you can't purchase the feature, the disciplined path is consistent stakes and patience until the round triggers organically.

The overarching lesson is simple: Rainbow Riches Megaways pays out in bursts. Protect your bankroll through the quiet base game and you'll be in a position to capitalise when the leprechaun finally hands over his pot of gold.
