## The Megaways™ Engine: 200,704 Ways to Read Your Fortune

### Understanding Megaways™ in Madame Destiny

At the core of Madame Destiny Megaways is the licensed **Megaways™** engine, the variable-ways system that abandons fixed paylines in favour of a grid that reshapes itself on every spin. Each of the six reels can display a different number of symbols, and the total ways to win are recalculated dynamically. This is what produces the game's eye-catching headline of up to **200,704 ways** — one of the largest figures in the genre.

**How Megaways™ Works in Madame Destiny:**

- **6 vertical reels**, each capable of showing between **2 and 7 symbols** per spin
- **Ways to win** are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
- The reels run an expanded layout that can reach **up to 200,704 ways** at maximum height
- **Minimum ways**: when reels land short, the way-count drops dramatically
- Wins pay for **matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right**, regardless of position

**Win Formation Principles:**

- Symbol size and exact row position are irrelevant — only the reel sequence matters
- A winning combination must start on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
- Multiple separate winning combinations can land and pay on a single spin
- Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your **total stake**, not a per-line bet
- The number of ways changes every spin, so no two spins ever feel mathematically identical

The 200,704 ceiling matters because it raises the probability of high-paying symbols connecting across all six reels. When a tall board lines up with a premium symbol, the resulting multi-way win can be substantial — and that is before the wild multipliers and cascades enter the picture.

### Cascading Reels (The Tumble Feature)

Like most Megaways™ slots, Madame Destiny Megaways employs **cascading reels**, which Pragmatic Play also markets as the **Tumble** feature. When you land a winning combination:

1. The symbols that formed the win are removed from the grid
2. The symbols above them drop down to fill the gaps
3. Fresh symbols tumble in from above to complete the reels
4. If the new arrangement creates another win, it pays and the process repeats
5. Cascades continue until no further wins land

This mechanic means a single paid spin can produce a chain of consecutive wins. Cascades are especially valuable here because the Madame Destiny wild multiplier can attach to multiple links in the chain, and during free spins the round's fixed multiplier compounds the effect of every tumble.

## The Madame Destiny Wild: A Built-In 2x Multiplier

### Fortune Favours the Wild

The signature symbol of the game is **Madame Destiny** herself, who appears as a **wild symbol** and is far more than a simple substitute. Whenever she helps complete a winning combination, she applies a **2x multiplier** to that win. She substitutes for all paying symbols (everything except the crystal ball scatter), so her appearances frequently turn near-misses into paid wins — and double them in the process.

**How the Wild Multiplier Works:**

- Madame Destiny substitutes for any standard paying symbol to complete a combination
- Each wild that contributes to a win applies a **2x multiplier** to that win
- When **two wilds** form part of the same winning combination, their multipliers **multiply together** for a **4x** boost
- Additional wilds continue to stack the multiplier (8x, 16x and beyond in rare cases)
- Because cascades reveal fresh symbols, a single spin can chain several wild-boosted wins

**Why the Wild Matters:**

- **Frequency**: Wilds appear often enough to meaningfully lift base-game returns
- **Stacking Potential**: Multiple wilds in one win create exponential multiplier growth
- **Cascade Synergy**: A wild can survive a tumble and contribute to the next win too
- **Free Spins Amplification**: During free spins, wild multipliers stack on top of the round's fixed multiplier for the game's most explosive moments

**Example — Stacked Wild Win:**

- £1.00 stake
- A symbol pays 15x across a tall board
- Two Madame Destiny wilds form part of the combination: 2x × 2x = **4x**
- The win is boosted: 15x × 4 = **60x stake = £60.00**

The wild is the engine of Madame Destiny Megaways' base game. It keeps the reels feeling alive between bonus triggers and provides the multiplicative spark that the slot's high-variance maths depends on.

## The Free Spins Wheel: Spin to Set Your Fortune

### Triggering the Bonus

### The Wheel Determines Spins and Multiplier

The bonus wheel sets **two values simultaneously**:

- **The number of free spins**, ranging from **5 to 12**
- **A win multiplier**, ranging from **2x up to 25x**

This is the crucial mechanic of the entire game. A larger spin count gives you more chances to hit, while a larger multiplier inflates every single win you land during the round. The very best outcome — a high multiplier paired with a healthy number of spins — is where Madame Destiny Megaways reaches toward its **5,000x** maximum win.

**How the Free Spins Round Plays Out:**

- The wheel locks in your spin count and your **fixed** multiplier for the round
- Every win during free spins is multiplied by that fixed value
- The Madame Destiny wild's own 2x (or stacked) multiplier **applies on top** of the round multiplier
- Cascading reels remain fully active, chaining multiplied wins together
- Landing further scatters during the feature **retriggers** additional spins, and retriggers can occur **indefinitely**

**Why the Wheel Matters:**

- **Variance Built In**: A 2x multiplier with 5 spins is a damp squib; 25x with 12 spins can be life-changing
- **Layered Multipliers**: The round multiplier and wild multipliers combine for huge potential
- **Retrigger Upside**: Indefinite retriggers mean a hot run can extend far beyond the initial award

**Example — Strong Free Spins Outcome:**

- £1.00 stake
- The wheel awards **10 free spins at a 25x multiplier**
- On one spin a symbol pays 8x across a tall board, with a single wild attached (×2): 8 × 2 = 16x
- The round multiplier applies: 16 × 25 = **400x stake = £400.00** from that spin alone
- Cascades and retriggers can build the total far higher

The wheel mechanic gives the bonus genuine drama. Watching it spin to reveal whether you have drawn a modest or monstrous multiplier is the highlight of the game, and it makes every free-spins trigger feel meaningfully different.

For players who want the bonus to arrive more frequently, Madame Destiny Megaways offers an **Ante Bet** option. Activating it **increases your stake by 25%** and, in exchange, **doubles your chance of triggering the free spins** round. It does not change the maximum win, but it does shift the slot toward more frequent feature play. The Ante Bet also slightly raises the effective RTP, making it a reasonable choice for players who value bonus frequency.

### The Bonus Buy

For the most direct route to the wheel, the game includes a **Bonus Buy** feature. For a cost of **100x your stake**, you can purchase immediate entry to the free spins round, skipping the base game entirely. This is ideal for players who want to head straight for the slot's headline feature.

**Important note:** The bonus buy is **not available to players in the United Kingdom** (and certain other regulated markets), where buy features are prohibited. UK players must trigger the feature organically or use the Ante Bet to improve their odds.
