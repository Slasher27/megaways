## The XtraGacha Mechanic: Tiki XtraLock Explained

### What XtraGacha Means

XtraGacha is Swintt's proprietary mechanic, and the name is a direct nod to the **gacha** concept popular in Japanese games — the capsule-toy idea where you collect items by repeated draws. In Royal Rumble XtraGacha, that collecting fantasy is reimagined as a **sticky-symbol respin feature** called the **Tiki XtraLock Feature**. Instead of pulling capsules, you are locking in Tiki Masks and spinning to gather more.

This mechanic is the defining identity of the game. Where Megaways slots vary the number of ways and cluster slots remove and refill symbols, XtraGacha is all about **accumulation and persistence**: symbols that land stay put while fresh spins try to add to your collection.

### How the Tiki XtraLock Feature Triggers

The feature begins when you land **6 or more Tiki Masks anywhere in view** on a single spin. When that happens:

1. All triggering Tiki Masks become **sticky**, locking into their positions
2. You are awarded **5 Tiki Spins** (sticky respins)
3. During Tiki Spins, the **regular paying symbols are removed** and the reels show only blanks and additional Tiki Masks

### How Tiki Spins Play Out

- Each spin, any **new Tiki Masks that land also become sticky** and lock in place
- The standard symbols do not appear during this phase — it is purely a hunt for more masks
- The respin counter resets behaviour around landing fresh masks as you attempt to fill more of the grid
- When the Tiki Spins conclude, the collected masks pay out based on how many you have accumulated

The appeal here is pure tension. Every respin is a fresh draw — a gacha pull — and the more masks you stack, the larger the eventual payout. It is a clean, readable feature that rewards a strong trigger and keeps you on the edge of your seat as the blanks spin past.

## Free Spins: Reel 3 Goes Fully Wild

### Triggering the Free Spins

The second major feature is a classic **Free Spins** round, triggered by landing **3 or more Bomb scatter symbols** anywhere on the reels. When you trigger it:

- You are awarded **12 free spins**
- You also collect an **immediate scatter pay** worth between **2x and 100x of your stake**, depending on how many scatters landed

### The Wild Reel

What elevates this round above a standard free-spins bonus is the **wild reel** mechanic. For the entire duration of the free spins:

- **The whole of reel 3 becomes wild**
- This central wild reel substitutes for all paying symbols, dramatically increasing the frequency of completed lines
- Because reel 3 sits in the middle of the grid, a permanently wild column helps bridge combinations across the five reels

With 50 paylines active and the middle reel guaranteed to contribute a wild on every position, line completions come thick and fast. This is where much of the game's payout potential lives, and it is the round most players will be chasing.

### Retriggering More Spins

The free spins can extend themselves. If you land **additional scatters during the round**, you **retrigger the feature with +12 extra spins**. There is no hard cap implied on stacking retriggers, so a hot run of scatters can keep the wild reel spinning well beyond the initial dozen.

**Example:**
- 1.00 stake, you land 3 Bomb scatters
- You collect an instant scatter pay and start 12 free spins with reel 3 fully wild
- Two spins in, another 3 scatters land and add 12 more spins
- The middle wild reel completes line after line across the extended round
