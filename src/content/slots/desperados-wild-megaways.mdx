## The Megaways™ Engine: 117,649 Ways to Wrangle

### Understanding Megaways™ in Desperados Wild

At the heart of Desperados Wild Megaways beats the licensed **Megaways™** engine, the variable-ways system that reshaped slot mathematics. Rather than fixed paylines, every reel can display a different number of symbols on each spin, and the total ways to win are recalculated dynamically. This is what gives the game its constantly shifting rhythm and its headline figure of 117,649 ways.

**How Megaways™ Works in Desperados Wild:**

- **6 vertical reels**, each capable of showing between **2 and 7 symbols** per spin
- **Ways to win** are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
- **Maximum ways**: 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 = **117,649 ways to win**
- **Minimum ways**: 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 64 ways to win
- Wins pay for **matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right**, regardless of their position on the reel

**Win Formation Principles:**

- Symbol size and exact row position are irrelevant — only the reel sequence matters
- A winning combination must start on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
- Multiple separate winning combinations can land and pay on a single spin
- Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your **total stake**, not a per-line bet
- The number of ways changes every spin, so no two spins feel mathematically identical

### Cascading Wins

Like most Megaways™ slots, Desperados Wild Megaways employs **cascading wins** (sometimes called tumbles or avalanches). When you land a winning combination:

1. The symbols that formed the win are removed from the grid
2. The symbols above them drop down to fill the empty spaces
3. New symbols fall in from above to complete the reels
4. If the new arrangement creates another win, it pays and the process repeats
5. Cascades continue until no further wins land

This mechanic means a single paid spin can produce a chain of consecutive wins. It is especially important in this game because the cascade engine is directly tied to the **increasing multiplier** in the Sunset Spins bonus, where every consecutive cascade pushes the multiplier higher. A well-timed cascade chain is where some of the game's most satisfying payouts originate.

**How Jail House Works:**

**Why Jail House Matters:**

**Example:**
