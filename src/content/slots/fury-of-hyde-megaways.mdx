## The Megaways™ Mechanic: 117,649 Ways to Horror Riches

### Understanding Megaways™ in Fury of Hyde

Fury of Hyde Megaways™ utilizes the licensed Megaways™ engine in a slightly modified configuration: **6 vertical reels** displaying 2-7 symbols each, plus a unique **4-symbol horizontal tracker reel** positioned above the main grid that displays the letters H, Y, D, and E.

**How Megaways™ Works:**

- **6 main vertical reels** with 2-7 symbols per reel
- **4-symbol horizontal tracker** (does NOT count toward ways calculation, only triggers Hyde feature)
- **Ways to win calculated** by multiplying symbols on the 6 main reels only
- **Maximum ways**: 7×7×7×7×7×7 = **117,649 ways to win**
- **Minimum ways**: 2×2×2×2×2×2 = 64 ways

**Win Formation:**

Wins are awarded for **left-to-right adjacent symbols regardless of size**:

- Symbol size irrelevant—small and large symbols count equally
- Only consecutive reels from left to right form winning combinations
- Multiple winning combinations can occur simultaneously
- All payouts multiplied by total stake
- **Cascading Reactions**: Winning symbols removed, new symbols drop from above
- **Tracker Reveal**: Cascades remove symbols from tracker, potentially revealing H-Y-D-E letters

**The Unique Tracker Reel:**

The 4-symbol horizontal reel above the main grid serves a specialized function:
- Displays potential H, Y, D, E letter symbols
- Letters are initially hidden behind other symbols
- Cascades from wins remove tracker symbols
- If all 4 letters (H-Y-D-E) are revealed, **Hyde Super Spin triggers**
- Tracker does NOT contribute to standard win ways calculation

This creates strategic depth: larger cascading sequences remove more tracker symbols, increasing probability of revealing all four letters needed for the transformation.

## Hyde Super Spin: Unleashing Mr. Hyde's Fury

### Triggering the Transformation

The **Hyde Super Spin** is Fury of Hyde Megaways™'s signature innovation and the mechanic that elevates it beyond standard Megaways™ slots. This feature embodies the game's Jekyll-to-Hyde transformation theme through genuine gameplay impact.

**How to Trigger Hyde Super Spin:**

2. **Cascades Remove Tracker Symbols**: Each cascade potentially reveals letters on the 4-symbol tracker
3. **Spell H-Y-D-E**: All four positions must display their respective letters
4. **Transformation Occurs**: After standard win calculation completes, Hyde Super Spin activates
5. **Super Spin Executes**: A special enhanced spin with three modifiers active

### The Three Hyde Super Spin Modifiers

When Hyde Super Spin triggers, **three modifiers apply simultaneously** to create a dramatically enhanced spin:

**1. Symbol Removal Modifier**

- **Effect**: Removes at least one low-value symbol type from the reels
- **Implementation**: Typically removes the lowest-paying symbol (often 9 or 10)
- **Benefit**: Increases density of premium symbols, improving win potential
- **Cascading Impact**: Removal creates additional cascades, potentially revealing more wins

**Example:**
- Before Hyde: Reels contain 9s, 10s, J, Q, K, A, and premium symbols
- Hyde Removes: All 9 symbols eliminated from visible reels
- Result: Higher proportion of valuable symbols, better win probability

**2. Multiplier Increase Modifier**

- **Effect**: Increases current win multiplier by **+2 or +3**
- **Base Game**: If no multiplier exists, creates x2 or x3 starting multiplier
- **Free Spins**: Adds to existing multiplier (e.g., x5 becomes x7 or x8)
- **Persistence**: During Free Spins, this boost remains active for all subsequent spins

**Example Multiplier Growth:**
- Base game Hyde Super Spin: x1 → x3 (adds +2)
- Free Spins at x12 multiplier: x12 → x15 (adds +3)
- All wins during/after Hyde spin benefit from enhanced multiplier

**3. Minimum Megaways Guarantee Modifier**

- **Effect**: Guarantees a minimum number of Megaways™ for the super spin
- **Range**: Can guarantee 1,000 / 2,000 / 5,000 / 10,000 / 50,000 / or maximum **117,649 ways**
- **Random Assignment**: Which minimum is awarded appears random
- **Benefit**: Ensures substantial ways even if natural reel configuration would be low

**Example Minimum Ways Impact:**
- Natural spin configuration: 3×4×2×5×3×4 = 1,440 ways
- Hyde guarantees: **50,000 minimum ways**
- Result: Reels adjust to display configuration yielding 50,000+ ways (e.g., 7×6×7×7×6×7)

**Combined Power of Triple Modifiers:**

The simultaneous application creates exponential enhancement:
- **Symbol removal** increases premium symbol density (better base wins)
- **Multiplier boost** multiplies those better base wins by x2-x3 more
- **Minimum ways** ensures extensive win line coverage

**Example Combined Impact:**
- Standard spin: 20x base win × x1 multiplier on 5,000 ways = 20x payout
