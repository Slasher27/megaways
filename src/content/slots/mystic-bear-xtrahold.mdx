## The XtraHold™ Re-Spin Feature: Hold for the Moons

### How XtraHold Works

The signature mechanic of the game — and the reason for the **XtraHold** in its name — is the studio's hold-and-win style **re-spin feature**. It is the heart of the slot and the most likely route to its largest payouts.

The feature triggers when **6 or more full moon symbols land anywhere on the reels** in a single spin. When this happens:

1. All visible **full moon symbols lock in place** and become sticky
2. The game awards an initial **3 re-spins**
3. During the feature, the **only symbol that can appear is the bonus full moon**
4. Each new full moon that lands **sticks to the grid** and **resets the re-spin counter back to 3**
5. Every locked full moon holds a **cash value** displayed on the symbol
6. When the re-spins run out, all the held cash values are **summed up and paid** as your total win

This is a classic respin-style hold-and-win mechanic: as long as you keep landing new moons, the feature continues, and your potential total grows with every additional symbol locked onto the board.

### The Grand Prize

There is an extra layer of reward built into XtraHold. If full moons come to occupy **every position across the entire reel display area** — a complete full-screen lock — the game awards its **Mystic Bear grand prize**, the top-tier hold-and-win payout. Filling all 20 positions is the dream scenario and the route toward the slot's 10,000x ceiling.

### Why XtraHold Matters

- **Self-Extending**: Each new symbol resets the counter, so a hot streak can run far longer than the base 3 re-spins
- **Sticky Certainty**: Once a value is locked, it cannot be lost — your total only ever grows
- **Clear Goal**: Filling the grid for the grand prize gives the feature a tangible big-win objective
- **The Headline Feature**: This is where the bulk of the slot's win potential and excitement is concentrated

## Free Spins: Mega Symbol Megawins

The second major feature is a more traditional **free spins** round, triggered by landing **paw print scatter symbols**:

### The Mega Symbol

What sets this round apart is the **Mega symbol**. During the free spins feature, the **three middle reels — reels 2, 3 and 4 — spin together as one giant symbol block**, all displaying the same symbol at once. Because these reels move in unison, matching symbols fill a large portion of the grid simultaneously, dramatically increasing the chance of completing big multi-line wins across the 25 paylines.

When a high-value animal lands as the Mega symbol and connects with matching symbols on reels 1 and 5, the result can be a sizeable payout that single-position reels could never deliver. The Mega symbol turns the free spins into a far punchier round than the base game.
