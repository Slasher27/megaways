## The Megaclusters Engine: How Cluster Pays Works

### A Diamond Grid, Not Reels

The foundation of Fuzzy Wuzzy Megaclusters is its **cluster-pays** system played across an **8x8 grid** rotated 45 degrees into a diamond shape. This is a fundamentally different way of winning compared with the classic reels-and-paylines or even the Megaways ways-to-win model. There are no fixed lines to follow, and the direction symbols face does not matter.

**How Cluster Pays Works in Fuzzy Wuzzy:**

- The grid holds up to **64 symbol positions** (8 across by 8 down)
- A win is formed when **9 or more matching symbols** touch one another
- Symbols connect **vertically and/or horizontally** — diagonal touches do not count
- The larger the cluster, the larger the payout
- Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your **total stake**

Because winning depends on grouping rather than alignment, the whole grid is always in play. A win can blossom in any corner, and a single dense cluster of high-value symbols can pay handsomely on its own.

### Cascading Avalanches

Like the best cluster slots, Fuzzy Wuzzy Megaclusters uses an **avalanche** (also called a cascade or tumble) mechanic on every win. When a cluster pays:

1. The symbols that formed the winning cluster are removed from the grid
2. The remaining symbols shift to fill the gaps left behind
3. Brand-new symbols drop in to refill the board
4. If the refreshed board creates another qualifying cluster, it pays too
5. The avalanche repeats until no new clusters form

This chain-reaction system means a single paid spin can snowball into a long sequence of consecutive wins. Each cascade is also a fresh chance for roaming multiplier wilds and the Fuzzy Time feature to escalate, which is exactly how the game builds toward its biggest payouts.

### Splitting & Introduced Clusters

What gives the "Megaclusters" name its meaning here is the way the board is constantly **enriched with new clusters**. As the game plays out, fresh groups of symbols are dropped into the grid in neat **2x2 and 3x3 blocks**, seeding the board with ready-made building material for the next big cluster. Combined with the avalanche mechanic, this keeps the density of matching symbols high and gives the engine its characteristic, ever-bubbling momentum. The more the board reshuffles and refills, the more chances you have for a sprawling cluster to form and pay.

## Roaming Multiplier Wilds

### Wandering Wilds That Grow

The most powerful base-game mechanic in Fuzzy Wuzzy Megaclusters is the **roaming multiplier wild**. The white **Fuzzy Wuzzy monster** symbol acts as a wild, substituting for any paying symbol to help complete or enlarge a winning cluster. But these are no ordinary wilds — they **roam** around the grid and carry a **multiplier** that **increases with every win** they take part in.

**How Roaming Multiplier Wilds Work:**

- A wild substitutes for paying symbols, helping turn near-misses into qualifying clusters
- Each wild carries a multiplier value that is applied to wins it contributes to
- With every successive win or cascade, the wild's multiplier **steps up**
- Multiple wilds can be active at once, and they wander to new positions between cascades

The genius of this design is the synergy with the avalanche engine. A long cascade chain gives a roaming wild several opportunities to grow, so a multiplier that begins modestly can climb to a fearsome figure by the end of a sequence — and that boosted multiplier is then applied to whatever cluster the wild helps complete.

**Why It Matters:**

- **Compounding Potential**: Cascades and climbing multipliers feed each other for explosive runs
- **Active in Free Spins**: The wilds — and crucially their multipliers — carry over into the bonus
- **Big-Win Engine**: This is the primary route toward the 15,000x ceiling

## Fuzzy Time: The Cluster-Munching Bonus

### A Hungry Helper Boosts Your Multipliers

**Fuzzy Time** is the slot's signature random feature, and it leans fully into the game's playful character. During play — and especially during the free spins — one of the adorable fuzzy creatures can appear and **devour the largest cluster of icons** on the board, converting it into **escalating multipliers**.

**How Fuzzy Time Works:**

- The feature can trigger randomly during the base game and the bonus round
- A creature targets the biggest cluster currently on the grid
- That cluster is consumed and turned into **multiplier value**
- The multipliers **escalate**, with the effect amplified during the free spins

Because Fuzzy Time specifically targets your **largest** cluster, it tends to fire at the most opportune moments — when the board is already crowded with matching symbols. Layered on top of the roaming multiplier wilds, it is a key contributor to the game's most rewarding sequences, particularly inside the free spins where its multipliers reach their highest.

## Free Spins

The headline bonus round in Fuzzy Wuzzy Megaclusters is its **free spins** feature, triggered by landing **three or more scatter symbols** during the base game. Remember that the Fuzzy Wuzzy monster does double duty as both wild and scatter, so the same charming character that boosts your clusters can also unlock the bonus.

**How the Free Spins Work:**

The defining quality of this bonus is **persistence**. Because the wild multipliers do not reset between spins, they can build steadily across the round, and a well-timed Fuzzy Time event can supercharge an already dense board. Six spins may sound brief, but with cascades extending each spin and multipliers compounding throughout, the round packs serious potential into a short window — and it is where the lion's share of the 15,000x max win is realistically chased.
