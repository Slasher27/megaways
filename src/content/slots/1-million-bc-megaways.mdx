## The Megaways™ Engine: From 117,649 to Over a Million Ways

### Understanding Megaways™ in 1 Million BC

At the core of 1 Million BC Megaways sits the licensed **Megaways™** engine, the variable-ways system that recalculates the number of ways to win on every single spin. Rather than fixed paylines, each reel can display a different number of symbols, and the total ways are determined dynamically.

**How Megaways™ Works in 1 Million BC:**

- **6 vertical reels** in the base game, each showing between **2 and 7 symbols** per spin
- **Ways to win** are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
- **Base-game maximum**: 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 = **117,649 ways to win**
- **Base-game minimum**: 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 64 ways to win
- Wins pay for **matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right**, regardless of their vertical position

**Win Formation Principles:**

- Only the reel sequence matters — exact symbol position and size are irrelevant
- A winning combination must start on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
- Multiple separate winning combinations can land and pay on a single spin
- Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your **total stake**, not a per-line bet
- The number of ways changes every spin, so no two spins feel mathematically identical

### Breaking the Megaways™ Ceiling

What truly sets this slot apart is that 117,649 is not the limit. When the **Mammoth Reel** activates during free spins, a seventh reel — nine symbols high — is inserted into the centre of the grid. With that extra reel in play, the maths explode upward to a colossal **1,058,841 ways to win**, roughly nine times the standard Megaways maximum. This is the mechanic that earns the slot its "1 Million" name, and it is where the game's biggest payouts are forged.

## Tumble Cascades & the Rising Multiplier

### Wins That Snowball

Like most Megaways™ slots, 1 Million BC Megaways uses a **tumble** (or cascade) mechanic, but it adds a crucial twist: an **increasing win multiplier** that climbs with every successful tumble.

**How the Tumble Mechanic Works:**

1. You land a winning combination, which pays out
2. The symbols that formed the win are removed from the grid
3. The symbols above drop down to fill the gaps, and fresh symbols fall in from above
4. If the new arrangement creates another win, it pays — and the multiplier increases
5. Tumbles continue until no further wins land

**The Increasing Multiplier:**

- Each consecutive cascade lifts the win multiplier one step higher
- In the **base game**, the multiplier can climb to a maximum of **8x**
- Inside the **free spins** round, the multiplier ceiling rises even further, rewarding extended tumble chains
- Because the multiplier resets between paid spins, the most lucrative outcomes come from long, unbroken cascade sequences

This snowballing multiplier is the beating heart of the base game. A single fortunate spin that produces four, five or more tumbles in a row can see modest individual wins amplified dramatically as the multiplier ramps up — and it is also the route to triggering the free spins.
