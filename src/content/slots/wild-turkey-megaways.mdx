## Understanding the Megaways™ Mechanic in Wild Turkey

The Megaways™ system licensed from Big Time Gaming forms the mathematical engine powering Wild Turkey's gameplay. Each of the six reels displays between 2 and 7 symbols on every spin, with the number varying randomly. This variability creates a dynamic win way calculation ranging from 64 ways (2×2×2×2×2×2 when all reels show minimum symbols) up to the maximum 117,649 ways (7×7×7×7×7×7 when all reels display their maximum).

**How Megaways™ Calculates Win Ways:**
The active paylines on any given spin equal the product of visible symbol positions across all six reels. If the reels display 5-7-4-6-3-7 symbols respectively, you have 5×7×4×6×3×7 = 17,640 ways to win on that particular spin. This calculation occurs automatically before each spin, with the current Megaways count typically displayed prominently in the game interface.

**Why Variable Reels Matter:**
Unlike fixed-payline slots where you know exactly how many winning combinations are possible, Megaways™ creates inherent variance through reel height fluctuation. A spin with 117,649 ways offers significantly more opportunities to form winning combinations than a spin with only 2,000 ways, meaning win potential varies dramatically even before considering symbol values. This mechanism creates an engaging rhythm where players anticipate high Megaways counts alongside favorable symbol arrangements.

**Symbol Adjacency Requirements:**
Winning combinations form when matching symbols appear on adjacent reels starting from the leftmost reel (reel 1). You need a minimum of three matching symbols to form a win, though the highest-value premium symbols may pay for two-of-a-kind in some paytable configurations. Symbols don't need to appear in specific positions on a reel—as long as they're present anywhere on adjacent reels, they contribute to winning ways.

**No Cascading Wins:**
Unlike many Megaways™ implementations that feature cascading reels (where winning symbols disappear and new symbols drop down), Wild Turkey Megaways uses a traditional spin-and-evaluate structure. When you land a winning combination, symbols remain in place, wins are paid, and the next spin brings an entirely new set of symbols. This absence of cascades simplifies gameplay and affects volatility, as you can't generate multiple consecutive wins from a single stake.

**Wild Turkey Feature and Megaways Interaction:**
When the Wild Turkey Feature activates and transforms high-paying symbols into Wild Turkeys, it doesn't change the Megaways count—the number of symbol positions remains constant. However, by converting multiple premium symbols into wilds across reels 2-5, the feature dramatically increases the probability of forming maximum-length winning combinations across the established Megaways, often creating multiple simultaneous wins across different symbol types.

The Wild Turkey Feature represents the game's signature mechanic and primary differentiation from standard Megaways™ implementations. 5. **Win Evaluation:** The game evaluates wins with the newly-created Wild Turkeys substituting for all regular paying symbols

This restriction means the feature cannot create six-of-a-kind wild combinations, but it can transform symbols in the crucial middle reels where they connect starting combinations from reel 1 with ending symbols on reel 6.

**High-Paying Symbols Only:** The feature exclusively transforms premium turkey symbols—it never converts low-value card symbols into wilds. This design ensures feature activations focus on creating higher-value wins rather than simply increasing overall win frequency with low-value wild substitutions.

**Multiple Symbol Positions:** If the selected premium symbol appears in 8 different positions across reels 2-5, all 8 positions transform into Wild Turkeys simultaneously. On a high Megaways spin (100,000+ ways) with multiple instances of the chosen premium, this can result in substantial wild coverage across the middle reels.

**Does Not Trigger Free Spins:** Wild Turkey symbols created through this feature do NOT count toward Free Spins activation. You still need three actual Scatter Wild symbols on reels 2-4 to trigger the bonus round. The Wild Turkeys substitute for paying symbols only, maintaining separation between base game enhancement and bonus trigger mechanics.

## Free Spins Feature: Extended Play with Extra Spin Opportunities

The Free Spins bonus round serves as Wild Turkey Megaways™' primary feature, triggered by landing three Scatter Wild symbols on reels 2-4. This specific reel requirement (not scattered across all six reels) creates distinct trigger conditions compared to many Megaways™ slots where scatters can land anywhere.

### Triggering Free Spins

**3 Scatter Wilds on Reels 2-4:**
To activate Free Spins, you must land Scatter Wild symbols on three of the four middle reels (reels 2, 3, 4, or 5). Importantly, Scatter Wilds never appear on reels 1 or 6, so the maximum possible scatter count on any spin is four. This restriction affects trigger frequency—you're essentially looking for 3+ scatters across a four-reel window rather than six reels.

**Trigger Frequency:**
Given medium hit frequency and the four-reel scatter limitation, Free Spins typically trigger approximately once every 100-140 spins on average. This frequency aligns with medium-high volatility expectations, providing regular enough feature access to maintain engagement while keeping bonus rounds special events rather than constant occurrences.

**Initial Award:**
Landing exactly 3 Scatter Wilds awards 10 Free Spins. Landing 4 Scatter Wilds (filling all four possible scatter positions) still awards 10 initial spins—there's no escalating initial spin count based on scatter quantity beyond the minimum three required. The value of additional scatters manifests during the feature through the full-stack mechanic described below.

### Free Spins Mechanics

**Core Gameplay:**
Free Spins operate identically to base game with the same Megaways™ reel structure, symbol set, and paytable values. The critical difference is the extra spin mechanism that can extend the feature significantly beyond the initial 10-spin allocation.

**Extra Spins from Full Wild Stacks:**
During Free Spins, if an entire reel fills completely with wild symbols (Scatter Wilds, Wild Turkeys from feature activation, or combinations thereof), you receive 2 additional spins. Key mechanics:

- **Any Reel Qualifies:** While wilds only appear on reels 2-5, any of these four reels can trigger extra spins when full
- **Variable Reel Heights:** Since Megaways™ reels display 2-7 symbols, a "full stack" might be just 2 wilds (if that reel shows minimum height) or 7 wilds (maximum height)
- **Multiple Reels:** If multiple reels fill with wilds on the same spin, you receive 2 extra spins per full reel (e.g., two full reels = 4 extra spins)
- **Unlimited Extensions:** There's no cap on total Free Spins—theoretically, you could continuously retrigger extra spins throughout the feature, though practical limits emerge from probability

**Wild Symbol Behavior:**
Both Scatter Wilds and Wild Turkeys (created via Wild Turkey Feature activation) contribute to full stack completion. This creates interesting scenarios where:

- A reel with 6 Scatter Wilds needs just one more to trigger extra spins
- Wild Turkey Feature activation might complete a near-full wild stack
- Strategic symbol placement can simultaneously deliver wins and trigger extra spins

**Wild Turkey Feature During Free Spins:**
The Wild Turkey Feature continues to activate randomly during Free Spins with similar frequency to base game. Critically, the factsheet states: "The Wild Turkey feature can randomly activate, but doesn't participate in initial or additional Free Spin activation." This means:

- Wild Turkey Feature activations don't count toward triggering Free Spins initially (you still need actual Scatter Wilds)

- The feature provides pure win enhancement during Free Spins without affecting spin count mechanics
