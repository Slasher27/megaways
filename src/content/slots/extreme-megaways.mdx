## The Megaways™ Engine: 117,649 Ways to Explore

### Understanding Megaways™ in Extreme

At the core of Extreme Megaways is the licensed **Megaways™** mechanic, the variable-ways system that transformed how slots calculate wins. Instead of fixed paylines, each reel can display a different number of symbols on every spin, and the total number of ways to win is recalculated dynamically. This is what gives the game its headline figure of 117,649 ways and its constantly changing rhythm.

**How Megaways™ Works in Extreme:**

- **6 vertical reels**, each capable of showing between **2 and 7 symbols** per spin
- **Ways to win** are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
- **Maximum ways**: 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 = **117,649 ways to win**
- **Minimum ways**: 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 64 ways to win
- Wins pay for **matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right**, regardless of their position

**Win Formation Principles:**

- Symbol size and exact row position are irrelevant — only the reel sequence matters
- A winning combination must start on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
- Multiple separate winning combinations can land and pay on a single spin
- Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your **total stake**, not a per-line bet
- The number of ways changes every spin, so no two spins feel mathematically identical

The top-paying **lucky 7s** in Extreme Megaways are notably generous — they begin paying from just **two adjacent reels**, whereas the other symbols require three or more. This small tweak adds a touch of classic fruit-machine character to the otherwise modern Megaways framework.

### Cascading Reels

Like most Megaways™ slots, Extreme Megaways employs **cascading reels** (also called tumbles or avalanches). When you land a winning combination:

1. The symbols that formed the win are removed from the grid
2. The symbols above them drop down to fill the empty spaces
3. New symbols fall in from above to complete the reels
4. If the new arrangement creates another win, it pays and the process repeats
5. Cascades continue until no further wins land

This mechanic means a single paid spin can produce a chain of consecutive wins. Crucially, cascades are also the engine behind the free-spins progressive multiplier, where each consecutive cascade pushes the multiplier higher — making the cascade feature far more than a simple base-game extra.

## The Top Bonus Reel: Five Modifiers, Endless Variety

### How the Bonus Reel Works

The defining feature of Extreme Megaways is the **top bonus reel** — a special reel positioned above the main grid that periodically slides into view. When it activates during the base game, it randomly delivers **one of five modifiers** that can dramatically reshape the spin. In the base game these arrive intermittently, but in free spins a modifier is **guaranteed on every single spin**, which is what makes the bonus round so explosive.

**The Five Bonus Reel Modifiers:**

1. **Random Wilds** — Adds a number of wild symbols across **reels 2 to 6**. Wilds substitute for paying symbols, helping to complete multi-way combinations that would otherwise fall short.

2. **Multiplier Wilds** — Drops **two multiplier wilds onto reels 2 to 5**. These not only substitute for paying symbols but also multiply the value of any win they help create, delivering a powerful boost.

3. **Destroyer** — **Removes symbols after a cascade**, clearing the way for fresh symbols to fall in. This can manufacture new winning combinations from a board that had stalled.

4. **Mystery Symbols** — Places **mystery tiles** that all reveal the **same paying symbol type**, allowing them to combine into substantial multi-way wins across the reels.

5. **Dropping Wilds** — **Fills one of reels 2 to 5 entirely with wilds**, which then cascade. A full reel of wilds turns that reel into a guaranteed contributor to any combination, and the cascading behaviour can chain further wins.

**Why the Bonus Reel Matters:**

- **Constant Variety**: With five possible outcomes, no two activations feel the same
- **Multiple Win Pathways**: Some modifiers add wilds, others manipulate cascades, and one converts mystery symbols
- **Free-Spins Synergy**: A guaranteed modifier every free spin compounds with the progressive multiplier for huge potential
- **Engagement Anchor**: The reel triggers often enough to keep base-game play lively without devaluing the bonus

The top bonus reel is the soul of Extreme Megaways. It injects unpredictability into every session and gives the slot a distinct identity that separates it from generic Megaways releases.

## Free Spins: The Unlimited Progressive Multiplier

### Triggering and Playing the Bonus

The free-spins round is where Extreme Megaways reaches for its 50,000x ceiling. It is triggered by landing **scatter symbols** anywhere on the reels:

- **4 scatters** award **5 free spins**
- **5 scatters** award **7 free spins**
- **6 scatters** award **10 free spins**

Before the round begins, you are offered a **gamble wheel**: you can lock in your awarded spins safely, or risk them for the chance to start with as many as **14 free spins**. It is an all-or-nothing decision that rewards the brave but can also cost you the bonus entirely.

**The Unlimited Progressive Multiplier:**

The standout mechanic of the free-spins round is the **unlimited progressive win multiplier**. It works like this:

- The multiplier **starts at 2x**
- Every winning cascade **increases the multiplier by 1x**
- There is **no cap** — the multiplier keeps climbing for as long as cascades continue
- The multiplier applies to **all wins** during the free-spins round

Because a **modifier from the top bonus reel is guaranteed on every free spin**, the round is engineered to keep cascades flowing. Each cascade pushes the multiplier higher, and a sustained chain of cascades during a single spin can send the multiplier soaring into territory that transforms even modest base wins into enormous payouts.

**Retriggers:**

Landing **four or more scatters during the feature** awards **an additional 5 free spins** per trigger, extending your time in the round and giving the progressive multiplier even more room to grow.

**Example — Multiplier in Action:**

- £1.00 stake, free spins active with the multiplier at 2x
- A win lands and cascades five times, lifting the multiplier to 7x by the end of the chain
- A subsequent symbol pays 40x across a high-ways board
- With the 7x multiplier applied: 40x × 7 = **280x stake = £280.00** from that spin alone

The combination of guaranteed modifiers, cascading reels and an uncapped multiplier is precisely why Extreme Megaways can deliver wins all the way up to **50,000x your stake**.
