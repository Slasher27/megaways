## Game Features

### Prize Builder (Hold and Win)

The Prize Builder is Panda Money's signature innovation and what sets it apart from standard Megaways™ slots. This hold and win mechanic triggers on **every single win** during both base game and free spins, creating a cascading respin system that dramatically improves win potential:

**How Prize Builder Works:**

1. **Win Formation:** Land a winning combination on any spin
2. **Winning Symbols Lock:** All winning symbols cascade to the bottom of the reels and lock in position
3. **Symbol Replacement:** All non-winning symbols are swept away and replaced with new symbols
4. **Win Reevaluation:** The game checks for improved wins with the new symbol configuration
5. **Repeat Process:** If the new symbols create a better win, Prize Builder triggers again
6. **Continue Until Complete:** The process repeats until no further improvements occur

**Key Advantages:**

- **Every Win Matters:** Unlike standard cascades, Prize Builder actively works to improve your wins
- **Extra Reel Interaction:** Winning symbols from the Extra Reel (horizontal top reel) cascade into the reels below, potentially increasing symbol count and Megaways™
- **Symbol Position Optimization:** Winning symbols falling to the bottom can create better alignment for subsequent wins
- **Multiple Iterations:** A single initial win can trigger multiple rounds of Prize Builder
- **No Additional Cost:** This happens automatically on every win – no extra bet required

**Strategic Importance:**

The Prize Builder essentially gives you multiple chances to improve every win. What might start as a modest 5x win could transform into 20x, 50x, or even 100x+ through successive Prize Builder rounds. This mechanic significantly boosts the base game experience, making it far more engaging than typical high volatility slots where you're simply waiting for free spins.

In the base game, Prize Builder keeps the action flowing and prevents the dead spins doldrums. During free spins, it becomes even more powerful when combined with the unlimited multiplier!
