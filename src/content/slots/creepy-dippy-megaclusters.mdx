## The Megaclusters Engine: Cluster Pays With Splitting Symbols

### How Cluster Pays Works in Creepy Dippy

At the heart of Creepy Dippy Megaclusters is a **cluster-pays** system that does away with traditional paylines entirely. Instead of matching symbols along fixed lines across the reels, the game pays whenever enough identical symbols form a connected group on the grid.

**How clusters are formed:**

- The game plays out on a **6×5 grid** with no bet lines
- Symbols are considered part of the same cluster when identical symbols are **horizontally or vertically adjacent** (touching side to side or top to bottom)
- A cluster must reach a **minimum size to pay** — small isolated groups do not award a win
- Larger clusters pay progressively more, with the biggest combinations delivering the strongest base-game returns

This horizontal-and-vertical adjacency rule is the defining feature of cluster slots. Because symbols only need to touch rather than land on a precise line, wins can form in organic, sprawling shapes across the grid, and the position of every symbol matters.

### The Splitting / Cascade Mechanic

What earns Creepy Dippy its **Megaclusters** billing is the way winning clusters behave after they pay. Rather than simply vanishing, the cluster-pays mechanic triggers a cascade:

1. A winning cluster forms and pays out its prize
2. The winning symbols are **removed from the grid**
3. Surviving symbols shift to fill the gaps and **new symbols tumble in** from above
4. If the new arrangement creates another qualifying cluster, it pays and the process repeats
5. Cascades continue until no further winning clusters land

This splitting-and-cascading loop is the engine room of the game. A single paid spin can chain into a string of consecutive wins, with each cascade offering another chance for a fresh cluster to form. It is the same satisfying domino effect that has made cluster and Megaclusters slots so popular, and it is where most of Creepy Dippy's base-game value is generated.

**Why the cascade mechanic matters:**

- **One spin, many wins**: A single bet can pay multiple times as cascades chain together
- **Constantly shifting board**: Every cascade reshuffles the grid, so no two outcomes feel identical
- **Builds toward features**: Cascades keep scatters in play, helping you reach the free spins trigger
- **Low-variance friendly**: Frequent smaller cascade wins suit the game's gentle volatility profile

## Free Spins: Where the Bombs Come Out

### Triggering the Bonus Round

The most rewarding part of Creepy Dippy Megaclusters is its **free spins** feature. To trigger it, you need to land enough **scatter symbols** on the grid during the base game.

**How to trigger free spins:**

- Land **four or more scatter symbols** anywhere on the 6×5 grid in the base game
- Triggering awards an initial allocation of **10 free spins**

Once the bonus round begins, the cluster-pays and splitting mechanics continue exactly as in the base game — but with the addition of the feature's defining multiplier symbols.

### Retriggering for More Spins

The free spins round is not a one-and-done affair. While the feature is active, you can extend it by landing additional scatters:

- Landing **three scatter symbols during the free spins round** awards **five additional free spins**

This retrigger mechanic means a lucky run of scatters can keep the most lucrative part of the game going considerably longer, stacking more cascade-and-multiplier opportunities on top of one another.

## Energy Bomb Multipliers: The Feature That Pays

### Multipliers Unlocked in the Bonus

The standout enhancement in the free spins round is the **Energy Bomb** symbol. These special symbols are **unlocked during the bonus game** and are what transform an ordinary cascade into a genuinely big payout.

**How the Energy Bomb works:**

- Each Energy Bomb symbol carries **its own multiplier value**
- When a bomb appears on a spin, **all winnings from that spin are multiplied by the bomb's multiplier**
- Multiple bombs and a strong cascade chain can compound into the game's most rewarding moments

Because the bombs apply to the entire spin's winnings rather than a single cluster, their value scales with how productive your cascades are. A spin that chains several winning clusters together and then has its total multiplied by an Energy Bomb is precisely the sort of moment players chase in the free spins round. With the low base volatility, these multiplier-boosted bonus spins are where Creepy Dippy delivers its biggest hits.
