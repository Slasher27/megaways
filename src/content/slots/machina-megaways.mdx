## Power Up the Reels: Relax Gaming's Machina Megaways

Released on January 15, 2019 as one of the studio's earliest and most ambitious Megaways titles, Machina Megaways pairs a polished futuristic aesthetic with a feature set built squarely around its free spins round — and a maximum win of **26,000x your stake** waiting at the end of the production line.

What gives Machina Megaways its identity is the way it routes nearly all of its potential through a single, well-designed bonus. The base game leans on the familiar **Megaways™ engine** and **cascading reels**, but the real fireworks arrive in the **free spins round**, where a clever **cyborg symbol-collection** mechanic transforms premium symbols and stacks up extra spins. Layered on top are **three fixed jackpots** and, for the impatient, a **Hyper Bonus** buy that fast-tracks you straight into the action.

With a base **RTP of 96.59%** (rising as high as 97.42% via the bonus buy), **high volatility**, that **26,000x ceiling**, and a betting range running from **€0.60 to €36.00**, Machina Megaways positions itself as a feature-driven, big-hit slot for players who do not mind weathering some variance in pursuit of the bonus. It is built for the patient hunter rather than the frequent-feature grinder.

Whether you are drawn by the sci-fi spectacle, the satisfying cyborg-collection bonus, or the promise of a five-figure-multiplier payday, Machina Megaways has the engineering to deliver. Let's open the panel and examine every cog, circuit and feature that drives this machine.

## The Megaways™ Engine: 46,656 Ways to Win

### Understanding Megaways™ in Machina

At the core of Machina Megaways sits the licensed **Megaways™** engine, the variable-ways system that replaced fixed paylines with a constantly shifting grid. On every spin, each of the six reels can display a different number of symbols, and the total ways to win are recalculated dynamically. This is what produces the game's headline figure of **46,656 ways**.

**How Megaways™ Works in Machina:**

- **6 vertical reels**, each capable of showing between **2 and 6 symbols** per spin
- **Ways to win** are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
- **Maximum ways**: 6 × 6 × 6 × 6 × 6 × 6 = **46,656 ways to win**
- **Minimum ways**: 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 64 ways to win
- Wins pay for **matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right**, regardless of their position on the reel

**Win Formation Principles:**

- Symbol position within a reel is irrelevant — only the reel sequence matters
- A winning combination must start on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
- Multiple separate winning combinations can land and pay on a single spin
- Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your **total stake**, not a per-line bet
- The number of ways changes every spin, so no two spins feel mathematically identical

### Cascading Reels

Like most Megaways™ slots, Machina Megaways uses **cascading reels** (also called tumbles or avalanches). When you land a winning combination:

1. The symbols that formed the win are removed from the grid
2. The symbols above them drop down to fill the empty spaces
3. New symbols fall in from above to complete the reels
4. If the new arrangement creates another win, it pays and the process repeats
5. Cascades continue until no further wins land

This mechanic means a single paid spin can produce a chain of consecutive wins. Because Machina Megaways is a high-volatility game, these cascades are less frequent than in a low-variance slot — but when they connect on a high-ways board, they can deliver substantial base-game payouts and help build toward the free spins trigger.

## Free Spins: The Heart of the Machine

### Triggering the Free Spins Round

The free spins round is where Machina Megaways comes alive, and it is the destination almost every spin is quietly working toward. You trigger it by landing **scatter symbols** anywhere on the reels, and crucially the number of scatters you land determines how many free spins you receive:

- **3 scatters** award **10 free spins**
- **4 scatters** award **12 free spins**
- **5 scatters** award **15 free spins**
- **6 scatters** award **18 free spins**

The more scatters you land on the trigger, the deeper your free-spins session — and the more opportunities you have to engage the cyborg collection mechanic that drives the round's potential.

### The Cyborg Collection Mechanic

The defining feature of the free spins is a **symbol-collection meter**. During the round, special **circuit symbols** (glowing yellow accumulator icons) appear on the reels, and each one you collect fills the meter a little further. When the meter reaches its threshold, two rewarding things happen:

1. **A premium character symbol transforms into the Machina cyborg** — the game's highest-paying symbol — for the remainder of the free spins
2. **You are awarded additional free spins**, extending the round

Because up to several premium symbols can be converted as you keep collecting, the free spins can snowball: more cyborg symbols on the reels means more high-value combinations, more wins, and more cascades. A free-spins round that gets rolling — collecting circuits, transforming symbols and earning extra spins — is where the game's largest payouts are forged.

**Why the Collection Mechanic Matters:**

- **Building Momentum**: Each transformation permanently upgrades the reels for the rest of the round
- **Compounding Value**: More cyborg symbols increase the frequency and size of multi-way wins
- **Extra Spins**: The meter rewards extra spins, lengthening your run at the big payouts
- **Synergy with Cascades**: Transformed cyborg symbols feed directly into the cascading engine for chained wins

## The Three Jackpots: Silver, Gold and Platinum

### Stacking the Cyborgs for Fixed Prizes

Beyond the win combinations themselves, Machina Megaways layers in **three fixed jackpots** tied to how many **cyborg symbols** you accumulate. These are not random progressive prizes — they are awarded for filling the reels with enough cyborg icons, which makes them a natural extension of the collection mechanic during the free spins.

**The Three Jackpot Tiers:**

- **Silver Jackpot** — awarded for landing **10 cyborg symbols**, worth **20x your stake**
- **Gold Jackpot** — awarded for landing **12 cyborg symbols**, worth **50x your stake**
- **Platinum Jackpot** — awarded for landing **16 or more cyborg symbols**, worth **1,000x your stake**

The jackpots give the free spins an extra dimension of reward: even as you chase big win combinations, you are simultaneously working toward these fixed bonuses by stacking cyborgs on the board. The Platinum tier in particular is a meaningful prize, and reaching it requires a genuinely productive free-spins run where multiple transformations have flooded the reels with cyborg symbols.

## The Hyper Bonus: Buy Your Way In

### Skipping Straight to the Free Spins

For players who would rather not grind out the scatters in the base game, Machina Megaways offers a **Hyper Bonus** — the slot's bonus-buy mechanic. Rather than waiting to land three or more scatters organically, you can pay a fixed multiple of your stake to be dropped straight into the free spins round. The amount you pay determines how many free spins you receive, mirroring the natural trigger tiers.

**Hyper Bonus Buy Costs (approximate, as multiples of stake):**

- **10 free spins** — roughly **52x** your stake
- **12 free spins** — roughly **89x** your stake
- **15 free spins** — roughly **185x** your stake
- **18 free spins** — roughly **337x** your stake

Buying the bonus also comes with a notable upside: the **RTP increases** when you purchase the feature, rising from the base **96.59%** to as high as **97.42%** on the bonus-buy entries. This makes the Hyper Bonus statistically efficient as well as convenient — though the higher tiers are expensive, so it remains a feature best used with a healthy bankroll.

**Strategic Considerations:**

- **Convenience vs. Cost**: The buy saves time but the top tiers cost hundreds of times your stake
- **Improved RTP**: The increased return on bonus buys partly offsets the premium you pay
- **Bankroll Discipline**: Treat each buy as a single high-variance bet — set limits accordingly
- **Practice First**: Use the free-play demo to understand the bonus rhythm before buying in

## How to Win: Strategy & Tips

Machina Megaways is a high-volatility game, which calls for a patient, disciplined approach. Here is how to give yourself the best run at the machine.

### Bankroll Management

- **Match your bet to your bankroll**: With a range of €0.60 to €36.00, divide your session budget so you can comfortably absorb a long stretch of spins between features. High volatility means dry spells are inevitable, so build in a generous buffer.
- **Set win and loss limits**: Decide in advance when you will walk away — both ahead and behind. The 26,000x ceiling is a big target, but the bulk of sessions will hinge on reaching the free spins.
- **Budget for the Hyper Bonus carefully**: If you intend to buy the bonus, treat each purchase as a single large bet and factor its cost into your overall plan.

### Base Game Strategy

- **Be patient**: There is no skill in spin outcomes, so focus on bet sizing and session length rather than chasing patterns.
- **Aim for the free spins**: Almost all of the game's potential lives in the bonus, so the base game is principally a vehicle for landing scatters.
- **Use cascades to your advantage**: A single paid spin can chain into multiple wins, especially on a high-ways board.

### Free Spins & Bonus Strategy

- **Value the collection mechanic**: Every circuit symbol you gather pushes you toward a transformation and extra spins, so the round rewards persistence.
- **Chase the jackpots through cyborgs**: Stacking cyborg symbols both improves your win combinations and works toward the Silver, Gold and Platinum prizes.
- **Weigh the Hyper Bonus**: The improved RTP makes the buy statistically efficient, but the higher tiers are costly — only buy what your bankroll comfortably allows.
- **Practice in the demo first**: Use the free-play version to learn how the collection meter and jackpots behave before committing real funds.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the RTP of Machina Megaways?**

Machina Megaways has a base Return to Player (RTP) of **96.59%**, which sits at the upper end of the modern Megaways average. When you purchase the free spins via the Hyper Bonus, the RTP increases, reaching as high as **97.42%** on the bonus-buy entries.

**What is the maximum win in Machina Megaways?**

The maximum win is **26,000x your stake**. This is a substantial ceiling for a Megaways slot, most realistically approached during a productive free-spins round where multiple cyborg transformations flood the reels with high-paying symbols.

**How volatile is Machina Megaways?**

The game is **high volatility (rated 4 out of 5)**. This means a big-hit profile with longer gaps between meaningful payouts, so it suits patient players with a sufficient bankroll rather than those seeking frequent, steady wins.

**Does Machina Megaways have a bonus buy?**

**Yes.** The Hyper Bonus lets you purchase direct entry to the free spins, with costs of roughly 52x, 89x, 185x or 337x your stake for 10, 12, 15 or 18 spins respectively. Buying the bonus also raises the RTP above the base 96.59%.

**How do the free spins work?**

You trigger the free spins by landing 3 or more scatters, awarding 10 to 18 spins depending on the scatter count. During the round you collect circuit symbols to fill a meter, which transforms premium symbols into the high-paying Machina cyborg and grants additional free spins.

**What are the jackpots in Machina Megaways?**

There are three fixed jackpots awarded for accumulating cyborg symbols: Silver (10 cyborgs for 20x stake), Gold (12 cyborgs for 50x stake) and Platinum (16 or more cyborgs for 1,000x stake).

**Who makes Machina Megaways and when was it released?**

Machina Megaways was developed by **Kalamba Games** and released through **Relax Gaming** on **January 15, 2019**. It was one of Relax Gaming's earliest Megaways titles and remains a popular sci-fi entry in the genre.
