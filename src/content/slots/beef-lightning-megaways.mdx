## The Megaways™ Mechanic: 117,649 Ways to Win

### Understanding Megaways™ in Beef Lightning

Beef Lightning Megaways™ utilizes Big Time Gaming's legendary **Megaways™** engine, which has become the gold standard for dynamic slot gameplay. Unlike traditional fixed-payline slots, Megaways™ creates a constantly shifting play environment where every spin offers different win potential.

**How Megaways™ Works**:
- 6 vertical reels with 2-7 symbols per reel
- Horizontal top reel above reels 2-5 (standard Megaways™ layout)
- Ways to win calculated by multiplying symbols on each reel
- Maximum ways: 7×7×7×7×7×7 = **117,649 ways to win**
- Minimum ways: 2×2×2×2×2×2 = 64 ways

**Win Formation**:
Wins are awarded for **left-to-right adjacent symbols regardless of size**. This means:
- Symbol size doesn't matter—a small symbol counts the same as a large one
- All that matters is landing matching symbols on consecutive reels from left to right
- Multiple winning combinations can occur simultaneously on a single spin
- Payouts are multiplied by your total stake

**Why Megaways™ Matters**:
The variable nature of Megaways™ creates several advantages:
- **Visual Variety**: Every spin looks different, maintaining engagement
- **Win Potential Variance**: Some spins offer modest possibilities, others explosive potential
- **Anticipation Building**: Watching reels fill with 7 symbols creates excitement
- **Mathematical Depth**: The system supports complex features like Wild Duplication effectively

In Beef Lightning, the Megaways™ system serves as the foundation upon which the Wild Duplication and auction mechanics are built, creating a comprehensive gameplay experience that rewards both patience and lucky streaks.

## Beef Lightning Auction: The Main Event

### Understanding the Auction Mechanic

The **Beef Lightning Auction** is where this slot truly distinguishes itself from other Megaways™ games. Instead of traditional Free Spins with multipliers or unlimited win multipliers, Beef Lightning introduces a unique collection system that's both thematically appropriate and mechanically innovative.

**Auction Basics**:

When Free Spins commence, you'll notice the game interface transforms:
- The left side displays your **Cow Collection** (starts at 1 cow)
- The center shows your **Current Bid** (starts at 0x)
- The right side features three **Trophy Progress Meters** (Bronze, Silver, Gold)
- Your Free Spins counter tracks remaining spins

**The Goal**:
Collect **Bonus Coins** during your Free Spins to increase your cows, bids, and trophy progress. When Free Spins conclude, your **Total Cows × Final Bid = Auction Winnings**, plus any trophy prizes you've earned.

### Bonus Coins: The Heart of the Auction

**Bonus Coin Appearance**:
During Free Spins, golden **Bonus Coins** can land on any reel. These coins are the key to building your auction prize and can reveal one of four rewards:

1. **Additional Cows** (+1 to your cow collection)
2. **Auction Bid Increases** (+1x, +2x, +3x, or higher to your multiplier)
3. **Trophy Progress** (fills Bronze/Silver/Gold progress meters)
4. **Retriggers** (+10 Free Spins to extend the auction)

**Bonus Coin Values**:
The coins reveal their contents when they land, with common distributions:
- **Cows**: Most frequent (approximately 40-50% of coins)
- **Bids**: Second most common (approximately 30-40%)
- **Trophy Progress**: Less frequent (approximately 15-20%)
- **Retriggers**: Rare but valuable (approximately 5-10%)

**Strategic Coin Collecting**:
The beauty of the system is how different coin types synergize:
- **More Cows**: Multiplies your final bid value (5 cows × 10x = 50x vs. 10 cows × 10x = 100x)
- **Higher Bids**: Increases value per cow (5 cows × 15x = 75x vs. 5 cows × 30x = 150x)
- **Balanced Approach**: Ideally, you want strong growth in both categories
- **Retriggers**: Extended sessions allow more time to collect cows and bids

### The Auction Calculation

**Final Prize Formula**:
```
Auction Winnings = (Total Cows × Final Bid) + Trophy Prizes
```

**Example Auction Sessions**:

**Modest Session**:
- Cows Collected: 5
- Final Bid: 8x
- Calculation: 5 × 8 = 40x stake
- Trophy Prizes: None
- **Total Win**: 40x stake

**Good Session**:
- Cows Collected: 12
- Final Bid: 15x
- Calculation: 12 × 15 = 180x stake
- Trophy Prizes: Bronze Trophy (500x)
- **Total Win**: 680x stake

**Epic Session**:
- Cows Collected: 35
- Final Bid: 50x
- Calculation: 35 × 50 = 1,750x stake
- Trophy Prizes: Gold Trophy (25,000x)
- **Total Win**: 26,750x stake

The three trophy meters add a jackpot-style element to the Beef Lightning Auction:

**Bronze Medal**:
- **Prize**: 500x stake
- **Requirement**: Fill the bronze progress meter with Bonus Coins
- **Frequency**: Achievable in approximately 25-35% of Free Spins sessions

**Silver Medal**:
- **Prize**: 2,500x stake

**Gold Trophy**:
- **Prize**: 25,000x stake

- **Frequency**: Rare—approximately 1-2% of Free Spins sessions

**Trophy Strategy**:
Trophy progress is incremental—each relevant Bonus Coin moves you closer to the prize. The meters provide visual feedback showing how close you are to each threshold, creating mounting excitement as meters fill during successful bonus rounds.

**Combining Multipliers**:
The most explosive wins occur when strong auction multiplication combines with trophy prizes:
- 20 cows × 40x bid = 800x stake

- **Total**: 25,800x stake

### Retriggers: Extending the Auction

Landing **retrigger Bonus Coins** awards an additional **+10 Free Spins**, which dramatically increases your potential to:
