## How The Dog House Megaways Works

The Dog House Megaways is a six-reel slot built on Big Time Gaming's licensed [Megaways™ engine](/megaways/): each reel shows between two and seven symbols per spin, the ways count is recalculated every time, and the board tops out at **117,649 ways to win** (with a floor of 64). Wins pay for three to six matching symbols on adjacent reels from the left, position on the reel is irrelevant, and every payout is a multiple of your total stake. It is a wholesale rebuild of the original Dog House, which ran a fixed 5×3 grid with 20 paylines.

The structural surprise is that **there are no cascading reels**. Where most Megaways games clear winning symbols and drop replacements in for chain reactions, this one resolves each spin in a single pass, like a classic slot wearing a Megaways coat. Pragmatic Play made that omission deliberately: all of the game's power is routed through its 1x–3x multiplier wilds instead of cascade churn. The practical consequence is a base game with a slower, more traditional rhythm — some players find it clean, others find it flat — and a design where every meaningful moment runs through the wilds and the bonus round they feed.

Stakes run from **0.20 to 100.00** per spin, one of the widest ranges in the genre. Given the 5/5 volatility, the sensible way to use that range is downward: a bankroll covering 200–300 spins at your chosen stake is a realistic buffer for the droughts this maths model produces.

## What Are the Bonus Features in The Dog House Megaways?

The feature set is built entirely around multiplier wilds and a player-choice free spins round, with a bonus buy for those who refuse to wait.

### Multiplier Wilds (1x–3x): the engine of every mode

Wild symbols land on **reels 2, 3, 4 and 5**, and every one arrives stamped with a **1x, 2x or 3x multiplier**. A wild substitutes for all paying symbols except scatters and applies its multiplier to any win it completes — and when two or more multiplier wilds participate in the same combination, their values **multiply together** rather than adding. A 2x meeting a 3x makes 6x; two 3x wilds make 9x; three 3x wilds in one chain make 27x. That geometric compounding is present in the base game and both bonus modes, and it is the mechanism behind every headline win the game can produce, up to and including the 12,305x maximum.

### Sticky Wilds Free Spins: the compounding board

Landing **three or more doghouse scatters** opens a selection screen, and Sticky Wilds is the high-ceiling option: **7 to 20 free spins** (7 for three scatters, 12 for four, 15 for five, 20 for six) in which every multiplier wild that lands **locks in place for the rest of the round**. The board only ever gets more dangerous — by the closing spins, a good round can have the middle reels studded with 2x and 3x multipliers that transform every win passing through them. This is the game's realistic route to its biggest payouts, and also its cruellest mode: if the wilds refuse to land early, a 7-spin round can end with almost nothing.

### Raining Wilds Free Spins: the steady downpour

Raining Wilds is the consistency pick: **15 to 30 free spins** (15/18/25/30 for three through six scatters), and on **every single spin up to six multiplier wilds rain into random positions**. Nothing sticks — each spin gets its own fresh scatter of wilds, evaluated with the usual compounding multiplication. You trade the apocalyptic board states Sticky mode can build for guaranteed wild involvement on every spin and roughly double the spin count, which makes a total blank far less likely. Neither mode changes the long-term RTP; the choice is purely about how you want the variance distributed.

### Bonus Buy: 100x to skip the queue

Where regulations permit, you can buy the free spins round outright for **100x your current stake**, and the Sticky-versus-Raining choice remains yours after purchase. It suits the game's personality — almost all of the value lives in the bonus — but the 5/5 volatility applies to bought rounds exactly as it does to triggered ones, so a purchase is a ticket, not a guarantee. One honest loss against the original Dog House: the pick-me mini-game from that title did not make the journey over.

## Symbols & What They Pay

The paytable is a two-tier cartoon hierarchy led by the dogs. The **rottweiler** is the top premium, followed by the **dalmatian**, **pug** and **dachshund**, with a **dog collar and bone** bridging the gap down to the card royals (A, K, Q, J, 10, 9) that fill the low tier. All wins are quoted as multiples of your total stake for three to six matching symbols on consecutive reels from the left.

The two symbols that matter most never pay on their own. The **multiplier wild** appears only on reels 2–5 with its 1x, 2x or 3x badge and exists to substitute and multiply; the **doghouse scatter** exists to trigger the bonus, with more scatters meaning more spins in whichever mode you choose. Individual symbol payouts are deliberately modest — this paytable is designed to be read with a mental "× multiplier" beside every figure, because the wilds, not the dogs, are what turn ordinary combinations into the wins this game is known for.
