## The Megaways™ Engine: 16,807 Ways to Strike It Lucky

### Understanding Megaways™ in Luck O' The Irish

At the heart of Luck O' The Irish Megaways beats the licensed **Megaways™** engine, the variable-ways system that transformed slot mathematics. Rather than fixed paylines, every reel can display a different number of symbols on each spin, and the total ways to win are recalculated dynamically. This is what gives the game its constantly shifting rhythm and its headline figure of **16,807 ways**.

**How Megaways™ Works in Luck O' The Irish:**

- **5 vertical reels**, each capable of showing between **2 and 7 symbols** per spin
- **Ways to win** are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
- **Maximum ways**: 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 = **16,807 ways to win**
- **Minimum ways**: 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 32 ways to win
- Wins pay for **matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right**, regardless of their position on the reel

**Win Formation Principles:**

- Symbol size and exact row position are irrelevant — only the reel sequence matters
- A winning combination must start on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
- Multiple separate winning combinations can land and pay on a single spin
- Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your **total stake**, not a per-line bet
- The number of ways changes every spin, so no two spins feel mathematically identical

Because the engine spans five reels rather than six, the maximum ways figure here (16,807) is lower than the 117,649 ceiling of six-reel Megaways titles. That does not make the game any less volatile — the swings remain steep — but it is a key distinction worth understanding when you compare it to other Megaways slots.

### Mystery Symbols

Luck O' The Irish Megaways employs Blueprint's well-known **Mystery Symbols** mechanic. When mystery symbols land on the reels, they all reveal the **same randomly chosen regular symbol**. Because every mystery symbol transforms into an identical symbol, a cluster of them can suddenly line up across multiple reels and complete substantial multi-way wins.

**How Mystery Symbols Work:**

1. One or more mystery symbols appear on the grid during a spin
2. All visible mystery symbols simultaneously reveal the same paying symbol
3. If that revealed symbol forms a winning combination across consecutive reels, it pays
4. A board full of mystery symbols revealing a high-value symbol can produce a large payout

This mechanic is the engine of the base game's excitement. When several mystery symbols all flip to the pot of gold or a high-value gem, the result can be a genuinely big win, and the unpredictability of which symbol will appear keeps every reveal tense.

## Fortune Spins: Four Reel Sets, Four Times the Luck

### The Game's Signature Feature

The standout mechanic in Luck O' The Irish Megaways is **Fortune Spins**, a feature that fundamentally changes how the game plays. Instead of spinning a single Megaways™ grid, Fortune Spins opens a new screen with **four separate sets of reels playing at once**, multiplying your chances of landing a significant win on a single spin.

**How Fortune Spins Works:**

- Activating the feature costs **20x your stake**
- The screen expands to display **four independent reel sets**
- Each reel set spins with its own Megaways™ layout and ways to win
- Wins can land across any or all of the four sets simultaneously
- The combined potential of four grids is where the feature's value lies

By quadrupling the number of active reel sets, Fortune Spins dramatically increases the volume of potential winning combinations on every spin. It transforms a standard Megaways™ spin into a high-intensity, high-potential event — and it is the most direct route to the game's 10,000x ceiling.

### The Luck O' The Irish Special Symbol

The real magic of Fortune Spins comes from the **Luck O' The Irish special symbol**. During the feature, if you land any number of these special symbols on the **first reel set**, they **replicate across all the other reel sets** in the same positions.

**Why the Replicating Symbol Matters:**

- A single special symbol on the first reel set is copied to all four sets
- This can blanket the grids with matching symbols simultaneously
- When the replicated symbols line up across reels, they complete wins on multiple sets at once
- It is the mechanic responsible for the feature's biggest payouts

This replication effect is what turns Fortune Spins from a simple "more reels" feature into a genuinely explosive one. A well-placed cluster of special symbols on the first set can cascade its influence across all four grids, stacking up multi-way wins that combine into a substantial total.

**Example Fortune Spins Scenario:**

- £1.00 stake
- You pay 20x stake (£20.00) to enter Fortune Spins
- Several Luck O' The Irish special symbols land on the first reel set
- They replicate across all four reel sets
- The replicated symbols complete winning ways on multiple sets at once
- The combined payout across the four grids far exceeds a single-grid spin
