## Game Features

### Dual Reactions

The Dual Reactions mechanic is BTG's innovative take on cascading reels and represents a perfect marriage with the Megaways™ functionality. This bidirectional cascading system propels the game into an entirely new genre:

**How Dual Reactions Works:**

1. **Initial Win:** Land a winning combination on any spin
2. **Winning Symbols Removed:** Standard cascade removes winning symbols
3. **Symbols Drop Down:** New symbols fall from above (like traditional cascades)
4. **Extra Reel Addition:** Here's the innovation – **Dual Reactions adds a symbol to reels 2, 3, 4, and 5 from BELOW**
5. **Symbol Count Increases:** This bidirectional mechanic can increase the number of symbols on a reel
6. **More Megaways™ Created:** Additional symbols = more ways to win
7. **Win Reevaluation:** The game checks for new winning combinations
8. **Process Repeats:** Continues until no more wins occur

**Why Dual Reactions Matters:**

Traditional cascades replace winning symbols with new ones from above, maintaining symbol count. Dual Reactions adds symbols from below on the four central reels, actually **increasing the number of symbols** and potentially raising the Megaways™ count with each reaction.

This creates an escalating win potential where:
- First win might be on 10,000 ways
- Dual Reaction adds symbols, creating 30,000 ways
- Next win is bigger due to more ways
- Another Dual Reaction adds more symbols, creating 60,000 ways
- The cascade continues building ways and wins

**Base Game Impact:**

Even in the base game without multipliers, Dual Reactions creates exciting escalating action. A modest initial win can snowball into something substantial as the Megaways™ count climbs with each successive reaction. It's engaging, innovative, and keeps players on the edge of their hot seat!

### Hot Seat Free Spins Gamble

This is where Millionaire Megaways™ truly captures the magic of the TV show. Before entering Free Spins, players get an authentic Who Wants to be a Millionaire? experience, climbing the iconic 12-rung prize ladder!

**The Prize Ladder Structure:**

The ladder ranges from **8 Free Spins (bottom rung)** to **50 Free Spins (top rung 12)**, with incremental increases at each level. The more Millionaire Scatters you land to trigger the feature, the higher your starting rung:

- **3 Scatters:** Start at rung 1 (8 free spins guaranteed)
- **4 Scatters:** Start at rung 2 (10 free spins guaranteed)
- **5 Scatters:** Start at rung 3 (higher starting position)
- **6+ Scatters:** Start even higher up the ladder

**The Three Lifelines:**

On each question as you attempt to climb, you have access to the show's famous lifelines (each usable once per gamble session):

**50:50**
- Removes two of the four possible answers (A, B, C, D)
- Leaves you with a 50% chance of guessing correctly
- Great for when you have no idea

**Phone a Friend**
- Marks the most likely correct answer with a visual indicator
- Doesn't guarantee correctness but significantly improves odds
- Best used on higher-value questions

**Ask the Audience**
- Reveals the percentage probability of each answer being correct
- Shows you audience polling data for all four options
- Most informative lifeline, excellent for critical decisions

**Gameplay Decisions:**

After using (or not using) lifelines, you face a crucial decision:

1. **Select an Answer (A, B, C, or D):** Click your choice
2. **Press "Final Answer":** Commits to gambling for the next rung
3. **Or "Walk Away":** Take the guaranteed free spins you currently have

**Safety Net Rungs:**

- **Rung 2 (10 spins):** First safety net – you cannot walk away, but can't fall below this if you answer wrong on rung 3
- **Rung 7 (20 spins):** Second safety net – same protection applies
- These are **risk-free rounds** since the rung before them provides protection

**Risk vs. Reward:**

The tension builds as you climb:
- Lower rungs (1-6): Walk away safely or risk for more
- Rung 7 safety net: Get to at least 20 spins guaranteed
- Rungs 8-12: High risk territory – wrong answer sends you back to the safety net
- Top rung (50 spins!): Massive potential but requires brave gambling or good lifeline usage

**Strategic Depth:**

This feature adds genuine skill to the slot. Smart lifeline management, risk assessment, and knowing when to walk away with guaranteed spins versus gambling for more creates engaging decision-making that perfectly captures the TV show's essence.

### Free Spins with Unlimited Multiplier

Once you've completed the Hot Seat Gamble (or walked away), you enter the Free Spins bonus with your earned spins. This is where the unlimited multiplier transforms the game:

**Free Spins Mechanics:**

**Unlimited Win Multiplier:**
- **Starts at x1** (default)
- **Increases by +1 after every Dual Reaction**
- **No cap** – can grow infinitely
- **Applies to all wins** during the feature

**How It Escalates:**

The combination of Dual Reactions and unlimited multiplier is explosive:

1. Land a win in free spins
2. Dual Reactions trigger, adding symbols bidirectionally
3. Win multiplier increases to x2
4. New win formed (now multiplied by x2)
5. Another Dual Reaction triggers
6. Multiplier increases to x3
7. Next win is x3 the normal payout
8. Pattern continues...

**Example Scenario:**

- Spin 1: Land 20x base win, Dual Reactions trigger 5 times
- Multiplier reaches x6 by the end of the cascade
- Spin 2: Land 50x base win at x6 multiplier = 300x total
- Dual Reactions trigger again, multiplier climbs to x10
- Spin 3: Land 100x base win at x10 = 1000x!

The multiplier **carries over between spins** and continues growing, so by spin 5-6 with a healthy multiplier built up, even modest wins become substantial.

**Retrigger Mechanism:**

- Landing **3 or 4 Millionaire Scatters** on the Extra Reel (horizontal reel) awards additional free spins
- **3 Scatters = 4 extra spins**
- **4 Scatters = 8 extra spins**
- Multiplier carries over and continues increasing
- Retriggering late in the bonus with a high multiplier already built = jackpot potential

**Why It Works:**

The unlimited multiplier means that even if your first few spins are quiet, a late-bonus retrigger combined with big Dual Reactions can result in life-changing wins. The 72,310x max win becomes achievable when everything aligns: high starting spins, multiple retriggers, and cascades building massive multipliers.
