## The Megaways™ Engine: 117,649 Ways to Conquer

### Understanding Megaways™ in Power of Thor

At the heart of Power of Thor Megaways beats the licensed **Megaways™** engine, the variable-ways system that reshaped slot mathematics. Rather than fixed paylines, each reel can display a different number of symbols on every spin, and the total ways to win are recalculated dynamically. This is what gives the game its constantly shifting rhythm and its headline figure of 117,649 ways.

**How Megaways™ Works in Power of Thor:**

- **6 vertical reels**, each capable of showing between **2 and 7 symbols** per spin
- An additional **horizontal top reel** that houses extra symbols and Thor's Hammer
- **Ways to win** are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
- **Maximum ways**: 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 = **117,649 ways to win**
- Wins pay for **matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right**, regardless of their position on the reel

**Win Formation Principles:**

- Symbol size and exact row position are irrelevant — only the reel sequence matters
- A winning combination must start on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
- Multiple separate winning combinations can land and pay on a single spin
- Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your **total stake**, not a per-line bet
- The number of ways changes every spin, so no two spins feel mathematically identical

### Tumbling Reels

Like most Megaways™ slots, Power of Thor Megaways employs **tumbling reels** (also called cascades or avalanches). When you land a winning combination:

1. The symbols that formed the win are removed from the grid
2. The symbols above them drop down to fill the empty spaces
3. New symbols fall in from above to complete the reels
4. If the new arrangement creates another win, it pays and the process repeats
5. Tumbles continue until no further wins land

This mechanic means a single paid spin can produce a chain of consecutive wins. It is also the engine behind the game's most important free-spins mechanic — the **progressive multiplier**, which climbs by 1x with every successive tumble, turning a long cascade into a potential bankroll-changing event.

## Thor's Hammer: The Wild-Stamping Signature Feature

### The Hammer That Forges Wins

The signature mechanic of Power of Thor Megaways is the **Thor's Hammer** feature, and it is the reason the base game stays so explosive. Above the six main reels sits a **horizontal top reel**, and on this reel Thor's mighty hammer can land. When the hammer appears and is **fully visible**, it occupies **two positions** on the top reel and unleashes its power on the symbols below.

**How Thor's Hammer Works:**

- The hammer lands on the horizontal top reel that sits across the grid
- When fully visible, it transforms the positions directly beneath it into **wilds**
- Each affected reel receives **between 2 and 6 stacked wilds**
- These wilds then **nudge into place** to complete additional winning combinations
- The hammer can **trigger again** after tumbles reposition it, chaining multiple wild drops

**Why Thor's Hammer Matters:**

- **Massive Base-Game Wins**: Stacked wilds across several reels can complete enormous multi-way wins
- **Synergy with Tumbles**: Each hammer-driven win triggers a tumble, which can drop fresh symbols and even re-trigger the hammer
- **Wild Substitution**: Wilds substitute for all paying symbols (except scatters), so they fill gaps in high-value combinations
- **Engagement Anchor**: The hammer can appear on any base-game spin, keeping every spin tense and full of potential

**Example Hammer Scenario:**

- £1.00 stake
- Thor's Hammer lands fully visible across two top-reel positions
- The positions below it on reels two, three and four become stacked wilds (4 wilds per reel)
- A high-value Thor symbol on reels one, five and six is completed across the full board for a large multi-way win
- The win tumbles, and fresh symbols drop the hammer back into view for a second wild stamp

The Thor's Hammer feature is the engine room of the base game. It is dramatic, generous in frequency, and consistently capable of producing the kind of mid-spin surge that high-volatility players live for.

## Free Spins: Where the Multiplier Climbs Without Limit

### Triggering the Free Spins

The headline bonus of Power of Thor Megaways is the **Free Spins** round, and it is triggered by landing the **THOR scatter symbols** — special letter scatters that must spell out the word **THOR** across the reels. You need **4 or more scatters** to trigger the feature, and each additional scatter beyond the fourth adds extra spins to your tally.

**Free Spins Trigger and Awards:**

- **4 scatters** spelling THOR award **10 free spins**
- Each additional scatter beyond the fourth adds **+4 free spins**
- With the maximum number of scatters in view, you can begin with up to **22 free spins**

### The Unlimited Progressive Multiplier

What makes the free spins so dangerous — in the best possible way — is the **progressive win multiplier**. Unlike many slots that cap their multipliers, Power of Thor Megaways lets the multiplier **climb without limit** throughout the round.

**How the Multiplier Works:**

- The win multiplier starts at **1x** when the free spins begin
- Every time a tumble occurs, the multiplier **increases by 1x**
- The multiplier **does not reset between spins** — it carries over and keeps building for the entire round
- This means a long sequence of tumbling wins can push the multiplier to dizzying heights

Because the multiplier persists for the whole feature, the longer you can keep the reels tumbling, the more devastating each subsequent win becomes. A modest payout early in the round can become a colossal one once the multiplier has climbed into double digits — and this is the primary route to the game's **5,000x maximum win**.

**Example Free Spins Scenario:**

- £1.00 stake, free spins triggered with 5 THOR scatters = 14 free spins
- Across the early spins, a series of tumbles lifts the multiplier to 8x
- A high-ways board then pays 40x across multiple reels
- With the 8x multiplier applied: 40x × 8 = **320x stake = £320.00** from that spin alone, before any further tumbles

The free spins round is where Power of Thor Megaways earns its high-volatility reputation. The wins can be slow to arrive, but when the multiplier has been allowed to build, the payouts can be truly thunderous.
