## The Megaways™ Engine: 117,649 Ways Beneath the Waves

### Understanding Megaways™ in Atlantis

At the heart of Atlantis Megaways beats the licensed **Megaways™** engine, the variable-ways system that transformed slot mathematics. Instead of fixed paylines, every reel can display a different number of symbols on each spin, and the total ways to win are recalculated dynamically. This is what gives the game its constantly shifting rhythm and its headline figure of 117,649 ways.

**How Megaways™ Works in Atlantis:**

- **6 vertical reels**, each capable of showing between **2 and 7 symbols** per spin
- **Ways to win** are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
- **Maximum ways**: 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 = **117,649 ways to win**
- **Minimum ways**: 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 64 ways to win
- Wins pay for **matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right**, regardless of their position on the reel

**Win Formation Principles:**

- Symbol size and exact row position are irrelevant — only the reel sequence matters
- A winning combination must start on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
- Multiple separate winning combinations can land and pay on a single spin
- Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your **total stake**, not a per-line bet
- The number of ways changes every spin, so no two spins feel mathematically identical

A horizontal reel above the main grid also contributes extra symbols to the top row, which is a familiar Megaways flourish that helps push the active ways toward the 117,649 maximum on the most generous spins.

## Cascading Wins: One Spin, Many Payouts

Like most Megaways™ slots, Atlantis Megaways employs **cascading wins** (sometimes called tumbles or avalanches). The mechanic transforms a single paid spin into a potential chain of consecutive wins:

1. The symbols that formed a winning combination are removed from the grid
2. The symbols above them drop down to fill the empty spaces
3. New symbols fall in from above to complete the reels
4. If the new arrangement creates another win, it pays and the process repeats
5. Cascades continue until no further wins land

Because Atlantis Megaways does not feature a multiplier ladder during its cascades, the value here comes from volume rather than escalation. A single high-ways board can shed several winning combinations in quick succession, and on a full 117,649-way spin a well-timed cascade can chain mid-value sea creatures into a satisfying combined payout. The cascade mechanic is the engine that keeps the base game flowing between bonus triggers.

## Pearl Jackpot Respins: The Treasure Hunt Bonus

### Triggering the Feature

The signature feature of Atlantis Megaways is the **Pearl Jackpot Respins** round, a treasure-collection bonus that replaces the free spins round you might expect from a Megaways title. The feature is triggered when **six scattered treasure chest symbols** land in view across the reels. These glowing chests are the key to the entire bonus, and each one carries an attached value when it locks into place.

When the feature begins, all symbols except the collected treasure chests are cleared from the grid, and you enter a respin sequence focused entirely on gathering more chests and their values.

### How the Respins Work

The Pearl Jackpot Respins round uses a classic "hold and respin" structure:

1. The triggering treasure chests lock into their positions, each displaying its own value
2. You are awarded a set number of respins — typically **three to begin**
3. Every time a new chest lands during the respins, it **locks in place** and the respin counter **resets back to three**
4. The feature continues for as long as fresh chests keep arriving
5. The round ends when the respins run out, or when every position on the grid is filled with chests

The longer you keep landing chests, the more values you accumulate, and filling the entire grid is the route to the very top of the prize structure.

### The Four Jackpot Tiers

The real prize of the Pearl Respins feature is the chance to win one of four fixed jackpots, each expressed as a multiple of your stake:

- **Mini Jackpot** — 10x your stake
- **Minor Jackpot** — 50x your stake
- **Major Jackpot** — 250x your stake
- **Grand Jackpot** — 2,000x your stake

The lower jackpots appear far more frequently, while the **Grand jackpot is heavily restricted** and genuinely rare — it is the headline reward reserved for the very best runs in the feature, typically those that flood the grid with treasure chests. Combined with the accumulated chest values, the jackpots are where the bonus round delivers its biggest hits and where most of the game's largest payouts are forged.

**Why the Pearl Respins Round Matters:**

- **Clear Objective**: The hunt for chests gives the feature a focused, tension-building rhythm
- **Reset Mechanic**: Each new chest resets your respins, so a hot streak can extend the round considerably
- **Tiered Rewards**: Four jackpot levels create a satisfying ladder to climb toward
- **Big-Win Pathway**: Filling the grid is the realistic route to the Grand jackpot and the game's ceiling
