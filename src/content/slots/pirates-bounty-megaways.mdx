## The Megaways™ Engine: 117,649 Ways to Plunder

### Understanding Megaways™ in Pirates Bounty

At the heart of Pirates Bounty Megaways beats the licensed **Megaways™** engine, the variable-ways system that transformed slot mathematics. Instead of fixed paylines, every reel can display a different number of symbols on each spin, and the total ways to win are recalculated dynamically. This is what gives the game its constantly shifting rhythm and its headline figure of 117,649 ways.

**How Megaways™ Works in Pirates Bounty:**

- **6 vertical reels**, each capable of showing between **2 and 7 symbols** per spin
- An additional horizontal reel sits above the main grid, feeding extra symbols and scatters into play
- **Ways to win** are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
- **Maximum ways**: 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 = **117,649 ways to win**
- Wins pay for **matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right**, regardless of their position on the reel

**Win Formation Principles:**

- Symbol size and exact row position are irrelevant — only the reel sequence matters
- A winning combination must start on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
- Multiple separate winning combinations can land and pay on a single spin
- Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your **total stake**, not a per-line bet
- The number of ways changes every spin, so no two spins feel mathematically identical

### Cascading Reactions

Like most Megaways™ slots, Pirates Bounty Megaways employs **cascading reactions** (also known as tumbles or avalanches). When you land a winning combination:

1. The symbols that formed the win are removed from the grid
2. The symbols above them drop down to fill the empty spaces
3. New symbols fall in from above to complete the reels
4. If the new arrangement creates another win, it pays and the process repeats
5. Cascades continue until no further wins land

Cascades are doubly important in this game because they do more than chain wins together — each successive cascade also fills the **Bounty Bonus Meter** that triggers the bonus tiers. A single paid spin can therefore both pay multiple times and march you steadily toward the wheel, which is where the largest treasure is buried.

## The Bounty Bonus Meter & Cascade Tiers

### Cascades That Unlock the Treasure

What sets Pirates Bounty Megaways apart from a standard Megaways tumbler is how the bonus is triggered. Rather than relying solely on scatters in the base game, the bonus is reached by stringing together **consecutive cascades** from a single spin. Each cascade fills the **Bounty Bonus Meter**, and the number of cascades you achieve determines which of three escalating bonus tiers you enter.

**The Three Cascade Tiers:**

1. **Bonus (Level 1)** — Triggered by reaching the first cascade threshold. Awards the entry-level wheel, offering smaller free-spin counts or instant cash prizes.

2. **Super Bonus (Level 2)** — Triggered by a longer cascade streak. The wheel improves, offering more free spins and higher prize values.

3. **Mega Bonus (Level 3)** — Triggered by the longest cascade streaks. This is the premium tier, delivering the most free spins and the biggest treasure on the wheel.

**Why the Meter Matters:**

- **Skill-Free Progression**: You simply chase cascades; the meter does the rest
- **Escalating Reward**: The further you climb, the more lucrative the bonus becomes
- **Built-In Tension**: Watching a cascade streak build toward the next tier is genuinely thrilling
- **Synergy With Mystery Symbols**: Mystery-symbol wins cascade and push the meter higher

This meter-driven approach gives the base game a satisfying sense of momentum. Every cascade counts, and a long, lucky chain can vault you straight into the Mega Bonus.

## Free Spins: The Progressive Multiplier That Never Resets

### Building a Bigger Booty Every Cascade

The crown jewel of Pirates Bounty Megaways is its **free-spins round**, won via the Bounty Wheel. What elevates it above a standard free-spins feature is the **progressive win multiplier** that climbs throughout the round and, crucially, **never resets between spins**.

**How the Free Spins Work:**

- You are awarded a set number of free spins based on the tier and wheel outcome
- The full 117,649-way Megaways™ engine remains active throughout
- The win multiplier starts at **1x** and **increases by 1x with every cascade**
- The multiplier carries over from one free spin to the next without ever resetting
- Cascading wins are multiplied at the current multiplier value, compounding your rewards

**Spin Counts by Tier (typical):**

- **Bonus (Level 1)**: around 8 to 12 free spins, or instant prizes of roughly 12x to 40x bet
- **Super Bonus (Level 2)**: around 12 to 18 free spins
- **Mega Bonus (Level 3)**: up to 20 free spins

**Scatter Re-Triggers:**

Scatter symbols can land on the upper reel during the free-spins round to award **additional spins**, extending your time on the water and giving the multiplier more opportunities to climb.

**Why It Matters:**

Because the multiplier never resets, a long free-spins round with plenty of cascades can see the multiplier reach very high values, and any big multi-way win that lands late in the round is multiplied by that accumulated figure. This is the mechanic most likely to deliver the game's **10,000x** maximum win, and it is where patient players find their richest hauls.

**Example:**
- £1.00 stake, Mega Bonus awards 20 free spins
- A run of cascades early in the round pushes the multiplier to 8x
- A high-value symbol lands across the full board for a 25x base win
- With the 8x multiplier: 25x × 8 = **200x stake = £200.00** from that spin alone, before any further cascade chains
