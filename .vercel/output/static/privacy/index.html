Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 July 2026

Who we are

Megaways Online (megawaysonline.com) is an informational website publishing reviews of online slot games and casinos. We do not operate gambling services and we do not process payments or wagers. For anything in this policy, contact hello@megawaysonline.com.

What we collect

Very little. This site has no user accounts, no comment forms and no newsletter. We do not run analytics or advertising trackers, and we do not set tracking cookies.

Theme preference. If you switch between light and dark mode, that choice is saved in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device, is not transmitted to us, and you can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

Hosting logs. Our hosting provider (Vercel Inc.) records standard technical server logs — such as IP address, browser type and pages requested — to serve the site and protect it from abuse. These logs are processed under Vercel's own privacy policy.

Casino links and third parties

Links to casinos on this site are affiliate links. If you follow one, the casino operator (and its tracking platform) may set cookies and collect data on their own sites under their own privacy policies — we encourage you to read them before registering. We receive aggregate referral statistics from these programmes, not your personal details.

Outbound links are sent with a no-referrer policy, meaning your browser does not tell the destination site which of our pages you came from.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or UK GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. Since we hold essentially none, requests will usually be a short conversation — but email us and we will help, including passing requests to our hosting provider where relevant.

Age restriction

This site is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect any information from minors, and our content is not directed at them.

Changes to this policy

If we add features that change how data is handled (for example privacy-first analytics or embedded game demos), this policy will be updated first and the date above revised.
