Xtraways Slots

Developer: Stakelogic
Released: 2020

What is Xtraways?

Xtraways is a slot mechanic developed by Stakelogic as a direct competitor to Big Time Gaming’s Megaways. Introduced in 2020, Xtraways offers variable reel sizes with up to 262,144 ways to win – more than double Megaways’ 117,649 ways. It’s an alternative take on dynamic reel mechanics with its own unique features.

How Xtraways Works

Variable Reel System

Similar to Megaways but with key differences:

  • 6 reels with variable symbol heights
  • 2-8 symbols per reel (vs Megaways’ 2-7)
  • Creates 64 to 262,144 ways to win
  • Changes dynamically on every spin

Maximum Ways Calculation

The increased maximum comes from the extra symbol:

  • Megaways: 7×7×7×7×7×7 = 117,649 ways
  • Xtraways: 8×8×8×8×8×8 = 262,144 ways

Core Mechanics

Xtraways typically includes:

  • Cascading symbols (winning symbols removed)
  • Wild substitutions
  • Free spins bonuses
  • Progressive multipliers

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Xtraways better than Megaways?

Not necessarily “better” – it’s different. Xtraways offers more maximum ways (262,144 vs 117,649), but the actual playing experience depends on individual game design, RTP, and features.

Who created Xtraways?

Xtraways was developed by Stakelogic as a competing mechanic to Big Time Gaming’s Megaways.

Why haven’t I heard of Xtraways?

Megaways launched earlier (2015 vs 2020) and has dominated marketing and licensing. Xtraways is less widely known but growing in availability.

Are Xtraways slots fair?

Yes, all licensed Xtraways slots use certified RNGs and are audited for fairness, just like Megaways games.

Do more ways to win mean better odds?

Not necessarily. More ways to win creates more potential combinations, but individual win values, hit frequency, and RTP matter more for actual returns.

Can other providers license Xtraways?

Stakelogic owns Xtraways and can license it to other providers, though it hasn’t achieved the widespread licensing of Megaways.

Which is more volatile, Megaways or Xtraways?

Both are typically high volatility, but individual games vary. Check each game’s volatility rating rather than assuming based on the mechanic.
