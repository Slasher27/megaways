Megaways

Megaclusters

Innovative cluster-pays mechanic where symbols split into smaller symbols. Created by Big Time Gaming, Megaclusters offers cascading wins and massive symbol grids.

Megapays

Progressive jackpot mechanic combined with Megaways. Created by Relax Gaming under license from Big Time Gaming, Megapays adds life-changing jackpots to Megaways gameplay.

Megaquads

Split symbol mechanic where single symbols can split into 4 matching symbols. Created by Big Time Gaming, Megaquads multiplies winning potential on every spin.

Megadrop

Hourly jackpot mechanic that must drop within a specific time. Created by Relax Gaming, Megadrop adds time-sensitive progressive jackpots with guaranteed winners.

Megaways Slots FAQs

What is a Megaways slot?

A Megaways slot is a game built on Big Time Gaming’s variable-reel engine. Each reel shows a different number of symbols on every spin — typically two to seven — so the number of ways to win changes constantly, reaching up to 117,649 ways on most titles.

How many ways to win can a Megaways slot have?

Most Megaways slots max out at 117,649 ways — six reels with up to seven symbols each. Some go further: NFT Megaways reaches 200,704 ways with an extra top reel, and Wheel of Fortune Megaways can reach 1,000,000 ways during free spins.

Can I play Megaways slots for free?

Yes. Most Megaways slots offer a free demo version, and our reviews break down every feature so you can try a game risk-free before playing for real money at a casino.

What is the RTP of Megaways slots?

Most Megaways slots return between 95% and 96.5% to players over time. Providers increasingly publish several RTP configurations, so the figure at a specific casino can be lower — our reviews list the default RTP and flag known variants.

Is it worth taking a casino bonus?

It can be, if you read the terms first. A bonus extends your playtime, but wagering requirements decide its real value — check the wagering multiplier, game weighting and maximum bet rules before opting in.

Is it safe to claim a casino bonus?

Claiming a bonus is safe at a licensed casino. Stick to licensed sites, read the bonus terms and conditions carefully, and never deposit more than you can afford to lose.

Which providers make Megaways slots?

Big Time Gaming invented the Megaways engine and licenses it to studios including Blueprint Gaming, Pragmatic Play, Red Tiger, iSoftBet and Relax Gaming. Our provider pages list every studio’s Megaways games.
