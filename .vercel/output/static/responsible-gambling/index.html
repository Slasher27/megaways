What is Responsible Gambling?

Responsible gambling means keeping gambling as a fun form of entertainment, not a way to earn income or escape problems. It involves:

  • Only gambling with money you can afford to lose
  • Setting time and money limits before you start
  • Never chasing losses
  • Taking regular breaks
  • Not gambling when upset, stressed, or under the influence
  • Keeping gambling in balance with other activities

Remember: The house always has an edge. Over time, the casino will always win. Gambling should never be seen as a way to make money or solve financial problems.

Warning Signs of Problem Gambling

Problem gambling can affect anyone. Watch for these warning signs in yourself or others:

Behavioral Signs

  • Gambling with money needed for bills or essentials
  • Borrowing money to gamble
  • Lying about gambling activities
  • Chasing losses
  • Gambling for longer than intended
  • Neglecting work, school, or family
  • Gambling to escape problems or relieve anxiety

Emotional Signs

  • Feeling guilty or ashamed about gambling
  • Mood swings related to wins and losses
  • Becoming defensive when questioned
  • Restlessness or irritability when not gambling
  • Depression or anxiety
  • Relationship problems
  • Feeling unable to stop despite wanting to

If you recognize any of these signs:

Take action now. Problem gambling is a progressive condition that typically gets worse over time without intervention. The sooner you seek help, the easier it is to regain control.

Self-Help Tools

Most online casinos offer tools to help you stay in control:

Deposit Limits

Set daily, weekly, or monthly limits on how much you can deposit.

Loss Limits

Set a maximum amount you're willing to lose in a given period.

Time Limits

Set reminders or automatic logouts after a set playing time.

Reality Checks

Get pop-up reminders showing how long you've been playing.

Take a Break

Temporarily suspend your account for 24 hours to 6 weeks.

Self-Exclusion

Block access to your account for 6 months to 5 years (or permanently).

Tips for Safe Gambling

✓ Do

  • Set a budget before you start and stick to it
  • Use deposit and time limits
  • Take regular breaks
  • Gamble for fun, not to make money
  • Keep track of time and money spent
  • Balance gambling with other activities
  • Only gamble when you're in a good mood

✗ Don't

  • Never chase your losses
  • Don't gamble with money you can't afford to lose
  • Don't gamble on credit
  • Don't gamble when upset, stressed, or depressed
  • Don't lie about your gambling
  • Don't neglect responsibilities to gamble
  • Don't gamble under the influence of alcohol or drugs

Underage Gambling

Online gambling is illegal for anyone under 18 (or 21 in some jurisdictions). All reputable casinos verify age and identity before allowing real money play.

Parents and guardians should use parental controls and monitor online activity to prevent underage gambling.

Gambling Should Be Fun

When gambling stops being fun and starts causing problems, it's time to take a break or seek help. There's no shame in asking for support—problem gambling is a recognized addiction that affects millions of people worldwide.

Remember: Help is always available, and recovery is possible.
