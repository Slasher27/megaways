Chaos in Jotunheim: Lucksome’s Loki Lord of Mischief Megaways

The trickster god of Norse legend takes centre stage in Loki Lord of Mischief Megaways, an atmospheric, feature-rich slot from Lucksome, the boutique studio operating under the Blueprint Gaming umbrella. Released on September 23, 2021, this Norse-themed adventure drops you into the frozen giant realm of Jotunheim, where the unpredictable Loki bends the rules of the reels in his favour — and, if you play your spins right, in yours too.

A quick word of clarity before we begin, because honesty matters on a review site. Despite the “Megaways” tag carried in this game’s title and URL, Loki Lord of Mischief is not built on the licensed Megaways™ engine. It is a five-reel game with 20 paylines whose grid can expand from three rows up to five rows during its bonus features. The “Megaways” label here is a naming quirk rather than a mechanical reality — what you are actually getting is a money-scatter, lock-and-respin slot with an expanding reel matrix. We will treat the game on its true terms throughout this review so you know exactly what you are spinning.

That clarification aside, Loki Lord of Mischief Megaways is a genuinely entertaining high-volatility slot. Loki himself appears as a mystery symbol that transforms into the all-important money scatters, which are your key to the two-tier bonus system: the Lux Reels lock-and-respin round and the more lucrative Super Lux Reels. With multipliers climbing as high as 5x, instant cash prizes of up to 500x, and a maximum win of 5,500x your stake, the trickster has plenty of mischief to reward the patient.

With a 96% RTP, high volatility (rated 8 out of 10), and a betting range that stretches all the way from £0.20 to £200.00 per spin, this is a slot built for players who appreciate a slow burn punctuated by explosive feature moments. Whether you summon the bonus organically, upgrade your way in, or buy it outright, Jotunheim is waiting. Let’s see what the Lord of Mischief has in store.

The Reels & Paylines: How the Base Game Works

A 20-Line Foundation, Not a Megaways Engine

At its core, Loki Lord of Mischief plays on a familiar and easy-to-read structure: five reels, three rows, and twenty fixed paylines. Unlike a Megaways™ slot, the number of ways to win does not change from spin to spin in the base game. Instead, you are chasing matching symbols along twenty predefined lines, each running from the leftmost reel toward the right.

How the Base Game Works:

  • 5 vertical reels, displaying three symbols each in the standard base-game state
  • 20 fixed paylines that pay for matching symbols on adjacent reels from the left
  • Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your total stake
  • The expanding rows (up to five per reel) are reserved for the Lux Reels and Super Lux Reels features

This straightforward base layout is deliberate. Lucksome has front-loaded the excitement into the features, so the base game’s primary job is to deliver the money scatters that unlock them. That is where the trickster’s real chaos — and the bulk of the 5,500x potential — lives.

The Expanding Reel Matrix

The headline visual flourish of Loki Lord of Mischief is its expanding reel matrix. While the base game shows three rows, the grid can grow to as many as five rows when a feature activates. As Lucksome puts it, the reel matrix expands to “give more space for those cash symbols” — and that extra real estate matters, because more visible positions means more room for money scatters to land and accumulate during the lock-and-respin rounds.

This expansion is the closest the game comes to justifying its “Megaways”-style branding: the playing field genuinely changes shape. But it does so in a controlled, feature-driven way rather than randomising the ways-to-win on every spin.

The Loki Mystery Symbol: The Trickster’s Calling Card

One God, Many Disguises

True to his mythological reputation, Loki appears on the reels as a mystery symbol — a symbol whose identity is hidden until it reveals itself. In the base game, the Loki mystery symbol transforms into a money scatter, the cash-value symbol that powers the entire bonus system.

How the Loki Mystery Symbol Works:

  • Loki lands on the reels disguised as a mystery symbol
  • When it reveals, it converts into a money scatter carrying a cash value
  • These converted scatters count toward triggering the Lux Reels feature
  • The more money scatters Loki conjures, the closer you are to summoning the bonus

Because Loki is the bridge between an ordinary spin and the lucrative features, every appearance of the mystery symbol carries a jolt of anticipation. He is, fittingly, the agent of chaos who decides whether your spin stays quiet or erupts into a bonus. The trickster giveth.

Money Scatters: The Currency of Chaos

Cash Symbols That Trigger and Pay

The money scatter is the single most important symbol in Loki Lord of Mischief. Each money scatter carries a cash value expressed as a multiple of your stake, and it performs two jobs at once: it helps trigger the bonus rounds, and it pays out directly within them.

How Money Scatters Work:

  • Money scatters display individual cash values on their faces
  • Landing six or more money scatters in view triggers the Lux Reels feature
  • Inside the lock-and-respin rounds, money scatters are locked in place and contribute their values to your running total
  • Higher-value scatters and multipliers stack up the eventual payout

This collect-style mechanic is the engine of the game’s volatility. A bonus that lands a handful of low scatters will fizzle, while one that fills the expanded grid with high-value scatters and multipliers can rocket toward the 5,500x ceiling. That spread between outcomes is exactly what high volatility feels like in practice.

Lux Reels: The Lock & Respin Bonus

Locking In the Trickster’s Treasure

The main bonus of Loki Lord of Mischief is the Lux Reels feature, a classic lock-and-respin (also called a hold-and-respin) round triggered by landing six or more money scatters on screen at once.

How Lux Reels Works:

  1. The six-or-more triggering money scatters are locked in place on the grid
  2. You are awarded a set number of respins (three lives to begin with)
  3. Each respin that lands a new money scatter locks it and resets your respins back to three
  4. As the feature plays, additional covered rows can unlock, expanding the grid to give scatters more room to land
  5. When you run out of respins, all locked money scatter values are totalled and paid

The reset-on-hit mechanic is the heart of the tension. As long as Loki keeps delivering scatters, the feature keeps going and your potential payout keeps climbing. The round ends only when the trickster’s luck finally runs dry — and with the grid expanding as you go, a hot Lux Reels session can blossom into something special.

Example:

  • £1.00 stake, Lux Reels triggers with six money scatters
  • Across multiple resetting respins you lock fourteen scatters carrying a combined 60x in values
  • The grid expands to five rows mid-feature, giving room for the final few scatters
  • Payout: £60.00 from this single bonus

Super Lux Reels: The Premium Bonus

Bigger Scatters, Bigger Multipliers, Your Choice of Risk

Sitting above the standard Lux Reels is the Super Lux Reels feature — the game’s premium bonus round, triggered by landing three Tree of Life bonus symbols. The Super version is more generous in every dimension: larger scatter values, higher multipliers, and a longer guaranteed run of spins. Crucially, it also hands you a rare degree of control.

Choose Your Volatility:

Before the Super Lux Reels round begins, you select one of three volatility profiles, each shaping the balance between scatter values and multipliers:

  • Low Volatility: money scatters worth up to 50x, with multipliers of 4x to 5x
  • Medium Volatility: money scatters worth up to 100x, with multipliers of 2x to 5x
  • High Volatility: money scatters worth up to 500x, with a 5x multiplier

How Super Lux Reels Works:

  • The round opens with six initial non-resetting spins before transitioning to the standard three resetting respins
  • Money scatters lock and accumulate just as in the standard Lux Reels
  • The selected volatility profile determines how big individual scatters and multipliers can be
  • The grid expands to give the larger cash symbols room to land

This volatility selector is a genuine highlight. A cautious player can lean toward the low or medium profiles for steadier, multiplier-driven totals, while a thrill-seeker can pick the high profile and chase those colossal 500x money scatters that bring the 5,500x maximum win into view. The instant cash prizes within the feature can reach 500x in their own right, with the win focus centred around 500x for a strong session.

Example:

  • £1.00 stake, Super Lux Reels with the high-volatility profile selected
  • A 500x money scatter locks, and a 5x multiplier later attaches to the running total
  • Combined with the other locked scatters, the round resolves at a four-figure multiple of stake

Lucksome Locks & The Feature Upgrade

Extra Routes Into the Action

Beyond the two main bonuses, Loki Lord of Mischief includes two mechanics that give players more agency over how and when they engage the features.

Lucksome Locks:

The Lucksome Locks mechanic lets you lock two bonus scatters and spin only the remaining positions for a fixed cost of 5x your stake per spin. It is a targeted, pay-to-chase tool: by holding the scatters you already have, you focus each paid spin entirely on completing the trigger or building toward the bonus. It rewards players who want to commit to a near-miss rather than walking away from it.

Feature Upgrade:

When you trigger the standard Lux Reels organically, the game offers an optional Feature Upgrade for around 69.85x your stake, converting your Lux Reels round into the more lucrative Super Lux Reels. For players who land the base bonus but crave the bigger scatter values and multipliers of the premium round, it is a direct — if pricey — escalation.

These optional mechanics, alongside the full Bonus Buy, mean Loki Lord of Mischief offers an unusually rich set of ways to reach its features, catering to patient grinders and impatient thrill-seekers alike.

Bonus Buy: Skip Straight to the Mischief

Buying Into the Feature

Where local regulations permit, Loki Lord of Mischief offers a Bonus Buy option that lets you purchase direct entry into the feature for approximately 116.1x your stake. Rather than grinding the base game for six money scatters, you pay the premium and drop straight into the lock-and-respin action.

Bonus Buy Considerations:

  • Cost: roughly 116.1x your current bet per purchase
  • Value: best suited to players who specifically want the feature and have the bankroll to absorb several non-paying buys
  • Risk: at high volatility, individual bought features can still resolve below the purchase price, so it is far from a guaranteed profit
  • Availability: the Bonus Buy is operator-selectable and restricted in certain markets (notably the UK, where bonus buys are prohibited)

The Bonus Buy is a useful shortcut for feature-focused players, but the 116.1x price tag against a 5,500x ceiling demands respect. Treat it as accelerated entertainment rather than a strategy to beat the maths.

How to Win: Strategy & Tips

Loki Lord of Mischief is a high-volatility slot, so a disciplined, patient approach is essential. Here is how to give yourself the best shot at the trickster’s gold.

Bankroll Management

  • Size your bet for endurance: With a £0.20 to £200.00 range, keep your stake small enough to weather the inevitable dry spells. High volatility means features can take a while to arrive, so plan for a long session rather than a quick win.
  • Set firm win and loss limits: Decide in advance when you will stop — both when ahead and when behind. The 5,500x ceiling is a big target, but most sessions will hinge on more modest feature totals.
  • Respect the Bonus Buy cost: At 116.1x per buy, repeated purchases can drain a bankroll fast. Only buy if you have the budget to absorb several below-cost outcomes.

Base Game Strategy

  • Watch for Loki: The mystery symbol is your link to money scatters, so every Loki appearance is a chance to edge toward the bonus.
  • Consider Lucksome Locks selectively: When you are one or two scatters short of triggering, the 5x-per-spin lock mechanic can be worth a few targeted spins — but it adds up quickly, so use it with discipline.

Bonus Round Strategy

  • Choose your Super Lux Reels volatility wisely: Pick the low or medium profile for steadier, multiplier-driven totals; choose high only when you are specifically hunting the 500x scatters and the 5,500x dream.
  • Weigh the Feature Upgrade: Converting Lux Reels to Super Lux Reels for ~69.85x is tempting, but it is another sizeable outlay — reserve it for when your bankroll can comfortably cover it.
  • Practice in the demo first: Use the free-play version to learn how the lock-and-respin resets and the volatility profiles behave before committing real funds.

If you enjoy money-collect lock-and-respin mechanics, appreciate a strong theme, and can stomach the swings of a high-variance game, the Lord of Mischief is well worth a visit to Jotunheim. Just go in knowing exactly what — and what isn’t — behind the “Megaways” name.
