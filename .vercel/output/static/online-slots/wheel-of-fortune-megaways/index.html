Spin the Wheel: Big Time Gaming Takes the Ways Counter to One Million

Few phrases in television are as instantly recognisable as “spin the wheel”, and few names in slot development are as synonymous with the Megaways™ mechanic as Big Time Gaming. Wheel of Fortune Megaways unites the two, taking IGT’s legendary game-show licence — the most famous wheel in entertainment — and bolting it onto the engine BTG invented. The result, released on February 25, 2021, is a six-reel spectacle where the base game serves up the classic 117,649 ways and the free spins round expands all the way to a staggering 1,000,000 Megaways.

That million-way headline is not marketing fluff. Through a combination of Expansion Symbols, reels that grow up to ten symbols high, and the famous wheel itself deciding your starting ways count, Wheel of Fortune Megaways builds toward one of the largest dynamic ways totals ever put on a slot. And when reels stand at full height, Wild Megastacks ten wilds tall can crash through the grid to complete colossal multi-way combinations.

The presentation channels prime-time Las Vegas glamour: a glittering gold-and-purple studio backdrop, gem symbols that sparkle under stage lighting, and of course the multicoloured prize wheel that spins into view whenever the bonus begins. Purists may note it feels more “luxury Vegas” than a faithful recreation of the TV studio, but the wheel moments themselves land with genuine game-show drama.

Under the hood the numbers are serious: a 96.46% RTP that sits comfortably above the modern average, high volatility, a maximum win of 80,150x your stake, and a betting range from £0.20 to £50.00 per spin. This is a genuine BTG heavyweight — a slot built for players who want the ways counter, not gimmicks, to do the heavy lifting.

Ready to buy a vowel? Let’s spin through every mechanic, from the base-game Megastacks to the million-way free spins, and see whether this wheel deserves a place in your rotation.

The Megaways™ Engine: From 117,649 to One Million Ways

The Base Game Foundation

Big Time Gaming invented Megaways™, and Wheel of Fortune Megaways runs the mechanic in its classic configuration during base play. There are no fixed paylines; instead, every reel independently displays between 2 and 7 symbols on each spin, and the total number of ways to win is recalculated every time the reels stop.

How the Base Game Works:

  • 6 vertical reels, each showing between 2 and 7 symbols per spin
  • Ways to win equal the product of symbol counts on all six reels
  • Maximum base-game ways: 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 = 117,649
  • Minimum ways: 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 64
  • Wins pay for matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right, regardless of position

Win Formation Principles:

  • Only the reel sequence matters — the exact row a symbol occupies is irrelevant
  • Combinations must begin on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
  • Several separate winning combinations can pay on the same spin
  • All payouts are multiples of your total stake
  • Because reel heights shift every spin, the mathematical texture of the game changes constantly

The Million-Way Free Spins Grid

Where Wheel of Fortune Megaways breaks from the standard formula is in its free spins round. There, reels are no longer capped at seven symbols — Expansion Symbols can grow each reel up to 10 symbols high. When all six reels stand at full height, the ways calculation becomes 10 × 10 × 10 × 10 × 10 × 10 = 1,000,000 Megaways.

That is more than eight times the standard Megaways ceiling, and it fundamentally changes what a single free spin can deliver. A premium symbol landing on every reel of a million-way board produces win totals the base game simply cannot reach — and it is precisely this expansion arc that carries the game toward its 80,150x maximum.

One structural note: unlike many BTG titles, Wheel of Fortune Megaways does not use cascading reels or progressive win multipliers. Each spin resolves in a single drop, and the game’s entire potential is channelled through ways expansion and wild coverage instead. It is a purer, more old-school volatility profile than the Bonanza-style reaction chains BTG fans may expect.

Wild Megastacks: Towers of Wilds

Base Game Megastacks

The signature base-game weapon in Wheel of Fortune Megaways is the Wild Megastack — enormous towers of stacked wilds that can descend onto the reels and cover them completely.

How Wild Megastacks Work in the Base Game:

  • Wild Megastacks are 7 symbols tall, enough to blanket a reel at its maximum base-game height
  • Up to three reels can be completely covered in wilds on a single spin
  • Wilds substitute for every paying symbol, turning a covered reel into a universal connector

Why Wild Megastacks Matter:

  • Full-reel substitution: A completely wild reel connects with every symbol on its neighbours, so no combination passing through it can break
  • Ways multiplication: A 7-high wild reel contributes its full height to the ways calculation, maximising that reel’s multiplier in the equation
  • Chain completion: Wilds bridge gaps between premium symbols on distant reels, converting near-misses into six-of-a-kind chains
  • Stacked adjacency: When two or three covered reels sit side by side, the compounding effect is dramatic — every crossing chain multiplies through each wild reel in turn

When two or three adjacent reels are fully wild, every symbol chain crossing them multiplies its ways count dramatically. Three fully wild reels on a high-ways board is the base game’s jackpot moment — a single premium symbol on the flanking reels can suddenly complete thousands of simultaneous ways.

Example — Base Game Megastack Hit:

  • £1.00 stake, reels land at 7-6-7-7-6-7 symbols high
  • Wild Megastacks fully cover reels two, three and four
  • The top gem lands on reels one, five and six
  • The wild bridge completes a six-of-a-kind chain across a massive number of ways for a payout far beyond any ordinary spin

Free Spins Megastacks: Ten Wilds High

Inside the bonus round the Megastacks scale up with the reels:

  • Free-spins Wild Megastacks are 10 symbols tall, matching the expanded maximum reel height
  • Up to two reels can display full 10-high wild coverage per spin

Two fully wild reels on a board approaching 1,000,000 ways is where Wheel of Fortune Megaways produces its most explosive results. The slight trade-off — two wild reels in the bonus versus three in the base game — is more than offset by the sheer scale of the expanded grid.

Example — Free Spins Megastack Moment:

  • £1.00 stake, deep into the bonus with four reels at full 10-symbol height
  • 10-high Wild Megastacks blanket reels three and four
  • A premium gem lands on reels one, two, five and six
  • The wild bridge completes the chain across hundreds of thousands of simultaneous ways — the kind of single-spin result that defines a session

The interplay between Megastacks and expansion is deliberate design: expansion raises the ways ceiling, and Megastacks are the mechanism that converts a tall board into a paid outcome. Neither is at its best without the other, which is why the game’s biggest hits cluster in the second half of long bonus rounds.

The Wheel Decides Your Starting Megaways

Before the first free spin plays, the game delivers its signature moment: the Wheel of Fortune wheel rolls onto the screen and spins to determine your starting Megaways count — in other words, how tall your reels begin the feature. A strong wheel result means you start the round already elevated toward the million-way ceiling; a weaker one leaves you more building to do through Expansion Symbols.

It is a smart piece of licence integration. Rather than the wheel being a cosmetic cameo, it directly sets the mathematical foundation of your entire bonus, and watching the pointer slow toward a high segment carries authentic game-show tension.

Why the Wheel Bonus Matters:

  • Real mathematical stakes: The wheel result determines your opening reel heights, and therefore the ways count of every early bonus spin
  • Momentum head start: A generous wheel spin can shave several Expansion Symbols’ worth of building off the round
  • Authentic drama: The tick-tick-tick of the slowing pointer is the licence’s signature sound, deployed at the moment of highest consequence
  • Repeat appearances: The wheel returns on every retrigger, so a good bonus features multiple wheel moments rather than one

Expansion Symbols: Growing Toward a Million

During free spins, special Expansion Symbols can land on the reels. Each one that appears is replaced by two new symbols, permanently increasing that reel’s height by one position for the remainder of the feature — up to the maximum of 10 symbols per reel.

Why Expansion Matters:

  • Every added position increases that reel’s contribution to the ways multiplication
  • Growth is persistent — reels never shrink back during the bonus
  • The longer the feature runs, the closer the grid climbs toward 1,000,000 Megaways
  • Late-bonus spins are therefore structurally more valuable than early ones

The Compounding Effect in Numbers:

To appreciate how quickly expansion snowballs, consider the ways calculation at different average reel heights:

  • All reels at 5 high: 5⁶ = 15,625 ways
  • All reels at 7 high: 7⁶ = 117,649 ways (the standard Megaways ceiling)
  • All reels at 8 high: 8⁶ = 262,144 ways
  • All reels at 9 high: 9⁶ = 531,441 ways
  • All reels at 10 high: 10⁶ = 1,000,000 ways

Each single step of average height roughly doubles the board’s power in the later stages. This is why a bonus that starts slowly can still finish spectacularly — the final few Expansion Symbols are worth far more than the first few.

Wheel Retriggers: Full Height, Fresh Spins

Reaching full height is rewarded twice over. Whenever any reel expands to the maximum 10 symbols, the wheel spins again — this time awarding up to 12 additional free spins. On a long bonus, multiple reels reaching full height means multiple wheel retriggers, extending the round exactly when the grid is at its most potent.

This creates the game’s ideal scenario: a deep into the bonus, several reels at full height, retriggered spins in the bank, and 10-high Wild Megastacks slamming down on a near-million-way board. That confluence is the engine behind the 80,150x maximum win.

Example Bonus Arc:

  • £1.00 stake, four scatters award 14 free spins
  • The wheel sets a solid starting height across the reels
  • Expansion Symbols land steadily; by spin 9, two reels hit 10-high, each spinning the wheel for extra spins
  • With over 20 total spins played and the board above 700,000 ways, a 10-high Wild Megastack bridges a premium chain for the session’s defining win

How It Compares to Other Big Time Gaming Megaways Slots

Big Time Gaming’s catalogue is the reference library of the Megaways genre, so it is worth placing Wheel of Fortune Megaways within it:

  • Versus Bonanza: BTG’s genre-defining classic pairs 117,649 ways with cascades and an unlimited free-spins multiplier. Wheel of Fortune Megaways trades both of those for raw ways expansion — no cascades, no multiplier, but a ceiling more than eight times higher on the ways counter.
  • Versus White Rabbit Megaways: The closest structural cousin. White Rabbit pioneered extending reels (up to 248,832 ways) via caterpillar symbols; Wheel of Fortune Megaways pushes the same expanding-reel concept to a full million ways and adds the wheel to set the starting position.
  • Versus Who Wants to Be a Millionaire Megaways: BTG’s other big TV licence leans on a gamble-ladder trigger and multiplier free spins. Wheel of Fortune Megaways integrates its licence more directly into the maths — the wheel decides your grid rather than just your entry.

Where it stands: Wheel of Fortune Megaways is the purest expression of “more ways” in the BTG stable. Players who love watching a multiplier climb may prefer Bonanza’s lineage; players who find cascade-multiplier games chaotic will appreciate this title’s cleaner, expansion-driven potential.

Symbols & Paytable

Wheel of Fortune Megaways dresses its paytable in full Vegas glamour, with a conventional two-tier hierarchy beneath the specials.

High-Value Symbols:

  • Precious gems in multiple colours — the game’s premium tier, glittering in emerald, sapphire, ruby and amber tones
  • Landing six premium symbols in a chain pays between 0.9x and 25x your stake depending on the gem

Low-Value Symbols:

  • The standard royal card values (9, 10, J, Q, K, A), rendered in polished gold game-show lettering

Special Symbols:

  • Wild (Megastack) — substitutes for all paying symbols; appears as towering stacks up to 7 high in the base game and 10 high in free spins
  • Scatter (Wheel of Fortune Logo) — three or more trigger the free spins; more scatters mean more starting spins
  • Expansion Symbol — free spins only; splits into two symbols and grows its reel by one position

Reading the Paytable:

At first glance the premium pays look modest — 25x for a six-of-a-kind top hit is unremarkable. But Megaways paytables must always be read against the ways count: that same 25x chain landing across tens or hundreds of thousands of simultaneous ways, or bridged by full wild reels, compounds into enormous totals. The paytable is deliberately calibrated for a game whose ways counter can hit seven figures.

Paytable Principles to Remember:

  • All wins are expressed as multiples of your total stake, not a line bet
  • Chains pay for matching symbols on consecutive reels from the left
  • Wilds substitute for every paying symbol but do not appear as standalone scattered wilds — they arrive in Megastacks
  • Multiple different chains routinely pay on the same spin when the board is tall
  • On a full 1,000,000-way board, even the 9-A royals can contribute meaningful sums through sheer chain volume

How to Win: Strategy & Tips

Wheel of Fortune Megaways is a high-volatility slot with no bonus buy, which shapes how you should approach it.

Bankroll Management

  • Budget for the long haul: High volatility means extended stretches without a bonus. With bets from £0.20 to £50.00, size your stake so your session bankroll covers 200+ spins — the free spins round is where the game’s value is concentrated, and you need runway to reach it.
  • Set firm limits: Decide your stop-loss and walk-away-win figures before you spin. An 80,150x ceiling is a powerful lure, but the overwhelming majority of sessions resolve well below it.
  • Respect the variance profile: With no multipliers or cascades smoothing the maths, results are lumpy by design. Do not chase a cold streak by raising stakes.

Base Game Strategy

  • Wild Megastacks are your base-game event: With up to three fully wild reels possible, the base game can deliver genuinely large hits — but treat them as bonuses, not expectations.
  • Ways counts drive everything: A big win requires both wild coverage and a naturally tall board. Low-ways spins are structurally limited no matter what lands.
  • No bonus buy means patience: The only route to the million-way round is landing three scatters organically. Factor that into session length.

Free Spins Strategy

  • The wheel sets your ceiling: A strong opening wheel spin front-loads your ways count. There is no player decision here, but understanding it helps calibrate expectations for each bonus.
  • Value late-bonus spins: Because Expansion Symbol growth is persistent, the final spins of a long feature are mathematically your best. Retriggers that arrive after reels reach full height are the game’s most valuable outcome.
  • Try the demo first: The free-play version is the ideal place to internalise the bonus rhythm — how often expansion lands, how the retrigger wheel behaves — before committing real money.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the RTP of Wheel of Fortune Megaways?

Wheel of Fortune Megaways has a Return to Player (RTP) of 96.46%, which sits above the industry standard of around 96% and is a healthy figure for a high-volatility Megaways title. As always, check your casino’s game panel, as operators can sometimes offer reduced RTP configurations.

What is the maximum win in Wheel of Fortune Megaways?

The maximum win is 80,150x your stake on a single spin. Reaching it requires the free spins round with reels expanded toward the full 1,000,000 Megaways, combined with 10-high Wild Megastacks bridging premium symbol chains.

How do I get 1,000,000 Megaways?

Only in the free spins round. Expansion Symbols grow each reel by one position when they land, up to a maximum of 10 symbols per reel. With all six reels at full height, 10⁶ = 1,000,000 ways to win. The base game is capped at the classic 117,649.

How are free spins triggered and how many do I get?

Land 3, 4, 5 or 6 Wheel of Fortune logo scatters to receive 12, 14, 16 or 18 free spins respectively. Before the spins begin, the famous wheel spins to determine your starting Megaways count.

Can the free spins be retriggered?

Yes — whenever any reel expands to its full 10-symbol height, the wheel spins again and awards up to 12 additional free spins. Multiple reels reaching full height means multiple retrigger wheel spins.

What are Wild Megastacks?

Wild Megastacks are towers of stacked wilds that can completely cover reels. In the base game they are 7 symbols tall and can cover up to three reels; in free spins they grow to 10 symbols tall across up to two reels, substituting for all paying symbols.

Does Wheel of Fortune Megaways have a bonus buy?

No. There is no feature buy option, so the free spins round must be triggered organically by landing three or more scatters. There are also no win multipliers or cascading reels — the game’s potential comes entirely from ways expansion and wild coverage.

Who makes Wheel of Fortune Megaways and when was it released?

It was developed by Big Time Gaming, the inventors of the Megaways™ mechanic, under licence from IGT, holders of the Wheel of Fortune casino rights. The game had its wide release on February 25, 2021, following an earlier exclusive launch in New Jersey in January 2021.
