The Megaways™ Engine with Extra Reel

Vegas Megaways™ uses BTG’s standard Megaways™ engine with an additional twist: the extra reel.

Standard Megaways Configuration

Main Reels:

  • 6 reels, each displaying 2-7 symbols randomly per spin
  • Ways to win calculated by multiplying symbols on each reel
  • Maximum: 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 = 117,649 ways

Win Evaluation:

  • Wins form from left to right on adjacent reels
  • Matching symbols on consecutive reels starting from reel 1
  • Multiple ways evaluated simultaneously

Cascade Reactions:

  • Winning symbols are removed from reels
  • New symbols cascade down to fill empty positions
  • Process repeats until no new wins form
  • Each cascade in free spins increases unlimited win multiplier by +1

The Extra Reel Mechanic

Vegas Megaways™ includes an extra horizontal reel positioned above reels 2, 3, 4, and 5 (NOT above reels 1 and 6).

How the Extra Reel Works:

  1. The extra reel contains one symbol position above each of reels 2, 3, 4, and 5
  2. Each spin, the extra reel displays one symbol for each of these four positions
  3. These symbols are added to their respective reels below, increasing the symbol count
  4. This effectively gives reels 2-5 one additional symbol per spin

Example:

Spin Result:

  • Reel 1: 5 symbols
  • Reel 2: 6 symbols (including 1 from extra reel)
  • Reel 3: 7 symbols (including 1 from extra reel)
  • Reel 4: 4 symbols (including 1 from extra reel)
  • Reel 5: 7 symbols (including 1 from extra reel)
  • Reel 6: 5 symbols

Megaways calculation: 5 × 6 × 7 × 4 × 7 × 5 = 29,400 ways

The extra reel consistently adds symbols to the middle four reels, increasing average Megaways per spin and creating more winning opportunities. It’s a subtle but impactful mechanic that differentiates Vegas Megaways™ from standard Megaways implementations.

How does the VIP Gamble work?

When you trigger free spins with 3+ scatters, you enter the VIP Gamble—a Video Poker-style ladder with 11 rungs. You start at rung 1 (8 spins) and gamble Red or Black. Successful gambles move you up the ladder toward rung 11 (50 spins). Failed gambles drop you to the nearest safety net (rung 2 = 10 spins, rung 7 = 20 spins). You receive helpful tips before each gamble and can collect at any time.

What are the safety nets in the VIP Gamble?

There are two safety nets: Rung 2 (10 spins) and Rung 7 (20 spins). Once you reach these rungs, any failed gamble will drop you back to that rung rather than lower. This means reaching rung 7 guarantees you at least 20 free spins no matter how many gambles you fail afterward.

What are the VIP Gamble tips?

Three tip types appear before each gamble: Remove Two (eliminates two wrong answers, essentially revealing the correct answer), Winning Odds (shows which option has better probability), and All Odds (displays exact percentages for both Red and Black). Tips help you make informed gamble decisions.

Should I gamble to rung 11 (50 spins)?

It depends on your risk tolerance. Conservative players should collect at rung 7 (20 spins guaranteed). Balanced players can attempt 1-2 gambles beyond rung 7, collecting at rung 8-9 (30-35 spins) if successful. Aggressive players can gamble all the way to rung 11, knowing failed gambles fall to the rung 7 safety net (20 spins).

How does the unlimited win multiplier work?

The unlimited win multiplier starts at 1x when free spins begin and increases by +1 with every cascade reaction. The multiplier never decreases—it only increases or stays static. This creates exponential growth where late-game spins with 30x-50x multipliers can deliver massive wins.

Can you retrigger free spins?

Yes. During free spins, scatters appear only in the extra reel (above reels 2-5). Landing 3 scatters awards +4 free spins, and 4 scatters awards +8 free spins. Retriggers during high multiplier phases (20x-40x) are where the biggest wins occur.

What is the extra reel?

The extra reel is a horizontal reel above reels 2, 3, 4, and 5 (not above reels 1 and 6). Each spin, it adds one symbol to each of these four reels, increasing the symbol count and creating more ways to win. This consistently boosts average Megaways per spin.
