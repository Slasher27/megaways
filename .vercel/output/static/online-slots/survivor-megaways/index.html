The Megaways™ Engine Explained

Before diving into Survivor Megaways™‘s unique features, let’s establish what you’re working with mechanically. The game utilizes Big Time Gaming’s proprietary Megaways™ engine, which means the number of symbols appearing on each reel varies randomly with every spin. Each of the six reels can display anywhere from 2 to 7 symbols, and the total number of ways to win is calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together.

Maximum Megaways calculation: 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 = 117,649 ways

However, Survivor Megaways™ caps the maximum at 110,842 Megaways, which is still substantially higher than standard Megaways implementations and provides an enormous number of potential winning combinations on maximum expansion spins.

The reel set operates dynamically, meaning you’ll experience significant variation in how many ways to win are active from spin to spin. A minimum spin might offer just 64 ways (2×2×2×2×2×2), while a fully expanded reel set delivers the maximum 110,842 ways. This variability is core to the Megaways experience and creates the potential for dramatic swings in both directions.

Winning combinations form when matching symbols land on adjacent reels starting from the leftmost reel (in traditional Megaways fashion) OR starting from the rightmost reel (thanks to the Dual Reaction™ mechanic, which we’ll explore in depth shortly). When symbols form a winning combination, they’re removed from the reels, and new symbols cascade down to fill the empty positions, potentially creating additional wins in a chain reaction sequence.

Dual Reaction™: The Revolutionary Mechanic

Here’s where Survivor Megaways™ separates itself from every other Megaways slot in existence. Big Time Gaming has introduced the Dual Reaction™ mechanic, which fundamentally changes how winning combinations are evaluated and how cascades function.

How Dual Reaction™ Works

In traditional Megaways slots, winning combinations are evaluated strictly from left to right. You need matching symbols on consecutive reels starting from reel 1 (leftmost) to form a valid win. Survivor Megaways™ evaluates wins in BOTH directions simultaneously:

Left-to-Right Evaluation:

  • Standard Megaways behavior
  • Winning combinations start from reel 1 and move right
  • Requires consecutive matching symbols on adjacent reels

Right-to-Left Evaluation:

  • Mirror image evaluation
  • Winning combinations start from reel 6 and move left
  • Requires consecutive matching symbols on adjacent reels

This bidirectional evaluation happens on the SAME spin, effectively doubling your chances of forming winning combinations from any given symbol arrangement.

Practical Dual Reaction™ Example

Let’s examine a practical scenario to understand the impact:

Spin Result:

  • Reel 1: A, A, K, Q
  • Reel 2: K, K, K
  • Reel 3: J, J, 10, 10
  • Reel 4: 10, 10, 10, 10
  • Reel 5: 10, 10, 10, 10
  • Reel 6: 10, 10, 10

Traditional Megaways Evaluation (Left-to-Right Only):

  • 10 symbols: Reel 4 (4) × Reel 5 (4) × Reel 6 (3) = 48 ways
  • No other complete combinations from left to right
  • Total: 48-way win on 10s

Dual Reaction™ Evaluation (Both Directions):

  • Left-to-Right: 10s from reels 4-5-6 = 48 ways
  • Right-to-Left: 10s from reels 6-5-4 = 48 ways (evaluated separately)
  • Right-to-Left: A symbols from reels 6…wait, no As on reel 6
  • Right-to-Left: K symbols from reels 6-5-4-3-2-1 checking… K on reel 2 = 3 ways × checking reel 1… no consecutive connection

Actually, let me recalculate this more carefully:

Left-to-Right wins:

  • 10 on reels 4-5-6: (4 × 4 × 3) = 48 ways

Right-to-Left wins:

  • 10 on reels 6-5-4: (3 × 4 × 4) = 48 ways

The same symbol combination pays TWICE because it’s evaluated from both directions. This is the genius of Dual Reaction™—symbol arrangements that would generate one payout in traditional Megaways can generate multiple separate payouts when evaluated bidirectionally.

The Cascade Multiplier Implications

Where Dual Reaction™ becomes truly powerful is in how it interacts with the cascade mechanic and the unlimited wild multipliers. When you form wins in both directions, symbols are removed, new symbols drop in, and the process repeats. Because wins are evaluated from both directions with each cascade, you’re essentially getting two opportunities to continue the cascade sequence with every new symbol arrangement.

After First Cascade:

  • Left-to-Right win: 7 symbols removed
  • Right-to-Left win: 7 symbols removed

  • Second Cascade: New symbols drop, wild multipliers increase to 3x (both multipliers)

  • Third Cascade: New symbols drop, wild multipliers increase to 4x (both multipliers)

In this example, the bidirectional evaluation allowed the cascade sequence to continue for three cascades, building both wild multipliers up to 4x. In a traditional Megaways slot, this same symbol sequence might have only triggered one or two cascades because symbols on the right side of the grid wouldn’t have contributed to wins.

The Dual Reaction™ mechanic doesn’t just double your winning potential—it exponentially increases your multiplier-building potential, which is where the truly massive wins happen in Survivor Megaways™.

The Multiplication Effect

Here’s the crucial detail: when a winning combination benefits from both multipliers (which happens frequently due to the bidirectional evaluation), the multipliers MULTIPLY TOGETHER, not add.

Example Scenario:

  • Traditional additive: €10 × (5 + 7) = €10 × 12 = €120

The multiplicative relationship means that as both multipliers increase through continued cascades, the potential win values grow exponentially rather than linearly. This is how the game achieves its massive maximum win potential.

The multipliers only reset to 1x / 1x when the free spins feature ends. After the final free spin is played and all cascades are complete, both multipliers reset to 1x, and you return to the base game with a clean slate.

This means:

The strategic implication: if you build massive multipliers in the base game but don’t trigger free spins, you’ll eventually have to trigger the feature (or stop playing), at which point you’ll lose those accumulated multipliers. How does the Dual Reaction™ mechanic work?

Dual Reaction™ evaluates winning combinations in both directions—left to right AND right to left—on every spin. When symbols form wins in both directions, they’re paid separately, effectively doubling the win potential from certain symbol arrangements.
