The Megaways™ Engine: 117,649 Ways to Conquer

Understanding Megaways™ in Centurion

At the heart of Centurion Megaways beats the licensed Megaways™ engine, the variable-ways system that revolutionised slot mathematics. Rather than fixed paylines, every reel can display a different number of symbols on each spin, and the total ways to win are recalculated dynamically. This is what gives the game its constantly shifting rhythm and its headline figure of 117,649 ways.

How Megaways™ Works in Centurion:

  • 6 vertical reels, each capable of showing between 2 and 7 symbols per spin
  • Ways to win are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
  • Maximum ways: 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 = 117,649 ways to win
  • Minimum ways: 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 64 ways to win
  • Wins pay for matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right, regardless of their position on the reel

Win Formation Principles:

  • Symbol size and exact row position are irrelevant — only the reel sequence matters
  • A winning combination must start on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
  • Multiple separate winning combinations can land and pay on a single spin
  • Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your total stake, not a per-line bet
  • The number of ways changes every spin, so no two spins feel mathematically identical

Cascading Reactions

Like most Megaways™ slots, Centurion Megaways employs cascading reactions (sometimes called tumbles or avalanches). When you land a winning combination:

  1. The symbols that formed the win are removed from the grid
  2. The symbols above them drop down to fill the empty spaces
  3. New symbols fall in from above to complete the reels
  4. If the new arrangement creates another win, it pays and the process repeats
  5. Cascades continue until no further wins land

This mechanic means a single paid spin can produce a chain of consecutive wins, and it is especially valuable when the Maximus Winnus feature has loaded the board with a high number of ways or a swarm of matching mystery symbols. A well-timed cascade during a full 117,649-way board can be where some of the game’s most satisfying base-game payouts originate.

Maximus Winnus: The Mystery Feature That Changes Everything

One Symbol, Five Possible Rewards

The signature mechanic of Centurion Megaways is the Maximus Winnus feature, embodied by a special golden mystery symbol that can land during base-game play. What makes it so compelling is unpredictability: when Maximus Winnus activates, the game randomly selects one of five different boosts, each of which can dramatically improve your next spin or your immediate win.

The Five Maximus Winnus Outcomes:

  1. Big Win — Awards you a guaranteed minimum prize of 20x your stake. This is the instant-gratification outcome that rewards you directly without needing additional symbols to align.

  2. Maximum Ways — Guarantees that the next spin plays with the full 117,649 ways to win. With every reel locked to seven symbols, the probability of large multi-way combinations and explosive cascades soars.

  3. Minimum Ways — Sets a guaranteed minimum number of winning ways for the next spin. Even if the reels would naturally land short, you are protected from a low-ways dud spin, ensuring solid win potential.

  4. Mystery Symbol — Scatters up to 100 matching mystery symbols across the reels. When these all reveal the same paying symbol, they can combine into substantial multi-way wins, particularly when paired with cascades.

  5. Super Bonus Reels — Adds extra scatter symbols to the reels, significantly increasing your chance of triggering the main bonus round on the spin.

Why Maximus Winnus Matters:

  • Constant Variety: Because the outcome is random, no two activations feel the same
  • Multiple Win Pathways: Some outcomes pay instantly, others set up the next spin, and one pushes you toward the bonus round
  • Engagement Anchor: The feature triggers often enough to keep base-game play lively without devaluing the bonus rounds
  • Synergy with Cascades: The Maximum Ways and Mystery Symbol outcomes both feed directly into the cascading engine for chained wins

Example Maximus Winnus Scenarios:

Scenario 1 — Big Win Outcome:

  • £1.00 stake
  • Maximus Winnus reveals the Big Win boost
  • You receive a guaranteed minimum of 20x stake = £20.00 instantly

Scenario 2 — Maximum Ways Into a Cascade:

  • £1.00 stake
  • Maximus Winnus locks the next spin at 117,649 ways
  • A high-paying symbol lands across all six reels, paying a strong multi-way win
  • Cascades then drop fresh symbols, producing two further chained wins
  • The combined result far exceeds what a low-ways spin could have delivered

Scenario 3 — Mystery Symbol Swarm:

  • £1.00 stake
  • Maximus Winnus places dozens of mystery symbols that all reveal the gladiator helmet
  • The helmets line up across multiple reels for a sizeable multi-way payout

The Maximus Winnus feature is the engine room of Centurion Megaways’ base game. It is generous in frequency, rich in variety, and consistently entertaining — and it gives the slot a personality that distinguishes it from generic Megaways clones.

The Bonus Round: Choose Your Path to Glory

Triggering the Bonus

The main bonus round in Centurion Megaways is triggered by landing three or more scatter symbols anywhere on the reels. Scatters can appear naturally, but the Maximus Winnus Super Bonus Reels outcome can also add extra scatters to nudge you over the threshold. Once you trigger the bonus, you are presented with a selection screen offering three different bonus types — and the freedom to choose is part of what makes this round so engaging.

The three options are:

  • Prizes on Parade — a pick-a-prize cash bonus
  • Caesar’s Free Spins — a free spins round with a starting multiplier
  • Wild Power Spins — free spins featuring stacked wilds

Crucially, each of these comes in two tiers: a regular version and a more lucrative Super version. The Super tier is where the bigger numbers live, and the Gamble feature (covered later) is your route to upgrading from regular to Super.

Prizes on Parade: The Pick-a-Prize Bonus

Choosing Your Cash Rewards

Prizes on Parade is the instant-cash option in the bonus selector. Rather than spinning reels, you make picks to reveal cash prizes expressed as multiples of your stake. It is the lower-variance choice within the bonus round — ideal if you prefer a guaranteed payout over the swings of free spins.

How Prizes on Parade Works:

  • You are presented with a parade of Roman-themed objects to pick from
  • Each pick reveals a cash prize tied to your stake
  • The round continues until you reveal a “collect” or end marker

Prize Ranges:

  • Regular Prizes on Parade: awards between 10x and 43x your stake
  • Super Prizes on Parade: awards between 18x and 65x your stake

The Super version not only raises the ceiling but also lifts the floor — its minimum (18x) is nearly double the regular round’s minimum (10x). This makes the Gamble upgrade especially tempting when you draw the Prizes on Parade option, because the improved range is substantial and the outcome is guaranteed to pay something.

Example:

  • £1.00 stake
  • You select Super Prizes on Parade
  • Your picks reveal a combined total of 47x stake
  • Payout: £47.00

Caesar’s Free Spins: Multiplied Megaways™ Glory

Free Spins With a Building Multiplier

Caesar’s Free Spins is the headline free-spins option, combining the 117,649-way Megaways™ engine with a win multiplier that elevates every payout you land. This is the choice for players chasing the bigger end of the game’s potential, since multipliers stacked onto multi-way cascades are where serious wins are forged.

How Caesar’s Free Spins Works:

  • You are awarded a set number of free spins with a starting multiplier
  • The Megaways™ engine remains fully active, so the full 117,649 ways are in play
  • Cascading reactions during free spins can chain multiple multiplied wins together

Spin Counts and Multipliers:

  • Regular Caesar’s Free Spins: 6 to 12 free spins with a starting multiplier of 1x to 3x
  • Super Caesar’s Free Spins: 8 to 16 free spins with a starting multiplier of 1x to 5x

The Super version is meaningfully stronger on both fronts — more spins and a higher potential starting multiplier (up to 5x versus 3x). When a multiplied win lands on a high-ways board and triggers a cascade, the multiplier applies to the entire chain, which is how Caesar’s Free Spins reaches toward the game’s 12,500x ceiling.

Example:

  • £1.00 stake, Super Caesar’s Free Spins awards 12 free spins at a 4x multiplier
  • On one spin a symbol pays 30x across a high-ways board
  • With the 4x multiplier: 30x × 4 = 120x stake = £120.00 from that spin alone, before any cascade chains

Wild Power Spins: Stacked Wilds Storm the Reels

Free Spins Powered by Stacked Wilds

The third bonus option, Wild Power Spins, is a free-spins round built around stacked wild symbols. Wilds substitute for paying symbols to complete combinations, and when they arrive in stacks they can blanket entire reels, dramatically boosting the odds of landing wins across all six reels at once.

How Wild Power Spins Works:

  • A round of free spins plays out with wilds appearing in stacks on the reels
  • Stacked wilds can fill a full reel, turning it into a guaranteed contributor to any combination
  • The Super version layers win multipliers on top of the stacked-wild action

Regular vs. Super:

  • Regular Wild Power Spins: free spins with stacked wilds substituting across the reels
  • Super Wild Power Spins: the same stacked-wild action enhanced with multipliers for bigger payouts

Wild Power Spins is arguably the most volatile of the three bonus options. A single spin where stacked wilds align on multiple reels can complete enormous multi-way wins, and with the Super version’s multipliers attached, these moments are where some of the slot’s most explosive payouts occur.

Example:

  • £1.00 stake, Super Wild Power Spins
  • Stacked wilds fill reels two, three and four
  • A high-value symbol on reels one, five and six is completed across the full board for a large multi-way win, then boosted by the round’s multiplier

The Gamble Feature: Risk It for the Super Tier

Doubling Down on Your Bonus

One of the most distinctive elements of Centurion Megaways is the Gamble feature, which lets you risk your awarded bonus for the chance to upgrade it to the more generous Super version. After the bonus selector hands you a regular bonus, you can choose to lock it in safely — or gamble it.

How the Gamble Feature Works:

  1. You are awarded a regular bonus (e.g., Caesar’s Free Spins at the regular tier)
  2. The game offers you the choice to gamble for the Super version
  3. If the gamble succeeds, your bonus is upgraded to the Super tier
  4. If the gamble fails, you can lose the upgrade — and potentially the bonus itself

Strategic Considerations:

  • Risk vs. Reward: The Super tiers are significantly more valuable, so the upside is real
  • All-or-Nothing: A failed gamble is genuinely costly, so this is not a risk-free button
  • Practice in Demo: The Centurion Megaways free-play demo is the ideal place to learn the gamble’s rhythm before risking real money
  • Bonus-Dependent Value: The upgrade is most worthwhile on Prizes on Parade (guaranteed payout) and on free-spins rounds where the higher multiplier ceiling can transform results

The Gamble feature injects genuine player agency into the bonus round. It turns a passive “watch and hope” bonus into a decision point, and for many players that extra layer of control is a highlight of the game.

Symbols & Paytable

Centurion Megaways uses a classic two-tier symbol hierarchy dressed in full Roman regalia, with special symbols layered on top to drive the features.

High-Value Symbols:

  • The Centurion — the game’s namesake and top-paying symbol, depicted in full battle armour
  • Roman Shield — emblazoned with imperial insignia
  • Gladiator Helmet — a crested bronze helm
  • Laurel Wreath — the victor’s crown of ancient Rome

The premium symbols deliver the largest base-game payouts when they land across multiple reels, and they are the symbols you most want the Mystery Symbol boost to reveal.

Low-Value Symbols:

  • The traditional royal card values (A, K, Q, J, 10, 9), styled in carved stone or metallic Roman fonts to fit the theme

Special Symbols:

  • Maximus Winnus Symbol — the golden mystery symbol that triggers one of the five random base-game boosts
  • Scatter Symbol — three or more anywhere trigger the bonus selection round
  • Wild Symbol — substitutes for paying symbols (most prominent in the Wild Power Spins round, where it appears in stacks)
  • Mystery Symbol — placed in bulk by the Maximus Winnus Mystery Symbol boost, all revealing the same paying symbol

Reading the Paytable:

Because this is a Megaways™ slot, all payouts are quoted as multiples of your total stake, and wins are awarded for matching symbols on consecutive reels from the left. With up to 117,649 ways active, even mid-value symbols can produce respectable returns when they appear on every reel.

How to Win: Strategy & Tips

Centurion Megaways is a medium-volatility game, which means a sensible, patient approach pays dividends. Here is how to give yourself the best campaign.

Bankroll Management

  • Match your bet to your bankroll: With a range of £0.20 to £20.00, divide your session budget so you can comfortably absorb at least 100-150 spins. Medium volatility means features arrive at a reasonable pace, so you do not need the enormous buffer that a very-high-volatility slot demands.
  • Set win and loss limits: Decide in advance when you will walk away — both if you are ahead and if you are down. The 12,500x ceiling is a big target, but the bulk of sessions will revolve around the more frequent feature wins.
  • Treat the Gamble as optional: Because a failed gamble can cost you the entire bonus, factor its risk into your bankroll plan. Conservative players can simply decline it and still enjoy the regular bonuses.

Base Game Strategy

  • Let the Megaways™ engine do the work: There is no skill in spin outcomes, so focus on bet sizing and session length rather than chasing patterns.
  • Value the Maximus Winnus feature: This is where most of your base-game excitement lives. Outcomes like Maximum Ways and Mystery Symbol can produce strong wins, and Super Bonus Reels actively pushes you toward the bonus round.
  • Use cascades to your advantage: Remember that a single paid spin can chain into multiple wins, especially after a Maximum Ways boost loads the full 117,649 ways.

Bonus Round Strategy

  • Match the bonus to your goal: Choose Prizes on Parade for a guaranteed, lower-variance payout; pick Caesar’s Free Spins for multiplier-driven potential; select Wild Power Spins when you want the most volatile, big-hit-oriented option.
  • Weigh the Gamble carefully: The Super upgrade is genuinely worthwhile — Super Prizes on Parade nearly doubles the floor, and the free-spins rounds gain extra spins and higher multipliers — but only gamble what you are willing to lose.
  • Practice in the demo first: Use the free-play version to understand how each bonus and the gamble behave before committing real funds.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the RTP of Centurion Megaways?

Centurion Megaways has a Return to Player (RTP) of 95.9%. This sits slightly below the modern Megaways average of around 96%, so it is worth factoring into your expectations, though the game’s frequent feature activity and medium volatility keep play engaging.

What is the maximum win in Centurion Megaways?

The maximum win is 12,500x your stake. While that is more modest than some ultra-volatile Megaways titles, it remains a substantial target, most realistically approached through the Super Caesar’s Free Spins or Super Wild Power Spins rounds where multipliers compound multi-way wins.

How volatile is Centurion Megaways?

The game is rated medium volatility (3 out of 5). This means a balanced experience with reasonably frequent feature triggers and wins, making it a comfortable choice for players who dislike the long dry spells associated with very-high-volatility slots.

Does Centurion Megaways have a bonus buy?

No. Centurion Megaways does not offer a bonus buy feature, so you must trigger the bonus rounds organically by landing three or more scatters. The Maximus Winnus “Super Bonus Reels” boost can help by adding extra scatters to the reels.

What is the Maximus Winnus feature?

Maximus Winnus is the game’s signature mystery feature. When the golden mystery symbol activates, it randomly awards one of five boosts: Big Win (minimum 20x), Maximum Ways (full 117,649 ways next spin), Minimum Ways (a guaranteed minimum), Mystery Symbol (up to 100 matching symbols), or Super Bonus Reels (extra scatters).

What bonus rounds can I choose from?

When you trigger the bonus, you select from three options: Prizes on Parade (a pick-a-prize cash bonus), Caesar’s Free Spins (free spins with a starting multiplier), and Wild Power Spins (free spins with stacked wilds). Each has a more generous Super version.

How does the Gamble feature work?

The Gamble feature lets you risk your awarded bonus for the chance to upgrade it to the higher-value Super version. If the gamble succeeds you get the Super tier; if it fails you can lose the upgrade and potentially the bonus, so use it carefully.

Who makes Centurion Megaways and when was it released?

Centurion Megaways was developed by Inspired Gaming and released on July 3, 2020. It is a Megaways remake of the popular land-based Centurion slot brand widely found in UK arcades and bookmakers.
