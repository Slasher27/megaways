Set Sail for Treasure with BTG’s Pirate Adventure Megaways™

Navigate treacherous waters and claim massive doubloons in Pirate Pays Megaways™, a swashbuckling high-seas adventure from Big Time Gaming that launched on October 12, 2021. This dramatically improves your minimum possible award—you’re guaranteed to negotiate only with the better half of available outcomes, eliminating disappointing low-spin/low-multiplier combinations from consideration.

The Free Spins feature channels unlimited win potential through an increasing multiplier that rises by +1 after every single cascade throughout the entire feature. Whatever starting multiplier you negotiate in the Kraken Bonus (ranging from x1 to x10) is merely your foundation—from there, the multiplier grows persistently with no upper limit. Combined with Big Time Gaming’s cascading Megaways™ system where winning symbols are removed and replaced, sessions with consistent cascades build multipliers to x50-x100+ levels where even card symbol wins produce massive payouts.

With very high volatility, an RTP of 96.23% (95.50% variant also available), a respectable hit frequency of 37.22% (more than 1 in 3 spins produces wins), and maximum win potential of 104,400x bet, Pirate Pays Megaways™ delivers an experience that balances regular action with explosive feature potential. The Megaways™ Mechanic: 117,649 Ways to Pirate Riches

Understanding Megaways™ in Pirate Pays

Pirate Pays Megaways™ utilizes the licensed Megaways™ engine in Big Time Gaming’s standard configuration: 6 vertical reels displaying 2-7 symbols each, plus a horizontal tracker reel positioned below the main grid that displays Wild Compass symbols and regular symbols.

How Megaways™ Works:

  • 6 main vertical reels with 2-7 symbols per reel
  • Horizontal tracker reel (counts toward ways, also displays Wild Compass)
  • Ways to win calculated by multiplying symbols across all reel positions
  • Maximum ways: 7×7×7×7×7×7 = 117,649 ways to win
  • Minimum ways: 2×2×2×2×2×2 = 64 ways

Win Formation:

Wins are awarded for left-to-right adjacent symbols regardless of position:

  • Symbol size irrelevant—small and large symbols count equally
  • Only consecutive reels from left to right form winning combinations
  • Multiple winning combinations can occur simultaneously
  • All payouts multiplied by total stake
  • Cascading Reactions: Winning symbols removed, new symbols drop from above
  • Multiplier Growth: During Free Spins, +1 multiplier added after each cascade

The Horizontal Tracker Reel:

The tracker reel below the main grid serves dual functions:

  • Displays regular symbols that count toward win ways calculation
  • Wild Compass exclusive landing zone: Compasses only appear here, not on main reels
  • When Wild Compass lands, it reveals directional arrow before activating

This creates strategic significance to the tracker reel: every spin, you’re watching both for symbol combinations AND for that Wild Compass appearance that could fill entire rows or columns with Bird Wilds.

Wild Compass: Directional Bird Wild Navigation

How Wild Compass Works

The Wild Compass is Pirate Pays Megaways™‘s signature base game innovation—a feature that adds substantial win potential through directional wild placement.

Wild Compass Mechanics:

Landing:

  • Wild Compass appears exclusively on the horizontal tracker reel
  • Cannot land on the 6 main vertical reels
  • Can appear on any spin during base game and Free Spins
  • Only one Wild Compass per spin (not multiple simultaneously)

Direction Reveal:

  • Compass displays ornate gold design with directional indicators
  • When it lands, it reveals one of four directions:
    • North: Points upward
    • South: Points downward
    • East: Points right
    • West: Points left
  • Direction determines where Bird Wilds will be added

Bird Wild Placement:

Once direction is revealed, Bird Wilds (represented by the scallywag parrot) are added to all available positions in that direction:

North (Up):

  • Adds Bird Wilds to all positions directly above the Wild Compass
  • Fills the entire vertical column on whichever reel the compass landed
  • Example: Compass on tracker position 3 → Fills entire reel 3 with Bird Wilds (up to 7 wilds)

South (Down):

  • Since compass is already at bottom (tracker reel), typically no additional positions available
  • Rarely triggers meaningful wild additions
  • Least valuable direction

East (Right):

  • Adds Bird Wilds to all tracker positions to the RIGHT of the compass
  • Can create multiple wilds across the horizontal tracker
  • Example: Compass lands on tracker position 2 → Positions 3, 4, 5, 6 filled with Bird Wilds

West (Left):

  • Adds Bird Wilds to all tracker positions to the LEFT of the compass
  • Example: Compass lands on tracker position 5 → Positions 1, 2, 3, 4 filled with Bird Wilds

Bird Wild Function:

  • Substitute for ALL regular pay symbols
  • Do NOT substitute for Wild Compass or Scatter symbols (treasure chests)
  • Count as matching symbols for win calculation
  • Remain on reels until part of winning combination

Cascade Fuel:

Free Spins Enhancement:

Kraken Bonus Round: Deal-or-No-Deal Treasure Negotiation

Triggering the Kraken Bonus Round

The Kraken Bonus Round is Pirate Pays Megaways™‘s most innovative feature—a strategic Free Spins negotiation system inspired by Deal or No Deal game shows.

How to Trigger:

Landing 3 or more Scatter symbols (treasure chests) anywhere on the reels triggers the Kraken Bonus Round:

  • Gold Chest Scatter: Standard scatter appearing on any reel
  • Purple Chest Megascatter: Special scatter appearing only on Reel 6
  • 3+ total scatters (any combination) = Kraken Bonus Round activation

Scatter Requirements:

  • Minimum 3 scatters required
  • No maximum scatter limit (landing 4-6 scatters possible)
  • Scatters do NOT award direct Free Spins—they trigger negotiation round

Trigger Frequency:

With very high volatility and 37.22% hit frequency:

  • Kraken Bonus triggers approximately every 200-300 base game spins on average
  • Significant variance means 400+ spin droughts or back-to-back triggers both occur
  • 3-scatter triggers most common; 4+ scatter triggers rare but beneficial

The Kraken Bonus Negotiation Mechanic

Once triggered, a dramatic sequence unfolds where you negotiate with the kraken for your Free Spins award:

Step 1: 20 Outcomes Displayed

20 different Free Spin outcomes appear on screen, each showing:

  • Number of Free Spins: Ranging from 6 to 25 spins
  • Starting Multiplier: Ranging from x1 to x10
  • Examples: “10 Spins, x3 Multiplier” or “15 Spins, x5 Multiplier”

All 20 outcomes visible initially, creating full transparency.

Step 2: Initial Eliminations (Based on Scatter Count)

For each scatter beyond the third, one outcome is removed:

  • 3 Scatters: Zero initial eliminations (all 20 remain)
  • 4 Scatters: 1 outcome removed (19 remain)
  • 5 Scatters: 2 outcomes removed (18 remain)
  • 6 Scatters: 3 outcomes removed (17 remain)

Removed outcomes are random—not necessarily the worst ones.

Step 3: Cannon Eliminations

Cannons fire and eliminate up to 5 outcomes at random:

  • Each cannon blast removes one outcome from the screen
  • 1-5 cannons fire (random number)
  • Outcomes eliminated are random (could be good or bad)
  • Remaining outcomes still visible

Step 4: First Free Spin Offer

After initial and cannon eliminations, one of the remaining outcomes is offered:

  • Offered outcome highlighted with special effect
  • You see the exact number of spins and starting multiplier
  • Decision time: Accept or Reject

Step 5: Accept or Reject

If you ACCEPT:

  • Free Spins begin immediately with the offered spins/multiplier combination
  • No further negotiations occur
  • Feature starts with your chosen outcome

If you REJECT:

  • The rejected outcome is eliminated permanently
  • More cannons fire, removing additional outcomes
  • Another offer is presented from remaining options
  • Process repeats

Step 6: Repeated Negotiations

You can continue rejecting offers until:

  • You accept an offer (most common end)
  • Only 2 outcomes remain (forced choice)

Step 7: Final Choice (2 Outcomes Remaining)

If you reject offers until only 2 outcomes remain:

  • Two treasure chests displayed
  • You must choose one chest
  • Selected chest reveals your Free Spins award (50/50 chance)
  • No further negotiation possible

Strategic Depth:

  • Conservative players accept first reasonable offer

Transparency:

  • You see all 20 possible outcomes initially
  • You know what’s available before making decisions

  • Classic Deal-or-No-Deal tension applies perfectly

Variance Amplification:

  • Greedy rejections can lead to ending with “6 spins, x1 multiplier”
  • Lucky persistence can secure “25 spins, x10 multiplier”
  • Adds additional volatility layer beyond standard bonus RNG

Outcome Range Analysis:

Weak Outcomes (avoid if possible):

  • 6-8 spins with x1-x2 multiplier
  • Total potential: 200-500x stake typically
  • Accept only if most options eliminated and risky to continue

Moderate Outcomes (reasonable):

  • 10-15 spins with x3-x5 multiplier
  • Total potential: 500-1,500x stake typically
  • Most players accept these as solid middle-ground

Strong Outcomes (ideal):

  • 18-25 spins with x6-x10 multiplier

Optimal Strategy:

There is no mathematically “correct” strategy since eliminations are random, but general guidance:

  1. Track remaining outcomes: Note how many strong vs weak options remain
  2. First offer assessment:
    • If strong (18+ spins or x6+ multiplier): Consider accepting
    • If moderate: Reject if 10+ outcomes remain
    • If weak: Always reject unless only 2-3 outcomes left
  3. Risk tolerance alignment: Conservative players accept moderate offers early; aggressive players push to final 2 outcomes
  4. Elimination pattern: If cannons keep removing strong outcomes, accept next reasonable offer

The 10 automatically removed outcomes typically include:

The 10 outcomes available for negotiation typically include:

When it lands, it reveals a direction—North, South, East, or West—and adds Bird Wild symbols to all available positions in that direction. North (pointing up) fills the entire vertical reel above the compass with wilds (up to 7 wilds). East/West spread wilds horizontally across the tracker. South typically adds no wilds since the compass is already at the bottom. Bird Wilds substitute for all regular pay symbols, creating instant winning combinations.

What is the Kraken Bonus Round?

The Kraken Bonus Round is triggered by landing 3+ Scatter symbols (treasure chests) anywhere on the reels. It presents 20 different Free Spin outcomes on screen, each showing a specific number of spins (6-25) and starting multiplier (x1-x10). Cannons randomly eliminate some outcomes, then you’re offered one. You can accept and start Free Spins immediately, or reject and watch more outcomes get eliminated. Continue until you accept an offer or only 2 outcomes remain, at which point you choose one chest for a 50/50 final award.

What is the Enhanced Bonus Round?

The Enhanced Bonus Round activates when a Purple Chest Megascatter (appears only on Reel 6) is part of the 3+ scatter combination triggering the bonus. Before the Kraken negotiation begins, the 10 weakest Free Spin outcomes are automatically removed. This means you’ll only negotiate among the top 10 outcomes (better spins, higher multipliers), dramatically improving your minimum possible award and increasing average bonus value by approximately 40-60%.

How does the Free Spins multiplier work?

Free Spins begin with whatever starting multiplier you negotiated in the Kraken Bonus (ranging from x1 to x10).
