Understanding the Megaways™ Engine in Celestial Sisters

The Megaways™ mechanic, licensed from Big Time Gaming, forms the mathematical foundation of Celestial Sisters. Each of the six reels displays a variable number of symbols on every spin—randomly showing between 2 and 7 symbols per reel. This variability creates between 64 ways to win (when all reels show 2 symbols: 2×2×2×2×2×2) up to the maximum 117,649 ways (when all reels display 7 symbols: 7×7×7×7×7×7).

How Megaways™ Calculates Win Ways: The number of active paylines on each spin equals the product of symbol positions across all reels. If reels show 4-7-3-6-5-7 symbols respectively, you have 4×7×3×6×5×7 = 17,640 ways to win on that particular spin. This dynamic system means win potential fluctuates dramatically from spin to spin, creating an inherently volatile gameplay experience even before considering symbol values and multipliers.

Cascading Wins Mechanic: When you land a winning combination, those symbols explode and disappear from the reels. New symbols cascade down from above to fill the empty positions, potentially creating additional wins from a single spin. This cascade process continues indefinitely as long as new winning combinations form, with each successive cascade contributing to the progressive multiplier in Free Spins mode.

In Celestial Sisters, cascading wins serve a dual purpose: they extend the value of successful spins in the base game, and they become exponentially more valuable in Free Spins where each cascade adds +1 to the win multiplier. A lucky sequence of cascades during the bonus round can see multipliers climb from 1x to 10x, 15x, or even higher, applying to every win formed during that cascade chain.

Symbol Mechanics: Symbols pay left-to-right on adjacent reels, starting from the leftmost reel. You need a minimum of 3 matching symbols to form a winning combination (2 symbols for the highest-value Purple Sister premium). The wild symbol substitutes for all regular pay symbols but cannot replace the scatter symbol needed to trigger Free Spins.

Mystery Symbols: These special symbols can land on any reel during base game and Free Spins. When Mystery Symbols appear, they all transform into the same randomly-selected symbol before wins are evaluated. If multiple positions show Mystery Symbols, they all convert to matching symbols, potentially filling entire reels with identical symbols for huge win potential. Mystery Symbols can transform into any regular pay symbol but will not become wilds or scatters.

Free Spins Mechanics During the Feature

Regardless of which option you select, these core mechanics apply throughout Free Spins:

Progressive Multiplier System: The multiplier increases by +1 with every cascade, not per spin. A single spin could theoretically generate 10+ cascades, pushing your multiplier from 1x to 11x+ during a single spin’s cascade sequence. The multiplier applies to all wins formed during that cascade level, then increases for the next cascade. Critically, the multiplier never resets between spins—it maintains your accumulated value throughout the entire feature, only resetting when Free Spins end.

Mystery Symbols in Free Spins: Mystery Symbols continue to appear during the bonus round and can transform into matching symbols just as in the base game. When Mystery Symbols land during Free Spins, they transform before the multiplier is applied, meaning a full reel of Mystery Symbols converting to the Purple Sister premium could deliver 50x bet per payline, then multiplied by your current progressive multiplier for massive potential.

Retrigger Possibility: Landing 3+ additional scatter symbols during Free Spins awards extra spins based on your original selection. If you chose the 15-spin option, retriggering awards +15 spins. The 10-spin option grants +10, and the 5-spin option adds +5 more spins. Your accumulated multiplier carries forward when spins are added, maintaining momentum rather than resetting progress.

Pot System During Free Spins: Special overlay symbols can appear during Free Spins that contribute to the Pot System jackpot ladder. Collecting these symbols fills meters corresponding to Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum pots. Completing any pot tier immediately awards that fixed prize:

  • Bronze Pot: 20x bet
  • Silver Pot: 100x bet
  • Gold Pot: 500x bet
  • Platinum Pot: 10,000x bet

The Platinum Pot award of 10,000x bet represents the game’s maximum win potential and requires filling the entire pot progression ladder during a single Free Spins session.

Understanding Medium-High Volatility in Celestial Sisters

Volatility (also called variance) measures how wins distribute over time. Celestial Sisters’ medium-high volatility rating indicates a balance between frequent small wins and occasional large payouts, with a slight bias toward the latter.

This hit frequency means you’ll experience 1-2 consecutive losing spins regularly, followed by a winning spin, creating a rhythm where your balance decreases gradually between hits rather than plummeting rapidly (high volatility) or remaining relatively stable (low volatility).

Win Size Distribution: Most wins (approximately 70-75%) will return less than your bet amount—typically 0.3x to 0.8x bet from low-value symbol combinations or partial paylines.
