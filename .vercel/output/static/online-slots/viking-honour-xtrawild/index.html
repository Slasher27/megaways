Raise Your Shield: Set Sail With Viking Honour XtraWild

Sharpen your axe and take your place at the oars, because Viking Honour XtraWild is calling you north. Released on 23 February 2022, this Norse raiding adventure from Swintt is built around one of the studio’s signature “Xtra” mechanics — the XtraWild system, a wild-selector engine that guarantees a wild-transformed premium symbol on every single base-game spin and then sends it marching across the reels like a warrior on campaign. If you have sailed with Swintt’s other Xtra-series titles, such as Aloha Spirit XtraLock, you will recognise the philosophy: take a classic reel layout and bolt on one clever, ever-present twist that changes how every spin plays out.

It is worth saying plainly up front: despite living alongside the Megaways giants in our collection, Viking Honour XtraWild is not a Megaways slot. There is no variable-reel engine and no six-figure ways count here. Instead, the game plays out on a traditional 5-reel, 3-row grid with 30 fixed paylines — a deliberately old-school battlefield on which the XtraWild mechanic can shine. Wins form left to right on adjacent reels along those 30 lines, and the constantly repositioning wilds do the heavy lifting.

The atmosphere is superbly judged. The reels sit within a carved stone slab crowned by snarling dragon-ship figureheads, set against a dark, storm-brooding Scandinavian backdrop. Helmets, shields and battle axes populate the paytable, while the soundtrack swings between ominous northern dread and rousing raid-day energy. It is a moodier, more grounded take on the Viking theme than many of its cartoonish rivals.

Under the hood, Viking Honour XtraWild carries a default RTP of 96.02%, medium volatility, and a maximum win of 6,261x your stake, with bets running from £0.30 to £30.00 per spin. The headline feature ladder is short but purposeful: the XtraWild wild selector rules the base game, and a free spins round of 8, 15 or 25 spins upgrades those wilds into full expanding reel wilds that continue their march across the grid.

Whether you are drawn by the novelty of a guaranteed wild on every spin, the hypnotic rhythm of walking wilds, or simply the pull of a well-crafted Norse saga, Viking Honour XtraWild offers a distinctive alternative to the cascading ways-to-win formula. Not Megaways — Something Different: The 30-Payline Battlefield

How Wins Form in Viking Honour XtraWild

Regular readers of this site know their way around variable-reel Megaways mathematics, so let’s be clear about what Viking Honour XtraWild is instead. This is a fixed-format slot: five reels, three rows, and 30 predetermined paylines that never change from spin to spin.

The Fundamentals:

  • Wins require matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right, starting on reel one
  • Symbols must land on one of the 30 fixed payline patterns — position matters here, unlike in a ways-to-win game
  • There are no cascades or tumbles; each spin is a single, self-contained event
  • Payouts are awarded per line, with your 30-line stake spread across the board

Why the Fixed Format Works Here:

The XtraWild mechanic is fundamentally positional — wilds land, hold their ground, and then walk one reel to the right each spin. That choreography only makes sense on a stable grid. On a shifting Megaways board the walking wild concept would dissolve; on this 5×3 battlefield, you can track every wild’s march and anticipate exactly where it will stand on the next spin. The result is a slower, more readable rhythm than cascade-driven games — closer to a game of strategy on a war table than an avalanche.

If you arrive expecting 117,649 ways and chained tumbles, recalibrate. Viking Honour XtraWild trades raw combinatorial chaos for a persistent, predictable wild presence that touches literally every spin you play.

The XtraWild Feature: A Wild on Every Single Spin

How the Wild Selector Works

The XtraWild mechanic is the heart, soul and namesake of this game, and its core promise is remarkable in its simplicity: every base-game spin features a wild-transformed symbol. No trigger conditions, no lucky landings required — the feature is always on.

The XtraWild Sequence:

  1. Before each spin, the Wild Selector chooses one of the game’s top-paying symbols (the selection is displayed beside the reels)
  2. When that symbol lands on the grid, it is transformed into a wild, substituting for all regular paying symbols
  3. On the next spin, that wild walks one reel to the right, holding its row position
  4. The wild continues marching rightward, one reel per spin, until it exits the grid past reel five
  5. Meanwhile, a new symbol is selected every spin, so fresh wilds keep entering the fray

Why This Matters:

  • Constant wild presence: Most slots make you wait for wilds; here they are woven into every spin
  • Multiple simultaneous wilds: Because selections refresh each spin while older wilds are still walking, you can have several wilds marching across the grid at once
  • Escalating board states: A run of well-placed selections can gradually fill the middle reels with wilds, setting up strong multi-line hits
  • Readable anticipation: You always know where existing wilds will stand next spin, which gives the base game an unusual layer of foresight

Example XtraWild Sequence:

Spin 1:

  • The Wild Selector chooses the Viking helmet
  • A helmet lands on reel two, middle row, and transforms into a wild

Spin 2:

  • The reel-two wild walks to reel three, middle row
  • The selector now picks the battle axe; an axe lands on reel one and becomes a second wild
  • Two wilds are now on the board simultaneously

Spin 3:

  • The first wild marches to reel four, the second to reel two
  • A shield combination lands and both wilds substitute, completing two separate line wins across the grid

This drip-feed of walking wilds is what gives Viking Honour XtraWild its identity. The base game never goes fully cold, because every spin either introduces a new wild, advances an existing one, or both.

Free Spins: The Wilds Go to War

Triggering the Bonus

The free spins round is triggered by landing three, four or five scatter symbols anywhere on the reels:

  • 3 scatters award 8 free spins
  • 4 scatters award 15 free spins
  • 5 scatters award 25 free spins

A five-scatter, 25-spin trigger is a rare and genuinely valuable event, but even the baseline eight spins can deliver, because of what happens to the wilds once the bonus begins.

Expanding Walking Wilds

During free spins, the XtraWild mechanic is promoted from soldier to jarl. The rules change in two crucial ways:

  1. Wilds expand to cover their entire reel. Instead of occupying a single position, each wild blankets all three rows of its reel, turning it into a guaranteed contributor to every payline passing through it.

  2. The expanded reels keep walking. Just as in the base game, each full-reel wild shifts one reel to the right on every subsequent free spin until it marches off the grid — and new selections keep arriving behind it.

There is a third, subtler benefit: the symbol that was selected as wild at the moment the bonus triggered remains wild throughout the free spins round, giving you a persistent head start on top of the expanding reels.

Why the Bonus Is Where the Gold Is:

  • A full wild reel completes every line win that crosses it, dramatically raising the value of ordinary symbol landings
  • With new wilds selected as older ones march on, it is possible to have two or even three fully wild reels on the board at once
  • When multiple adjacent reels turn wild while premium symbols land around them, the game’s biggest payouts — up the slope toward the 6,261x maximum — come into view
  • Longer bonus rounds (15 or 25 spins) give the walking-wild conveyor belt more time to produce overlapping wild reels

Example Free Spins Sequence:

  • You trigger the bonus with four scatters: 15 free spins
  • Spin 3: a selected symbol lands on reel two and expands into a full wild reel
  • Spin 4: the wild reel walks to reel three; a new wild lands on reel one and expands
  • Spin 5: with reels one (walking toward two) and three fully wild, a high-value helmet line across the remaining reels pays across multiple lines at once — the kind of stacked moment the bonus is built for

The free spins round is short on gimmicks — no multiplier ladders, no pick screens — but the expanding-walking-wild combination is a genuinely satisfying escalation of the base mechanic rather than a bolted-on afterthought.

Individual symbol values are not prominently published outside the in-game paytable, so check the game’s own info screen for exact line payouts at your stake. The practical takeaway is straightforward: the top-paying Viking gear symbols are doubly valuable here, both as line-win payers and as the raw material the XtraWild selector turns into wilds.
