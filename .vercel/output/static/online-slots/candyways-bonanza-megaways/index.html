The Megaways™ Engine: 512 Ways in a Compact Grid

Understanding Megaways™ in Candyways Bonanza

At the core of Candyways Bonanza Megaways is the licensed Megaways™ mechanic, but in a smaller, faster form than the six-reel giants most players know. Instead of fixed paylines, each reel can display a different number of symbols on every spin, and the total ways to win are recalculated dynamically.

How Megaways™ Works in Candyways Bonanza:

  • 3 vertical reels, each capable of showing between 3 and 8 symbols per spin
  • Ways to win are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
  • Maximum ways: 8 × 8 × 8 = 512 ways to win
  • Wins pay for matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right, regardless of their exact position on the reel

Win Formation Principles:

  • Only the reel sequence matters — symbol size and exact row position are irrelevant
  • A winning combination must start on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
  • Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your total stake, not a per-line bet
  • Because the symbol count shifts each spin, the number of active ways changes constantly

The 512-ways figure is modest next to the 117,649 ways of a full-size Megaways slot, and that is an honest limitation to acknowledge. The trade-off is pace and clarity: with only three reels to track, Candyways Bonanza Megaways plays quickly and stays easy on the eye, making it well suited to mobile and to players who find the six-reel grids overwhelming.

Tumbling Reels

Like most Megaways™ slots, Candyways Bonanza Megaways uses a tumble (cascade) mechanic on its winning spins:

  1. The symbols that formed a winning combination are removed from the grid
  2. The remaining symbols drop down to fill the gaps
  3. Fresh symbols fall in from above to complete the reels
  4. If the new arrangement creates another win, it pays and the process repeats
  5. Tumbles continue until no further wins land

This means a single paid spin can chain into several consecutive wins, and in a candy-themed slot the satisfying “pop” of dissolving sweets gives every tumble a pleasing sense of momentum.

Free Spins: Where the Sugar Rush Begins

Triggering the Bonus Round

The headline attraction in Candyways Bonanza Megaways is its Free Spins round. You trigger it by landing three or more bonus symbols anywhere on the reels, which awards an initial 10 free spins. This is the moment the game truly comes alive, because the base game is comparatively quiet — almost everything that makes this slot exciting is packed into the bonus.

How the Free Spins Round Works:

  • Three or more bonus symbols trigger 10 free spins
  • During the round, special multiplier symbols can land on the reels
  • Each multiplier symbol reveals a value, and those values are collected and held
  • The collected multiplier is then applied to your wins as the round progresses

Because the round is built around multipliers rather than wilds, the size of your free spins payout is determined almost entirely by how many multiplier symbols you land and how high their values climb.

Accumulating Multipliers: The Engine of the Big Win

Stacking 2x to 100x for a 20,000x Ceiling

The defining feature of Candyways Bonanza Megaways is its accumulating multiplier system. Throughout the free spins round, bonus symbols reveal multiplier values ranging from 2x all the way up to 100x. Crucially, these multipliers do not reset between spins — they accumulate, building a running total that is applied to your wins as the round unfolds.

How the Multipliers Work:

  • Multiplier symbols can land during free spins, each carrying a value between 2x and 100x
  • Collected multiplier values add together and persist for the remainder of the round
  • The accumulated multiplier is applied to qualifying wins, magnifying every payout
  • The longer you keep landing multiplier symbols, the larger your running multiplier grows

Retriggers and Additional Spins:

  • Landing three or more multiplier symbols during the round awards extra free spins
  • More spins means more chances to collect multipliers, which in turn feeds the accumulating total
  • This loop — collect multipliers, win spins, collect more multipliers — is how the round snowballs

This compounding structure is exactly why Candyways Bonanza Megaways can reach its 20,000x maximum win. A long free spins run, fed by repeated retriggers and topped up with high-value multiplier symbols, is the only realistic route to the game’s ceiling. It is a classic high-reward setup: most bonus rounds will pay something modest, but the rare run where the multipliers and retriggers align can produce a colossal result.

The absence of wild symbols means the multiplier mechanic carries the entire weight of the game’s win potential — but it carries it well, and the accumulating design gives the free spins a genuine sense of building tension.

Super Stake: Boost Your Bonus Chances

Paying More to Trigger More

Candyways Bonanza Megaways includes a Super Stake side bet, a popular Stakelogic feature. Activating Super Stake increases your stake by 50% in exchange for a higher chance of landing the bonus symbols that trigger the free spins round.

How Super Stake Works:

  • Toggle Super Stake on to add 50% to your total stake (so a £10 max bet becomes £15)
  • In return, the game boosts the frequency of bonus symbols appearing on the reels
  • This raises your chance of triggering the lucrative free spins round
  • Super Stake does not alter the paytable values of the regular symbols

Strategic Notes:

  • Super Stake is a higher-cost, higher-engagement way to play that suits players hunting for the bonus
  • Because it does not change base-game symbol payouts, its value lies purely in faster bonus access
  • It pairs naturally with the medium-high volatility profile, where the free spins are where the real money is

For players who find the base game too quiet, Super Stake is a sensible lever — but it raises your cost per spin, so factor it into your bankroll plan.
