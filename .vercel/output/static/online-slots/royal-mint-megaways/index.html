Game Features

Triple Reaction™

The crown jewel of Royal Mint Megaways™ is the Triple Reaction™ mechanic, an innovative evolution of cascading wins that adds a thrilling three-dimensional element to every winning combination. This feature fundamentally changes how reactions work in Megaways™ slots, creating extended win sequences with spectacular multiplier growth.

How Triple Reaction™ Works:

When you land a winning combination, all winning symbols except scatters trigger a chain reaction with three distinct components:

1. Traditional Cascade (From Above):

  • Winning symbols are removed from the reels
  • New symbols cascade down from above to fill empty positions
  • This is the standard cascading mechanic familiar to Megaways™ players

2. Extra Reels (From the Right):

  • Additional symbols appear from the right side on the Extra Reels
  • These Extra Reels are positioned to the right of the main grid
  • They introduce fresh symbols into the play area

3. Dual-Position Extra Symbols:

  • The Extra Reels add new symbols both above AND below reels 2, 3, 4, and 5
  • This creates two rows of additional symbols
  • These positions can generate new winning combinations

Visual Layout:

The game features a main 6×7 grid with Extra Reels positioned to the right. When reactions occur, symbols cascade down on the main reels while simultaneously, the Extra Reels introduce symbols that appear in positions flanking the middle four reels, creating a dynamic multi-directional cascade effect.

Strategic Impact:

Triple Reaction™ significantly increases the potential for extended win chains compared to traditional cascading mechanics:

  • More Symbol Positions: Each reaction brings in symbols from multiple directions, creating more opportunities for new wins
  • Multiplier Building: Every reaction increases the win multiplier (in Free Spins), and Triple Reaction™ generates more reactions
  • Win Potential: The three-dimensional cascade can transform modest wins into spectacular payouts through extended reaction sequences

Base Game + Free Spins:

Triple Reaction™ operates in both base game and Free Spins, but its true power emerges during bonus rounds where unlimited multipliers apply to every win. A single initial win can trigger 5, 10, or even 15+ consecutive reactions as symbols pour in from above and the right, with each reaction building the multiplier higher.

This mechanic is what distinguishes Royal Mint from standard Megaways™ slots – it’s not just about cascading wins, it’s about multi-directional symbol infusion that keeps reactions flowing and multipliers climbing.

Gold Bar Collection System

Royal Mint Megaways™ features a unique progressive collection mechanic that runs throughout your entire base game session, rewarding persistent play with access to the game’s most powerful feature: Heartstopper™ Enhanced Free Spins.

How Collection Works:

Scatter Appearances:

  • During base game play, 3 scatter symbols can land in any position
  • These scatters are Golden Mint symbols featuring the game’s logo on a purple background

Gold Bar Rewards:

  • Each time you land exactly 3 scatters, you’re awarded 1 Gold Bar
  • The Gold Bar is added to your collection meter
  • Collection persists throughout your entire play session

Collection Target:

  • Collect 40 Gold Bars to unlock a permanent enhancement
  • Upon reaching 40 bars, an extra scatter is added to EVERY spin until your next feature trigger
  • This extra scatter dramatically increases your chances of landing 4+ scatters

Enhanced Trigger:

  • With 4+ scatters, you trigger Heartstopper™ Enhanced Free Spins
  • This premium bonus features doubled multiplier values (starts at x2, increases by +2)
  • Significantly superior to regular Free Spins

Strategic Implications:

The Gold Bar collection system adds several interesting dimensions to gameplay:

  1. Progressive Power: The longer you play, the closer you get to the enhanced feature
  2. Session Value: Extended sessions have increasing value as you approach 40 bars
  3. Risk/Reward: Do you continue playing to reach 40 bars, or are you satisfied with regular bonuses?
  4. Anticipation: Knowing you’re close to Heartstopper™ Enhanced adds excitement to every spin

Practical Timeline:

Collecting 40 Gold Bars requires landing 3 scatters 40 times in the base game. Given scatter frequency, this typically requires several hundred to over a thousand base game spins, making it a long-term goal that rewards patient, persistent players. However, once achieved, that extra scatter on every spin makes Heartstopper™ Enhanced Free Spins significantly more accessible.

Free Spins

The standard Free Spins feature in Royal Mint Megaways™ provides access to the high-security vault where unlimited multipliers turn winning reactions into potentially life-changing payouts.

Triggering:

  • Land 4 Golden Mint scatter symbols in any position during base game
  • Each scatter beyond the 4th adds +4 extra Free Spins
  • Example: 4 scatters = 12 spins, 5 scatters = 16 spins, 6 scatters = 20 spins

Starting Conditions:

  • Begin with 12 Free Spins (base amount)
  • Win multiplier starts at x1
  • Triple Reaction™ is active throughout
  • Extra Reels continue introducing symbols from the right

Unlimited Multiplier Progression:

The multiplier is what makes Free Spins extraordinarily powerful:

  1. Initial wins are multiplied by x1
  2. After the first reaction, multiplier increases to x2
  3. After the second reaction, multiplier increases to x3
  4. Pattern continues: +1 to multiplier after every reaction
  5. No cap exists – multiplier can climb infinitely

Example Multiplier Sequence:

  • Initial win: 10x stake × x1 multiplier = 10x payout
  • First reaction: 8x stake × x2 multiplier = 16x payout
  • Second reaction: 12x stake × x3 multiplier = 36x payout
  • Third reaction: 15x stake × x4 multiplier = 60x payout
  • Fourth reaction: 20x stake × x5 multiplier = 100x payout
  • Total from this win chain: 222x stake!

Retriggering:

During Free Spins, Mint scatters appear exclusively on the Extra Reels (the right-side reels that feed symbols during reactions):

  • Land 3 scatters during Free Spins = +4 additional spins
  • Each scatter beyond the 3rd adds +4 more spins
  • Multiplier persists through retriggering
  • Retriggering with an already-built multiplier (e.g., x8) creates massive potential

Strategic Highlights:

Free Spins win potential is directly tied to reaction chains. A bonus round with one 15-reaction sequence will dramatically outperform one with frequent small wins that don’t cascade. The Triple Reaction™ mechanic’s multi-directional symbol introduction significantly increases the probability of extended reaction chains compared to traditional cascading slots.
