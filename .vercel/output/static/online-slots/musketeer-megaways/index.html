The Megaways™ Engine: 117,649 Ways to Duel

Understanding Megaways™ in Musketeer

At the heart of Musketeer Megaways beats the licensed Megaways™ engine, the variable-ways system that revolutionised slot mathematics. Rather than fixed paylines, every reel can display a different number of symbols on each spin, and the total ways to win are recalculated dynamically. This is what gives the game its constantly shifting rhythm and its headline figure of 117,649 ways.

How Megaways™ Works in Musketeer:

  • 6 vertical reels, each capable of showing between 2 and 7 symbols per spin
  • Ways to win are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
  • Maximum ways: 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 = 117,649 ways to win
  • Minimum ways: 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 64 ways to win
  • Wins pay for matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right, regardless of their position on the reel

Win Formation Principles:

  • Symbol size and exact row position are irrelevant — only the reel sequence matters
  • A winning combination must start on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
  • Multiple separate winning combinations can land and pay on a single spin
  • Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your total stake, not a per-line bet
  • The number of ways changes every spin, so no two spins feel mathematically identical

Cascading Reactions

Like most Megaways™ slots, Musketeer Megaways employs cascading reactions (sometimes called tumbles or avalanches). When you land a winning combination:

  1. The symbols that formed the win are removed from the grid
  2. The symbols above them drop down to fill the empty spaces
  3. New symbols fall in from above to complete the reels
  4. If the new arrangement creates another win, it pays and the process repeats
  5. Cascades continue until no further wins land

This mechanic is the lifeblood of Musketeer Megaways, because the game’s all-important win multiplier rises with every cascade. A single paid spin can chain into a sequence of consecutive wins, and during free spins each link in that chain is worth more than the last as the multiplier climbs.

Quad Action: 1ForAll vs All4One

Two Ways to Play One Slot

The defining innovation of Musketeer Megaways is its Quad Action system — iSoftBet’s clever twist that lets you choose between two completely different gameplay experiences before you spin. The mode you select changes the layout, the betting range, the volatility profile and even the maximum win on offer.

1ForAll Mode — The Single-Grid Experience:

  • Plays out on a single Megaways™ reel set with up to 117,649 ways
  • RTP of 95.53% and a betting range of £0.20 to £20.00
  • Carries the bigger top prize: a 33,980x maximum win
  • Includes two exclusive base-game modifiers — Lose to Win and Max Megaways
  • This is the classic, familiar Megaways™ format and the natural starting point for most players

All4One Mode — The Four-Grid Quad Action:

  • Plays across four simultaneous reel sets at once, quadrupling the on-screen action
  • A slightly higher RTP of 95.99% but a higher minimum stake to cover the four grids
  • A lower individual max win of 19,507x, balanced by four chances at a result every spin
  • Free spins activate across all four grids, each tracking its own independent multiplier
  • A special symbol can sync all four multipliers to the highest value, a genuinely explosive moment

Choosing Your Mode:

  • Play 1ForAll if you want the traditional single-grid feel, the lower entry stake, and the higher 33,980x ceiling
  • Play All4One if you crave maximum spectacle, the higher RTP, and the thrill of four grids racing toward synced multipliers

This dual-mode design is what gives Musketeer Megaways its identity. It is rare to find a single slot that genuinely offers two different mathematical experiences under one roof, and experimenting with both is half the appeal.

The Unlimited Win Multiplier

A Multiplier With No Ceiling

The standout reward mechanic in Musketeer Megaways is the unlimited win multiplier that powers the free spins round. Unlike many Megaways™ slots that cap their multipliers, here the multiplier rises by 1x after every winning cascade and never stops climbing for the duration of the bonus.

How the Multiplier Works:

  • The multiplier begins at 1x when free spins start
  • Each time a win cascades, the multiplier increases by +1x
  • The multiplier does not reset between free spins — it carries forward and keeps growing
  • There is no upper limit, so a hot streak of cascades can drive it to enormous values
  • In All4One mode, each of the four grids tracks its own multiplier, and a sync symbol can lift them all to the highest current value

Why It Matters:

  • Compounding Potential: Because the multiplier persists and only ever rises, the later spins of a bonus can be exponentially more valuable than the early ones
  • Cascade Synergy: The more cascades you trigger, the faster the multiplier climbs — and the bigger every subsequent win becomes
  • Path to the Max Win: The game’s 33,980x ceiling is realistically only reachable when a high multiplier lands on top of strong multi-way cascades during free spins

Example:

  • £1.00 stake, free spins in progress
  • The multiplier has climbed to 15x after a run of cascades
  • A symbol pays 40x across a high-ways board
  • With the 15x multiplier: 40x × 15 = 600x stake = £600.00 from that single win, before any further cascades push the multiplier even higher

The unlimited multiplier is the engine of Musketeer Megaways’ biggest moments. It rewards patience and momentum, and it is the single most important reason the free spins round is where the game truly comes alive.

Free Spins: Charge Into the Bonus

Triggering the Round

The free spins round in Musketeer Megaways is triggered by landing enough bonus (scatter) symbols — represented by the musketeer logo — anywhere on the reels. Once triggered, you are awarded a starting allocation of free spins, with additional scatters granting extra spins on top.

How Free Spins Work:

  • Landing the required number of bonus symbols launches the round
  • Each extra bonus symbol beyond the trigger adds further free spins
  • The unlimited win multiplier is active throughout and climbs with every cascade
  • In All4One mode, the free spins play across all four grids simultaneously, each with its own multiplier

The free spins round is the heart of the game’s win potential. Because the multiplier never resets and only rises, the longer your cascades chain and the more spins you accumulate, the more dramatically your payouts can escalate. Retriggering by landing further bonus symbols extends the round and gives the multiplier even more room to grow.

Example:

  • £1.00 stake in 1ForAll mode
  • You trigger free spins and a strong run of cascades lifts the multiplier to 20x
  • A 50x multi-way win lands and is boosted to 50x × 20 = 1,000x stake = £1,000.00

Mystery Symbols

Transforming the Reels

Adding another layer of unpredictability, mystery symbols can land randomly on any spin in both the base game and free spins. When they appear, each mystery symbol transforms into the same regular paying symbol, often revealing a matching set that combines into fresh winning combinations.

How Mystery Symbols Work:

  • Mystery symbols can appear randomly on the reels at any time
  • When they reveal, they all turn into a single matching pay symbol
  • This can suddenly create multi-way wins that were not present before the transformation
  • The transformed wins then cascade like any other, contributing to the multiplier during free spins

Why They Matter:

  • Instant Win Creation: A cluster of mystery symbols revealing a high-value symbol can manufacture a substantial win from nowhere
  • Cascade Fuel: Because the resulting wins cascade, mystery symbols help drive the multiplier higher during the bonus
  • Base-Game Excitement: They keep standard spins lively even before you reach the free spins round

Mystery symbols are a smart complement to the unlimited multiplier, since the wins they create feed straight into the cascading engine and help build momentum during the all-important free spins.

Base-Game Modifiers: Lose to Win & Max Megaways

Exclusive to 1ForAll Mode

The 1ForAll mode includes two extra random base-game modifiers that can trigger on standard spins, giving the single-grid format some additional spice.

Lose to Win:

  • This modifier can intervene on a spin that would otherwise miss
  • It is designed to guarantee a win on the spin, turning a dead spin into a paying one
  • A welcome safety net during the medium-high-volatility base game

Max Megaways:

  • This modifier locks the spin at the full 117,649 ways to win
  • With every reel showing seven symbols, the probability of large multi-way combinations and explosive cascades soars
  • It is one of the most exciting base-game outcomes, offering a glimpse of the engine at full power

These modifiers are exclusive to 1ForAll mode and are part of what makes that format distinct from the four-grid All4One experience. They add variety to standard spins and occasionally deliver a meaningful base-game payout.

Bonus Buy

Skipping Straight to the Action

For players who prefer not to wait for the free spins to arrive organically, Musketeer Megaways offers a bonus buy option. This lets you purchase direct entry into the free spins round for a set multiple of your stake, taking you straight to the unlimited-multiplier action.

Bonus Buy Considerations:

  • Instant Gratification: You skip the base game and head straight to the feature where the biggest wins live
  • Higher Cost, Higher Risk: The purchase price is a multiple of your stake, so it is a high-variance decision
  • Check Local Availability: Bonus buy is restricted or unavailable in some jurisdictions (including the UK), so it may not appear in every casino lobby
  • Budget Carefully: Because the multiplier-driven bonus is volatile, a purchased round can still come up short — never buy more than you can afford to lose

The bonus buy suits players who specifically want to chase the free spins and the unlimited multiplier without grinding the base game. As always, it should be used responsibly and only by those comfortable with its higher-risk profile.

What is the maximum win in Musketeer Megaways?

The maximum win is 33,980x your stake in 1ForAll mode and 19,507x in All4One mode. The 1ForAll ceiling in particular is a substantial five-figure-multiplier target, most realistically approached when a high unlimited multiplier lands on top of strong multi-way cascades during free spins.

What is the difference between 1ForAll and All4One mode?

1ForAll plays on a single Megaways™ grid with a 95.53% RTP, a 33,980x max win, and two exclusive modifiers (Lose to Win and Max Megaways). During free spins, an unlimited win multiplier starts at 1x and rises by 1x after every winning cascade. It never resets between spins and has no cap, so a strong run of cascades can drive it to very high values — which is where the game’s biggest wins are forged.
