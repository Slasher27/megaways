The Game Is Afoot: Sleuthing for Gold in Shamrock Holmes Megaways

Grab your magnifying glass and follow the rainbow, because Shamrock Holmes Megaways turns the hunt for leprechaun gold into a full-blown detective case. Released on December 8, 2020, this six-reel adventure from All41 Studios, an independent partner studio on the Microgaming platform, holds a special place in Megaways history: it was the first Megaways™ title to arrive on Microgaming’s network, bringing the famous variable-ways engine and up to 117,649 ways to win to one of the industry’s biggest platforms for the very first time.

The premise is as playful as it sounds. Shamrock Holmes himself — a pipe-puffing leprechaun in a deerstalker cap — patrols an enchanted Irish forest with his pixie assistant, investigating the whereabouts of the pot of gold at the rainbow’s end. The artwork strikes a lovely balance between storybook cartoon and genuinely mystical Gaelic atmosphere: glowing toadstools, tangled ancient trees and drifting fireflies frame the reels, while a lilting Celtic soundtrack keeps the mood light. It is a refreshing take on the Irish theme, which so often defaults to lazy clichés.

Beneath the whimsy sits a serious Megaways machine. The base game is powered by Rolling Reels™ cascades and a signature Magic Forest Respins feature that can lock in a guaranteed number of Megaways — up to the full 117,649 — for a respin. The main event is a free spins bonus with an unlimited progressive multiplier that starts at 2x and climbs by one with every winning cascade, never resetting for the duration of the round. That mechanic is the engine behind the game’s headline figure: a maximum win of 28,000x your stake.

The numbers back up the spectacle. Shamrock Holmes Megaways offers an RTP of 96.44%, comfortably above the modern Megaways average, alongside medium-high volatility and an unusually generous hit frequency of 35.34% — so more than a third of spins land a win. Stakes run from £0.20 to £20.00 per spin, making the forest accessible to casual players and committed gold-hunters alike.

Whether you are drawn by the historic status of Microgaming’s first Megaways slot, the guaranteed-ways respins, or the prospect of an ever-growing multiplier chasing that 28,000x ceiling, there is plenty to investigate here. Let’s open the case file and examine every mechanic in detail.

The Megaways™ Engine: 117,649 Ways Through the Forest

Understanding Megaways™ in Shamrock Holmes

Shamrock Holmes Megaways runs on the licensed Megaways™ engine pioneered by Big Time Gaming — the variable-ways system in which every reel displays a different number of symbols on every spin, and the total ways to win are recalculated dynamically. Its arrival here was genuinely significant: this was the first time Microgaming’s vast platform carried the mechanic, with All41 Studios handling development duties.

How Megaways™ Works in Shamrock Holmes:

  • 6 vertical reels, each showing between 2 and 7 symbols per spin
  • An extra horizontal reel sits above reels two, three, four and five, adding four more symbols to every spin
  • Ways to win are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
  • Maximum ways: 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 = 117,649 ways to win
  • Wins pay for matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right, regardless of position

Win Formation Principles:

  • Symbol size and exact row position are irrelevant — only the reel sequence matters
  • A winning combination must begin on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
  • Multiple separate winning combinations can land and pay on a single spin
  • Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your total stake
  • The ways count changes on every spin, so the grid never feels static

The Extra Top Reel

The horizontal tracker reel above the main grid is more than decoration. It contributes one symbol each to reels two through five, effectively deepening those middle reels — and it is the exclusive home of two crucial symbols. The rainbow Wild appears only here, substituting for all paying symbols when it lands. The top reel is also where retrigger scatters land during free spins, so your eyes will be drawn upward at the most dramatic moments.

Rolling Reels™: The Cascade Mechanic

Shamrock Holmes uses Microgaming’s long-running Rolling Reels™ system — its house version of the cascading wins found across the Megaways genre. When you land a winning combination:

  1. The symbols that formed the win are removed from the grid
  2. Symbols above tumble down to fill the gaps
  3. Fresh symbols roll in from above to complete the reels
  4. If the new arrangement forms another win, it pays and the process repeats
  5. Rolling continues until a spin produces no further wins

In the base game, Rolling Reels™ simply extend your winning chains. In the free spins round they become the beating heart of the whole game, because every winning cascade pushes the progressive multiplier one step higher — and that multiplier never resets during the bonus.

Magic Forest Respins: Guaranteed Megaways on Demand

The Signature Base-Game Feature

The most distinctive clue in this case file is the Magic Forest Respins feature — a base-game mechanic that hands you a respin with a guaranteed minimum number of Megaways, sidestepping the usual lottery of the variable-ways engine. On a normal spin you might land a stingy 64-way board; during a Magic Forest Respin, a huge ways count is locked in before the reels even move.

How Magic Forest Respins Trigger:

The feature activates when three scatter symbols land in combination with special character symbols on the extra top reel. The character that joins the scatters determines which tier of respin you receive:

  1. Pixie Respin — three scatters plus the pixie symbol award a respin with a guaranteed 10,000 Megaways
  2. Shamrock Holmes Respin — three scatters plus the Shamrock Holmes symbol award a respin with a guaranteed 32,400 Megaways
  3. Full Board Respin — three scatters plus both Holmes and the pixie award a respin with the maximum 117,649 Megaways

Why Magic Forest Respins Matter:

  • Guaranteed Potential: A locked-in ways count removes the worst-case low-ways outcomes entirely
  • Cascade Synergy: A 117,649-way board feeding into Rolling Reels™ can chain several substantial wins from one respin
  • Consolation Value: Landing three scatters normally means just missing the four-scatter free spins trigger — the respin turns that near-miss into a reward
  • Base-Game Excitement: The feature gives ordinary spins a second layer of anticipation whenever scatters start appearing

Example Magic Forest Scenario:

  • £1.00 stake
  • Three scatters land alongside both Shamrock Holmes and the pixie on the top reel
  • The respin plays out with the full 117,649 ways locked in
  • A premium symbol connects across all six reels for a large multi-way win
  • Rolling Reels™ then chain two further wins from the same respin

The three-tier structure is elegant: even the entry-level 10,000-way respin is far above the average natural board, while the full-board version is effectively a free shot at the game’s best base-game outcome.

Free Spins: The Unlimited Multiplier Bonus

Triggering the Bonus

The main bonus round is where Shamrock Holmes Megaways earns its 28,000x reputation. It triggers when you land four or more scatter symbols anywhere on the reels — a slightly steeper requirement than the three-scatter standard used by many Megaways slots, but the reward structure justifies the climb.

Free Spins Awards:

  • 4 scatters = 10 free spins
  • Each additional scatter beyond the fourth adds 2 extra spins
  • So five scatters award 12 spins, six scatters award 14 spins, and so on

The Unlimited Progressive Multiplier

The defining mechanic of the bonus is its win multiplier, which follows the classic Bonanza-style blueprint:

  • The multiplier starts at 2x — every win in the bonus is at least doubled from the very first spin
  • It increases by 1 with each winning cascade delivered by Rolling Reels™
  • There is no cap — the multiplier can climb indefinitely
  • It never resets between spins for the duration of the bonus

This is where the mathematics turn explosive. A long Rolling Reels™ chain doesn’t just pay several wins — it ratchets the multiplier higher for every subsequent spin of the round. Ten spins into a lively bonus you might be sitting on a 15x or 20x multiplier, at which point even a modest multi-way hit becomes a serious payout. The 28,000x maximum win lives at the far end of this curve: a high multiplier meeting a big multi-way combination on a wide board.

Retriggers

The bonus can extend itself. During free spins, scatters landing on the extra top reel add more spins:

  • 3 scatters on the top reel = 3 additional free spins
  • 4 scatters on the top reel = 4 additional free spins

Because the multiplier never resets, every retrigger is disproportionately valuable — extra spins late in a bonus are played at whatever elevated multiplier you have already built.

Example Free Spins Sequence:

  • £1.00 stake; five scatters trigger 12 free spins at a starting 2x multiplier
  • Early spins chain four winning cascades, lifting the multiplier to 6x
  • Midway through, a retrigger adds 3 more spins
  • On a late spin, a premium symbol connects across six reels for a 40x base win
  • At the now-10x multiplier, that single hit pays 400x stake = £400.00

The structure rewards patience within the round itself: the final spins of a good bonus are always the most valuable ones you play.

Symbols & Paytable

Shamrock Holmes Megaways dresses a conventional two-tier paytable in enchanted-forest finery, with the special symbols confined to precise roles.

High-Value Symbols:

  • Shamrock Holmes — the leprechaun detective himself is the premium symbol, paying 50x your stake for six across the reels
  • The Pixie — Holmes’s winged assistant and the second-strongest payer
  • Three-Leaf Clover — a lucky charm of the forest
  • Magic Flower — a glowing woodland bloom

Six-of-a-kind premium wins beyond the Holmes symbol land in the 2x to 5x stake range, which sounds modest until you remember that Megaways slots pay the same combination multiple times across parallel ways — and that the free spins multiplier scales everything upward.

Low-Value Symbols:

  • The royal card values (9, 10, J, Q, K, A), carved in mossy stone and woodland styling to fit the theme

Special Symbols:

  • Rainbow Wild (W) — appears only on the extra top reel, substituting for all paying symbols; it cannot replace scatters or the respin-triggering character symbols
  • Scatter (Pot of Gold) — three trigger Magic Forest Respins in combination with character symbols; four or more trigger the free spins bonus
  • Shamrock Holmes & Pixie trigger symbols — landing on the top reel alongside scatters, these determine the tier of your Magic Forest Respin

Reading the Paytable:

As with all Megaways™ slots, payouts are quoted as multiples of your total stake and wins require matching symbols on consecutive reels from the left. Individual symbol values look small on paper, but multi-way hits, cascades and the bonus multiplier are where the real accounting happens.

How to Win: Strategy & Tips

Shamrock Holmes Megaways is a medium-high volatility game with an unusually friendly hit frequency, and your approach should reflect both facts.

Bankroll Management

  • Budget for the four-scatter trigger: The free spins need four scatters rather than the usual three, so bonuses arrive less often than in some rivals. Size your bets so your session can absorb 150-200 spins without strain.
  • Let the hit frequency work for you: At 35.34%, more than one spin in three pays something. These frequent small returns slow your burn rate considerably compared with harsher high-variance titles — but they are mostly small, so do not mistake activity for profit.
  • Set win and loss limits in advance: The 28,000x ceiling is real but rare. Decide before you spin when you will bank a good bonus result and walk away.

Base Game Strategy

  • Treat Magic Forest Respins as your base-game engine: Three scatters plus a character symbol is a far more common event than the full bonus trigger, and the guaranteed-ways respins are where most meaningful base-game wins originate.
  • Watch the top reel: The wild, the respin trigger characters and the retrigger scatters all live on the horizontal reel — it is the most information-dense part of the grid.
  • Remember there is no bonus buy: You cannot purchase your way into the free spins, so every session is played the organic way. Factor that into your expectations for session length.

Bonus Round Strategy

  • Value retriggers above all: Because the multiplier never resets, three or four extra spins earned late in a bonus are played at your peak multiplier — they are the most valuable spins in the entire game.
  • Understand the multiplier curve: Early bonus spins build the multiplier; late spins cash it in. A slow start does not doom a bonus if cascades begin chaining in the second half.
  • Try the demo first: The free-play version lets you watch how the respin tiers and the progressive multiplier behave before committing real money.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the RTP of Shamrock Holmes Megaways?

Shamrock Holmes Megaways has a Return to Player (RTP) of 96.44%, which sits comfortably above the modern Megaways average of around 96%. Combined with a high 35.34% hit frequency, it is one of the friendlier maths models in the genre.

What is the maximum win in Shamrock Holmes Megaways?

The maximum win is 28,000x your stake — an exceptional ceiling for a 2020 release. It is most realistically approached during the free spins bonus, where the unlimited progressive multiplier can compound a large multi-way win.

How volatile is Shamrock Holmes Megaways?

The game is rated medium-high volatility. The 35.34% hit frequency keeps base-game action ticking over, but the bulk of the win potential is concentrated in the free spins bonus, so results between bonuses can still swing noticeably.

How do I trigger the free spins bonus?

Land four or more scatter symbols anywhere on the reels. Four scatters award 10 free spins, and each additional scatter adds 2 extra spins. Note this is a steeper requirement than the three-scatter trigger used by many Megaways slots.

How does the free spins multiplier work?

The bonus multiplier starts at 2x and increases by 1 with every winning Rolling Reels™ cascade. It has no upper limit and never resets during the round, so long bonuses with plenty of cascades become progressively more valuable.

What are Magic Forest Respins?

Magic Forest Respins is the base-game feature triggered by three scatters plus character symbols on the extra top reel. It awards a respin with a guaranteed Megaways count: 10,000 ways with the pixie, 32,400 ways with Shamrock Holmes, or the full 117,649 ways when both characters land.

Does Shamrock Holmes Megaways have a bonus buy?

No. There is no bonus buy option, so the free spins round must be triggered organically by landing four or more scatters.

Who makes Shamrock Holmes Megaways and when was it released?

The game was developed by All41 Studios, an independent partner studio distributing exclusively through the Microgaming platform. It was released on December 8, 2020, and holds the distinction of being the first Megaways™ slot on Microgaming’s network.
