Game Features

Rasputin Wilds

The core innovation of Rasputin Megaways™ is the Rasputin Wild mechanic, which transforms every wild symbol into a reel-extending powerhouse. This feature is active in both base game and Free Spins, creating dynamic gameplay where Rasputin truly acts as your “holy healer” by expanding win potential.

How It Works:

Every time a Rasputin Wild symbol lands, it reveals a number and adds that many additional symbols to the screen. This mechanic creates several exciting outcomes:

Reel Extension:

  • Each reel can hold up to 10 symbols in the base game
  • In Free Spins, reels can extend up to 12 symbols
  • The additional symbols increase the number of active Megaways™
  • More symbols = more ways to win on that spin

Symbol Multiplier Effect:

  • Additional symbols can create or extend winning combinations
  • Each added symbol participates in win evaluation
  • Wild itself substitutes for all symbols except scatters
  • Can transform modest spins into significant wins

Strategic Impact:

The Rasputin Wild is what separates this game from standard Megaways™ slots. Rather than just substituting for symbols, it actively increases the game’s win potential by literally adding more symbols to the reels. This creates exciting moments where a single wild can trigger cascading wins by both substituting AND expanding the reel structure.

In the base game, landing multiple Rasputin Wilds that add substantial symbols can push you toward the 1,000,000 Megaways™ maximum. In Free Spins, this mechanic becomes even more powerful, potentially unlocking the game’s extraordinary 2,985,984 Megaways™ ceiling.

Big + Strong Free Spins

The first of two Free Spins options, Big + Strong offers 7 free spins with an innovative extending reels mechanic that builds momentum as you play. This mode is perfect for players who enjoy watching features grow in power with each spin.

Triggering:

  • Land 3+ golden disco ball scatter symbols
  • Choose “Big + Strong” from the Free Spins selection screen
  • Receive 7 Free Spins to start

Core Mechanic - Extending Reels:

What makes Big + Strong unique is its persistent reel growth system:

  1. First Free Spin: Reels start at standard size
  2. Symbol Carryover: After each spin, the number of symbols on each reel is carried forward to the next spin
  3. Rasputin Boosts: When Rasputin Wilds add symbols, those additions are locked in for remaining spins
  4. Progressive Growth: Each spin maintains the maximum reel size achieved so far
  5. 12-Symbol Reward: When any reel reaches 12 symbols, you’re awarded +3 extra Free Spins!

Example Sequence:

  • Spin 1: Standard reels (6-7 symbols), Rasputin Wild adds 4 to reel 3 = now 11 symbols
  • Spin 2: Reel 3 starts at 11 symbols, Rasputin Wild adds 2 more = 12 symbols achieved! (+3 spins awarded)
  • Spin 3: Reel 3 locked at 12, other reels continue growing
  • Result: Progressively more Megaways™ as reels expand

Strategy:

Big + Strong rewards patience and reel-building. Early Rasputin Wilds that add significant symbols set the foundation for massive Megaways™ in later spins. The +3 spin bonus for hitting 12 symbols can extend your feature considerably, and having multiple reels at maximum size creates spectacular win potential.

Ecstasy + Fire Free Spins

The second Free Spins option, Ecstasy + Fire, takes a different approach with 10 free spins powered by an unlimited increasing multiplier. This high-risk, high-reward mode appeals to players seeking exponential win growth.

Triggering:

  • Land 3+ golden disco ball scatter symbols
  • Choose “Ecstasy + Fire” from the Free Spins selection screen
  • Receive 10 Free Spins to start

Core Mechanic - Unlimited Multiplier:

Ecstasy + Fire builds win potential through an innovative multiplier progression system:

  1. Starting Multiplier: Begins at x1
  2. Symbol-Based Increase: Multiplier increases by +1 for every 5th symbol that Rasputin Wilds add to the screen
  3. Cumulative Tracking: The game tracks total symbols added throughout the feature
  4. No Cap: Multiplier can increase indefinitely with no upper limit
  5. Persistent: Multiplier applies to all wins and carries through entire feature

Multiplier Progression Example:

  • Rasputin Wild adds 3 symbols → Total: 3 (multiplier stays x1)
  • Rasputin Wild adds 4 symbols → Total: 7 (multiplier increases to x2)
  • Rasputin Wild adds 6 symbols → Total: 13 (multiplier increases to x3)
  • Continue pattern throughout feature

Retriggering:

Landing 3 or more scatter symbols during Ecstasy + Fire free spins will retrigger the feature, proving Rasputin is “no wheeler dealer”! Retriggering with an already-built multiplier can lead to astronomical wins in the extended bonus round.

Win Exchange™

Win Exchange™ adds a strategic layer to Rasputin Megaways™, allowing you to trade substantial base game wins for an opportunity to access the lucrative Free Spins feature. This mechanic rewards bold decision-making and adds excitement to big base game hits.

How It Works:

For wins between 25x-100x stake:

  • You can gamble your entire win for a chance to be awarded 7 or 10 Free Spins
  • The probability of success is displayed in green on a wheel spinner
  • Larger wins = better odds (more green on the wheel)
  • Risk: You could lose the entire win
  • Reward: Access to Free Spins with your choice of mode

For wins of 100x stake or more:

  • You can exchange exactly 100x your stake for a guaranteed 7 or 10 Free Spins
  • This is a secure exchange - no gambling involved
  • You keep any amount over 100x
  • Example: 150x win = Keep 50x + Get guaranteed Free Spins

Strategic Considerations:

  1. 25x-50x wins: Lower gamble odds (less green), high risk but could be worth it if Free Spins potential outweighs base game win
  2. 50x-80x wins: Sweet spot for gambling - decent odds and manageable risk
  3. 80x-99x wins: Very close to guaranteed territory, might be worth waiting for 100x
  4. 100x+ wins: Often makes strategic sense to exchange 100x for guaranteed Free Spins, especially if you believe in unlimited multiplier potential

Free Spins Choice:

When you successfully win or exchange for Free Spins via Win Exchange™, you’ll still get to choose between Big + Strong (7 spins) or Ecstasy + Fire (10 spins), maintaining the game’s strategic flexibility.

Win Exchange™ is particularly valuable in very high volatility slots like Rasputin Megaways™, where Free Spins often deliver significantly better returns than base game wins. A 60x base game win might seem substantial, but with Ecstasy + Fire potentially delivering thousands of times your stake through unlimited multipliers, the gamble can make excellent sense!
