The Megaways™ Engine: 117,649 Ways to Hunt

Understanding Megaways™ in Big Cat King

At the heart of Big Cat King Megaways beats the licensed Megaways™ engine, the variable-ways system that transformed slot mathematics. Instead of fixed paylines, every reel can display a different number of symbols on each spin, and the total number of ways to win is recalculated dynamically. This is what gives the game its constantly shifting rhythm and its headline figure of 117,649 ways.

How Megaways™ Works in Big Cat King:

  • 6 vertical reels, each capable of showing between 2 and 7 symbols per spin
  • A horizontal top reel adds extra symbols across reels two to five, expanding the win potential
  • Ways to win are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
  • Maximum ways: 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 = 117,649 ways to win
  • Wins pay for matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right, regardless of their row position

Win Formation Principles:

  • Symbol size and exact vertical position are irrelevant — only the reel sequence matters
  • A winning combination must start on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
  • Multiple separate winning combinations can land and pay on a single spin
  • Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your total stake, not a per-line bet
  • The number of ways changes every spin, so no two spins feel mathematically identical

Cascading Reels

Like most Megaways™ slots, Big Cat King Megaways employs cascading reels (also called tumbles or avalanches). When you land a winning combination:

  1. The symbols that formed the win are removed from the grid
  2. The symbols above them drop down to fill the empty spaces
  3. New symbols fall in from above to complete the reels
  4. If the new arrangement creates another win, it pays and the process repeats
  5. Cascades continue until no further wins land

This mechanic means a single paid spin can produce a chain of consecutive wins. It becomes especially important during free spins, where every cascade also pushes the win multiplier higher — turning a long cascade chain into a compounding engine of escalating payouts.

Mystery Symbols: Leaves That Reveal the Pride

One Symbol Type, Many Possible Reveals

One of Big Cat King Megaways’ most distinctive base-game touches is its mystery symbol mechanic. These appear as green leaf symbols scattered across the reels, and once the spin settles they all transform to reveal the same matching symbol.

How Mystery Symbols Work:

  • Multiple green leaf mystery symbols can land anywhere across the grid on a spin
  • Once the reels stop, the leaves simultaneously reveal as one shared symbol
  • Because they all become the same symbol, they can combine into substantial multi-way wins
  • When they reveal as a high-value symbol across several reels, the payout can be considerable

Why Mystery Symbols Matter:

  • Multi-Way Potential: A board peppered with leaves that all reveal a premium symbol can pay across many of the 117,649 ways at once
  • Cascade Synergy: A big mystery-symbol reveal feeds straight into the cascading engine, clearing the board and dropping fresh symbols for potential follow-up wins
  • Base-Game Excitement: They keep the base game lively while you wait to trigger the free spins round

Example Mystery Symbol Scenario:

  • £1.00 stake
  • Eight green leaf symbols land scattered across the reels
  • They all reveal the lion (a premium symbol)
  • The lions line up across multiple reels for a sizeable multi-way payout, which then cascades

Mystery symbols are the spice of the base game. They are unpredictable, occasionally explosive, and they give Big Cat King Megaways a personality beyond a generic Megaways clone.

Roaring Free Spins: The Unlimited Rising Multiplier

Triggering the Free Spins

The marquee feature of Big Cat King Megaways is its free spins round, triggered by landing four or more scatter symbols anywhere on the reels. The trigger is generous in its scaling:

  • 4 scatters award 12 free spins
  • Each additional scatter in view tops up the total with 5 more free spins

So a lucky spin that reveals five or six scatters at once can launch you into the bonus with a healthy reserve of spins before the round even begins.

The Multiplier That Never Resets

What makes the free spins round so special is its progressive win multiplier:

  • The multiplier starts at x1
  • It rises by +1 after every winning cascade
  • Crucially, it does not reset between spins — it persists for the entire round

This is the engine room of the game. In many Megaways slots the multiplier resets each spin, blunting its impact. Here, every cascade across the whole free spins round nudges the multiplier higher, so by the back half of a productive bonus you could be applying a double-digit multiplier to every win that lands. A single high-ways spin late in the round, multiplied by an accumulated tally, is exactly how Big Cat King Megaways reaches toward its 50,000x ceiling.

Retriggers

During the free spins round, landing scatters tops up your remaining spins:

  • 3 or 4 visible scatters award 5 or 10 extra free spins respectively

These retriggers extend the round, giving the multiplier more time to climb and compound — and the longer you stay in, the more dangerous each subsequent win becomes.

Example Free Spins Scenario:

  • £1.00 stake, free spins round in progress
  • A series of cascades has pushed the multiplier up to x12
  • A high-ways spin lands a 25x base win
  • With the x12 multiplier: 25x × 12 = 300x stake = £300.00 from that single spin, before any further cascades push the multiplier even higher

The Spin Boost Wheel: Gamble for More Spins

Pressing Your Luck

A standout interactive element of Big Cat King Megaways is the Spin Boost Wheel, a gambling feature that lets you chase additional free spins before or during the bonus. Rather than passively accepting the spins you were awarded, you can spin a wheel divided into red and green segments in pursuit of extra spins.

How the Spin Boost Wheel Works:

  • The wheel displays a mix of red and green segments
  • You spin in the hope of landing on green to win additional free spins
  • A successful run can award up to 50 extra free spins
  • Each wheel spin carries a cost, typically scaling from around 10x to 20x your stake depending on how it was triggered

Strategic Considerations:

  • Risk vs. Reward: More spins mean more chances for the multiplier to climb — but the wheel costs stake and can end early
  • Gamble Mentality: This is an optional press-your-luck feature; conservative players can skip it entirely
  • Multiplier Synergy: Because the free spins multiplier never resets, additional spins are unusually valuable here — every extra spin is a chance to push the tally higher

The Spin Boost Wheel injects genuine player agency into the bonus. It transforms the free spins entry from a fixed allocation into a decision point, and for thrill-seekers it is one of the game’s most memorable moments.

Skipping Straight to the Hunt

For players who would rather not wait for four scatters to align, Big Cat King Megaways offers a flexible bonus buy — branded as the Roaring Free Spins Bet. This lets you pay a multiple of your stake to launch directly into the free spins round.

Roaring Free Spins Buy Tiers:

The higher tiers cost considerably more but launch you into the round with a deeper bank of spins — and given the never-resetting multiplier, more starting spins translate directly into a higher potential multiplier ceiling. The bonus buy also lifts the effective RTP slightly compared with the base game.

Strategic Considerations:

  • Cost Awareness: The 250x tier is a serious outlay, so size your stake to your bankroll accordingly
  • Variance: Buying does not guarantee a profitable round — the multiplier still has to build through cascades
  • Best Value: The higher tiers maximise the chance the multiplier reaches dangerous heights, but they also amplify the swings

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the RTP of Big Cat King Megaways?

Big Cat King Megaways has a Return to Player (RTP) of 96.5%. This is a solid, industry-standard figure, and it rises modestly — toward 97% — when you use the Roaring Free Spins bonus buy.

What is the maximum win in Big Cat King Megaways?

The maximum win is 50,000x your stake, one of the higher ceilings in Blueprint’s Megaways catalogue. It is most realistically approached through a long free spins run where the never-resetting multiplier climbs into double digits and compounds with a high-ways spin.

How volatile is Big Cat King Megaways?

The game is rated medium-high volatility. Some sources describe it as high; either way you should expect meaningful dry spells in the base game punctuated by larger, multiplier-driven wins in the free spins round.

Does Big Cat King Megaways have a bonus buy?

Yes. The Roaring Free Spins Bet lets you buy directly into the free spins round across multiple tiers: 50x stake for 8 spins, 75x for 10, 100x for 12, and 250x for 25 spins.

How does the free spins multiplier work?

The free spins win multiplier starts at x1 and rises by +1 after every winning cascade. Critically, it does not reset between spins, so it accumulates throughout the entire round — the longer you stay in, the higher it can climb.

How do I trigger the free spins?

Land four or more scatter symbols anywhere on the reels. Four scatters award 12 free spins, and each extra scatter adds 5 more. During the round, additional scatters retrigger extra spins.

What is the Spin Boost Wheel?

The Spin Boost Wheel is an optional gambling feature with red and green segments. By spinning it (for a cost scaling with your stake) you can win up to 50 additional free spins, giving the never-resetting multiplier even more time to build.

Who makes Big Cat King Megaways and when was it released?

Big Cat King Megaways was developed by Blueprint Gaming and released on September 22, 2020. It uses the licensed Megaways™ engine under licence from Big Time Gaming.
