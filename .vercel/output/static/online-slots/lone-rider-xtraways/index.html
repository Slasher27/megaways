The XtraWays Engine: From 729 to 262,144 Ways

Understanding XtraWays in Lone Rider

At the heart of Lone Rider XtraWays beats the studio’s proprietary XtraWays mechanic — Swintt’s answer to the variable-ways concept popularised by Megaways. This was the very first game to feature it, and the system is built to feel both familiar and refreshingly different.

In a standard Megaways slot, each reel lands a random number of symbols every spin, and the total ways are recalculated on the fly with no warning. XtraWays takes a more deliberate, transparent approach. Above each of the six reels sits a counter that tells you exactly how many symbols that reel will display. As you collect special XtraWays symbols, those counters climb, and the reels grow taller.

How XtraWays Works in Lone Rider:

  • The game begins as a 6x3 grid, producing 729 ways to win (3 × 3 × 3 × 3 × 3 × 3 = 729)
  • Special XtraWays symbols carry a value (a number that adds to the reel counters)
  • As counters build, individual reels expand to show more symbols, up to a maximum of eight per reel
  • At full expansion the grid becomes 6x8, delivering the maximum 262,144 ways to win (8 × 8 × 8 × 8 × 8 × 8 = 262,144)
  • Wins pay for matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right, regardless of their position

Win Formation Principles:

  • Only the reel sequence matters — exact symbol position within a reel is irrelevant
  • A winning combination must start on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
  • Multiple separate winning combinations can land and pay on a single spin
  • Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your total stake, not a per-line bet
  • The more reels that have expanded, the more ways are active and the bigger the win potential

Why the Counters Matter

The genius of XtraWays is the visibility it gives you. Because every reel shows its upcoming height on the counter above it, you always know how close you are to a fully expanded, 262,144-way board. This transforms the moment-to-moment experience: instead of passively hoping the reels land tall, you actively watch the counters climb and feel the tension build as the grid grows toward its maximum.

It is a subtle but meaningful design choice. The anticipation of seeing a counter tick from five to six to seven symbols — knowing the board is one step away from its full, payout-rich form — gives Lone Rider XtraWays a personality that distinguishes it from the random-reel rhythm of most variable-ways slots.

Free Spins: The Frontier Bonus Round

Triggering the Free Spins

The main bonus round in Lone Rider XtraWays is the Free Spins feature, triggered by landing the game’s horse Scatter symbols. The number of free spins you receive scales with how many Scatters land on the triggering spin, rewarding you for filling the reels with more of them.

Free Spins Trigger Table:

  • 3 Scatters → 10 free spins
  • 4 Scatters → 15 free spins
  • 5 Scatters → 20 free spins
  • 6 Scatters → 25 free spins

The horse Scatter is thematically perfect for a Wild West slot — the loyal steed that carries the lone rider toward his fortune. Landing a full six of them for the maximum 25-spin award is the dream trigger, giving you the longest possible run at an expanded board.

Persistent Reel Counters

What makes the free spins round in Lone Rider XtraWays special is how the XtraWays counters behave during the bonus. In the base game, the reel counters reset and rebuild each spin. In the free spins round, however, the reel counters do not decrease between spins — they hold their accumulated height.

Why This Matters:

  • Reel expansions you earn are carried forward rather than lost each spin
  • This means the grid can stay large — and stay close to its full 262,144 ways — for the duration of the bonus
  • A long free spins run with high counters maximises the number of active ways on every single spin
  • It rewards players who manage to build the counters up early in the round

This persistence is the engine behind the slot’s biggest wins. A 25-spin bonus that builds the counters toward a fully expanded board keeps the maximum ways in play far longer than the base game ever allows, dramatically increasing your chances of stacking multiple multi-way wins before the round ends.

Reading the Paytable:

Because this is a variable-ways slot, all payouts are quoted as multiples of your total stake, and wins are awarded for matching symbols on consecutive reels from the left. With up to 262,144 ways active on a fully expanded board, even mid-value symbols can produce respectable returns when they appear on every reel.

What is the XtraWays mechanic?
