The Megaways™ Mechanic: 117,649 Ways to Horror Riches

Understanding Megaways™ in Fury of Hyde

Fury of Hyde Megaways™ utilizes the licensed Megaways™ engine in a slightly modified configuration: 6 vertical reels displaying 2-7 symbols each, plus a unique 4-symbol horizontal tracker reel positioned above the main grid that displays the letters H, Y, D, and E.

How Megaways™ Works:

  • 6 main vertical reels with 2-7 symbols per reel
  • 4-symbol horizontal tracker (does NOT count toward ways calculation, only triggers Hyde feature)
  • Ways to win calculated by multiplying symbols on the 6 main reels only
  • Maximum ways: 7×7×7×7×7×7 = 117,649 ways to win
  • Minimum ways: 2×2×2×2×2×2 = 64 ways

Win Formation:

Wins are awarded for left-to-right adjacent symbols regardless of size:

  • Symbol size irrelevant—small and large symbols count equally
  • Only consecutive reels from left to right form winning combinations
  • Multiple winning combinations can occur simultaneously
  • All payouts multiplied by total stake
  • Cascading Reactions: Winning symbols removed, new symbols drop from above
  • Tracker Reveal: Cascades remove symbols from tracker, potentially revealing H-Y-D-E letters

The Unique Tracker Reel:

The 4-symbol horizontal reel above the main grid serves a specialized function:

  • Displays potential H, Y, D, E letter symbols
  • Letters are initially hidden behind other symbols
  • Cascades from wins remove tracker symbols
  • If all 4 letters (H-Y-D-E) are revealed, Hyde Super Spin triggers
  • Tracker does NOT contribute to standard win ways calculation

This creates strategic depth: larger cascading sequences remove more tracker symbols, increasing probability of revealing all four letters needed for the transformation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Hyde Super Spin mechanic work?

Hyde Super Spin triggers when you spell H-Y-D-E on the horizontal tracker reel above the main grid. Cascading wins remove symbols from the tracker, revealing letters beneath. When all four letters (H, Y, D, E) are visible simultaneously, Hyde Super Spin activates after standard wins calculate. It applies three modifiers: (1) removes at least one low-value symbol type, (2) increases multiplier by +2 or +3, and (3) guarantees minimum Megaways™ ranging from 1,000 to 117,649 ways.

What are Mystery Pocket Watch symbols?

Mystery Symbols appear as Victorian pocket watches that can land on any position on the main 6 reels. When they land, all pocket watch symbols simultaneously reveal the same matching regular symbol. This creates synchronized winning combinations, as multiple watches transforming into premiums can generate instant big wins. They cannot reveal Scatters or Wilds, only regular pay symbols.

How does the Free Spins multiplier work?

Free Spins begin with an x1 multiplier that increases by +1 after every single cascade throughout the entire feature. The multiplier never resets between spins and has no upper limit. For example: first spin has 3 cascades (multiplier grows to x4), second spin starts at x4 and adds 2 more cascades (reaches x6), continuing to build cumulatively. Sessions can reach x60-x100+ multipliers with excellent cascading sequences.

Can Hyde Super Spin trigger during Free Spins?

Yes! Hyde Super Spin remains fully active during Free Spins and is actually enhanced. Spelling H-Y-D-E during the feature awards +1 Free Spin as a bonus, plus the standard triple modifiers still apply. Critically, the multiplier boost from Hyde (+2 or +3) adds to your already-growing Free Spins multiplier and persists for all remaining spins, creating compound multiplier growth where features can reach x80-x120 total multipliers.

What is the RTP and volatility?

Fury of Hyde Megaways™ has an RTP of 96% and super high volatility. The 96% return is fair but not exceptional (premium slots offer 96.5-97%). Super high volatility means extremely dramatic swings—expect frequent dead spins (63.65% based on 36.35% hit frequency), long droughts between features (250-350 spins average), and when wins occur, they’re substantial. This demands very significant bankroll (400-500x stake minimum recommended).

How do I trigger Free Spins?

Land 4 or more scatter symbols anywhere on the main 6 reels to trigger Free Spins. Awards are: 4 scatters = 10 spins, 5 scatters = 12 spins, 6 scatters = 14 spins, 7 scatters = 16 spins. You can retrigger during the feature by landing 4+ scatters again, awarding the same number of additional spins. Hyde Super Spin triggers during Free Spins also award +1 spin each time.

The maximum win is 10,000x your bet (capped at €200,000 cash maximum). This is achieved through extended Free Spins sessions (20-30+ spins from retriggers and Hyde bonuses) where the multiplier reaches x80-x120 through cascades and Hyde boosts, combined with premium symbol coverage on high Megaways™ counts.
