The Megaways™ Engine: 15,625 Ways to the Heavens

Understanding Megaways™ in Gods of Olympus

At the core of Gods of Olympus Megaways sits the licensed Megaways™ engine, the variable-ways system that reshaped how modern slots are built. Instead of fixed paylines, every reel can display a different number of symbols on each spin, and the total number of ways to win is recalculated dynamically. This is what gives the game its constantly shifting feel and its headline figure of 15,625 ways.

How Megaways™ Works in Gods of Olympus:

  • 6 vertical reels, each capable of showing between 2 and 5 symbols per spin
  • Ways to win are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
  • Maximum ways: 5 × 5 × 5 × 5 × 5 × 5 = 15,625 ways to win
  • Minimum ways: a far smaller count when each reel lands short
  • Wins pay for matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right, regardless of their exact position

Win Formation Principles:

  • Symbol size and row position are irrelevant — only the reel sequence matters
  • A winning combination must start on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
  • Multiple separate winning combinations can land and pay on a single spin
  • Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your total stake, not a per-line bet
  • The number of ways changes every spin, so no two spins feel mathematically identical

Cascading Reactions

Like most Megaways™ slots, Gods of Olympus Megaways employs cascading reactions (also called tumbles or avalanches). When you land a winning combination:

  1. The symbols that formed the win are removed from the grid
  2. The symbols above them drop down to fill the empty spaces
  3. New symbols fall in from above to complete the reels
  4. If the new arrangement creates another win, it pays and the process repeats
  5. Cascades continue until no further wins land

This mechanic means a single paid spin can produce a chain of consecutive wins. Cascades are especially powerful during the free spins rounds, where building multipliers attach to every successive win in the chain — turning a single lucky drop into a thunderous payout.

Zeus Power Spins: Five Modifiers From the Thunder God

The Heart of the Base Game

The signature base-game mechanic of Gods of Olympus Megaways is Zeus Power Spins, a modifier system controlled by the king of the gods himself. At any moment during ordinary play, Zeus can intervene and trigger one of five different random modifiers, each capable of transforming an otherwise routine spin into a winning one.

The Five Zeus Power Spins Modifiers:

  1. Bonus Thunder — Adds extra bonus symbols to the reels, dramatically increasing your chance of triggering the free spins round on that spin. This is the modifier that pushes you toward the slot’s headline feature.

  2. Wild Thunder — Scatters multiplying wild symbols across the reels. Wilds substitute for paying symbols to complete combinations, and the attached multipliers boost the value of any win they help form.

  3. Colossal Thunder — Converts up to six reels into a single colossal reel, where giant blocks of matching symbols can land. When a colossal block of a high-value god appears, it can complete wins across the entire grid at once.

  4. Mystery Thunder — Places matching mystery symbols across all six reels. When these mystery tiles reveal the same paying symbol, they can combine into substantial multi-way wins, particularly when paired with a cascade.

  5. Respin Thunder — Holds winning symbols in position while the remaining reels respin, giving you a second chance to extend or build on an existing win.

Why Zeus Power Spins Matters:

  • Constant Variety: Because the modifier is random, no two activations feel the same
  • Multiple Win Pathways: Some modifiers pay immediately, others set up bonus access or extend a win
  • Engagement Anchor: Power Spins fire often enough to keep the base game lively without devaluing the bonus
  • Synergy with Cascades: Colossal and Mystery Thunder both feed directly into the cascading engine

The Zeus Power Spins system is the engine room of the base game. It is generous in frequency, rich in variety, and gives the slot a personality that distinguishes it from generic Megaways clones.

Bonus Thunder Free Spins: Choose Your Divine Path

Triggering the Bonus

The main bonus round in Gods of Olympus Megaways is the Bonus Thunder Free Spins, triggered by landing the required number of bonus symbols anywhere on the reels. The Bonus Thunder Power Spins modifier can help by adding extra bonus symbols to nudge you over the threshold, and impatient players can simply buy direct access for 100x their stake.

Once triggered, you are presented with four of Zeus’ sacred stones. Picking a stone reveals which of four completely distinct free spins rounds you will play — and each one offers a wildly different route toward the game’s 10,000x ceiling.

The four free spins rounds are:

  • Wild Storm — wild overlays storm the central reels
  • Rise of Olympus — an unlimited, ever-climbing win multiplier
  • Colossal — a roaming colossal wild with escalating multipliers
  • Almighty Wild — multiplying wilds that scale toward 625x

Wild Storm Free Spins: Thunderbolts of Wilds

Wild Overlays Strike the Reels

Wild Storm is the free spins round where Zeus hurls thunderbolts across the grid. During the round, thunderbolt overlays convert symbols into wilds, most prominently across the central reels (reels 2 to 5). With wilds appearing frequently, the chance of completing multi-way combinations soars.

How Wild Storm Works:

  • A set number of free spins plays out
  • Each spin, thunderbolt overlays land and convert symbols into wilds on the central reels
  • Wilds substitute for all paying symbols to help complete winning combinations
  • Combined with cascades, a single overlay-heavy spin can chain multiple wins

Wild Storm is the most consistent of the four rounds — wilds land often, so wins arrive at a steady pace rather than relying on one colossal hit. It is an excellent choice for players who prefer regular feature activity within the bonus.

Rise of Olympus Free Spins: The Unlimited Multiplier Climb

A Multiplier That Never Stops Growing

Rise of Olympus is built around a single, irresistible idea: an unlimited win multiplier that increases by 1 every time a wild symbol lands. Because there is no cap, the longer your spins keep producing wilds, the higher the multiplier climbs — and the bigger every subsequent win becomes.

How Rise of Olympus Works:

  • You are awarded a number of free spins with a starting multiplier
  • Every time a wild lands, the multiplier permanently increases by 1 for the rest of the round
  • All wins during the round are multiplied by the current multiplier value
  • Cascading wins all benefit from the running multiplier, compounding the effect

This is the round with the highest theoretical ceiling. A run of wild-heavy spins early in the feature can build a towering multiplier that turns even modest late-round wins into spectacular payouts. It is the choice for players chasing the very top end of the slot’s potential.

Colossal Free Spins: The Roaming Giant Wild

A Colossal Wild on the March

The Colossal free spins round introduces a two-symbol roaming colossal wild that moves across the reels as the round progresses. As a giant block, it can cover large portions of the grid and complete enormous multi-way wins, while a built-in win multiplier climbs as high as 625x.

How Colossal Free Spins Works:

  • A roaming colossal wild (typically two symbols wide) travels across the reels each spin
  • Wherever it lands, it substitutes as a giant wild block to complete combinations
  • An attached multiplier increases over the course of the round, reaching up to 625x
  • The combination of a board-covering wild and a huge multiplier creates explosive potential

Colossal is one of the most volatile of the four rounds. When the roaming wild lines up with high-value gods and the multiplier is near its peak, the payouts can be among the largest the slot produces.

Almighty Wild Free Spins: Multiplying Divine Power

Multiplier Wilds That Scale to 625x

The fourth round, Almighty Wild, fills the reels with multiplying wild symbols. These wilds begin with modest multiplier values — typically around x2 or x3 — but can scale all the way up to 625x as the round develops, layering multiplier on top of multiplier when several wilds contribute to the same win.

How Almighty Wild Works:

  • Multiplying wilds appear throughout the free spins round
  • Each wild carries its own multiplier value, starting around x2/x3
  • Multiplier values escalate as the round progresses, reaching as high as 625x
  • When multiple multiplier wilds combine in a single win, their values multiply together for huge results

Almighty Wild blends the consistency of frequent wilds with the explosive upside of escalating multipliers. It is a strong all-round choice that balances regular wins against genuine big-hit potential.

The Bonus Buy: Two Routes to the Action

Skip the Wait With a Feature Buy

Gods of Olympus Megaways offers two distinct bonus buy options for players who would rather not wait for features to arrive organically:

  • Zeus Power Spins (Power Spins Bonus Bet) — costs 50x your stake and guarantees one of the five Zeus Power Spins modifiers on the next spin.
  • Bonus Thunder (Bonus Guarantee Bet) — costs 100x your stake and grants direct access to the free spins selector, where you pick one of the four bonus rounds.

Things to Know About the Bonus Buys:

  • Higher RTP: Using the feature buys raises the game’s RTP from 96.03% to 96.91%, a meaningful improvement for value-focused players
  • Variance Trade-off: Buying features costs a fixed multiple of your stake, so it accelerates both wins and losses — manage your bankroll accordingly
  • Direct Bonus Access: The 100x Bonus Thunder buy is the fastest way to reach the four-way free spins selector and the 625x multiplier rounds

The dual bonus buy is a genuine strength for feature hunters, and the higher buy-mode RTP is an unusual and welcome bonus.

High-Value Symbols:

  • Poseidon — god of the sea, wielding his trident

The premium symbols deliver the largest base-game payouts when they land across multiple reels, and they are the symbols you most want the Mystery Thunder modifier to reveal.

Low-Value Symbols:

Special Symbols:

  • Bonus Symbol — landing enough triggers the Bonus Thunder Free Spins; the Bonus Thunder modifier adds extras
  • Mystery Symbol — placed in bulk by the Mystery Thunder modifier, all revealing the same paying symbol

Reading the Paytable:

Because this is a Megaways™ slot, all payouts are quoted as multiples of your total stake, and wins are awarded for matching symbols on consecutive reels from the left. With up to 15,625 ways active, even mid-value symbols can produce respectable returns when they appear on every reel.

How to Win: Strategy & Tips

Gods of Olympus Megaways is a high-volatility game, which means a patient, disciplined approach is essential. Here is how to give yourself the best ascent.

Bankroll Management

  • Match your bet to your bankroll: With a range of £0.10 to £10.00, divide your session budget so you can comfortably absorb at least 150-200 spins. High volatility means meaningful wins can be spaced out, so a generous buffer is important.
  • Set win and loss limits: Decide in advance when you will walk away — both when ahead and when down. The 10,000x ceiling is a big target, but most sessions revolve around the more frequent modifier and free spins wins.
  • Factor in the bonus buys: If you choose to buy features, remember they cost a fixed 50x or 100x your stake. Budget for several buys, since a single bonus can run dry.

Base Game Strategy

  • Let the Megaways™ engine do the work: There is no skill in spin outcomes, so focus on bet sizing and session length rather than chasing patterns.
  • Value the Zeus Power Spins: This is where most of your base-game excitement lives. Modifiers like Colossal Thunder and Mystery Thunder can produce strong wins, while Bonus Thunder pushes you toward the free spins.
  • Use cascades to your advantage: A single paid spin can chain into multiple wins, especially after a modifier loads the board with wilds or mystery symbols.

Bonus Round Strategy

  • Understand the four rounds: Choose nothing — the round is revealed by the stone you pick — but knowing each round’s character helps you appreciate the result. Rise of Olympus has the highest ceiling, Colossal is the most explosive, Wild Storm is the most consistent, and Almighty Wild balances both.
  • Consider the buy-mode RTP: If you intend to chase features anyway, the feature buys raise RTP to 96.91%, which is a genuine value edge over triggering organically.
  • Practice in the demo first: Use the free-play version to understand how each modifier and bonus round behaves before committing real funds.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the RTP of Gods of Olympus Megaways?

Gods of Olympus Megaways has a default Return to Player (RTP) of 96.03%, which sits right around the modern Megaways average. Notably, the RTP rises to 96.91% when you use the feature buy options, giving value-focused players a reason to consider the bonus buys.

What is the maximum win in Gods of Olympus Megaways?

The maximum win is 10,000x your stake. While that is more modest than some ultra-volatile Megaways titles, it remains a substantial target, most realistically approached through the Rise of Olympus and Colossal free spins rounds, where building multipliers can reach as high as 625x.

How volatile is Gods of Olympus Megaways?

The game is high volatility. This means wins can be spaced out with longer dry spells between features, balanced by the potential for large payouts when the modifiers and free spins rounds align. A disciplined bankroll is recommended.

Does Gods of Olympus Megaways have a bonus buy?

Yes. There are two bonus buy options: Zeus Power Spins for 50x your stake (guaranteeing one of the five base-game modifiers), and Bonus Thunder for 100x your stake (granting direct access to the free spins selector). Using these buys also raises the RTP to 96.91%.

What are the Zeus Power Spins?

Zeus Power Spins is the base-game modifier feature. At random, Zeus fires one of five modifiers: Bonus Thunder (extra bonus symbols), Wild Thunder (multiplying wilds), Colossal Thunder (colossal reels), Mystery Thunder (matching mystery symbols) and Respin Thunder (winning symbols held for a respin).

What free spins rounds can I get?

When you trigger the Bonus Thunder Free Spins, you pick one of Zeus’ four stones to reveal one of four rounds: Wild Storm (wild overlays), Rise of Olympus (an unlimited climbing multiplier), Colossal (a roaming colossal wild) and Almighty Wild (multiplying wilds up to 625x).

Who makes Gods of Olympus Megaways and when was it released?

Gods of Olympus Megaways was developed by Blueprint Gaming under licence for the Megaways™ mechanic from Big Time Gaming, and was released on August 21, 2019. It remains one of Blueprint’s most popular Greek mythology Megaways titles.
