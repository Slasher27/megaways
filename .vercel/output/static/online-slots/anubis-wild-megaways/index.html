The Megaways™ Engine: 117,649 Ways to Plunder

Understanding Megaways™ in Anubis Wild

At the heart of Anubis Wild Megaways beats the licensed Megaways™ engine, the variable-ways system that reshaped slot mathematics. Instead of fixed paylines, every reel can display a different number of symbols on each spin, and the total ways to win are recalculated dynamically. This is what gives the game its constantly shifting rhythm and its headline figure of 117,649 ways.

How Megaways™ Works in Anubis Wild:

  • 6 vertical reels, each capable of showing between 2 and 7 symbols per spin
  • Ways to win are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
  • Maximum ways: 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 = 117,649 ways to win
  • Minimum ways: 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 64 ways to win
  • Wins pay for matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right, regardless of their position on the reel

Win Formation Principles:

  • Symbol size and exact row position are irrelevant — only the reel sequence matters
  • A winning combination must start on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
  • Multiple separate winning combinations can land and pay on a single spin
  • Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your total stake, not a per-line bet
  • The number of ways changes every spin, so no two spins feel mathematically identical

Cascading Reactions

Like most Megaways™ slots, Anubis Wild Megaways uses cascading reactions (also called tumbles or avalanches). When you land a winning combination:

  1. The symbols that formed the win are removed from the grid
  2. The symbols above them drop down to fill the empty spaces
  3. New symbols fall in from above to complete the reels
  4. If the new arrangement creates another win, it pays and the process repeats
  5. Cascades continue until no further wins land

This mechanic means a single paid spin can produce a chain of consecutive wins. It is especially valuable when the top wild reel is feeding wilds into the grid, or when mystery symbols reveal a matching high-value icon across multiple reels.

The Top Wild Reel: Anubis Watches From Above

A Dedicated Reel of Wilds in the Base Game

The first thing that sets Anubis Wild Megaways apart is its 4x1 horizontal reel that runs along the top of the main grid. During base-game play, this is where the wild symbols live. As the top reel spins, it can drop wilds down onto the six main reels, helping to complete winning combinations that the standard symbols alone might not.

How the Top Wild Reel Works:

  • A separate horizontal reel sits above the six Megaways™ reels
  • Wild symbols appear on this top reel during the base game
  • When wilds land, they substitute for paying symbols to complete combinations
  • This gives the base game an additional, reliable source of wilds that many Megaways slots lack

Why the Top Wild Reel Matters:

  • It keeps the base game engaged by regularly introducing wilds
  • Wilds feeding into a high-ways board can complete substantial multi-way wins
  • Combined with cascades, a well-placed wild can trigger a chain of follow-on wins
  • It establishes the wild as the game’s signature symbol before the free spins amplify it dramatically

The top wild reel is a clever piece of design. It prevents the base game from feeling flat between feature triggers and lays the groundwork for the far more powerful wild mechanic that defines the free spins round.

Mystery Symbols: One Symbol, Many Possibilities

The ”?” That Transforms the Board

Anubis Wild Megaways also employs mystery symbols, represented by a question-mark icon. When mystery symbols land, they all reveal the same regular paying symbol — never a wild or bonus scatter. Because they transform in unison, a cluster of mystery symbols can suddenly populate the reels with a single high-value icon, completing multi-way wins that ordinary spins would miss.

How Mystery Symbols Work:

  • One or more ”?” symbols can appear on the reels
  • When they reveal, they all transform into the same standard paying symbol
  • They cannot become wilds or bonus scatters
  • The transformation can convert a scattered, unconnected board into a winning one

Why Mystery Symbols Matter:

  • They add an extra layer of variance and surprise to base-game spins
  • A swarm of mystery symbols revealing a premium icon can produce a strong multi-way payout
  • They synergise with cascades, since the transformed symbols may trigger chain wins
  • They keep the base game lively alongside the top wild reel

Mystery symbols are a familiar Megaways embellishment, but they pair well with this game’s other mechanics and give the base game a second route to bigger wins.

Free Spins: Where the Wilds Come Alive

Triggering the Bonus Round

The headline feature of Anubis Wild Megaways is its free spins round, triggered by landing three or more pyramid scatter symbols anywhere on the reels. Three scatters award an initial 12 free spins. During the round, every additional pyramid scatter that lands grants 2 extra spins, and the total can build all the way up to a generous maximum of 82 free spins.

Triggering and Extending:

  • 3+ pyramid scatters anywhere trigger the round with 12 free spins
  • Each additional scatter during the round adds 2 more spins
  • The free spins can extend to a maximum of 82 spins in total

The free spins are not simply more of the same — they fundamentally change how wilds behave, transforming Anubis Wild Megaways from a solid base game into a feature with real escalation potential.

Locking, Merging Multiplier Wilds

The defining mechanic of the free spins round is the way wilds now behave. During free spins, wilds appear on reels 2 to 5 rather than the top reel. When a wild lands, it drops to the bottom of its reel and locks in place for the remainder of the round. Crucially, when wilds merge together, the merged wild takes on a multiplier equal to the number of wilds that have merged.

How the Free Spins Wilds Work:

  1. Wilds land on reels 2-5 during the free spins
  2. Each wild drops to the bottom of its reel and locks there permanently for the round
  3. As more wilds stack on the same reel, they merge into a single wild
  4. The merged wild carries a multiplier equal to the number of merged wilds (two wilds = 2x, three wilds = 3x, and so on)
  5. These locked, multiplied wilds remain in play for every remaining spin

Covered Reels Award Extra Spins:

  • If wilds completely cover an entire reel, you are awarded additional free spins
  • This rewards long, productive rounds and extends them further

Why This Matters:

  • Locked wilds accumulate, so the board grows steadily more wild-rich as the round progresses
  • Merged multipliers compound the value of every win that uses those wilds
  • The combination of permanent wilds and escalating multipliers is where the 12,500x ceiling becomes reachable
  • Covered-reel bonus spins can keep a hot round alive far longer than the initial 12 spins

This is the beating heart of the game. A free spins round that starts modestly can snowball as wilds lock, merge and multiply across reels 2-5, turning the later spins into genuinely explosive opportunities.

Example:

  • £1.00 stake, free spins underway
  • Wilds lock on reels 3 and 4; two merge on reel 3 for a 2x multiplier
  • A high-value symbol completes a multi-way win using those wilds
  • The merged-wild multiplier doubles the contribution, and a cascade adds a follow-on win

The Bonus Top Up: Gamble for More Spins

Risk Your Spins for a Bigger Round

When the free spins are triggered, Anubis Wild Megaways offers an optional Bonus Top Up gamble. Rather than simply accepting your awarded spins, you can choose to gamble them for the chance to win more. It is a decision point that adds player agency to the bonus, much like the gamble mechanics found in other Inspired Megaways titles.

How the Bonus Top Up Works:

  1. You are awarded an initial set of free spins (12 for three scatters)
  2. You can choose to keep those spins safely, or gamble them
  3. A successful gamble awards you additional free spins
  4. If the gamble loses, you instead receive a mystery multiplier prize rather than the extra spins

Strategic Considerations:

  • Risk vs. Reward: More spins mean more chances for wilds to lock and merge, so the upside is real
  • Consolation Built In: Because a losing gamble still pays a mystery multiplier, the downside is softened compared to all-or-nothing gambles
  • Practice in Demo: The Anubis Wild Megaways free-play demo is the ideal place to learn the gamble’s rhythm before risking real money

The Bonus Top Up turns the start of the free spins into a meaningful choice. With the consolation mystery multiplier reducing the sting of a loss, many players will find it a tempting button to press.

Reading the Paytable:

Because this is a Megaways™ slot, all payouts are quoted as multiples of your total stake, and wins are awarded for matching symbols on consecutive reels from the left. With up to 117,649 ways active, even mid-value symbols can produce respectable returns when they appear on every reel.

How to Win: Strategy & Tips

Anubis Wild Megaways is a medium-volatility game, which means a sensible, patient approach pays dividends. Here is how to give yourself the best shot at the pharaoh’s gold.

Bankroll Management

  • Match your bet to your bankroll: With a range of £0.20 to £20.00, divide your session budget so you can comfortably absorb at least 100-150 spins. Medium volatility means features arrive at a reasonable pace, so you do not need the enormous buffer that a very-high-volatility slot demands.
  • Set win and loss limits: Decide in advance when you will walk away — both if you are ahead and if you are down. The 12,500x ceiling is a big target, but most sessions will revolve around the more frequent feature wins.
  • Treat the Bonus Top Up as optional: A losing gamble still pays a mystery multiplier, so the risk is softer than an all-or-nothing gamble — but it can still cost you spins. Factor it into your plan.

Base Game Strategy

  • Let the Megaways™ engine do the work: There is no skill in spin outcomes, so focus on bet sizing and session length rather than chasing patterns.
  • Value the top wild reel: This is a reliable source of base-game wilds. Combined with cascades, a well-placed wild can chain into multiple wins.
  • Watch for mystery symbols: A cluster revealing a premium symbol across several reels can produce a strong multi-way payout.

Free Spins Strategy

  • The free spins are the prize: This is where locking, merging multiplier wilds make the difference. The longer the round runs, the more wilds accumulate and the bigger the multipliers grow.
  • Weigh the Bonus Top Up carefully: More spins give wilds more chances to lock and merge, and the consolation mystery multiplier softens a loss — but only gamble what you are comfortable risking.
  • Practice in the demo first: Use the free-play version to understand how the locking wilds, covered-reel spins and Bonus Top Up behave before committing real funds.
