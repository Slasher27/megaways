Thunder Buddies for Life: Blueprint Gaming’s Ted Megaways

Grab a beer, clear the couch, and get ready for the most foul-mouthed teddy bear in Hollywood. Ted Megaways is Blueprint Gaming’s riotous follow-up to its hugely popular original Ted slot, released on July 30, 2020, and this time the walking, talking bear brings the full Megaways™ engine to the party. Built on six reels with up to 117,649 ways to win, the game pairs the official movie licence with cascading wins, a reel-flooding Mystery Ted feature, and a Thunder Buddies free spins round where an unlimited progressive multiplier can climb spin after spin toward a colossal 50,000x max win.

The presentation is pure Ted. The action unfolds in the familiar Boston living room, with the sofa, television and scattered beer bottles setting the scene while Ted himself lounges beside the reels, chipping in with wisecracks and voice lines lifted straight from the films. The symbols are packed with movie references — Tami-Lynn, rubber ducks and beer bottles all make an appearance — and the soundtrack swings between laid-back slacker vibes and the crackling energy of a thunderstorm when the bonus round approaches.

What makes Ted Megaways more than a licensed cash-in is the strength of its mechanics. In the base game, Mystery Ted can wake at random and hurl up to 100 mystery symbols onto the reels, all of which transform into the same paying symbol for potentially enormous multi-way hits. Trigger the Thunder Buddies Bonus and you face a genuine decision: bank a safe 12 free spins, or spin the Thunder Wheel to gamble for more spins and a higher guaranteed Megaways floor. Once the free spins begin, every cascade nudges the win multiplier up by one with no ceiling, and collecting Ted symbols extends the round while pushing the minimum ways ever closer to the full 117,649.

With an RTP of 96.03%, high volatility, a betting range of £0.10 to £10.00, and that headline 50,000x potential (capped at £250,000), Ted Megaways is aimed squarely at players who can stomach lean stretches in exchange for a bonus round with genuinely elite win potential.

Whether you are here for the movie nostalgia, the Megaways mathematics, or the dream of an endless multiplier chain, this thunder buddy has plenty to offer. Let’s break down every feature in the game.

The Megaways™ Engine: 117,649 Ways to Party

Understanding Megaways™ in Ted

At the core of Ted Megaways sits the licensed Megaways™ engine, the variable-ways system pioneered by Big Time Gaming and put to prolific use under Blueprint’s licence. Instead of fixed paylines, each reel displays a different number of symbols on every spin, and the total ways to win are recalculated dynamically each time.

How Megaways™ Works in Ted Megaways:

  • 6 vertical reels, each capable of showing between 2 and 7 symbols per spin
  • A horizontal top reel adds one extra symbol position above each of reels two, three, four and five
  • Ways to win are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
  • Maximum ways: the full board delivers 117,649 ways to win
  • Minimum ways: the leanest possible board still provides 324 ways
  • Wins pay for matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right, regardless of position

Win Formation Principles:

  • Symbol size and exact row position are irrelevant — only the reel sequence matters
  • A winning combination must start on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
  • Multiple separate winning combinations can land and pay on a single spin
  • Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your total stake
  • The ways count changes every spin, so no two spins feel mathematically identical

The 324-way floor is worth noting: it is meaningfully higher than the 64-way minimum found on many classic Megaways grids, thanks to the top reel and the game’s reel configuration, so even Ted’s laziest spins keep a reasonable number of ways in play.

Cascading Reactions

Like all the best Megaways™ titles, Ted Megaways runs on cascading reactions. When you land a winning combination:

  1. The symbols that formed the win are removed from the grid
  2. The symbols above drop down to fill the empty spaces
  3. New symbols fall in from above to complete the reels
  4. If the new arrangement creates another win, it pays and the process repeats
  5. Cascades continue until no further wins land

In the base game, cascades are a pleasant way to stretch a single spin into a chain of payouts. In the Thunder Buddies free spins, they become the entire engine of the game’s win potential — because every cascade there pushes the unlimited multiplier one step higher.

Mystery Ted: The Bear Wakes Up

Up to 100 Mystery Symbols in One Hit

The signature base-game moment in Ted Megaways is the Mystery Ted feature. At random, Ted stirs from the couch and launches up to 100 mystery symbols onto the reels. Once the dust settles, every mystery symbol on the board transforms into the same randomly chosen paying symbol (scatters excluded), instantly rearranging the grid into what can be a monstrous multi-way win.

How Mystery Ted Works:

  • The feature triggers randomly on any base-game spin
  • Ted throws a volley of mystery symbols across the six reels
  • Anywhere from a handful to 100 mystery symbols can land
  • All mystery symbols reveal simultaneously as one matching symbol
  • Resulting wins then feed into the cascade mechanic as normal

Why Mystery Ted Matters:

  • Base-Game Lifeline: In a high-volatility game, this is the primary source of big wins outside the bonus
  • Scales With Coverage: A heavy mystery drop that reveals a premium symbol can blanket the board
  • Cascade Synergy: A big transformation win clears huge sections of the grid, inviting follow-up cascade wins
  • Anticipation: The reveal moment — will it be the Ted logo or a lowly nine? — is the game’s best base-game theatre

Example Mystery Ted Scenarios:

Scenario 1 — Premium Reveal:

  • £1.00 stake
  • Mystery Ted lands roughly 40 mystery symbols
  • All reveal as the high-paying Ted logo, connecting across all six reels for a large multi-way payout

Scenario 2 — Cascade Chain:

  • £1.00 stake
  • A moderate mystery drop reveals beer bottle symbols for a mid-sized win
  • The cascade removes them, and the refill lands a second natural win on the restocked board

Mystery Ted is the feature that keeps base-game sessions alive. It does not hit constantly — this is a high-volatility slot, after all — but when the bear wakes up in a good mood, it can deliver bonus-round-sized money from a single spin.

The Thunder Buddies Bonus: Choose Your Storm

Triggering the Bonus

The main event in Ted Megaways is the Thunder Buddies Bonus, triggered by landing four or more scatter symbols anywhere in view. True to the films — where Ted and John sing the Thunder Buddies song to get through storms — the bonus opens with a rumble of thunder and a genuine strategic choice.

You have two paths:

  • Play it safe — collect a guaranteed 12 free spins and start the round immediately
  • Spin the Thunder Wheel — gamble your trigger for a potentially better package

This decision point is the heart of the game’s personality. Cautious players can lock in a solid round every time, while thrill-seekers can chase the wheel for upgraded spin counts and, crucially, higher guaranteed Megaways during the round.

The Thunder Wheel

Choose to gamble and the Thunder Wheel spins, awarding one of several packages that combine different numbers of free spins with a raised minimum Megaways floor. A higher guaranteed Megaways value means every free spin plays with more ways to win, dramatically improving the quality of each spin in the round.

Thunder Wheel Considerations:

  • Outcomes vary the spin count and the guaranteed minimum Megaways together
  • A boosted Megaways floor makes every cascade — and therefore every multiplier step — more likely
  • As with any gamble, results can land below the safe option, so weigh your appetite for risk
  • Where the bonus buy is available, purchasing the feature takes you straight to the Thunder Wheel

Thunder Buddies Free Spins: The Unlimited Multiplier

Cascades That Never Stop Counting

Once the free spins begin, Ted Megaways reveals its true win engine: a progressive win multiplier that starts at 1x and increases by +1 with every cascading win, with no upper limit and no reset between spins for the duration of the round.

How the Free Spins Multiplier Works:

  • The round begins with the multiplier at 1x
  • Every cascade during the round — from any spin — adds +1 to the multiplier
  • The multiplier never resets during the bonus; it only climbs
  • All wins are multiplied by the current value, so late-round wins are worth far more than early ones

This is the classic Blueprint-style Megaways bonus mathematics, and it is where the 50,000x ceiling lives. A long chain of cascades early in the round sets up a towering multiplier, and any substantial multi-way win landed afterwards gets amplified accordingly.

Ted Symbol Collection: Extending the Party

Layered on top of the multiplier is a Ted symbol collection mechanic that gives the round its escalating structure:

  • Ted symbols landing during free spins are collected
  • Every three Ted symbols collected awards two additional free spins
  • Each collection step also increases the guaranteed minimum Megaways, climbing toward a locked 117,649 ways

Why the Collection Mechanic Matters:

  • Self-Extending Rounds: A hot round keeps feeding itself extra spins
  • Compounding Quality: More guaranteed ways means more frequent wins, which means more cascades, which means a higher multiplier
  • Snowball Potential: The combination of unlimited multiplier, extra spins and a rising Megaways floor is how this game produces its most explosive results

Example Free Spins Sequence:

  • £1.00 stake, Thunder Wheel awards 15 free spins with a raised Megaways floor
  • Early spins chain several cascades, lifting the multiplier to 8x
  • Three collected Ted symbols add two spins and push the guaranteed ways higher
  • A premium multi-way hit worth 25x base lands at an 11x multiplier: 25x × 11 = 275x stake from one spin
  • The round continues, with every further cascade still raising the multiplier

Bonus Buy

Where regulations permit (not in the UK), Ted Megaways offers a bonus buy priced at 150x your stake, which takes you directly to the Thunder Wheel. Note that the buy does not hand you the safe 12-spin option — you are committed to the wheel’s outcome, which fits the feature’s gambling spirit but adds variance to an already volatile purchase.

How to Win: Strategy & Tips

Ted Megaways is a genuinely high-volatility game, and your approach should respect that. Here is how to party responsibly.

Bankroll Management

  • Budget for drought: High volatility means the base game can run cold for long stretches. Size your bets so your session bankroll covers at least 200-250 spins; the £0.10 minimum makes this achievable on modest budgets.
  • Set win and loss limits: The 50,000x ceiling is real but rare. Decide in advance when you will walk away, and treat any bonus round that pays well as a natural exit point.
  • Price the bonus buy honestly: At 150x stake, the buy is a large outlay that lands you on a gamble wheel. Only use it with money you can comfortably lose, and never chase a poor wheel result with another immediate purchase.

Base Game Strategy

  • Mystery Ted is your base-game engine: Outside the bonus, the transformation feature is where the meaningful wins come from. There is no way to influence it — patience is the only strategy.
  • Respect the wild’s limits: With wilds confined to the horizontal top reel, base-game combinations lean heavily on natural symbol alignment and cascades. Do not expect wild-driven rescues.
  • Use the demo first: The free-play version lets you learn the rhythm of Mystery Ted and the bonus trigger frequency before staking real money.

Bonus Round Strategy

  • Safe spins versus the Thunder Wheel: The guaranteed 12 spins are a solid, known quantity. The wheel offers upgraded packages with higher guaranteed Megaways — the key stat for round quality — but can also land below the safe option. Conservative players should collect; variance-tolerant players chasing the big number should spin.
  • Value the Megaways floor over raw spin count: A round with fewer spins but a high guaranteed Megaways floor will usually outperform a longer round on a lean board, because more ways means more cascades and a faster-climbing multiplier.
  • Let the multiplier do the work: The unlimited, never-resetting multiplier is the entire win model. Long rounds that keep collecting Ted symbols are where the game’s headline potential lives.

The game is rated high volatility. Expect extended quiet spells in the base game, punctuated by Mystery Ted transformations and the occasional bonus round where the real win potential is concentrated.

How do I trigger the Thunder Buddies Bonus?

Land four or more scatter symbols anywhere in view. You then choose between a guaranteed 12 free spins or a spin of the Thunder Wheel, which awards varying combinations of free spins and higher guaranteed Megaways.

It is one of Blueprint’s better licensed Megaways efforts: mechanically deep, thematically charming, and armed with a bonus round that can genuinely deliver on its headline number. If you enjoy high-variance Megaways slots and do not mind a teddy bear insulting you between spins, this thunder buddy deserves a spot on your playlist.
