The Megaways™ Engine: 117,649 Ways to Crack the Vault

Understanding Megaways™ in Casino Heist

At the core of Casino Heist Megaways sits the licensed Megaways™ engine, the variable-ways system that recalculates the number of ways to win on every single spin. Instead of fixed paylines, each reel can display a different number of symbols, so the math constantly shifts beneath your feet.

How Megaways™ Works in Casino Heist:

  • 6 vertical reels, each capable of showing between 2 and 7 symbols per spin
  • Ways to win are calculated by multiplying the symbol counts across all reels
  • Maximum ways: 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 = 117,649 ways to win
  • Minimum ways: 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 64 ways to win
  • Wins pay for matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right, regardless of their position

Win Formation Principles:

  • Only the reel sequence matters — symbol size and exact row are irrelevant
  • A winning combination must start on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
  • Multiple separate winning combinations can land and pay on a single spin
  • Every payout is a multiple of your total stake, not a per-line bet
  • The number of ways changes every spin, so the rhythm of play never settles

A full 117,649-way board is where Casino Heist Megaways is at its most explosive, especially when the tumble feature and a multiplier wild combine on the same spin.

The Tumble Feature

Like most Pragmatic Play Megaways titles, Casino Heist Megaways uses a tumble feature (also called cascades or avalanches). When you land a winning combination:

  1. The symbols that formed the win are removed from the grid
  2. The symbols above them drop down to fill the empty spaces
  3. Fresh symbols fall in from above to complete the reels
  4. If the new arrangement creates another win, it pays and the process repeats
  5. Tumbles continue until no further wins land

This means a single paid spin can chain into a sequence of wins. The tumble feature is especially powerful during free spins, where any multiplier wild that lands stays in play and applies to every subsequent tumble in the same sequence.

Multiplier Wilds: The Engine of Every Big Score

Wilds That Pay Off in a Big Way

The signature mechanic of Casino Heist Megaways is the multiplier wild. The wild symbol substitutes for any paying symbol to help complete combinations, but its real value is the multiplier it carries when it lands as part of a win.

How Multiplier Wilds Work:

  • A wild substitutes for all paying symbols (it does not replace the scatter)
  • When a wild contributes to a winning combination, its multiplier is applied to that win
  • Multiplier values can range from modest boosts all the way up to a massive 40x
  • If multiple multiplier wilds combine in the same win, their values can stack for an even bigger payout

Why Multiplier Wilds Matter:

The multiplier wild is the heart of Casino Heist Megaways. In the base game it provides welcome boosts, but in free spins — where it appears more freely and feeds the tumble chain — it is the difference between a quiet session and a vault-cracking score.

Ante Bet: Improve Your Odds of the Score

Paying More to Trigger More Often

For players who prefer to earn their way into the bonus rather than buy it outright, Casino Heist Megaways offers an Ante Bet option. Activating the Ante Bet increases your total stake per spin in exchange for a significantly improved chance of triggering the free spins feature by landing the suitcase scatters more frequently.

How the Ante Bet Works:

The Ante Bet is a middle ground between standard play and the Bonus Buy. It keeps you in the base game, where wins can still land, while shortening the average wait for the free spins round.
