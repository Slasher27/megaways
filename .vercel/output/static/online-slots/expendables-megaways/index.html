The Megaways™ Engine: 117,649 Ways to Win

Understanding Megaways™ in The Expendables

At the core of The Expendables Megaways is the licensed Megaways™ engine, the variable-ways system that transformed slot mathematics. Instead of fixed paylines, each reel can display a different number of symbols on every spin, and the total ways to win are recalculated dynamically. This is what powers the game’s headline figure of 117,649 ways.

How Megaways™ Works in The Expendables:

  • 6 vertical reels, each capable of showing between 2 and 7 symbols per spin
  • Ways to win are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
  • Maximum ways: 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 = 117,649 ways to win
  • Minimum ways: when the reels land short, the active ways drop into the low hundreds
  • Wins pay for matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right, regardless of their position on the reel

Win Formation Principles:

  • Symbol size and exact row position are irrelevant — only the reel sequence matters
  • A winning combination must start on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
  • Multiple separate winning combinations can land and pay on a single spin
  • Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your total stake, not a per-line bet
  • The number of ways changes every spin, so no two spins feel mathematically identical

Cascading Reels

Like most Megaways™ slots, The Expendables Megaways uses cascading reels (also called tumbles or avalanches). When you land a winning combination:

  1. The symbols that formed the win are removed from the grid
  2. The symbols above them drop down to fill the empty spaces
  3. New symbols fall in from above to complete the reels
  4. If the new arrangement creates another win, it pays and the process repeats
  5. Cascades continue until no further wins land

This mechanic means a single paid spin can produce a chain of consecutive wins. Crucially, cascades are not just about extra wins — during free spins, every cascade also raises the win multiplier, which is where the game’s biggest payouts are forged. A long cascade chain in the bonus round can stack a formidable multiplier onto every subsequent win.

The Bullet Bonus: Fill the Chamber, Spray the Wilds

Collecting Your Ammunition

The standout base-game mechanic in The Expendables Megaways is the Bullet Bonus, a persistent collection feature that builds toward an explosive payoff. Whenever a bullet symbol lands on the reels, it is collected and stored in a gun chamber displayed on screen. The chamber holds five bullets, and each one you collect carries over from spin to spin until the chamber is full.

How the Bullet Bonus Works:

  1. Bullet symbols that land are collected and added to the on-screen chamber
  2. The chamber tracks your progress toward the five required bullets
  3. Once the chamber is filled with five bullets, the Bullet Bonus triggers
  4. The bullets are fired onto the reels, converting struck symbols into wilds
  5. Those new wilds substitute for paying symbols to help complete winning combinations

Why the Bullet Bonus Matters:

  • Persistent Progress: Because collected bullets carry over, every bullet symbol moves you closer to the next trigger
  • Guaranteed Wild Injection: When it fires, the feature plants wilds directly onto the grid, improving your immediate win potential
  • Base-Game Excitement: It gives the base game a tangible goal beyond simply waiting for free spins
  • Synergy with Cascades: Wilds created by the Bullet Bonus can help spark a winning combination that then cascades into further wins

The Bullet Bonus is the engine room of the base game. It keeps you engaged between free-spins triggers and provides a reliable source of wild-assisted wins, giving the slot a sense of momentum even during quieter stretches.

Free Spins: The Unlimited Multiplier Mission

Triggering the Bonus

The headline feature of The Expendables Megaways is its Free Spins round, triggered by landing skull scatter symbols. The number of free spins you receive scales directly with how many scatters you land in the triggering spin.

Free Spins Trigger Table:

  • 4 skull scatters → 12 free spins
  • 5 skull scatters → 16 free spins
  • 6 skull scatters → 20 free spins
  • 7 skull scatters → 24 free spins

Better still, the round is retriggerable. Landing additional skull scatters while the feature is running awards extra free spins, extending your mission and giving the multiplier more time to climb.

The Unlimited Win Multiplier

What elevates the free spins round above a standard bonus is the unlimited win multiplier. The multiplier begins at 1x and increases by +1 with every cascade that occurs during the feature. Crucially, there is no cap — the multiplier persists and grows for the entire round.

How the Multiplier Builds:

  • Each free spin can produce one or more cascades from a single winning hit
  • Every cascade adds +1 to the running multiplier
  • The multiplier does not reset between spins, so it carries forward across the whole round
  • A long, win-heavy round can push the multiplier into genuinely huge territory

This persistent, uncapped multiplier is the primary route to the game’s 20,000x maximum win. The longer you can keep cascades landing, the higher the multiplier climbs, and the more devastating each subsequent win becomes.

Example:

  • You trigger free spins and the multiplier has climbed to 10x after several cascades
  • A symbol then pays 50x across a high-ways board
  • With the 10x multiplier: 50x × 10 = 500x stake from that single hit, before any further cascades push the multiplier even higher

The Expendables Feature

Working hand in hand with the multiplier is the Expendables Feature, which activates during free spins. As the round progresses, low-value combo symbols (the 10 through Ace royals) are removed from the reels for the remainder of the feature.

How the Expendables Feature Helps:

  • Each time a low-value symbol wins, it is permanently cleared from the reels for the rest of the round
  • With the low-payers gone, the reels become increasingly populated by high-value mercenary symbols
  • This raises the probability of landing larger, higher-paying combinations as the round goes on
  • Combined with the rising multiplier, the later spins of a free-spins round can be dramatically more rewarding than the early ones

The Expendables Feature is a clever piece of design: it makes the free spins round build toward a crescendo, with both the multiplier and the quality of the symbol pool improving as the mission unfolds.

Super Stake Mode: Force the Action

Doubling Down for a Guaranteed Feature

For players who do not want to wait for features to arrive organically, Super Stake Mode offers a way to force the pace. By doubling your total bet, you activate Super Stake, which guarantees that a random feature is added to the reels on every spin.

How Super Stake Mode Works:

  • Toggle Super Stake on to double your stake per spin
  • On every spin, the game guarantees one random in-play boost
  • Typical boosts include a wave of additional wild symbols or the removal of low-value symbols to improve your board
  • The mode persists until you switch it off

Super Stake vs. Bonus Buy:

It is worth being clear that Super Stake is not a traditional bonus buy — it does not let you purchase direct entry into the free spins round for a fixed multiple of your stake. Instead, it raises the intensity of every base-game spin by guaranteeing a feature, which in turn improves your chances of building toward free spins or the Bullet Bonus. If you enjoy a more aggressive, feature-dense base game and are comfortable betting double, Super Stake delivers it.

The Gamble Feature: Risk It for More

Doubling or Quadrupling Your Win

The Expendables Megaways also includes an optional Gamble Feature that lets you risk a win for the chance to multiply it. After a winning spin, you can choose to gamble rather than bank your payout.

How the Gamble Feature Works:

  • Choose the colour of a playing card to double your win
  • Choose the suit of a playing card to quadruple your win
  • A correct guess multiplies your win; an incorrect guess forfeits it
  • You can keep gambling to chase progressively larger multiples, up to the feature’s ceiling

The Gamble Feature is purely optional and entirely down to your appetite for risk. Conservative players can simply collect their wins, while thrill-seekers can roll the dice for a bigger return — fitting neatly into the high-volatility, high-stakes spirit of the game.

Symbols & Paytable

The Expendables Megaways uses a classic two-tier symbol hierarchy dressed in full action-movie regalia, with special symbols layered on top to drive the features.

High-Value Symbols:

  • The mercenary characters from the franchise serve as the premium symbols
  • These deliver the largest base-game payouts when they land across multiple reels
  • They are also the symbols that benefit most from the Expendables Feature, which clears the low-payers to make room for them

Low-Value Symbols:

  • The traditional royal card values (10, J, Q, K, A), styled in a gritty, metallic font to match the military theme
  • These are the symbols removed from the reels by the Expendables Feature during free spins

Special Symbols:

  • Skull Scatter — four or more trigger the Free Spins round (12, 16, 20 or 24 spins for 4-7 scatters)
  • Bullet Symbol — collected in the chamber; five fill it and trigger the Bullet Bonus
  • Wild Symbol — substitutes for paying symbols to complete combinations; also injected by the Bullet Bonus and Super Stake Mode

Reading the Paytable:

Because this is a Megaways™ slot, all payouts are quoted as multiples of your total stake, and wins are awarded for matching symbols on consecutive reels from the left. With up to 117,649 ways active, even mid-value symbols can produce respectable returns when they appear on every reel — and during free spins, the running multiplier applies on top.

How to Win: Strategy & Tips

The Expendables Megaways is a very high volatility game (rated 5 out of 5), which demands a disciplined, patient approach. Here is how to give yourself the best mission.

Bankroll Management

  • Bet small relative to your bankroll: With 5/5 volatility, dry spells can be long. Size your bet so you can comfortably absorb several hundred spins, because the big wins are infrequent but potentially huge.
  • Set firm win and loss limits: Decide in advance when you will walk away. The 20,000x ceiling is a major target, but most sessions will revolve around the Bullet Bonus and the occasional free-spins trigger.
  • Be cautious with Super Stake: Doubling your bet intensifies the action but also doubles your spend, which accelerates a bankroll’s decline during a cold streak. Use it deliberately, not reflexively.

Base Game Strategy

  • Track the Bullet Bonus chamber: Because bullets carry over, you always have a tangible goal in the base game. The closer the chamber is to full, the closer you are to a guaranteed wave of wilds.
  • Let the Megaways™ engine work: There is no skill in spin outcomes, so focus on bet sizing and session length rather than chasing patterns.
  • Use cascades to your advantage: A single paid spin can chain into multiple wins, and in free spins each cascade also raises the multiplier.

Free Spins Strategy

  • Aim for high scatter counts: Landing six or seven skulls awards 20 or 24 spins, giving the multiplier far more time to climb.
  • Value long cascade chains: Since the multiplier rises +1 per cascade and never resets, the rounds with frequent cascades are where the biggest wins live.
  • Trust the Expendables Feature: As low-value symbols are stripped away, the back half of a free-spins round becomes increasingly potent — patience within the round pays off.
