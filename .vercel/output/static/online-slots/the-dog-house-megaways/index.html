Who Let the Dogs Out? Pragmatic Play Unleashes The Dog House Megaways

Grab a leash and a pocketful of treats, because The Dog House Megaways turns Pragmatic Play’s most beloved backyard into a 117,649-way playground. Released in August 2020 as the follow-up to the smash-hit original, this sequel takes everything players adored about The Dog House — the goofy pups, the picket fences, the multiplier wilds — and rebuilds it on the licensed Megaways™ engine from Big Time Gaming. The result is one of the most popular dog-themed slots ever made, and a game that still commands a devoted following years after release.

The setting is pure suburban sunshine. Six reels sit inside a bright cartoon backyard, framed by a wooden fence, a kennel, and a sky full of fluffy clouds. The canine cast returns in full: a slobbering rottweiler, a spotty Dalmatian, a wrinkly pug and a long-suffering dachshund, backed up by bones, collars and card royals. A bouncy, whistling soundtrack keeps the mood light — which is a deliberate contrast, because underneath the cheerful exterior lurks one of Pragmatic Play’s most volatile maths models.

Make no mistake: this is a very high volatility (5/5) slot with a 12,305x max win, and its heart beats inside the bonus round. Land three or more doghouse scatters and you face the game’s defining decision — Sticky Wilds Free Spins, where multiplier wilds lock in place and compound with every spin, or Raining Wilds Free Spins, where up to six multiplier wilds shower onto the reels on every single spin. Both routes are powered by the game’s signature 1x, 2x and 3x wild multipliers, which multiply together when they share a winning combination.

With a default RTP of 96.55%, a betting range of £0.20 to £100, and a bonus buy for players who cannot wait for the scatters to land, The Dog House Megaways offers a complete modern package. It is a slot built for players who accept lean stretches in exchange for bonus rounds that can genuinely bite.

So whistle for the pack, pick your kennel, and let’s dig up every bone this backyard has buried.

The Megaways™ Engine: 117,649 Ways to Fetch

How Megaways™ Works in The Dog House

The original Dog House played out on a fixed 5×3 grid with 20 paylines. The Megaways sequel throws that rulebook in the kennel. Under Big Time Gaming’s licensed Megaways™ system, each of the six reels displays a different number of symbols on every spin — anywhere from two to seven — and the number of ways to win is recalculated dynamically each time.

The Core Mechanics:

  • 6 vertical reels, each showing between 2 and 7 symbols per spin
  • Ways to win equal the number of symbols on each reel multiplied together
  • Maximum ways: 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 = 117,649 ways to win
  • Minimum ways: 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 64 ways to win
  • Wins pay for 3 to 6 matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right, regardless of position

Win Formation Principles:

  • Only the reel sequence matters — the exact row a symbol lands on is irrelevant
  • Combinations must begin on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
  • Several separate winning combinations can pay on a single spin
  • All payouts are expressed as multiples of your total stake
  • The ways count shifts every spin, so the grid never feels static

No Cascades — And Why That Matters

Here is the twist that surprises Megaways veterans: The Dog House Megaways has no cascading or tumbling reels. Most Megaways titles remove winning symbols and drop new ones in for potential chain reactions; this game resolves each spin in one pass, exactly like a classic slot.

The omission is deliberate. Pragmatic Play concentrated the game’s power in its multiplier wilds rather than in cascade chains. It gives the base game a slower, more traditional rhythm — some players find it refreshingly clean, others miss the extra churn of tumbling symbols. Either way, it means every big moment in this game flows through the wilds, so let’s meet them.

Multiplier Wilds: The Teeth Behind the Bark

1x, 2x and 3x Wilds That Multiply Together

The signature mechanic carried over from the original Dog House is the multiplier wild. Wild symbols land on reels 2, 3, 4 and 5 and every one of them arrives stamped with a 1x, 2x or 3x multiplier. A wild substitutes for all paying symbols (scatters excepted), and its multiplier is applied to any win it completes.

The magic happens when wilds gang up. If two or more multiplier wilds participate in the same winning combination, their values multiply together rather than simply adding:

  • Wild 2x + Wild 3x in one win = 6x the payout
  • Wild 3x + Wild 3x in one win = 9x the payout
  • Three 3x wilds in one chain = 27x the payout

Why This Mechanic Defines the Game:

  • Every mode benefits: Multiplier wilds appear in the base game and both free spins modes
  • Geometric growth: Compounding multiplication is how ordinary hits become screenshot material
  • Megaways synergy: On a high-ways board, a single multiplied symbol chain can pay across dozens of ways simultaneously
  • The path to 12,305x: The max win is reached when stacked multipliers meet a full-board premium hit during free spins

Example — Base Game Multiplier Chain:

  • £1.00 stake, high-ways spin
  • The rottweiler lands on reels 1 through 6, with a 2x wild completing the win on reel 3 and a 3x wild on reel 5
  • The combined 6x multiplier turns a solid multi-way hit into a session-defining payout

In the base game these moments are sporadic — this is a 5/5 volatility slot, and quiet stretches are part of the deal. But the wilds are also the raw material for both bonus rounds, which is where the game truly slips its leash.

Sticky Wilds Free Spins: Build Your Own Minefield

Wilds That Lock In and Compound

Sticky Wilds Free Spins is the patient hunter’s choice. You receive between 7 and 20 free spins, and every multiplier wild that lands on reels 2-5 locks in place for the remainder of the round. Nothing resets; the board only ever gets more dangerous.

How Sticky Wilds Works:

  1. A wild lands with its 1x, 2x or 3x multiplier attached
  2. It freezes in position and stays there for every remaining spin
  3. Each new spin can add further sticky wilds to the collection
  4. Every win that passes through the locked wilds is multiplied — and multiple wilds in one combination multiply together

Why Sticky Wilds Is the High-Ceiling Choice:

  • Compounding board state: By the closing spins, a good round can have the middle reels studded with multipliers
  • Multiplied everything: Once wilds are locked, even low-value royal wins can be transformed
  • The max-win route: The game’s biggest recorded outcomes typically come from a Sticky Wilds board packed with 3x wilds meeting a premium symbol chain
  • Genuine drama: Early wild placement shapes the entire round, giving each bonus its own narrative arc

The Risk:

The flipside is brutal simplicity — if the wilds refuse to land early, a 7-spin round can evaporate with almost nothing to show. Sticky Wilds is the more volatile of the two modes: lower floor, dramatically higher ceiling. That trade-off is exactly why many players consider it the “true” Dog House Megaways experience.

Example Round:

  • £1.00 stake, 12 free spins from four scatters
  • Spins 1-4 lock a 2x, a 3x and another 3x wild across reels 3 and 4
  • On spin 7, the Dalmatian connects across five reels through all three wilds: the win is multiplied by 2 × 3 × 3 = 18x
  • The remaining spins continue to feed wins through the locked multipliers

Raining Wilds Free Spins: Let It Pour

Fresh Wilds on Every Single Spin

Raining Wilds Free Spins is the steadier alternative. You receive between 15 and 30 free spins — roughly double the Sticky Wilds allocation — and on every spin, up to six multiplier wilds rain down onto random positions on the reels. The wilds do not stick; each spin gets its own fresh downpour.

How Raining Wilds Works:

  1. Each free spin begins with the reels spinning as normal
  2. Up to 6 wilds drop into random positions, each carrying a 1x, 2x or 3x multiplier
  3. Wins are evaluated with the rained-in wilds included, multipliers compounding as usual
  4. The board clears and the next spin brings a brand-new scatter of wilds

Why Raining Wilds Is the Consistency Choice:

  • Guaranteed action: Wild involvement on every spin means far fewer dead spins than Sticky mode
  • More spins overall: 15-30 spins gives the round room to breathe and accumulate
  • Lower variance within the bonus: You are far less dependent on a lucky early setup
  • Still real potential: Several 3x wilds landing in one winning chain can produce major spikes

The Trade-Off:

Because nothing accumulates, Raining Wilds rarely reaches the apocalyptic board states that Sticky Wilds can build. Its ceiling is lower in practice, but its floor is much friendlier. If you have ever watched a Sticky Wilds round end in silence, you will understand the appeal.

Example Round:

  • £1.00 stake, 25 free spins from five scatters
  • Spin 9: four wilds rain in, two of them (2x and 3x) join a pug combination for a 6x-multiplied win
  • Spin 17: a single 3x wild completes a rottweiler chain across a near-full board
  • The round accumulates steadily rather than exploding all at once

Bonus Buy: Skip the Wait, Pay the Toll

For players who would rather not sit through the dry spells that very high volatility demands, The Dog House Megaways includes a bonus buy (where local regulations permit it). Paying 100x your current stake triggers the free spins round instantly, complete with the usual choice between Sticky Wilds and Raining Wilds.

Points to Weigh Before Buying:

  • Cost scales with stake: At £1.00 a spin, each purchase costs £100
  • The choice remains yours: Buying does not lock you into either mode
  • Variance stays extreme: A bought Sticky Wilds round can still come up empty — 100x buys entry, not a result
  • Budget discipline matters: Repeated buys burn through a bankroll far faster than organic play

The buy option is a natural fit for a game whose personality lives almost entirely inside its bonus. Just remember that the 5/5 volatility applies to bought rounds every bit as much as triggered ones.

How to Win: Strategy & Tips

The Dog House Megaways is a very high volatility game, and it should be approached with the respect that rating deserves. Here is how to keep your session on a leash.

Bankroll Management

  • Budget for droughts: With 5/5 volatility, stretches of several hundred spins between bonuses are entirely realistic. Size your bet so your session bankroll covers at least 200-300 spins.
  • Use the full bet range: The £0.20 minimum lets cautious players experience every feature at minimal cost, while the £100 ceiling accommodates high rollers.
  • Set hard limits: Decide your stop-loss and your walk-away-win figure before you spin. A 12,305x ceiling is seductive; discipline is what keeps the chase healthy.
  • Check your RTP version: The default is 96.55%, but 95.53% and 94.55% builds exist. The game’s info screen shows which one your casino runs — always look before you deposit.

Choosing Your Free Spins Mode

  • Chasing the max win? Pick Sticky Wilds. Compounding locked multipliers are the only realistic road to the game’s biggest payouts.
  • Protecting a modest bankroll? Pick Raining Wilds. Twice the spins and guaranteed wild activity give you a far steadier return profile.
  • Big stake on the line? Many experienced players choose Raining Wilds at higher stakes to reduce the chance of a total blank, and Sticky Wilds at lower stakes where a dead round hurts less.
  • Accept the variance: Neither choice changes the long-term RTP — it only changes how the value is distributed.

Base Game and Bonus Buy Strategy

  • Temper base-game expectations: With no cascades, the base game is a waiting room for the bonus. The multiplier wilds provide occasional jolts, but the feature is the destination.
  • Treat the bonus buy as a calculated splurge: At 100x per purchase, a handful of dry buys will dent any bankroll. If you buy, pre-commit to a fixed number of attempts.
  • Try the demo first: The free-play version is the ideal place to feel out both bonus modes and the game’s rhythm before staking real money.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the RTP of The Dog House Megaways?

The default Return to Player is 96.55%, which sits above the typical Megaways average of around 96%. Be aware that Pragmatic Play also distributes reduced versions at 95.53% and 94.55%, so it is worth checking the in-game information screen at your chosen casino.

What is the maximum win in The Dog House Megaways?

The maximum win is 12,305x your stake. It is most realistically approached through the Sticky Wilds Free Spins mode, where locked 2x and 3x multiplier wilds compound together beneath a premium symbol chain on a high-ways board.

How volatile is The Dog House Megaways?

Very. Pragmatic Play rates it at the top of its scale — very high volatility (5/5). Expect long quiet stretches in the base game, balanced by free spins rounds capable of genuinely large payouts.

How do I trigger the free spins?

Land three or more doghouse scatter symbols in a single spin. Three scatters award 7 spins (Sticky mode) or 15 spins (Raining mode), scaling up to 20 or 30 spins respectively for six scatters.

Should I choose Sticky Wilds or Raining Wilds?

Sticky Wilds (7-20 spins) locks every multiplier wild in place for the whole round — higher ceiling, riskier floor, and the route to the biggest wins. Raining Wilds (15-30 spins) drops up to six fresh multiplier wilds on every spin — steadier, more consistent action with a lower practical ceiling. Neither changes the RTP; it is purely a variance preference.

Does The Dog House Megaways have a bonus buy?

Yes. Where regulations allow, you can purchase the free spins round for 100x your stake, and you still get to choose between the Sticky Wilds and Raining Wilds modes after buying.

How do the wild multipliers work?

Wilds land on reels 2-5 carrying a 1x, 2x or 3x multiplier. A wild’s multiplier applies to any win it completes, and when two or more multiplier wilds appear in the same winning combination, their values multiply together — two 3x wilds mean a 9x boost, three mean 27x.

Who makes The Dog House Megaways and when was it released?

It was developed by Pragmatic Play using Big Time Gaming’s licensed Megaways™ engine and released on August 13, 2020, as the sequel to the studio’s hugely popular original, The Dog House.
