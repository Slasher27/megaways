The Megaways™ Mechanic: 16,807 Ways to Royal Riches

Understanding 5x7 Megaways™ in Kingmaker

Kingmaker Fully Loaded employs a 5-reel configuration of Big Time Gaming’s legendary Megaways™ engine, creating a distinct mathematical profile compared to the more common 6-reel Megaways™ slots. This results in 16,807 maximum ways to win—a number that represents perfect alignment where every reel displays its maximum 7 symbols simultaneously.

How 5x7 Megaways™ Works:

  • 5 vertical reels with 2-7 symbols per reel
  • No horizontal top reel (unlike 6-reel Megaways™ variants)
  • Ways to win calculated by multiplying symbols on each reel
  • Maximum ways: 7×7×7×7×7 = 16,807 ways to win
  • Minimum ways: 2×2×2×2×2 = 32 ways

Win Formation:

Wins are awarded for left-to-right adjacent symbols regardless of size. Core principles:

  • Symbol size doesn’t matter—a small symbol counts identically to a large one
  • Only consecutive reels from left to right form winning combinations
  • Multiple winning combinations can occur simultaneously on a single spin
  • All payouts are multiplied by your total stake
  • Cascading reactions remove winning symbols, with new symbols dropping from above
  • Process repeats until no more winning combinations form

Why 5-Reel Megaways™ Matters:

The 5-reel configuration creates several distinctive characteristics:

  • Different volatility profile: Slightly lower maximum ways means different hit frequency dynamics
  • Visual focus: Cleaner 5-reel layout allows more attention on the 4 Gem Multipliers above
  • Grid height emphasis: 7-symbol maximum height is more prominent on fewer reels
  • Strategic simplicity: Fewer reels to track makes gameplay easier to follow
  • Gem positioning: 4 gems across 5 reels (skipping reel 1) creates asymmetric design

While 16,807 ways is significantly lower than the 117,649 maximum of 6-reel Megaways™, Kingmaker compensates through its Gem Multiplier system, which can amplify wins dramatically regardless of ways count. A modest 3,000-way spin with a x80 combined gem multiplier far exceeds a 100,000-way spin without multiplier enhancement.
