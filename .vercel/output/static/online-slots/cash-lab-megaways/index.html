The Megaways™ Engine: 200,704 Ways in the Lab

Understanding the Layout

At the core of Cash Lab Megaways sits the licensed Megaways™ system, but iSoftBet has wired it up with extra apparatus. The grid uses six vertical reels, and on the four middle reels (reels 2 to 5) it adds horizontal reels running along the top and bottom of the board. These horizontal reels feed additional symbols into the columns they touch, pushing the maximum symbol count per reel up to eight and inflating the total ways far beyond a standard Megaways slot.

How the Ways Are Calculated:

  • 6 vertical reels, with the outer reels (1 and 6) and the middle reels carrying a variable number of symbols
  • Horizontal reels on the top and bottom of reels 2-5 add extra symbols to those columns
  • Ways to win are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
  • Maximum ways: this configuration produces up to 200,704 ways to win
  • Wins pay for matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right, regardless of row position

Win Formation Principles:

  • Only the sequence of reels matters — symbol position within a reel is irrelevant
  • A winning combination must start on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
  • Several separate winning combinations can land and pay on a single spin
  • Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your total stake, not a per-line bet
  • The number of ways shifts every spin, so the board is in constant flux

Cascading Wins

Like most Megaways™ titles, Cash Lab Megaways uses cascading wins (also called tumbles). When a winning combination lands:

  1. The symbols that formed the win are removed from the grid
  2. Remaining symbols drop down to fill the gaps
  3. Fresh symbols fall in from above to complete the reels
  4. Any new winning combination pays, and the process repeats
  5. Cascades continue until no further wins appear

Cascades are especially important here because they interact directly with the Gold Link multiplier, which climbs with each successive reaction. A single paid spin that triggers a Gold Link and then cascades repeatedly can stack a powerful multiplier onto an already huge ways count.

Wild Link: Turning a Reel Wild

Full-Reel Wild Conversion

The Wild Link feature is the first of the slot’s two signature Link mechanics. It triggers when wild symbols appear on both the top and bottom horizontal reels of the same column. When this happens, the entire vertical reel in that column is converted into wilds.

How Wild Link Works:

  • Wilds must align on both horizontal reels of a single column
  • The whole vertical reel for that column then turns wild
  • Wilds substitute for all paying crystal and royal symbols
  • A fully wild reel acts as a guaranteed contributor to any combination crossing it

A wild reel is enormously powerful on a 200,704-way board. Because every position on that reel counts toward combinations on adjacent reels, a single Wild Link can complete numerous multi-way wins simultaneously, turning an ordinary spin into a substantial payout.

Example:

  • £1.00 stake
  • Wilds land on both horizontal reels of column three
  • The full third reel converts to wilds
  • A high-value red crystal on reels one, two, four and five completes across the wild reel for a sizeable multi-way win

Gold Link: Wilds With an Escalating Multiplier

The Upgraded Link Feature

Gold Link is the premium evolution of Wild Link, and it is where Cash Lab Megaways reaches for its biggest numbers. Like Wild Link, Gold Link converts a full reel to wilds — but it also attaches an escalating multiplier that grows as cascades chain together.

How Gold Link Works:

  • The feature converts an entire reel to wilds, exactly like Wild Link
  • A multiplier is applied, starting at x1 and increasing by x1 with every cascade
  • As long as cascades keep landing, the multiplier keeps climbing
  • The accumulated multiplier is applied to the wins it helps create

Why Gold Link Is the Star:

  • It marries a full wild reel with a rising multiplier — two powerful mechanics in one
  • The longer the cascade chain, the higher the multiplier and the bigger the payout
  • On a Max Megaways board, a Gold Link with a long cascade run can produce explosive results

Example:

  • £1.00 stake
  • Gold Link converts reel four to wilds and begins at a x1 multiplier
  • The first reaction pays, then cascades; the multiplier rises to x2, then x3 across successive cascades
  • Each subsequent multi-way win is boosted by the growing multiplier, compounding the total

Combined with the Wild Link and Gold Link mechanics that remain active during the bonus, the unlimited multiplier is the engine behind the slot’s largest possible payouts.
