The Megaways™ Engine: 117,649 Ways to Strike Gold

Understanding Megaways™ in Aztec Gold

At the core of Aztec Gold Megaways beats the licensed Megaways™ engine, the variable-ways system that transformed slot mathematics. Instead of fixed paylines, every reel can show a different number of symbols on each spin, and the total ways to win are recalculated dynamically. This is what produces the game’s headline figure of 117,649 ways and its constantly shifting feel.

How Megaways™ Works in Aztec Gold:

  • 6 vertical reels, each capable of displaying between 2 and 7 symbols per spin
  • Ways to win are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
  • Maximum ways: 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 = 117,649 ways to win
  • Minimum ways: 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 64 ways to win
  • Wins pay for matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right, regardless of their row position

Win Formation Principles:

  • Symbol size and exact row position do not matter — only the reel sequence counts
  • A winning combination must begin on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
  • Multiple separate winning combinations can land and pay on a single spin
  • Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your total stake, not a per-line bet
  • Because the number of ways changes every spin, no two spins feel mathematically identical

Cascading Reactions

Like most Megaways™ slots, Aztec Gold Megaways uses cascading reactions (also called tumbles or avalanches). When a winning combination lands:

  1. The symbols that formed the win are removed from the grid
  2. The symbols above them drop down to fill the gaps
  3. Fresh symbols fall in from above to complete the reels
  4. If the new arrangement creates another win, it pays and the process repeats
  5. Cascades continue until no further wins land

This mechanic means a single paid spin can generate a chain of consecutive wins. On a full 117,649-way board, a well-timed cascade is where some of the game’s most satisfying base-game payouts originate, and it keeps the pace of play brisk and engaging between the bigger feature triggers.

Gold Cash Respins: The Hold-and-Win Heart of the Game

Triggering the Bonus

The signature feature of Aztec Gold Megaways is the Gold Cash Respins round, a hold-and-win style bonus built around the golden Sun Disc scatter symbols. To trigger it, you need to land five or more Sun Disc scatters on the reels in a single spin. When you do, the base game pauses and the respins feature begins with three respins on the clock.

This is the moment every expedition is chasing: the temple opens, the Sun Discs lock into place, and the hunt for gold begins in earnest.

How the Respins Work

The Gold Cash Respins round follows the classic hold-and-win formula, which makes it instantly intuitive even for newcomers to the format:

  1. The Sun Disc scatters that triggered the feature lock in place on the reels
  2. You are awarded three respins to collect more scatters
  3. Each new Sun Disc that lands resets the respin counter back to three
  4. The round continues as long as you keep landing fresh scatters within the three-spin window
  5. When three consecutive respins pass with no new scatter, the feature ends and your collected values pay out

Because every new scatter resets the count, a hot streak can keep the feature alive far longer than the initial three spins suggest — and that is where the round’s biggest payouts are built.

Coin Values, Multipliers and Jackpots

What makes Gold Cash Respins more than a simple respin gimmick is the extra layers stacked on top of the sticky-scatter mechanic:

  • Coin Values: Many Sun Discs carry a cash value attached to them, expressed as a multiple of your stake. These values are collected when the round ends.
  • Reel Multipliers: Fill an entire reel with Sun Disc scatters and you trigger a multiplier of up to 7x, which can dramatically inflate the values collected during the round.
  • Jackpot Prizes: Certain Sun Discs reveal one of three fixed jackpots — the Mini, Major and Mega — adding a collectible chase on top of the standard coin values, with the top tier worth up to 1,000x your stake.

Example Respins Scenario:

  • £1.00 stake triggers the feature with five Sun Discs
  • Over several respins you fill an entire reel, activating a multiplier
  • Coin values across the locked scatters are collected and boosted by that multiplier
  • A Major jackpot disc lands among them, adding a substantial fixed prize to the total

The combination of resetting respins, stacking coin values, reel multipliers and jackpot discs is exactly how Aztec Gold Megaways reaches toward its 19,200x maximum win. A near-perfect respins round, where reels fill and multipliers compound, is the realistic route to the game’s biggest payouts.
