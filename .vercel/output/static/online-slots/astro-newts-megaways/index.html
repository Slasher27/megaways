The Megaways™ Engine: Up to 235,298 Ways to Win

Understanding Megaways™ in Astro Newts

At the heart of Astro Newts Megaways beats the licensed Megaways™ engine, the variable-ways system that revolutionised slot mathematics. Rather than fixed paylines, every reel can display a different number of symbols on each spin, and the total ways to win are recalculated dynamically. This is what gives the game its constantly shifting rhythm and its base figure of 117,649 ways to win.

How Megaways™ Works in Astro Newts:

  • 6 vertical reels, each capable of showing between 2 and 7 symbols per spin
  • Ways to win are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
  • Maximum base ways: 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 = 117,649 ways to win
  • Minimum ways: 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 64 ways to win
  • Wins pay for matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right, regardless of their position on the reel

Win Formation Principles:

  • Symbol size and exact row position are irrelevant — only the reel sequence matters
  • A winning combination must start on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
  • Multiple separate winning combinations can land and pay on a single spin
  • Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your total stake, not a per-line bet
  • The number of ways changes every spin, so no two spins feel mathematically identical

Doubling the Ways on Uranus

Astro Newts Megaways has a clever twist on the standard formula. When you progress deep enough into the free spins feature to reach Uranus, the game switches to a pay-both-ways mode. Wins are now counted from left to right and right to left, which effectively doubles the maximum ways to win from 117,649 to a colossal 235,298. This is the headline number the game is famous for, and reaching it is one of the central goals of any serious session.

Cascading Reactions

Like most Megaways™ slots, Astro Newts Megaways employs cascading reactions (sometimes called tumbles or avalanches). When you land a winning combination:

  1. The symbols that formed the win are removed from the grid
  2. The symbols above them drop down to fill the empty spaces
  3. New symbols fall in from above to complete the reels
  4. If the new arrangement creates another win, it pays and the process repeats
  5. Cascades continue until no further wins land

This mechanic means a single paid spin can produce a chain of consecutive wins. In Astro Newts Megaways, cascades are especially potent because of how they interact with the Mega Fission feature — every cascade is another chance for symbols to split and multiply.

Mega Fission: The Symbol-Splitting Mechanic That Defines the Game

One Win, Many Symbols

The signature mechanic of Astro Newts Megaways is Mega Fission, and it is the feature that gives the slot its unique identity. The concept is brilliantly simple but mechanically powerful: on every winning spin, the symbols that formed the win can split in two. Where you once had a single matching symbol, you now have two, and this multiplication ripples through the grid to create denser, more valuable combinations.

How Mega Fission Works:

  • When a winning combination lands, the contributing symbols undergo “fission”
  • Each winning symbol divides into two matching symbols on the reels
  • The newly created symbols can extend or complete additional winning ways
  • Because more symbols now occupy the reels, the number of active ways to win climbs
  • The split symbols feed directly into the cascade engine for chained payouts

Why Mega Fission Matters:

  • More Ways Per Spin: Splitting symbols increases the symbol count on a reel, which raises the total ways to win for that cascade
  • Compounding Wins: A single base win can fission into a much larger combination, then cascade again
  • Synergy With Megaways™: The variable-ways engine and symbol splitting reinforce one another, so the best spins snowball quickly
  • Constant Engagement: Because Mega Fission can trigger on any win, even small base-game hits feel meaningful

Example Mega Fission Scenario:

  • £1.00 stake
  • Three matching alien newt symbols land across the first three reels for a modest win
  • Mega Fission splits each of those symbols, turning three into six
  • The expanded combination now stretches across more reels, increasing the payout
  • A cascade then drops fresh symbols, and the process can repeat

Mega Fission is the engine room of Astro Newts Megaways. It transforms the feel of the base game from a standard Megaways grind into a constantly evolving puzzle where every win has the potential to multiply and snowball. It is the single biggest reason to take this slot for a spin.

Fuel Cells & Planetary Free Spins: The Journey to Uranus

Collecting Fuel to Trigger the Bonus

The free spins feature in Astro Newts Megaways is wrapped in a clever progression system built around Fuel Cells. Throughout play, you collect rocket fuel, and gathering enough propels you into the free spins round — and then deeper into the galaxy, planet by planet.

How Fuel Cell Collection Works:

  • Collecting 6 fuel cells triggers the Free Spins feature
  • Once in free spins, gathering 4 more fuel cells advances you to the next planet
  • Each planet represents a higher level of the bonus with improved rewards
  • The journey progresses through five planets toward the ultimate destination: Uranus

The Planetary Levels

The free spins feature is not a single static round — it is a tiered voyage. As you accumulate fuel during the bonus, you blast off from one planet to the next, with each level offering richer potential than the last.

  • Early Planets: Standard Megaways free spins with the Mega Fission and wild features active
  • Mid Planets: Enhanced rewards as your fuel collection advances the journey
  • Uranus (Final Level): The grand prize destination, where the game switches to pay both ways

Pay Both Ways on Uranus

Reaching Uranus is the holy grail of an Astro Newts Megaways session. At this final level, wins are paid in both directions — left to right and right to left — which doubles the maximum ways to win to 235,298. With Mega Fission and wilds still in play, the combination of doubled ways and symbol splitting creates the conditions for the game’s biggest payouts and its 20,000x maximum win.

Why the Progression System Matters:

  • Sense of Adventure: The planetary journey gives free spins genuine narrative momentum
  • Escalating Rewards: Each planet you reach improves your win potential
  • The Uranus Payoff: The pay-both-ways finale is where the 20,000x dream lives
  • Replayability: Chasing the deepest planet keeps every bonus round fresh

Bonus Buy Details:
