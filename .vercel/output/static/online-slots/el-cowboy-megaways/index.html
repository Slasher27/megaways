The Megaways™ Engine: 117,649 Ways to Win

Understanding Megaways™ in El Cowboy

At the heart of El Cowboy Megaways beats the licensed Megaways™ engine, the variable-ways system that reshaped slot mathematics. Instead of fixed paylines, every reel can show a different number of symbols on each spin, and the total ways to win are recalculated dynamically. This is what gives the game its restless rhythm and its headline figure of 117,649 ways.

How Megaways™ Works in El Cowboy:

  • 6 vertical reels, each capable of showing between 2 and 7 symbols per spin
  • Ways to win are calculated by multiplying the number of symbols on each reel together
  • Maximum ways: 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 × 7 = 117,649 ways to win
  • Minimum ways: 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 64 ways to win
  • Wins pay for matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right, regardless of their position

Win Formation Principles:

  • Symbol size and exact row position do not matter — only the reel sequence counts
  • A winning combination must begin on the leftmost reel and run across consecutive reels
  • Several separate winning combinations can land and pay on a single spin
  • Every payout is expressed as a multiple of your total stake, not a per-line bet
  • The number of ways changes every spin, so no two spins feel mathematically identical

Cascading Reels

Like most Megaways™ slots, El Cowboy Megaways employs cascading reels (sometimes called tumbles or avalanches). When you land a winning combination:

  1. The symbols that formed the win are removed from the grid
  2. The symbols above them drop down to fill the empty spaces
  3. New symbols fall in from above to complete the reels
  4. If the new arrangement creates another win, it pays and the process repeats
  5. Cascades continue until no further wins land

This mechanic means a single paid spin can produce a chain of consecutive wins. Cascades become especially valuable when wild modifiers are in play, because expanding and dropping wilds can keep new combinations forming as the reels tumble.

The Bonus Reels: Action and Wilds Above and Below

Two Extra Reels That Drive the Game

The signature mechanic of El Cowboy Megaways is the pair of bonus reels that flank the main grid — one mounted along the top of the reels and one along the bottom. These are not decoration; they are the engine that powers the slot’s most exciting moments.

The Top Bonus Reel — Action Symbols:

The top bonus reel spins alongside the main grid and can land Action symbols. When an Action symbol drops in, it triggers one of the game’s wild modifiers, transforming an ordinary cascade into something far more rewarding. Because the Action reel is always active, base-game spins can erupt into modifier-driven wins at any moment.

The Bottom Bonus Reel — Wild Symbols:

The bottom bonus reel deals out wild symbols that feed directly into the main grid. Wilds substitute for any paying symbol to complete combinations, so a wild injected from the bottom reel can be the difference between a near miss and a chunky multi-way win.

Together, the two reels create a constant push-and-pull: the Action reel supplies the modifiers, the wild reel supplies the wilds, and the cascading main grid turns both into chains of wins. It is this trio working in concert that gives El Cowboy Megaways its distinctive feel.

Wild Modifiers: Expanding, Dropping and Multiplier Wilds

Three Ways the Wilds Strike

When an Action symbol lands from the top bonus reel, El Cowboy Megaways activates one of three wild modifiers. Each changes how the wilds behave and creates a different kind of win potential.

1. Expanding Wilds

Expanding wilds stretch to fill their entire reel, turning a single wild into a full column of substitutes. With a whole reel locked as wild, the odds of completing high-paying combinations across the six-reel grid soar, particularly when the full 117,649 ways are active.

2. Dropping Wilds

Dropping wilds tumble down through the grid as cascades resolve, moving into new positions and helping fresh combinations form on each tumble. Because they travel with the cascade, dropping wilds can extend a winning chain that would otherwise have fizzled out.

3. Multiplier Wilds

Multiplier wilds carry a value of x2 to x5 and apply that multiplier to any win they help complete. When a multiplier wild lands inside a high-ways combination, the result can leap dramatically — and multiple multiplier wilds in the same win compound the effect.

Why the Modifiers Matter:

  • Variety: Each modifier feels distinct, so back-to-back Action triggers rarely play out the same way
  • Synergy with cascades: Expanding and dropping wilds both feed directly into the tumble mechanic for chained wins
  • Base-game excitement: Because the modifiers fire in the base game, you do not have to reach the free spins to enjoy big moments
  • Multiplier upside: The x2-x5 multiplier wilds are the quickest route to a standout base-game payout

Free Spins: Multipliers Up to x100

Triggering the Bonus Round

The free spins are the headline bonus in El Cowboy Megaways and the most realistic path to the 10,000x max win. They are triggered by landing scatter symbols across the reels:

  • 4 scatters award 15 free spins
  • 5 scatters award 20 free spins
  • 6 scatters award 25 free spins

Random Win Multipliers

What sets the free spins apart is the random win multiplier that activates during the round. On qualifying spins the game applies a multiplier drawn from a wide range — x2 all the way up to x100 — to the wins you land. When a large multi-way combination coincides with a high multiplier, the payout can rocket toward the game’s ceiling.

How the Free Spins Build Big Wins:

  • The Megaways™ engine remains fully active, so the full 117,649 ways are in play
  • The bonus reels continue to feed Action symbols and wilds into the grid
  • Cascades chain multiple wins together, and the random multiplier can apply to those wins
  • A high multiplier landing on a big cascade is exactly how the 10,000x potential is reached

Example:

  • A random x100 multiplier is in play on that spin
  • The result soars far beyond what the base game could deliver, demonstrating why the free spins are the slot’s beating heart

Bonus Buy: Skip Straight to the Free Spins

For players who do not want to wait for scatters to align, El Cowboy Megaways offers a bonus buy feature. Rather than spinning for the trigger, you can pay a multiple of your stake to enter the free spins immediately. The game provides four buy tiers, ranging from cheaper entries to premium purchases that offer enhanced starting conditions.

Things to Keep in Mind:

  • Cost vs. value: Higher tiers cost more but can offer better odds of a rewarding round
  • Volatility still applies: A bought bonus is not a guaranteed win — the high variance remains
  • Regulatory restrictions: The bonus buy is not available in some regulated markets, such as the United Kingdom, where buy features are prohibited
  • Bankroll discipline: Because each buy is a sizeable chunk of stake, budget carefully before committing

The bonus buy is a convenience feature rather than a shortcut to profit, but for players who enjoy the free spins it removes the grind of waiting for scatters to arrive organically.
