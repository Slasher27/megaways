Game Features

Megaways™ & Reaction Mechanics

Spicy Meatballs Megaways™ utilizes BTG’s iconic Megaways™ engine, delivering dynamic gameplay where the number of ways to win changes with every spin based on the symbols appearing on each reel. This creates a constantly shifting play field with up to 117,649 ways to win when all reels reach maximum height.

Megaways™ Basics:

  • 6 reels with 2-7 symbols per reel
  • Horizontal top reel above reels 2-5 (standard Megaways™ layout)
  • Ways to win = symbols per reel multiplied together
  • Maximum: 7×7×7×7×7×7 = 117,649 ways

Reaction Mechanic (Cascading Wins):

The heart of Spicy Meatballs is its reaction system, which keeps the action “hot, hot, hot”:

  1. Winning Combinations: When you land a win, all winning symbols are removed
  2. Symbols Cascade: New symbols drop from above to fill empty positions, “like a shower of Parmesan flakes”
  3. Multiple Reactions: If new symbols create additional wins, the process repeats
  4. Wild Meatballs Persist: Crucially, Wild Meatballs stay on screen throughout reactions (detailed next)
  5. Unlimited Chains: Reactions continue until no new wins form

Wild Hat Symbol:

Keep an eye out for the Wild Hat symbol, which appears on reels 2-5. This classic wild substitutes for all symbols except the Wild Meatball, adding extra substitution power to your winning combinations. Think of it as the chef’s hat – an essential ingredient in the kitchen!

Strategic Importance:

The Megaways™ + Reaction combination is what makes building Wild Meatball multipliers so powerful. Each reaction gives Wild Meatballs another opportunity to descend rows (increasing multipliers), substitute in new winning combinations, and extend the reaction chain further. A single initial win can trigger 5, 10, or even 15+ consecutive reactions, with Wild Meatball multipliers climbing higher with each cascade.

Wild Meatball - The Zesty Hero

The Wild Meatball is the undisputed star of this culinary adventure – a flavorful innovation that transforms standard Megaways™ gameplay into something truly special. These spicy spheres combine wild substitution with progressive multipliers and persistence mechanics for extraordinary win potential.

How Wild Meatballs Work:

Appearance:

  • Wild Meatballs can appear on reels 2, 3, 4, and 5 (not on reels 1 or 6)
  • Can drop during any spin and any reaction
  • Multiple Wild Meatballs can be active simultaneously

Multiplier System:

This is where the magic happens:

  1. Starting Position: When a Wild Meatball first appears, it displays a multiplier shown in the pot above its reel
  2. Initial Multiplier: The starting multiplier varies (typically x1-x3 in base game)
  3. Descending Multiplier Growth: As the Wild Meatball cascades down during reactions, the multiplier increases by +1 for every row descended
  4. Unlimited Growth: No cap on multiplier values

Example Multiplier Progression:

  • Wild Meatball appears at top of reel 3 with x2 multiplier
  • Reaction occurs, Meatball drops 1 row → multiplier becomes x3
  • Another reaction, drops 1 more row → multiplier becomes x4
  • Continues cascading down, eventually reaching x7 at bottom of reel
  • All wins involving this Wild Meatball are multiplied by current value!

Persistence Mechanic:

The game-changing element:

  • Wild Meatballs remain on screen throughout the entire reaction chain
  • They don’t disappear when wins are paid
  • They continue substituting in each new symbol configuration
  • Only disappear when:
    • They reach the bottom of the reel, OR
    • The reaction chain ends (no new wins)

Multiple Wild Meatballs:

When multiple Wild Meatballs appear:

  • Each has its own independent multiplier
  • Each descends at its own pace through reactions
  • All contribute to wins simultaneously
  • Multipliers can combine in spectacular ways

Strategic Impact:

The Wild Meatball mechanic creates incredible variance:

  • Early Appearance: Wild Meatballs appearing at the top of reels have maximum room to descend and build multipliers
  • Reaction Chains: Extended reaction sequences = more descending = higher multipliers
  • Multiple Meatballs: Having 2-3 Wild Meatballs active with growing multipliers creates explosive potential
  • Positioning: Meatballs on central reels (3 and 4) participate in more winning combinations

The brilliance of this feature is that it rewards extended play sequences – the longer your reaction chains, the more powerful your Wild Meatballs become, creating a snowball effect where wins beget bigger wins.

Triggering Free Spins:

The trigger mechanic is unique and directly tied to reaction chains:

Base Game Trigger:

Meatball Multiplier Carryover:

This is crucial:

  • Any Wild Meatball multipliers active when Free Spins trigger carry over!

Free Spins Gameplay:
