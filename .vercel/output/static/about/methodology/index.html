Our Review Methodology

Transparency is important to us. Here's exactly how we test, evaluate, and rate Megaways slots and online casinos.

How We Review Slots

1

Initial Research & Data Collection

We start by gathering official data from the game provider and casino partners:

  • Official RTP (Return to Player) percentage
  • Volatility rating from the provider
  • Maximum win potential
  • Minimum and maximum bet limits
  • Number of paylines or ways to win
  • Release date and provider information
2

Hands-On Gameplay Testing

We play each slot extensively to understand the actual player experience:

  • Minimum 100-200 spins at various bet levels
  • Trigger bonus features multiple times
  • Test bonus buy feature (if available)
  • Evaluate graphics, animations, and sound quality
  • Assess mobile compatibility and performance
  • Monitor base game hit frequency and win sizes
3

Feature Analysis

We analyze all game mechanics and special features:

  • Megaways mechanic implementation and variance
  • Bonus round frequency and potential
  • Wild symbols, multipliers, and modifiers
  • Cascading wins and reaction mechanics
  • Free spins features and retriggers
  • Unique mechanics specific to the slot
4

Rating & Review Writing

We compile our findings into a comprehensive review:

  • Overall rating (0-5 stars) based on all criteria
  • Detailed breakdown of pros and cons
  • Volatility and RTP analysis
  • Feature descriptions with examples
  • Where to play recommendations
  • Final verdict for different player types

Slot Rating Criteria

Our star ratings (0-5) are based on the following weighted criteria:

RTP & Volatility (30%)

Fair RTP (96%+) and appropriate volatility balance for the game type.

Features & Entertainment (25%)

Innovative features, exciting bonus rounds, and engaging gameplay.

Max Win Potential (20%)

Realistic chance of big wins relative to volatility.

Design & Usability (15%)

Graphics quality, mobile optimization, and user interface.

Hit Frequency (10%)

Balance between base game wins and bonus triggers.

Note: Ratings are subjective and reflect our team's assessment. Different players value different aspects, so we always provide detailed pros/cons to help you decide.

Keeping Reviews Current

The online gambling industry changes rapidly. To ensure our reviews stay accurate and helpful, we:

  • Monitor RTP changes: Providers occasionally adjust RTP settings, and we update our reviews accordingly
  • Track casino updates: We watch for changes in payment methods, licenses, and terms
  • Review player feedback: We consider user reviews and complaints when updating ratings
  • Re-test periodically: Major reviews are re-tested every 6-12 months
  • Display update dates: Every review shows when it was last updated

Editorial Independence

While we participate in affiliate programs, our reviews are never influenced by commercial relationships. We rate casinos and slots based solely on their merits.

If a casino offers a poor player experience, unfair terms, or slow payouts, we'll say so—regardless of commission rates. Your trust is more valuable to us than any affiliate payment.
