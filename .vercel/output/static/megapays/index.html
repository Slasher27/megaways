Megapays Slots

Developer: Relax Gaming (BTG License)
Released: 2021

What is Megapays?

Megapays is an innovative slot mechanic that combines Megaways gameplay with progressive jackpots. Developed by Relax Gaming under license from Big Time Gaming in 2021, Megapays takes the excitement of variable reels and adds the potential for massive, life-changing jackpot wins.

How Megapays Works

Megaways Foundation

Megapays builds on the classic Megaways mechanic:

  • 2-7 symbols per reel on each spin
  • Up to 117,649 ways to win
  • Cascading wins (reactions)
  • Variable reel sizes create unpredictable gameplay

Progressive Jackpot System

The key innovation is the integrated jackpot mechanic:

  • Four progressive jackpots: Mini, Minor, Major, Mega
  • Jackpot symbols collect and trigger bonus rounds
  • Community jackpots shared across all casinos
  • Ever-increasing prizes until someone wins

Jackpot Trigger

Special jackpot symbols appear during gameplay:

  1. Collect jackpot symbols during base game and free spins
  2. Fill the jackpot meter
  3. Trigger the jackpot bonus game
  4. Spin to win one of four progressive jackpots

Key Features

Four-Tier Jackpot System

  • Mini Jackpot: Smallest, most frequent
  • Minor Jackpot: Modest wins
  • Major Jackpot: Substantial prizes
  • Mega Jackpot: Life-changing amounts (can exceed €1,000,000)

Megaways Gameplay

All the excitement of traditional Megaways:

  • Dynamic reel configurations
  • Cascading wins with unlimited potential
  • Free spins bonus rounds
  • High volatility gameplay

Jackpot Collection Feature

  • Special jackpot symbols appear randomly
  • Collection mechanic builds anticipation
  • Multiple collection paths to jackpot bonus
  • Can trigger during base game or free spins

Frequently Asked Questions

What’s the difference between Megapays and Megaways?

Megapays adds four progressive jackpots to standard Megaways gameplay. You get all the Megaways features plus the chance to win life-changing jackpot amounts.

Who created Megapays?

Megapays was developed by Relax Gaming under license from Big Time Gaming, combining Relax’s jackpot expertise with BTG’s Megaways mechanic.

How big can the Mega jackpot get?

The Mega jackpot has no cap and grows until won. It has exceeded €1,000,000 on multiple occasions across the Relax Gaming network.

Does Megapays have lower RTP than Megaways?

The base game RTP is lower because a portion of each bet funds the progressive jackpots. However, the total theoretical RTP (including jackpot contributions) is comparable to standard slots.

Can I win a jackpot in demo mode?

No, progressive jackpots are only available when playing with real money. Demo mode typically uses fixed jackpot values for display purposes.

Are the jackpots local or network-wide?

Megapays jackpots are network-wide progressives, meaning they’re shared across all casinos offering the game, allowing them to grow much larger than local jackpots.
